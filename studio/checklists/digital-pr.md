# Digital PR checklist

Each item is recorded in `self_qa.results` (or in the review findings) with
pass or fail and evidence. `auto` items can be checked mechanically; `rubric`
items need judgement.

| # | Check | Severity if failed | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outlets are relevant (luxury bridal, travel, destination) with authority data from a connector | major | auto |
| 2 | Each outlet names the target page for the link | major | auto |
| 3 | No paid links or link schemes | blocker | auto |
| 4 | Pitches marked DRAFT and nothing sent | blocker | auto |
| 5 | Pitches use only facts in facts.json | blocker | auto |
| 6 | Angles are newsworthy and specific, not generic | major | rubric |
