# Local SEO checklist

Each item is recorded in `self_qa.results` (or in the review findings) with
pass or fail and evidence. `auto` items can be checked mechanically; `rubric`
items need judgement.

| # | Check | Severity if failed | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Applicability decided and justified | blocker | auto |
| 2 | If applicable: NAP consistency checked with a connector | major | auto |
| 3 | If applicable: GBP actions specific to this page | minor | auto |
| 4 | Review responses in brand voice | minor | rubric |
