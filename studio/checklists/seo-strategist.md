# SEO Strategist checklist

Each item is recorded in `self_qa.results` (or in the review findings) with
pass or fail and evidence. `auto` items can be checked mechanically; `rubric`
items need judgement.

| # | Check | Severity if failed | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keyword volumes and difficulty come from Semrush or DataForSEO, with the source recorded | blocker | auto |
| 2 | Every market in config.json was checked | major | auto |
| 3 | Primary keyword intent matches the page type (package pages: commercial investigation) | major | rubric |
| 4 | No cannibalisation with another weddedwonderland.com page, or the risk is recorded with a plan | major | auto |
| 5 | Question keywords come from People Also Ask or related searches, not invented | major | auto |
| 6 | Every outline section lists the questions it must answer and the fact ids it needs | blocker | auto |
| 7 | link_plan follows studio/standards/linking.md (region hub, country hub, Concierge, 2 or more comparables) | major | auto |
| 8 | Link targets are confirmed to exist, or marked unconfirmed | major | auto |
| 9 | Title draft 50 to 60 characters and meta description draft 140 to 160 characters | minor | auto |
| 10 | Persona top questions for the target audience are covered by the outline | major | rubric |
| 11 | Any change to the reference section set has a recorded reason | minor | auto |
