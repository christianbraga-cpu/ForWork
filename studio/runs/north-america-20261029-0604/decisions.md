# Decisions: North America run (fast mode)

Standing decisions carried over from the Portugal run, plus this page's own.

## D1. Official sources win
Government sources of the place where the marriage happens outrank embassy summaries, the
research doc and third parties.

## D2. Live page: hold, do not noindex
The page is live and indexed. Lines that depend on a fact needing sign-off are written as
"[APPLY AFTER SIGN-OFF: <ids>, <owner>]" followed by "[FALLBACK UNTIL THEN]" on the next line.
No noindex.

## D3. No invented business claims
studio/brand-answers.md has no approved rows, so: no fee model, currency, named advisor,
on-the-day promise, positioning line, proof numbers or Privileges benefits. Use the brand
sentence only in its confirmed wording (facts.json "Brand entity sentence"), once in the hero
and once in the advisor section.

## D4. Headings
Use the research doc's section plan headings word for word (title case as written there).

## D5. Market order
US first (almost all demand), then AU and UK together (long-haul logistics and visas). The
Las Vegas line serves AU demand (210 a month in AU).

## D6. Mexico and the Caribbean
Not part of this page's places. One cross-link to /destinations/latin-america-caribbean.
Remove every claim that North America covers Mexico or the Caribbean.

## D7. Child pages are 404
/north-america/united-states, /canada, /united-states/california and /united-states/hawaii
are not linked until they return 200. Place cards route to the enquiry form with the place
prefilled, and list the planned URL in NOTES.md.
