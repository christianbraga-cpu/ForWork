## 5\. Facts and data

**Legal (official sources; stated by passport):**

  

  - **All couples:** the marriage process can be started online or at any Registo Civil, and must be organised at least 1 month before the date; the marriage is valid once registered. <https://justica.gov.pt/Registos/Civil/Casamento>
  - **AU passport, marriage:** some countries ask for proof you are not already married; the usual documents are a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI) or registry certificates. DFAT can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only one issued in their own country, so couples ask the destination's embassy whether they need one, where it must be issued and whether it needs an apostille. <https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas>
  - **AU passport, CNI:** at least one applicant must be an Australian citizen or permanent resident; issued through Australian Passport Offices, embassies and by mail; fees apply. <https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services/how-get-certificate-no-impediment-marriage>
  - **AU passport, recognition:** an overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia but is in most cases recognised if valid where it took place and valid under Australian law. Same getting-married-overseas URL.
  - **AU passport, entry:** Schengen short stays without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180; fingerprints and photo under the EES at the border; ETIAS has no confirmed start date. <https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/portugal>
  - **US passport, marriage:** the US Embassy in Lisbon does not issue a certificate of no impediment; a US citizen can sign a statement of eligibility to marry before a consular officer (notarial appointment, $50 or the euro equivalent per notarisation) for the Civil Registry. A licence is needed for civil and religious weddings; processing usually takes at least four weeks; the wedding must take place within six months of approval; residence is not required; birth certificate issued within six months, apostilled by the issuing state and translated; two witnesses on the day. <https://pt.usembassy.gov/marriage-in-portugal/> (curl 15 Oct: four weeks, six months, $50 and residence lines confirmed) These conditions are stated for US citizens only; do not apply them to AU or UK couples.
  - **US passport, entry:** no visa for 90 days or less; passport valid 3 months beyond departure with 2 blank pages. <https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/International-Travel-Country-Information-Pages/Portugal.html>
  - **UK passport, marriage:** gov.uk has no Portugal-specific marriage page (gov.uk/marriage-abroad/y/portugal resolves to the general guide, checked 15 Oct). The general rule: check local law with the registering authority; some countries ask for proof you are free to marry, such as a CNI or a marital status affirmation, applied for and paid through the FCDO online service, which needs a valid British passport. <https://www.gov.uk/marriages-civil-partnerships-abroad/get-documents-for-a-marriage-or-civil-partnership-abroad>
  - **UK passport, entry:** visa-free in Schengen for up to 90 days in any 180; EES may record fingerprints and a photo. <https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/portugal/entry-requirements>
  - **Planner source, verify on justica.gov.pt before use:** civil and Catholic weddings are both legally recognised; other faiths need a civil ceremony first; the Anglican churches in Lisbon, Estoril, Oporto and the Algarve and the Scottish church in Lisbon are not licensed for marriages; same-sex civil weddings permitted; minimum age 16 with written consent of both parents under 18. <https://www.weddingportugal.com/how-to-plan-your-wedding-in-portugal/> (AU \#4, 15 Oct)

  

**Venues and settings:**

  

  - Parques de Sintra event venues: Monserrate, National Palace of Sintra, Pena, Queluz, Portuguese School of Equestrian Art; Queluz and the National Palace of Sintra may be commandeered for state events; nothing may be fixed to the heritage. <https://www.parquesdesintra.pt/en/event-venues-1/> (curl 15 Oct: venue list, state-event cancellation and no-affixing rule confirmed)
  - Pousadas host weddings in castles, convents and monasteries. <https://www.pousadas.pt/en/weddings-events>
  - All beaches in Portugal are public; most Lisbon-area venues are 15 to 30 minutes from the centre. <https://www.lisbonweddingplanner.com/wedding-venues> (FAQ)
  - Beach weddings such as in the Algarve need extra permits; Forte da Cruz tent seats 200 to 250 and is 20 minutes from Lisbon airport; Palácio do Freixo is an 18th-century manor on the Douro. <https://www.theknot.com/content/destination-wedding-portugal>
  - Exclusivity means paying for unoccupied rooms; buy-out is often separate from the venue fee; fees may be per day or per guest with a minimum. <https://www.helloportugalconcepts.com/post/a-comprehensive-guide-to-wedding-venues-in-portugal> (2024)
  - Algarve: about 200 km of coastline; Lagos, Tavira, Silves and the Ria Formosa named. <https://www.ionianweddings.co.uk/destinations/portugal/>

  

**Season and travel:**

  

  - Spring and autumn are best; summer hot and peak-priced; Sintra has a cooler microclimate; direct US flights to Lisbon from Boston, Chicago, Miami, New York, Philadelphia, San Francisco and Washington DC; the Douro is 3 hours north of Lisbon. <https://www.theknot.com/content/destination-wedding-portugal> (2024; verify routes for 2027)
  - Flight times from Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, London and Dubai: no ranker gives them; source from airline schedules.
