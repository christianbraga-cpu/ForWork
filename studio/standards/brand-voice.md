# Wedded Wonderland brand voice

Used by: copywriter (writing), qa-reviewer (brand gate), persona panel (indirectly).

## Who we sound like

A well-travelled friend who has planned hundreds of weddings abroad: calm,
specific, quietly confident. We show rather than claim. We never oversell.

## Principles

1. **Specific beats superlative.** "Villa terraces facing San Gimignano's towers, 2 km
   from the old town" beats "breathtaking views".
2. **Proof beats promise.** Name the space, the capacity, the inclusion, the real
   wedding. If we cannot prove it, we do not say it.
3. **Calm beats urgent.** No countdowns, no "book now before it's gone", no
   exclamation marks in body copy.
4. **The couple is the subject.** "Your guests wake up on the hill" more than "We offer accommodation".
5. **Honest about what is unconfirmed.** "Confirmed for your date by your Wedded Advisor"
   is on brand. Inventing a certainty is not.
6. **Plain words.** Write for a couple reading on their phone at 10pm. Short sentences.
   One idea per paragraph.
7. **Reassure early.** Our couples are usually at the very start (they rate their
   planning 1 to 3 out of 10), and most are planning from another country. Explain
   what end-to-end means and who their contact is before asking them to enquire.

## Mechanics

- Australian English (colour, organise, centre, programme).
- Prices: "From AUD 38,550". Never a bare "$".
- Numbers: numerals for capacities, distances and prices ("20 to 300 guests", "2 km").
- Ranges: "20 to 300", never with a dash.
- No em dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, or brackets.
- Headings in sentence case.
- Brand names exactly: Wedded Wonderland, Wedded Concierge, Wedded Advisor, Wedded Privileges.
- Never mention competitors or marketplaces by name on the page.
- Banned words and phrases: `banned-phrases.txt`.

## Rubric (qa-reviewer scores 1 to 5)

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Generic, could describe any venue | Some concrete detail | Every paragraph has a fact a competitor page could not copy |
| Proof | Claims without support | Some facts and inclusions | Claims backed by named spaces, numbers, real weddings, press |
| Tone | Salesy, urgent or gushing | Mostly calm, a few lapses | Calm, warm, confident throughout |
| Clarity | Long, dense, jargon | Readable with effort | Effortless on a phone, scannable headings |
| Reassurance | Asks for the enquiry before building trust | Some process and contact info | Process, contact person, inclusions and next step are clear before the form |
| Honesty | Unconfirmed facts stated as certain | Mostly hedged | Every unconfirmed fact clearly hedged, nothing overstated |

Any score below 3 is a major finding. An off-brand or unsupported claim is a blocker.
