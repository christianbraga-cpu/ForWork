# Linking standard

Owners: SEO Strategist (plan), Copywriter (placement), Technical SEO (build and
crawl), Digital PR (inbound).

## Internal links

### Site structure

Three connected hub types. Every page belongs to a cluster.

```
                 Home
       ┌──────────┼───────────┐
 Destinations   Services    Real weddings
 (country, region hubs)  (Concierge, planning)  (proof)
     │             │             │
 Packages / venues ◄──► Planning guides ◄──► Real weddings
```

### Rules

1. A package or venue page links to: its region hub, its country hub, Wedded
   Concierge, at least 2 nearby or comparable packages, and real weddings at that
   venue or in that region where they exist.
2. A real wedding links back to its venue or package, destination and the services used.
3. A planning guide links to its destination hub and the enquiry path.
4. No orphan pages: at least 3 internal links point to every page. The brief lists
   `should_receive_links_from`, and NOTES.md records which pages still need updating.
5. Key pages are 3 clicks from the home page or fewer. Breadcrumbs on every page.
6. Anchors are descriptive and varied ("Tuscany wedding packages", "other Tuscan
   venues"). Never "click here", "read more" as the only text, or the same exact
   anchor on every page.
7. Body links count more than navigation. Place 3 to 8 contextual links in long pages.
8. Every page has one clear next step (enquiry or Concierge).
9. Do not link to URLs that are not confirmed to exist. A guessed slug is
   `unconfirmed` and listed in NOTES.md.

## Outbound links

Link out sparingly, only where it helps the reader trust the page:
- Official sources: government marriage requirements, embassy pages, tourism boards.
- The venue's own site only if the brief allows it (default: no, because enquiries
  route to Wedded Concierge).
- Press features about our weddings.

Never link to competitors, marketplaces or directories that rank for our terms.
Use `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`. Add `sponsored` for paid or affiliate links and
`nofollow` for anything we do not vouch for.

## Inbound links (Digital PR)

- Quality and relevance over volume. Luxury bridal, travel and destination media,
  tourism boards, venue partner pages.
- Point links at the page that should rank (packages, hubs), not only the home page.
- No paid links, link schemes, PBNs or link swaps.
- All pitches are drafts until the Project Lead approves them.
