# The Paying Parent: Margaret

**Snapshot.** Margaret, 62, retired school principal, Mosman, Sydney. Funding
most of her daughter's wedding in Italy, around AUD 250K. 90 guests, about 30
over 65, including her mother (88) who uses a walking frame. Has never
organised anything overseas.

**Goals**
- Her daughter is happy and nobody is embarrassed.
- Older guests are safe, comfortable and not stranded.
- She knows where the money goes and roughly what the total will be.
- One responsible person she can call.

**Fears**
1. Hidden costs, mark-ups and currency surprises.
2. The marriage not being legally valid in Australia.
3. Elderly guests stranded, overheated or unable to manage stairs, boats or cobblestones.
4. A company that disappears after the deposit, or has nobody on the ground.
5. Bad weather with no plan.

**Knowledge level.** Low on destination weddings. Does not know what a Nulla
Osta, an apostille or a room block is. Reads carefully, often twice.

**Trust signals.** Named people, written fee agreements, AUD pricing, a clear
wet-weather plan, accessibility detail, official sources for legal claims,
years in business and supplier relationships.

**Turn-offs.** Influencer tone, vague superlatives, no prices at all, prices
without explanation, anything that sounds like a sales funnel, jargon.

**Reading behaviour.** Desktop or iPad, reads the whole piece, forwards the
link to her husband with questions, may phone before filling in a form.

**Would enquire when.** Her top 2 fears are clearly answered with evidence and
she can see who she would be dealing with and how money is handled.

**Evidence behind traits** (replace with CRM evidence when available)
- Brand pages: "A fee agreed in writing before any work begins", "Suppliers paid at standardised industry rates" (mark-up fear).
- Brand pages: "Marrying legally... documents translated and legalised" (legal fear).
- Brand pages: wet-weather plans on package pages (weather fear).
