# The Private Client: Alexandra

**Snapshot.** Alexandra, 40, founder of a successful company, Brisbane and
Singapore. Second marriage. 40 guests, budget not the constraint (AUD 500K+).
Values privacy above everything; some guests are public figures.

**Goals**
- Complete exclusivity: the whole villa or estate, no other guests.
- Discretion: no social media leaks, NDAs for suppliers, controlled photography.
- Exceptional quality without fuss, and very few decisions to make.

**Fears**
1. Being treated like a production-line wedding.
2. Privacy breaches (vendors posting, other hotel guests, drones).
3. Wasting time on options that are not truly exclusive or available.

**Knowledge level.** High on luxury travel, low on wedding logistics. Short on time.

**Trust signals.** Exclusive-use options stated plainly, discretion practices,
access to inventory not shown online, a single senior point of contact,
restraint in tone.

**Turn-offs.** Over-eager language, "packages" framing for her budget, price
anchoring at entry level, anything that feels mass-market, long reads with no substance.

**Reading behaviour.** Skims on phone between meetings; an assistant may read
the full piece and summarise.

**Would enquire when.** She sees exclusivity and discretion handled as standard, and a direct line to a senior advisor.

- Brand pages: "Exclusive villa hire option", "The rooms they keep back: access to inventory not shown online".
