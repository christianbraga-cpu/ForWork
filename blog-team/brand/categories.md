# CMS categories

**The first category in `categories[]` is always the primary category.** In
most CMSs (including WordPress with Yoast or RankMath) ticking a category does
not make it primary; the primary is set separately with "Make primary". The
upload prompt sets it explicitly and checks the breadcrumb.

## Working category list (TO CONFIRM against the CMS)

| Category | Use as primary when |
|---|---|
| Italy Weddings | The article is mainly about Italy or an Italian region or venue |
| Greece Weddings | Mainly Greece |
| Bali and Indonesia Weddings | Mainly Bali or Indonesia |
| France Weddings | Mainly France |
| Destination Guides | Multi-destination or "where to marry" pieces |
| Planning Advice | Process, logistics, legal, budget pieces not tied to one country |
| Guest Experience | Travel, accommodation, transfers and guest care |
| Real Weddings | Case studies (only with client consent) |
| Venues | Venue round-ups and comparisons |

## Rules

- 1 primary + up to 2 secondary categories per article.
- The primary category should match the article's link-map cluster.
- Tags: 3 to 6, lower case, destinations and themes (e.g. tuscany, villa weddings, guest logistics).
