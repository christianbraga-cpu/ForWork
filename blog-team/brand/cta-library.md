# CTA library

The brief picks one primary CTA and optionally one soft CTA. The writer uses
the copy below as written (light edits for context are fine; never change the
promise). In the CMS, CTAs are placed with the shared CTA block, never typed
into the body.

| ID | Use when | Heading | Body | Button | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `concierge_advisor` | Default for consideration and decision pieces | Speak with a Wedded Advisor | One person plans everything with you from Australia, in your time zone, with the fee agreed in writing before any work begins. | Speak with your Wedded Advisor | `/concierge` |
| `package_enquiry` | Article centres on a venue we have a package for | Enquire about [Venue] | Your Wedded Advisor checks availability, costs it on your dates in AUD and holds the date once you agree. | Enquire now | `/packages/<country>/<region>/<venue>#enquire` |
| `destination_explore` | Awareness pieces, early-stage readers | Explore weddings in [Destination] | See venues, seasons, getting there and marrying legally, all in one place. | Explore [Destination] | `/destinations/<country>` |
| `packages_browse` | Comparison or round-up pieces | Compare wedding packages | See what each venue includes and where each offer starts, in AUD. | See the packages | `/packages` or `/packages/<country>` |
| `marketplace_diy` | Secondary only, for readers planning themselves | Prefer to plan it yourself? | Browse venues and vendor profiles and contact professionals through the Wedded Marketplace. | Browse vendors | `/marketplace` |
| `privileges` | Accommodation and guest-stay pieces | Arrive with Wedded Privileges | Benefits come with every room your guests book, through invitation-only hotel programs. Benefits vary by property. | See Wedded Privileges | `/privileges` |

## Soft CTA lines (mid-article, one sentence)

- "Your Wedded Advisor can hold a room block for your guests while you decide."
- "If you would rather not manage this yourself, a Wedded Advisor can [task] for you."
- "Every price we quote is in AUD, so there is no currency surprise."
