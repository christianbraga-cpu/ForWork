## Rules

- Every article links **up** to its destination hub and to 1 service page (usually `/concierge`).
- Every article links **across** to 2 or 3 related articles or packages when they exist.
- Anchor text describes the target page; never "click here".
- One exact-match anchor per target keyword site-wide: check the "owned" column.
- After publishing, list 2 or 3 existing pages that should link to the new one (`retro_links`).
