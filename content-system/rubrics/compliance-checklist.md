# Compliance checklist (editor stage)

Australian Consumer Law and brand rules. Any **No** is a blocking issue.

## Pricing (ACL: misleading or deceptive conduct, component pricing)
- [ ] Every price is shown as "From AUD x" with what it covers, or a note that the Wedded Advisor costs it on the couple's dates
- [ ] No "all inclusive", "no hidden costs", "best price", "cheapest" unless proven in the source pack
- [ ] Every price is listed in `needs_signoff` for Nic
- [ ] Comparison prices are sourced and dated

## Claims
- [ ] No statistics (weddings planned, years, satisfaction rates) unless sourced on a live page
- [ ] No awards, "best", "leading", "number one" without a cited source
- [ ] Testimonials and real weddings: couple consent is recorded in the source pack

## Legal and travel information
- [ ] Marriage legality, visa and document information is attributed to an official source (embassy, government) with an "as at <month year>" date
- [ ] Wording says the Wedded Advisor confirms current requirements, rather than giving legal advice

## Privacy
- [ ] No guest or couple personal details beyond what consent covers
- [ ] No children identifiable in images without consent

## Brand
- [ ] No competitor branding, copy or images
- [ ] No venue direct contact details (enquiries go to Wedded Concierge)
