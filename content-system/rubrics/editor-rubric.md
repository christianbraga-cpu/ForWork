# Editor rubric

Score each criterion 1 to 5. **Pass = average ≥ 4.0 and no criterion below 3 and no blocking issue.**

| # | Criterion | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Voice** | Restrained, specific, Australian English, no banned phrases, no em dashes | Clichés, superlatives, salesy |
| 2 | **Expertise** | 2+ insider details from the source pack that competitors don't have | Generic, could be any site |
| 3 | **Accuracy** | Every factual claim maps to a `fact_id` in the source pack or is a `[[VERIFY]]` item | Unsourced numbers or claims |
| 4 | **SEO fit** | Primary keyword in title, first 100 words and one H2, reads naturally; H2s match the outline's questions | Keyword stuffing or missing |
| 5 | **Commercial link** | Shows naturally why one Wedded Advisor / Wedded Concierge matters; links to a service page | No link to a service, or a hard sell |
| 6 | **Structure** | Key Takeaways present, short paragraphs, scannable, FAQ if briefed | Walls of text, no takeaways |
| 7 | **Brief fit** | Meets the brief's goal, reader, angle, must-include list, word count ±10% | Off-angle or missing must-includes |

## Blocking issues (fail regardless of score)
- Any banned phrase or em dash left in
- A price, capacity or legal statement with no source and no `[[VERIFY]]`
- A competitor brand named (outside an approved citation)
- An image slot pointing to a `reference-only` image
- Originality: any passage of 12+ consecutive words matching a scraped competitor page, or close paraphrase of a whole paragraph
- A compliance failure (see `compliance-checklist.md`)

## Edit instructions format
Each edit: `location` (heading + paragraph number), `issue`, `fix` (the replacement text, or exact instruction). The writer applies edits exactly; it doesn't rewrite unrelated parts.
