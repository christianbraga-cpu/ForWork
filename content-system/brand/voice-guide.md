# Voice guide

## In one line
A well-travelled friend who has planned hundreds of weddings abroad, speaking calmly and precisely to someone about to spend a great deal of money.

## Principles

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **Specific detail over adjectives.** "Terraces face San Gimignano's towers" | "Breathtaking views" |
| **Restraint.** Let the place do the work | Stacked superlatives, exclamation marks |
| **Honest trade-offs.** "Villas are private, but most have an 11pm music curfew" | Pretending every option is perfect |
| **Insider knowledge.** Logistics only a planner knows | Generic tips found on any listicle |
| **Money talked about plainly.** Ranges in AUD, what's included | "Contact us for pricing" with nothing else |
| **The couple's point of view.** "You", "your guests" | "We are passionate about…" |
| **Short paragraphs** (2 to 4 lines), clear H2s that answer real questions | Walls of text |

## Tone dial
- **Warm, not gushing.** One emotional line per section at most.
- **Confident, not salesy.** One clear invitation to speak with a Wedded Advisor, placed where a question has just been raised.
- **Expert, not technical.** Explain legal or logistic terms the first time.

## Mechanics
- Australian English: colour, organise, travelling, programme (for events), metres.
- **Never use em dashes.** Use a full stop, comma, colon or brackets.
- Numbers: numerals for 10+ and for all prices, distances, guest counts, times. "From AUD 38,550". "20 to 300 guests" (not 20–300 in body copy).
- Times: 11pm, 4.30pm.
- Distances: "2 km from San Gimignano", "about 1 hour by car from Florence Airport" (only if sourced).
- Headings: sentence case.
- Brand names exactly as in `brand-facts.md`.

## Structure of a typical post
1. **Intro (60 to 120 words):** the reader's real question, and a hint of the insider angle.
2. **Key Takeaways:** exactly 4 bullets, the most crucial points of the whole article, each a single concise sentence (a concrete fact or decision).
3. **Body H2s** that match search questions (from the SEO outline).
4. **One real-world detail per section** (from the source pack).
5. **FAQ** (3 to 6 questions) if the SEO brief asks for it.
6. **Close** that invites a conversation with a Wedded Advisor.

## Example rewrite
- ✗ "Say I do in a breathtaking fairytale setting where dreams come true!"
- ✓ "The ceremony terrace faces west, so a 5pm service in September lands in the softest light of the day."
