# Brand facts

The only brand claims agents may state as fact. Anything else about the business is a `[[VERIFY]]` item.

## Names (use exactly)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Wedded Wonderland** | The brand and website (weddedwonderland.com) |
| **Wedded Concierge** | The end-to-end destination wedding planning service. Also called Wonderland Concierge on the live site (`/wonderland-concierge`). |
| **Wedded Advisor** | The one named person who plans with the couple from first idea to the last dance |
| **Wedded Privileges** | Benefits on every room the couple's guests book |

## How Wedded Concierge works (5 steps, from the live package pages)

1. **Tell us anything.** Your date, your guest list and the feeling you want. Nothing is too early or too vague.
2. **Meet your Wedded Advisor, and agree the fee.** One person who stays with you to the end. The fee is agreed in writing before any work begins.
3. **See real options, and choose.** Venues, dates and costs that are actually free when you want them.
4. **We contract and hold.** Suppliers are paid at standardised industry rates, so you are never quoted a marked-up price.
5. **You show up.** Someone on the ground looks after the days that matter.

## Claims we can make
- One Wedded Advisor, start to finish, as a single point of contact.
- Advisor works in the couple's time zone and quotes in AUD for Australian couples.
- The fee is agreed in writing before any work begins.
- The advisor confirms inclusions in writing before the couple commits.
- Guest room blocks held by the advisor, with Wedded Privileges on every room.
- Plans venue, travel, vendors and payments, anywhere in the world (from `/wonderland-concierge`).

## Claims that always need checking
- Any specific price, "From AUD" figure or comparison price: **Nic signs off.**
- "17 years" of relationships, number of weddings planned, or any other statistic: `[[VERIFY]]` unless it appears on a live page the editor has checked.
- Any partner exclusivity or award.

## Pricing rules
- Always "From AUD 38,550", never a bare number or another currency only.
- Say what the From price covers (guest count, date type) or that the Wedded Advisor costs it on the couple's dates.
- Never claim "no hidden costs" or "all inclusive" unless the inclusions list supports it.
