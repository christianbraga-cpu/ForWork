# Banned and flagged phrases

The editor searches for these (case-insensitive). **Banned** phrases must be removed. **Flagged** phrases need a specific reason to stay.

## Banned
- fairytale, fairy tale, fairy-tale
- dream day, day of your dreams, dreams come true
- say I do in paradise, say "I do" in paradise
- breathtaking, breath-taking, jaw-dropping
- once in a lifetime, once-in-a-lifetime
- nestled
- hidden gem
- look no further
- without further ado
- in today's world, in this day and age
- tapestry, testament to, elevate your
- unforgettable experience
- second to none
- all-inclusive (unless the inclusions list proves it)
- no hidden costs (unless proven)
- cheap, budget (use "value", "smaller budgets", or give a range)
- — (em dash) and – used as a dash in body copy

## Flagged (needs a reason)
- stunning, beautiful, gorgeous, magical (max 1 per post, and only with a specific detail next to it)
- luxury, luxurious (max 2 per post; show it instead)
- perfect
- unique
- seamless, seamlessly
- bespoke (max 1)
- iconic
- world-class
- exclusive (only when it literally means sole use)

## Competitor terms (never)
- Easy Weddings, Easy Exclusive
- Any other planner's or marketplace's brand name, except in a cited external link the editor has approved
