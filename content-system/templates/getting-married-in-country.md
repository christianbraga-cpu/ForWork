## Who it is for
Australian couples (30s, destination weddings and luxury experiences, AUD 150k+) who want to know whether they can marry legally in {Place}, what to do, in what order, and how the marriage counts back home.

## Outline (H2s; reword to match real search questions, no pasted keywords)
1. **Intro and 4 one-sentence Key takeaways.** Answer the head question in the first two sentences.
2. **Can Australians marry legally in {Place}?** Legal, symbolic or religious, as the official sources state. Say plainly if a religious ceremony alone is not legally binding, only where a source says so.
3. **Before you go: entry and documents.** Smartraveller level and entry rules for {Place}; visas or travel authorities only from an official page; the documents the marriage authority asks for (ID, age, prior marriages, Certificate of No Impediment if needed).
4. **Step by step.** The licence, notice or application; appointments or appearances; waiting periods or residency rules; who can perform the ceremony; the ceremony; registration or recording; certified copies. Every step from the marriage authority's own pages.
5. **Getting your certificate ready for Australia.** Apostille or legalisation (the {Place} issuing body, and DFAT where relevant); translation if not in English; recognition back home (Smartraveller, AGD); link our expert-advice pages on the CNI, Apostille and registering an overseas marriage.
6. **Permits for beach, park or outdoor ceremonies.** Only if an official land or parks body says so; otherwise a question to ask the venue.
7. **When to marry.** Official weather service figures, labelled with the station; official storm or hurricane seasons as stated; Smartraveller heat or hazard lines. No best month.
8. **Choose your island or region.** One or two lines each from the official tourism board. No venue names, no ranking.
9. **Venues and planning.** Link our venue or booking guides for {Place} if any. One Wedded Advisor line from brand facts only.
10. **FAQ** (4 to 6, from People Also Ask and forums) **and close** (the brand-facts close).

## Rules (on top of CLAUDE.md and the voice guide)
- No prices or amounts. "A fee applies" only where a source says so.
- No "best" ranking claims; no best month.
- Weather figures always carry their station name.
- Per-line credits only where rules differ by body (for example "in Rhodes, for example..." or "the Hawaii State Department of Health says..."); otherwise no research talk.
- No same-sex marriage legal position, except in a dedicated same-sex guide; link that guide neutrally if one exists.
- One Advisor line in the body plus the close, from brand-facts.md only. Never imply the Advisor handles legal, church or religious paperwork.
- No Wedded Privileges, transfers, "free" or availability promises. Business details stay deferred.
- Exactly 4 one-sentence Key takeaways. Australian English, no em dashes.
- If the marriage authority's own pages cannot be read, stop at the source gate and ask.
