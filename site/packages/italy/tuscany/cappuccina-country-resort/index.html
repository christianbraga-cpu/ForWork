Rural resort luxury

Your wedding at Cappuccina Country Resort

Cappuccina Country Resort offers a serene Tuscan setting for weddings, where rolling vineyards, cypress-lined pathways and golden countryside views create a beautifully relaxed backdrop for uniquely personal and elegant celebrations.

Your wedding

Cappuccina Country Resort stands on a hill 2 km from San Gimignano, and its main draw for weddings is the view: the terraces of Villa Cappuccina face the town's medieval towers across the valley. In the verdant hills of Tuscany, vineyards, olive groves and golden countryside views set a naturally romantic scene.

Couples can exchange vows on the terraces overlooking the towers of San Gimignano, before gathering for a reception in the elegant indoor Torre Luce or the grand ballroom within the resort. Ceremonies and dinners on the villa terraces take up to 200 guests and are best covered with a marquee. After dinner the party moves indoors to the ballroom, which has its own bar, so guests can dance late without leaving the property. If the weather turns, the rooms of the Torre Luce restaurant take over and still keep a view of the hills.

For a truly immersive celebration, the estate also offers the possibility of an exclusive villa, or villa and resort reservation, allowing couples and their guests to stay together in the heart of the countryside.

A little history

The villa began as a Capuchin monastery, passed to the Guicciardini line through an 1877 marriage, and is now an 18th-century building beside the tower house at the heart of the resort.

Your stay

The resort's charming rooms and suites offer a peaceful retreat for couples and guests, blending traditional Tuscan character with modern comforts. Surrounded by vineyards and gardens, the accommodation invites guests to slow down and savour the atmosphere between wedding events, whether unwinding by the pool or soaking in the quiet beauty of the countryside.

There are 69 rooms across 6 categories, from Classic rooms to Terrace Suites with views, plus a separate set of rooms and suites in the historic tower with arched windows looking to San Gimignano. Rooms open onto private terraces or patios facing the Chianti hills or the gardens.

Tuscany

Located in the heart of central Italy, Tuscany is renowned for its rolling hills, historic villages, olive groves and world-famous vineyards. The region blends art, culture and natural beauty, offering visitors a quintessential Italian experience filled with warmth and timeless charm. Reaching Cappuccina from Florence Airport is a drive or private transfer through the countryside, arriving surrounded by picturesque landscapes and centuries of history.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How many guests can Cappuccina Country Resort host for a wedding?

This package is set up for 20 to 300 guests. The villa terraces take up to 200 guests, ideally under a marquee. Larger numbers are confirmed against the indoor spaces for your date by your Wedded Advisor.

What is the wet-weather plan at Cappuccina?

The rooms of the Torre Luce restaurant are set aside for guests if rain or poor weather arrives, and they still keep a view of the hills.

Where do we dance after dinner?

The resort has an indoor ballroom with its own bar corner for the after-party, so guests can dance late without leaving the property.

Can we have the villa to ourselves?

Yes. The historic Villa Cappuccina can be reserved exclusively, with 7 rooms and suites sleeping 16 and a panoramic terrace facing San Gimignano. The villa and the whole resort can also be reserved together.

How far is Cappuccina from San Gimignano and Florence?

The resort lists San Gimignano at 2 km, Siena at 43 km and Florence at 55 km.

Is the from price the final cost?

No. It is where the offer starts, for a set guest count on a standard date. Your Wedded Advisor costs your wedding at Cappuccina Country Resort on your dates, guest count and season before you commit.

When is the best time to marry at Cappuccina Country Resort?

May, June, September and early October bring warm days without the peak summer crowds.

How do guests get to Cappuccina Country Resort?

Guests fly into Florence or Pisa, usually through a Middle East or European hub, then drive or take a private transfer into the hills. Your Wedded Advisor can arrange transfers for the group.

Can we marry legally at Cappuccina Country Resort?

Visitors can marry in a civil ceremony at a town hall or approved venue, with documents translated and legalised. See the legal section on our Italy page, and your Wedded Advisor confirms the current process for your passports.

How do we book Cappuccina Country Resort?

Send an enquiry above or speak with a Wedded Advisor. We check availability, cost it on your dates and hold the date once you agree.
