Location spotlight

See why couples choose Sydney Harbour

Few cities let you say your vows on a public harbour lawn and be at dinner in a five-star ballroom ten minutes later. Sydney does, with the Opera House sails and the Harbour Bridge in the background of both.

  • The Opera House from Bennelong Point, where the Yallamundi Rooms look across the water to Kirribilli
  • The Royal Botanic Garden's harbour lawns on Farm Cove, a short walk from Park Hyatt, InterContinental, Four Seasons and The Langham
  • Mosman's sandstone headlands at Chowder Bay and Taronga's harbour slope, with the city skyline across the water
  • Guests crossing by ferry or water taxi between ceremony and reception. The ferry from Circular Quay stops at Taronga's own wharf

Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) bring mild days and long, gold light on the harbour at sunset.

Ask these 5 questions before you book

  1. Seated or cocktail?

    Capacity changes a lot between the two. Ask for the seated number with a dance floor.

  2. Where is the ceremony if it rains?

    Get the wet-weather room in writing, and how late you can make the call.

  3. When does the music stop?

    Many venues have a curfew. Ask about the finish time and the cost of an extra hour.

  4. What does the minimum spend cover?

    Food, drinks, room hire, staff, styling and GST: find out which are inside it.

  5. Where do guests sleep?

    Rooms on site, a group rate nearby, and how late guests can check out the next day.

How much does a wedding venue cost in Sydney?

Most Sydney venues price receptions per guest. Per-head ranges published by Sydney venues on Easy Weddings in October 2026 ran from about AUD 95 to AUD 320, and One Fine Day puts a typical reception at AUD 150 to AUD 300 or more per person. A ceremony in a public park, beach or harbour site adds about AUD 450 to AUD 2,000 in hire fees. Saturdays in spring and autumn cost the most, so ask each venue for its minimum spend on your date. See the cost guide.

Where can you find affordable wedding venues in Sydney?

Choose a Friday, Sunday or winter weekday, when many venues lower their minimum spend, and keep the guest list tight. A ceremony on a Harbour Trust lawn starts at AUD 400 for two hours, and a restaurant or lounge reception for under 80 guests usually costs less than a ballroom. Some listed venues publish lower prices: Clarence House in Belmore quotes from about AUD 80 to AUD 130 per head and says a Friday or Sunday can cost 15 to 20% less than a Saturday. Compare per-head prices on the same guest count.

What is the cheapest time of year to get married in Sydney?

Winter, June to August, and weekdays or Sundays in any month are usually the quietest dates for Sydney venues, so they are the best time to ask for a lower minimum spend. Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) are the busiest. June is also Sydney's wettest month on average, so book a venue with an indoor ceremony option.

What is the best wedding venue in Sydney?

It depends on your guest count and the view you want. For harbour views with 100 to 200 guests, look at the Yallamundi Rooms at the Sydney Opera House, The Langham or Park Hyatt Sydney for smaller numbers. For 250 or more, Crown Sydney (320 seated), Taronga (450 seated in The Marquee) and the CBD hotel ballrooms fit. For a large banquet in Western Sydney, look at The HIGHLINE Venue or Clarence House.

Can you have the ceremony and reception at the same Sydney venue?

Yes. Crown Sydney, Hyatt Regency Sydney, The Langham, W Sydney, The Brighton Hotel, Visy Dior, Clarence House and Taronga all host ceremonies on site. Use the Ceremony on site filter, and ask about the wet-weather plan for any outdoor ceremony.

How far in advance should you book a Sydney wedding venue?

Book 12 to 18 months ahead for a Saturday in spring or autumn. Weekday, Sunday and winter dates often open up closer to the day. Harbour venues also book out or charge more around New Year's Eve, the opera on the harbour season in March and April, and the harbour light festival in May and June.

Can overseas couples legally marry in Sydney?

Yes. You give a Notice of Intended Marriage to an authorised celebrant at least one month and no more than 18 months before the wedding, with your passports and birth certificates, and marry in front of two witnesses aged 18 or over. Some couples marry legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony in Sydney.

Can you bring your own caterer or suppliers to a Sydney venue?

It depends on the venue. Most hotels, including Crown Sydney, cater in-house only, while some banquet venues allow licensed external caterers for cultural menus. Styling, music and photography rules also vary, so check each venue profile and ask before you sign.
