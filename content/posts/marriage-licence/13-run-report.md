## Please note
- **The answer:** Australia issues no marriage licence. Under the Attorney-General's Department guidance, the step before the wedding is giving a Notice of Intended Marriage to an authorised celebrant; you marry before the celebrant and 2 witnesses, and the celebrant submits the paperwork to the registry in the state or territory where you married. The page never says Australia issues a licence.
- **Licence and certificate:** a licence permits a marriage and a certificate records one. The certificates signed on the day are not the official record; the official certificate comes from the state or territory registry (NSW and Victoria pages linked).
- **Overseas:** New Zealand and Hawaii licences in a few sentences each, linking our guides; "license" appears only in the Hawaii Department of Health page name. Other countries: ask the destination's embassy or consulate, plus Smartraveller. Recognition back in Australia and the name-change caveat come from AGD and Smartraveller.
