## Legal documents (as at November 2026)

The article states these from Smartraveller and the Australian Embassy in Athens. Reconfirm them for the couple's date before relying on them.

- Confirm with the municipality that will marry the couple (Municipality of Thira for Santorini, Municipality of Mykonos for Mykonos): which documents it needs, which must be translated into Greek and legalised, notice periods, ceremony days and locations, witnesses, and the current fee.
- Confirm whether a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI) is needed, where it should be issued (DFAT in Australia or the Australian Embassy in Athens) and whether it needs an authentication or apostille.
- Confirm any other documents the municipality asks for: divorce papers, a deceased spouse's death certificate, original birth certificates and passports.
- Confirm with the Australian Embassy in Athens: CNI, notice of intended marriage, authentication or apostille, appointment booking (needed for all notarial services) and fees. The embassy does not give legal advice.
- Allow for longer processing at the end of the school year and in peak holiday periods.
- Confirm with each venue whether it can host the legal civil ceremony or only a symbolic one, and where on the property a civil ceremony can be held.
- For a religious ceremony, confirm the church's requirements (Mykonos Grand states both partners must be Christian Orthodox for a religious wedding on its premises).
- Check Smartraveller and Schengen entry rules (90 days in any 180, Entry/Exit System) close to travel.
- After the wedding: check name-change requirements with the couple's state registry.

## General (ask every venue, in writing)

- Capacity: seated and standing for each ceremony and reception space
- Exclusive use: the wedding spaces only, one wedding per day, or the whole property; from what time
- Ceremony types: religious, civil, symbolic or vow renewal, and which spaces each is allowed in
- Step-free access: a step-free route from room to ceremony to reception (cliff-top steps on Santorini, sand and terraces on Mykonos)
- Rain and wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space (northerly summer winds on both islands)
- Music: the time amplified music must stop outdoors and indoors (curfew), and any noise rules
- Deposits: amount due to secure the date, when it is due, refund terms, and cancellation or postponement terms (including heat, wildfire, wind and ferry disruption)
- Quote currency: whether the venue quotes in EUR, and the payment schedule; the Wedded Advisor quotes the couple in AUD
- Rooms: room count, room blocks and minimum nights; set-up and pack-down windows; outside suppliers allowed
