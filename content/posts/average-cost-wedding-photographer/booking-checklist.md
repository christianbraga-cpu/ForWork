# Wedding photographer booking checklist

Use this when you speak with photographers and compare their quotes. Send every photographer the same written brief, and ask for the answers in the written quote rather than in a phone call.

## Before you ask for quotes
- [ ] Date, venue and guest numbers
- [ ] A timeline of the day: getting ready, ceremony, portraits, reception, when the camera stops
- [ ] Every event you want covered (welcome drinks, rehearsal dinner, morning-after shoot)
- [ ] The products you want: online gallery, album, prints
- [ ] What you want to do with the images: share online, print at home, give to a magazine

## Questions to ask a photographer

### Coverage
- [ ] What are the start and end times of coverage, and which events are covered?
- [ ] How is overtime charged if the evening runs late?
- [ ] Is an engagement or pre-wedding session included, an add-on or not offered, and where can it take place?

### Team
- [ ] Who exactly will photograph our wedding, and is their name in the contract?
- [ ] Is a second shooter included, optional or not offered, and for which parts of the day?
- [ ] If an assistant is listed, do they shoot or only carry equipment?
- [ ] For several events, how many photographers cover each one, and who leads the team?

### Travel
- [ ] Is travel within your home area included?
- [ ] Beyond it, are flights, accommodation, car hire and meals a separate line or part of the package?
- [ ] Is that travel figure fixed, or charged at cost afterwards?
- [ ] For a wedding abroad: who books and pays for flights, transfers and car hire, and in which currency is the quote written?
- [ ] How many nights of accommodation, where, and who books it?
- [ ] Is a permit needed to photograph on a public beach, in a piazza or at a heritage site, and who arranges it?
- [ ] Ask the venue: are there limits on where photographers can stand during the ceremony, is flash allowed in a church or chapel, and are drones permitted?

### Editing and delivery
- [ ] How many edited images should we expect, and in what editing style?
- [ ] Is retouching of individual portraits included?
- [ ] What is the date for a preview set, and the date for the full gallery?
- [ ] How is the gallery delivered, how long does it stay online, and can guests view or download?
- [ ] Is rush editing available, and is it an extra?
- [ ] Are raw, unedited files released at all?

### Albums
- [ ] Is an album included, and what are its specifications (paper, size, page count)?
- [ ] Who designs it, and how many rounds of changes do we get?
- [ ] When will it arrive?
- [ ] What do album upgrades, parent albums, extra prints and framing add?

### Usage, copyright and what the photographer may publish
- [ ] Who owns the copyright, and what may each of us do with the images?
- [ ] Does the contract exclude or modify the Australian default for commissioned photos taken for a private or domestic purpose?
- [ ] May you use any of our images in a portfolio, on social media or in the press without our written consent? If we would rather you didn't, write that limit into the contract.
- [ ] If the photographer is overseas or the contract is governed by another country's law, how are ownership and usage handled?

### Backup
- [ ] Who covers the day if you are ill or can't travel?
- [ ] For a wedding abroad, who replaces you if a flight is cancelled, and is that person named in the contract?

### Deposit and cancellation terms (in writing)
- [ ] How much is the deposit, when is it due and in which currency is it paid?
- [ ] When are the remaining payments due?
- [ ] What happens to money already paid if we cancel?
- [ ] What happens if you cancel?
- [ ] Can we postpone, and on what terms?
- [ ] What happens if travel is disrupted on a wedding abroad?
- [ ] Does a date hold require a deposit, and does pricing differ by season or day of the week?

### Before you choose
- [ ] Can we see several complete galleries from weddings like ours, not only a highlights reel?
