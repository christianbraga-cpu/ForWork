## Cross-region summary
- Guest counts: one PAA ties photographer budget to a 100-guest wedding (Sydney, New York); no result discusses guest count as a cost driver directly.
- Pricing shown: almost every result leads with a number (AUD, USD, AED, SGD). Most are single-photographer sales pages pricing their own packages.
- Top concern: is the quote fair, and what is actually in it (hours, second shooter, album, editing, travel). Forums and "perspectives" dominate New York; Reddit and Facebook threads appear in Melbourne, New York and Singapore.
- Gap we can own: a neutral, non-vendor explanation of what shapes the quote and what a package should list, with questions to ask and contract and consumer-rights points (copyright and usage, deposits and cancellations) linked to official Australian sources. No ranking result does this. Luxury and destination framing (multi-day, travel, second shooter) is missing from AU results.
- Because we give no figures, we cannot compete on "the number" (AIO and vendor pages own it). Win on the "how to read a quote" intent and the packages long tail.
