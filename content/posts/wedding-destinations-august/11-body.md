For an August wedding abroad, choose the hemisphere before the venue. Europe is at the height of its summer, with long evenings and its busiest season, while Fiji, Mauritius and New Zealand are in their cooler months and Bali sits outside its usual wet season.

The ten wedding destinations in August below are unranked, split by hemisphere and honest about the trade-offs, with an at-a-glance table after the ten entries. Still deciding on a region? Start with our [destination wedding locations by region](/destinations).

## Key takeaways

- The ten places are the Greek islands, Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, the French Riviera, Portugal and Switzerland in Europe's high summer, and Bali, Fiji, New Zealand and Mauritius south of the equator.
- On the Greek islands and the Riviera, August rain is slight on paper, so shade and ceremony timing matter more than a wet-weather plan.
- 15 August is a public holiday in Italy, so ask every Italian venue and supplier how they work on and around that date.
- In Fiji and New Zealand, August 2026 weather differed sharply from coast to coast and region to region, so ask every venue for a covered or indoor option.

## August suits a destination wedding when you choose the hemisphere first

August is a good month for a wedding abroad, as long as you know which August you are booking. In Europe it means full summer, long evenings and the busiest weeks for venues and towns. South of the equator it means a cooler season and, for many Australian guests, a shorter journey.

Neither is the safer choice: Europe means planning for heat and crowds; the south, for wind, cool nights and, in New Zealand, snow.

## How we chose these ten August destinations

Every place here is one we plan and write about. The right answer depends on your guests, your tolerance for heat, and whether you picture a sea terrace at dusk or a mountain lodge. Each entry gives the trade-off, official August weather with its station and period where the source publishes them, venue questions and our guide.

A city station can differ from a villa nearby, and averaging periods and rain-day definitions vary between countries, so read each figure on its own rather than comparing destinations directly.

## Northern summer in Europe

Europe in August means dinner outdoors and guests ready for a long weekend, at the price of heat by day and the busiest weeks of the season.

### Greek islands

**Why August.** The Cyclades suit an open-air terrace ceremony in August, with almost no rain in the Naxos station figures below. The trade-off is heat on an exposed terrace and islands at their busiest.

**August weather.** The [Hellenic National Meteorological Service's Naxos climatology](http://oldportal.emy.gr/emy/en/climatology/climatology_city?perifereia=South%20Aegean&poli=Naxos) (averaging period not published) gives an August mean temperature of 25.0°C and a mean maximum of 27.0°C. August rainfall at the same station averages 1.6 mm, across 0.3 days. For the months either side, see our [Greek island weather month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece).

### Lake Como, Italy

**Why August.** Villa gardens run down to the water, so the lake sits behind the ceremony and the dinner. The August trade-off is warm days, summer rain and [15 August, the Assumption, a public holiday in Italy set by law](https://presidenza.governo.it/ufficio_cerimoniale/cerimoniale/giornate.html).

**August weather.** [Milan's climate normals for 1971 to 2000](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=603), from the Italian Air Force meteorological service, give an August mean daily minimum of 17.6°C, a mean daily maximum of 28.5°C and 88.8 mm of rain. Milan is a city station, not a lakeside one. For other months, see [when to marry in Italy](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-italy).

**Questions for the venue.**
- Do you and your regular suppliers work on and around 15 August, and on what terms?

### Amalfi Coast, Italy

**Why August.** Terraces stacked above the sea and long dinners outdoors: August delivers both. The trade-off is midday heat and the same 15 August holiday.

**August weather.** [Naples' climate normals for 1971 to 2000](https://wwis.meteoam.it/it/city.html?cityId=604), from the Italian Air Force meteorological service, give an August mean daily minimum of 19.1°C, a mean daily maximum of 30.3°C, 35.5 mm of rain and about 3 days with rain. Naples is a city station, not on the Amalfi Coast itself. The question is shade and timing, not rain.

**Questions for the venue.**
- What time does the ceremony terrace come out of full sun?
- How do guests who find stairs hard reach the ceremony terrace and the dinner?

**Our guide.** Browse [Amalfi Coast wedding venues](/destinations/europe/italy/amalfi-coast-wedding).

### French Riviera

**Why August.** The Riviera suits evening weddings, with August nights at the Nice station warm enough to dine outdoors late. The trade-off is daytime heat.

**August weather.** [Nice's climate normals for 1971 to 2000](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=902), supplied by Météo-France, give an August mean daily minimum of 20.0°C, a mean daily maximum of 27.2°C and 23.9 mm of precipitation over 2.9 days with at least 1 mm. These figures are for the Nice station, not the wider coast or inland Provence. Summer 2026 was [the hottest in France since 1900](https://meteofrance.com/presse/bilan-climatique-de-lete-2026-juin-juillet-aout), with a heatwave running from 28 July to 19 August 2026. One summer, but reason enough to plan for heat.

**Questions for the venue.**
- Is there an indoor or shaded fallback if a heatwave is forecast for your week?
- How late can you dine and play music outdoors?

**Our guide.** See [France wedding venues and chateaux](/destinations/europe/france).

### Portugal

**Why August.** Atlantic light, coastal estates and city palaces, with very little August rain in Lisbon's long-term figures below. The trade-off is heat by day.

**August weather.** [Lisbon's older climate normals for 1961 to 1990](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=2), supplied by IPMA, give an August mean daily minimum of 17.7°C, a mean daily maximum of 27.9°C and 5.7 mm of rain over 2.0 days with at least 0.1 mm. Venues outside Lisbon will differ.

**Questions for the venue.**
- Where are the ceremony and dinner shaded, and from what time?
- Is there still a covered option, even in a month this dry?

**Our guide.** See [Portugal wedding venues](/destinations/europe/portugal).

### Switzerland

**Why August.** Switzerland lets you choose your altitude: a lakeside terrace or a mountain setting. The trade-off is heat low down and storms up high.

**August weather.** [MeteoSwiss](https://www.meteoswiss.admin.ch/climate/the-climate-of-switzerland.html) records the highest temperatures in July and August in lower-lying regions, and the most intense heatwaves, with daily maximums above 30°C, typically fall between June and August. In July and August, [daily average temperatures of 25°C or more are possible at lower altitudes](https://www.meteoswiss.admin.ch/climate/the-climate-of-switzerland/annual-cycle-of-temperature-precipitation-and-sunshine.html). Thunderstorm clouds frequently form along the main ridge of the Alps in summer, and in many places more precipitation falls in summer than in winter.

**Questions for the venue.**
- What is the indoor plan B for a mountain ceremony if a thunderstorm builds?
- At a lakeside venue, how is the ceremony shaded on a hot day?

**Our guide.** See [Swiss alpine and lakeside venues](/destinations/europe/switzerland).

## Southern winter escapes in August

South of the equator, August trades Europe's heat for wind, showers and, in New Zealand, snow.

### Bali

**Why August.** August falls outside Indonesia's usual wet season, which makes a clifftop or garden ceremony easier to plan. The trade-off: a drier outlook is not a guarantee.

**August weather.** As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia) puts Indonesia's wet season at usually around October to April, when weather can change quickly and heavy rain can flood roads in Bali. For June to August 2026, [BMKG's Bali climate station](https://staklim-bali.bmkg.go.id/?p=3621) rated the drought and wetness outlook across the province as generally "Normal and Slightly Dry". That outlook covered 2026 only, not a long-term average, so treat it as a guide rather than a forecast for your year. For the months either side, see [when to marry in Bali, month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-bali).

### Fiji

**Why August.** Cool nights and the dry season, as described in the met service summary below, suit an outdoor ceremony. The trade-off is wind and showers, which depend on which side of an island your venue faces.

**August weather.** The [Fiji Meteorological Service's August 2026 summary](https://www.met.gov.fj/media/climate_product_files/Fiji_Climate_Summary_August_2026_4hgZRFZ.pdf) calls August a winter month for Fiji and the dry season. In August 2026 it recorded cool nights, windy periods and regular trade showers, mainly on windward coasts, while the leeward sides of the larger islands were generally fine and drier. Extremes that month ran from 32.7°C by day at Rarawai Mill in Ba to 9.8°C at night at Nadarivatu, an inland highland station: one month's readings, not an average.

**Questions for the venue.**

### New Zealand

**Why August.** August in New Zealand is a true winter wedding, with snow in the mountains. The trade-off is cold, short days and mountain roads.

**August weather.** [Earth Sciences New Zealand](https://niwa.co.nz/climate-and-weather/overview-new-zealands-climate) notes that July is usually the coldest month and most snow falls in the mountains, though the east and south of the South Island may get some snow in winter. In August 2026, the [nationwide average temperature was 9.7°C](https://niwa.co.nz/climate-and-weather/monthly/climate-summary-august-2026), 0.7°C above the 1991 to 2020 August average, with dry conditions for much of the North Island and very wet weather in the west and south of the South Island: one year's reading, not a long-term average. As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/new-zealand) warns of ice, snow and fog on the roads, and notes that mountain and ski-field roads may need snow chains or a 4WD.

**Questions for the venue.**
- What is the indoor or covered plan for the ceremony?
- How do guests reach you safely in snow or ice, and can arrivals be timed for daylight?

**Our guide.** Read [how to get married in New Zealand](/destination-weddings/get-married-in-new-zealand).

### Mauritius

**Why August.** At Port Louis, August is cooler and drier than midsummer, which suits an afternoon beach ceremony. The trade-off is about five rain days in an average August at the same station.

**August weather.** [Port Louis' climate normals for 1971 to 2000](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=1219), supplied by Mauritius Meteorological Services, give an August mean daily minimum of 19.1°C, a mean daily maximum of 26.8°C and 19.0 mm of precipitation over 5.0 days with at least 1 mm. At the same station, January's mean daily maximum is 31.5°C, with 131.0 mm of precipitation.

**Questions for the venue.**
- Which coast is the resort on, and how do its August conditions differ from Port Louis?
- What is the covered option if showers pass through during the ceremony?

## What to plan for an August wedding abroad

### Peak season and booking

In Europe, August is high summer, so the questions come earlier. Ask each venue when it opens August bookings, how many weddings it hosts that week, whether its suppliers are already committed, and whether the property can be reserved for your party alone.

Ask, too, whether your date lines up with school holidays in the states your guests live in.

### Guest comfort in heat

In Europe, noon to late afternoon is hardest on guests in formal wear, and on older relatives most of all. Ask about ceremony times, shade, fans, water and a dress code that suits the heat. Your Wedded Advisor confirms inclusions in writing before you commit.

### Legal or symbolic ceremony

Legal requirements differ by country, so read [where you can legally marry overseas](/destination-weddings/weddings-abroad-where-can-you-legally-get-married) and weigh a [legal wedding abroad or a symbolic ceremony](/expert-advice/should-you-marry-legally-abroad-or-have-a-symbolic-ceremony).

### Guests travelling from Australia

As at November 2026, Smartraveller suggests sharing your itinerary with friends or family, keeping hard copies of flight, accommodation and event bookings, and [subscribing to travel advice for your destination](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/essentials-checklist). Pass it on with your save the date, and ask each venue how older or less mobile guests reach the ceremony and the dinner.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is August a good month to have a destination wedding?

Yes, if you match the destination to the season: high summer in Europe, the cooler months in Fiji, Mauritius and New Zealand.

### Where is warm but not too hot for a wedding in August?

Look south. In the 1971 to 2000 normals from Mauritius Meteorological Services, Port Louis has an August mean daily maximum of 26.8°C, and the Fiji Meteorological Service described August 2026 as a winter month in the dry season. Both sources are linked in the entries above.

### Where can you get married in Europe in August?

The Greek islands, Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, the French Riviera, Portugal and Switzerland all work, each with its own trade-off. Heat is the common thread.

### Is August peak season for weddings in Greece and Italy?

Plan as though it is. August falls in high summer in both countries, and 15 August is a public holiday in Italy, so ask venues early about availability that week and how they staff it.

### Can Australians marry legally in these August destinations?

It depends on the country, because legal requirements differ. Read the legal and symbolic ceremony guides linked above before you choose a destination.
