# Booking checklist: August destination shortlist

A shortlist sheet for couples weighing an August wedding abroad. These checks sit with the couple, the venue and the Wedded Advisor, so they don't block publishing. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the publishing blockers are the 7 image slots and the URL check after saving.

All items are as at November 2026. Reconfirm each one for the couple's own dates before relying on it. This sheet gives no weather figures: the figures in the article are official station averages or single-month readings, not forecasts, so check the national weather service for your dates (column below).

## Step 2: ask every venue (in writing)

- **Heat:** what time does the ceremony spot come out of full sun, and what is the plan if a heatwave is forecast for your week (shade, fans, water, an indoor or shaded fallback)?
- **Shade:** where is the shade at the ceremony and during drinks and dinner, at the time you want to marry?
- **Wet weather or wind:** what is the covered or indoor option, how quickly can you switch to it, and who makes the call? Ask this even for a dry month.
- **Crowds and peak season:** when do August bookings open, how many weddings does the venue host that week, and are its regular suppliers already committed?
- **Your party alone:** can the whole property be reserved for your party only, and how far ahead?
- **Older and less mobile guests:** how do they reach the ceremony and the dinner (stairs, slopes, distances, step-free routes), and are cool or rest areas close by?
- **Timing and noise:** how late can you dine and play music outdoors?
- Anything the venue doesn't publish (curfew, minimum nights, set-up times, inclusions): your Wedded Advisor confirms it in writing before you commit.

## Step 3: destination-specific questions

- **Greek islands:** shade on the ceremony terrace at your time; cover if the wind picks up during an outdoor ceremony; the islands are at their busiest in August.
- **Lake Como and the Amalfi Coast (Italy):** 15 August (the Assumption) is a public holiday in Italy set by law. Ask the venue and every supplier whether they work on and around 15 August, and on what terms. Lake Como: where the ceremony moves if it rains. Amalfi Coast: when the terrace comes out of full sun, and how guests who find stairs hard reach the ceremony and dinner.
- **French Riviera:** indoor or shaded fallback if a heatwave is forecast; how late you can dine and play music outdoors. Weather figures for Nice don't apply to inland Provence.
- **Portugal:** where the ceremony and dinner are shaded, and from what time; a covered option even in a dry month. Venues outside Lisbon will differ from the Lisbon station.
- **Switzerland:** lakeside venues, how the ceremony is shaded on a hot day; mountain venues, the indoor plan B if a thunderstorm builds.
- **Bali:** the wet-weather option even for an August date; where the ceremony is shaded and when it comes out of direct sun.
- **Fiji:** is the resort on the leeward or windward side of its island; the wet-weather ceremony option and how quickly you can switch.
- **New Zealand:** the indoor or covered ceremony plan; how guests reach you safely in snow or ice, and whether arrivals can be timed for daylight (Smartraveller warns of ice, snow and fog on roads, and snow chains or a 4WD on mountain roads).
- **Mauritius:** which coast the resort is on and how its August conditions differ from Port Louis; the covered option if showers pass through.

## Step 4: guests travelling from Australia

- Check whether your date lines up with school holidays in the states your guests live in.
- Ask each guest to check Smartraveller for the destination close to the dates and subscribe to its travel advice, share their itinerary with family or friends, and keep hard copies of flight, accommodation and event bookings. Pass this on with the save the date.
- Check the national weather service (Step 1) for your dates, and again closer to the day.

## Step 5: legal or symbolic

- Legal requirements differ by country. Read where you can legally marry overseas, and decide on a legal or symbolic ceremony, before you hold a date.
