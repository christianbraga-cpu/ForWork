## Cross-region summary
- Guest counts: snippets cite "under 50" to large; Tripadvisor forum on 15 guests. No page compares capacity like for like.
- Pricing shown: only destinationweddingdirectory.co (AUD ranges) and package-led sites; most lists show none.
- Top concern: which area (clifftop vs beach vs jungle), then cost and legality.
- Gap we can own: one luxury guide grouped by area with a like-for-like table (ceremony setting, seated capacity, rooms, exclusivity, sunset aspect), each fact from the venue's own site, plus legal/season/airport in brief. Many ranking pages are stale (2018 to 2023) or thin.
- Brand note: local pack venue "Wonderland Uluwatu" shares our word; writer must not imply affiliation.
- recommended_scope: au_focus (AU volume ~2x US; audience AUD 150k+), written to read globally.
