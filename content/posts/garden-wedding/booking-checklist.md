# Booking checklist: garden wedding venue questions

A question sheet for couples weighing a garden venue, at home or abroad. Take it to every venue visit and to your Wedded Advisor. These are questions only; the answers belong in writing from the venue. This sheet doesn't block publishing.

## Step 2: wet weather plan and covered space

- Where is the covered space, and does it hold every guest seated for both the ceremony and dinner?
- Who makes the call to move under cover, and by what time on the day?
- Can a marquee be added if the indoor space is too small, and what does the venue require for one?

## Step 3: heat, shade and sun

- What shade covers the ceremony area and the drinks lawn, and is it trees or a built structure?
- Where does the shade fall at the hour of the ceremony?
- Can a sunshade or marquee be added over the seating?
- Where can guests get drinking water outdoors?

## Step 4: ground and footwear

- Is the ceremony on lawn, gravel or paving, and is any of it on a slope?
- Is the lawn watered close to the day, and how soft does it get?
- Can flooring be laid under the dance floor and the tables?

## Step 5: access for older guests and a route without steps

- Is there a route without steps from arrival to the ceremony, the reception and the bathrooms?
- Is the surface firm underfoot the whole way?
- Is that route lit after dark?
- Is there a drop-off point close to the ceremony, and seating for guests who cannot stand for long?

## Step 6: insects

- Does the venue treat the gardens for insects, and how long before the event?
- Are there ponds or water features near the tables?
- Can citronella, fans or screens be used in the reception area?

## Step 7: noise and curfew

- Is there a limit on amplified music outdoors, and at what time must it stop?
- What time must guests leave, and when must pack down be finished?

## Step 8: power and lighting

- Where are the outdoor power points, and what can they run at once?
- Is a generator needed, and where can it sit out of earshot?
- Are the paths and the car park lit after dark?
- Are naked flames allowed outdoors, and who puts them out at pack down?

## Step 9: garden protection rules

- Are confetti, petals or rice allowed, and which kinds?
- Can arches, signs or lanterns be staked into the lawn?
- Where can marquee pegs go, and where are the irrigation lines?
- Which areas are closed to guests, and can vehicles drive onto the lawn for set up?
- Can the gardens be closed to the public and to other guests for the whole event?

## Step 10: destination garden weddings (a garden abroad)

Ask every question above, then these:

- Which months does the venue recommend for its garden, and what does the garden look like in yours?
- What is the covered backup, and is it on the same property?
- How do guests travel between the hotel and the garden, and how long does it take?
- Is that transfer suitable for guests with limited mobility, and where is the nearest medical help?
- Who is on the venue's side on the day, and do they stay until the end of the night?
- If something goes wrong on the day, who answers for it, and how do you reach them?
- Who holds your deposits, and is the contract in English with the currency of each payment stated?
- What does the garden allow for set up and pack down, and over how many hours?
