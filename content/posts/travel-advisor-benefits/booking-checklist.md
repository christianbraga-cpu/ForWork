# Questions to ask any travel advisor

These questions apply to any travel advisor, whoever you speak with. Ask them early, and ask for the answers in writing. They are prompts for a first conversation, not answers. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (5 slots) and the `/travel-guides` folder and Travel Guides category existing in the CMS.

As at November 2026. This is general information, not legal or financial advice.

## Fee and inclusions

- [ ] How is your fee charged, and will you put it in writing before any work begins?
- [ ] What exactly is included in each quote? Please confirm the inclusions in writing for every booking, not a summary.
- [ ] Do you receive commissions or mark-ups from the suppliers you book, and will you tell us?

## Terms and protection

- [ ] What are your terms, and what are the terms of each provider? Please show us both sets before we book.
- [ ] What happens if a provider cancels, if we cancel, or if a booking cannot go ahead for reasons outside anyone's control?
- [ ] Do you hold insolvency insurance, and what happens to our money if you or a provider becomes insolvent?
- [ ] Can we pay by credit card, and do any of your terms ask us to give up chargeback rights?
- [ ] Do you belong to an industry accreditation scheme? Which one, what does it require, and who would we go to with a complaint?

## Money in other currencies

- [ ] What exchange rate does each AUD quote use, and is it guaranteed?
- [ ] Can we see each supplier's price in its own currency alongside the AUD figure?
- [ ] How are overseas payments made, what transfer fees apply, and is any transfer business involved registered with AUSTRAC?

## Travel insurance

- [ ] When should we buy travel insurance? The earlier, the more likely you are to be covered for something unexpected before the trip.
- [ ] Does the policy cover travel provider or agent insolvency? It is commonly excluded and only a few insurers cover it.
- [ ] Does the policy cover every country on the route, including stopovers, and have we declared all pre-existing conditions?
- [ ] If we rely on credit card insurance, what does it cover and how is it activated?

## Guests and the wider trip

- [ ] Who tracks guest arrival dates, room allocations and dietary notes?
- [ ] How do guests get from the airport to the venue, and who checks each arrival time?
- [ ] Do the rooms and the venue suit older or less mobile guests?
- [ ] How will the wedding week and the honeymoon be planned together, ideally with flights booked as one journey?
- [ ] We have already booked some things ourselves: how will you plan dates and deposits around them?
