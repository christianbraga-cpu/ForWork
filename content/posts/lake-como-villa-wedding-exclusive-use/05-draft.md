For a Lake Como villa wedding, exclusive use means the whole property is yours: house, gardens, dock and bedrooms, closed to everyone but your guests. Not every famous name on the lake offers it. Some are hotels that host weddings in their function rooms; some are heritage gardens with no bedrooms.

This guide covers eight villas whose owners or operators confirm exclusive use on their own websites, with the same checks for each: who sleeps on site, how many you can seat, and the house rules to confirm. For the wider picture, see [our Lake Como wedding collection](/destinations/europe/italy/lake-como-wedding).

## Key takeaways

- Only eight villas on the lake could be confirmed as bookable for exclusive use from official sources. Several are boutique hotels that offer a whole-property option rather than private homes.
- Beds, not dinner seats, are the limit. The confirmed villas seat from about 70 to 200 for dinner but sleep from 12 to about 40, so most Australian guest lists need a nearby room block as well.
- A legal civil ceremony on site is the exception: Villa Làrio, Relais Villa Vittoria and Villa Cipressi state it, and Villa Lario Resort offers a symbolic ceremony.
- Australians marrying in Italy need a Nulla Osta, sworn in person at the Australian Embassy in Rome or the Consulate-General in Milan. A Certificate of No Impediment is not accepted.
- Minimum nights, music curfews and standing capacity are rarely published. Get them in writing for your dates before you commit.

## What exclusive use means for a Lake Como villa wedding, and what it covers

Exclusive use is not one product: some villas are private homes let whole, others small hotels that close when a wedding takes every room.

The clearest example is Relais Villa Vittoria in Laglio, where any wedding of more than 30 guests must take exclusive use of the villa and book every room. <!-- fact_45 --> Its lakeside gardens, rain option and cellar after-party space open only under that whole-villa hire. <!-- fact_47 --> <!-- fact_48 --> Ask every villa on your shortlist the same question: which spaces are yours only when you take the whole property? Then get the answer in writing.

### Legal civil ceremony or symbolic ceremony on site

In Italy, a civil ceremony is booked through the marriage office of the town where you marry. <!-- fact_06 --> A villa can host a legal ceremony only where the town recognises it as a wedding location. Villa Làrio and Relais Villa Vittoria both state that they can. <!-- fact_31 --> <!-- fact_47 --> Elsewhere, plan a symbolic ceremony at the villa and the legal one at a town hall.

For Australians, as at October 2026, the Australian Embassy in Rome says two documents are needed: the Atto Notorio and the Nulla Osta. <!-- fact_03 --> The Nulla Osta (a sworn declaration that you are free to marry) must be signed in person, by appointment, at the Australian Embassy in Rome or the Australian Consulate-General in Milan, with your Australian passport. <!-- fact_04 --> A Certificate of No Impediment, or a Nulla Osta sworn outside Italy, is not valid for use in Italy. <!-- fact_04 --> The Nulla Osta is then legalised at a Prefettura, which needs a revenue stamp (marca da bollo). <!-- fact_05 -->

The Embassy lists the fee as the euro equivalent of AUD 80 per Australian citizen, valid for six months, subject to change. <!-- fact_05 --> [[VERIFY: Nic sign-off on the Nulla Osta fee before publishing]] Between May and September, appointments with the celebrant and for the Apostille can be hard to get. <!-- fact_06 --> Your Wedded Advisor confirms current requirements. Read [how a legal ceremony works at Lake Como](/expert-advice/how-do-you-get-a-lake-como-wedding) and [the legal steps for marrying in Italy](/expert-advice/getting-married-in-italy-the-complete-legal-guide-for-destination-couples), along with [Smartraveller's advice on getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas).

## What drives the cost of an exclusive-use villa

None of the eight villas publishes an exclusive-use fee, so we have not printed one. Here is what moves the number.

- **Nights.** A minimum stay multiplies the fee. Villa Aura del Lago's three-night minimum means you are hiring a weekend, not a day. <!-- fact_43 -->
- **Guest count.** At Relais Villa Vittoria, crossing 30 guests moves you from a wedding at the hotel to taking the whole villa and every room. <!-- fact_45 -->
- **Season and date.** Villa Lario Resort opens from April to November. <!-- fact_37 --> Ask how the fee changes between a Saturday in July and a weekday in early autumn.
- **What is in-house.** Ask which items (catering, music, staff) are included and which are billed on top.
- **Ceremony logistics.** A civil ceremony elsewhere adds transport and a town hall fee, which the Embassy notes is customary and varies by place. <!-- fact_06 -->

For typical ranges across a whole wedding, read [what a Lake Como wedding costs](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost). Your Wedded Advisor costs the villas on your actual dates and quotes in AUD. <!-- fact_50 -->

### What happens if it rains on our Lake Como villa wedding?

It depends on the indoor rooms. Relais Villa Vittoria moves ceremony and dinner into the Salone, with exclusive hire. <!-- fact_48 --> Villa Lario Resort uses its hall, veranda or marquee. <!-- fact_34 --> Agree the rain plan, and when you will call it, at booking.

### Can you book Villa d'Este or Villa del Balbianello for exclusive use?

Villa d'Este is an operating hotel, and its official events page does not describe a whole-property buyout. <!-- fact_11 --> Villa del Balbianello is a FAI property that hosts weddings of up to 130 guests but has no overnight rooms. <!-- fact_10 --> Neither is an exclusive-use villa in the sense used in this guide.
