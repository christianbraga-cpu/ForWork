For a Lake Como villa wedding, exclusive use means the whole property is yours: house, gardens, dock and bedrooms, closed to everyone but your guests. Not every famous name on the lake offers it. Some are hotels that host weddings in their function rooms; some are heritage gardens with no bedrooms.

This guide covers ten villas whose owners or operators confirm exclusive use on their own websites, with the same checks for each: who sleeps on site, how many you can seat, and the house rules to confirm. For the wider picture, see [our Lake Como wedding collection](/destinations/europe/italy/lake-como-wedding).

## Key takeaways

- Ten villas on our shortlist could be confirmed as bookable for exclusive use from their own official sources. Several are boutique hotels that offer a whole-property option rather than private homes. None publishes an exclusive-use fee, so start with [what a Lake Como wedding costs](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost).
- Beds, not dinner seats, are the limit. Where they publish figures, these villas seat from 14 (Villa La Felice) to 200 for dinner but sleep 12 to about 40, so most Australian guest lists need a nearby room block as well.
- A legal civil ceremony on site is the exception: Villa Làrio and Relais Villa Vittoria state it, Varenna's tourism site lists Villa Cipressi (on a 2024 page), and Villa Lario Resort offers a symbolic ceremony.
- Australians marrying in Italy need a Nulla Osta, sworn in person at the Australian Embassy in Rome or the Consulate-General in Milan. A Certificate of No Impediment is not accepted.
- Minimum nights, music curfews and standing capacity are rarely published. Get them in writing for your dates before you commit.

## What exclusive use means for a Lake Como villa wedding, and what it covers

Exclusive use is not one product: some villas are private homes let whole, others small hotels that close for a wedding.

The clearest example is Relais Villa Vittoria in Laglio, where any wedding of more than 30 guests must take exclusive use of the villa and book every room. Its lakeside gardens, rain option and cellar after-party space open only under that whole-villa hire. Ask every villa on your shortlist the same question: which spaces are yours only when you take the whole property? Then get the answer in writing.

### Who sleeps on site, and how many rooms

Every villa on this list has bedrooms on site, but fewer than most couples expect. Villa Sola Cabiati's six suites sleep 12. Villa Aura del Lago sleeps up to 16. Plan the villa for your families and hold rooms nearby for everyone else.

### Minimum nights, check-in and set-up windows

Of the ten villas, only two publish a minimum stay, both three nights: Villa Aura del Lago for weddings, and Villino Rubini for exclusive hire all year. Villa La Felice's Intimate Wedding package is three nights, though the villa does not state it as a minimum. Build the weekend around three nights: welcome evening, wedding, farewell lunch. The others do not publish a minimum stay, check-in times or set-up windows; your Wedded Advisor confirms them in writing for your dates.

### Music curfew and where the party goes after

Only Villa La Felice addresses it: its weddings page says there is no curfew. None of the others publishes a curfew time; your Wedded Advisor confirms curfew and music rules in writing. At Relais Villa Vittoria, the rock-cut wine cellar, La Barrique, becomes a private disco only when you hire the entire villa.

### In-house caterers or an approved supplier list

Catering is often fixed. Villa Lario Resort's wedding menus come from its own Amandus Ristorante. Villa Aura del Lago works with one appointed banqueting and music partner. The others do not publish supplier rules; your Wedded Advisor confirms in writing whether outside suppliers are allowed, and on what terms. For how this fits into a full plan, see [what Lake Como wedding packages include](/expert-advice/what-is-included-in-lake-como-wedding-packages).

### Legal civil ceremony or symbolic ceremony on site

In Italy, a civil ceremony is booked through the marriage office of the town where you marry. A villa can host a legal ceremony only where the town recognises it as a wedding location. Villa Làrio and Relais Villa Vittoria both state that they can. Varenna's tourism site also lists Villa Cipressi, on a page last updated in July 2024. Elsewhere, plan a symbolic ceremony at the villa and the legal one at a town hall.

For Australians, as at October 2026, the Australian Embassy in Rome says two documents are needed: the Atto Notorio and the Nulla Osta. The Nulla Osta (a sworn declaration that you are free to marry) must be signed in person, by appointment, at the Australian Embassy in Rome or the Australian Consulate-General in Milan, with your Australian passport. A Certificate of No Impediment, or a Nulla Osta sworn outside Italy, is not valid for use in Italy. The Nulla Osta is then legalised at a Prefettura, which needs a revenue stamp (marca da bollo).

A consular fee applies, and the Nulla Osta is valid for six months. Between May and September, appointments with the celebrant and for the Apostille can be hard to get. Your Wedded Advisor confirms current requirements. Read [how a legal ceremony works at Lake Como](/expert-advice/how-do-you-get-a-lake-como-wedding) and [the legal steps for marrying in Italy](/expert-advice/getting-married-in-italy-the-complete-legal-guide-for-destination-couples), along with [Smartraveller's advice on getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas).

## 10 Lake Como villas you can book for exclusive use, by shore

The list runs by shore, not by rank: the western shore first, then four villas on the Como branch (Laglio on its western side, Villino Rubini on the hill above Como at Brunate, Torno and Pognana Lario on its eastern side), then Bellagio and the eastern shore.

Two names to keep apart: Villa Làrio and Villa Lario Resort in Mandello del Lario are different properties with different rules. Tell your Wedded Advisor which one you mean so quotes are not mixed up. ## Why some famous Lake Como names are not on this list

Several names you will see on Instagram are booked differently, or their official pages do not confirm a whole-property booking with guests sleeping on site. That is not a judgement on them.

- **Villa d'Este, Cernobbio:** an operating hotel. Its events page offers weddings in function rooms and gardens but does not describe a whole-property buyout.
- **Villa del Balbianello, Tremezzina:** a different property from Villa Balbiano. The [FAI (Fondo Ambiente Italiano)](https://fondoambiente.it/luoghi/villa-del-balbianello) offers it for weddings and receptions of up to 130 guests, but it is open to the public and has no overnight rooms.
- **Villa Monastero, Varenna:** a house museum and botanical garden that offers paid symbolic ceremonies.
- **Villa Erba, Cernobbio:** hosts large weddings, but its page does not mention overnight rooms or whole-property use.
- **Villa Pizzo, between Cernobbio and Moltrasio:** hosts weddings from May to October, but its official sites do not mention whole-property booking or guests sleeping on site.
- **Villa Regina Teodolinda, Laglio:** six suites for the couple and family, and a civil rite on the lakefront lawn, but the official site does not state that the whole villa is booked exclusively.
- **Villa La Cassinella and Villa Bonomi:** private estates for staying, whose official sites do not mention weddings.

If one is already on your list, your Wedded Advisor can ask how it is booked on your dates.

## Getting to the villa: airports, transfers and boats

Lake Como's official tourism site names four main airports: Milan Malpensa, Milan Linate, Bergamo Orio al Serio and Lugano-Agno. Lugano is across the border in Switzerland. The site puts the lake about 50 km from Milan, with both Como and Lecco under an hour from Milan by car.

Four of the ten villas publish airport travel times, all their own figures. Villa Pliniana puts Torno 45 minutes from Malpensa, 15 minutes by boat from Como and 20 minutes by helicopter from Milan. Villa Làrio puts Malpensa a short 45 minutes away, and Linate and Bergamo about an hour and a half; it can arrange a car pick-up at the airport. Villino Rubini puts Malpensa about an hour away by car, and Linate and Lugano about 1 hour 10 minutes. Villa La Felice puts Malpensa about 1 hour 15 minutes by car, and Linate and Bergamo around 1.5 to 2 hours. Villa Balbiano's owner puts Ossuccio 40 minutes from central Milan, not from an airport.

Fly guests into Malpensa where you can, and allow a guest coach extra time on lakeside roads. Coach transfer times are not published; your Wedded Advisor confirms them in writing for your date.

Boats are logistics, not just photographs. Eight of the ten villas have a dock, pier, jetty or pontoon, so guests and the couple can arrive from the water. Villa La Felice has a private gate to the waterfront, and Villino Rubini, on the hill above Como, is reached by road. Do not rely on the public ferry for wedding-night movements. Navigazione Laghi, the government-run lake service, publishes seasonal timetables, and in 2026 a new one took over on 4 October. On strike days, guaranteed services run only from 6am to 9am and from 5.55pm to 8.55pm.

Charter private boats for guest shuttles and your arrival, with a road transfer as backup. Before you travel, read [Smartraveller travel advice for Italy](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/italy).

### Older guests: steps, lifts and boats

When we checked the official sites of eight of these villas in October 2026, none published an accessibility statement: nothing on step-free access, lifts or accessible rooms. Assume the same for all ten until a villa tells you otherwise. If grandparents are coming, ask each villa in writing:

- How many steps are there from the car drop-off or the dock to the ceremony spot, the dinner terrace and the nearest bedroom?
- Is there a lift, or a ground-floor bedroom?
- Can guests board the boat from level ground, with crew to help?
- Can older guests come by road instead of by boat, and how close can the car get?

Your Wedded Advisor puts these questions to each villa on your shortlist and confirms the answers in writing.

## Where guests stay when the villa is full

If you are inviting 60 to 120 guests to a villa that sleeps 12 to about 40, most of them will sleep elsewhere. The usual plan is a hotel room block for the same nights as the villa, on the same branch of the lake, with transfers to the wedding. Guest lists larger than one villa sleeps usually pair it with one or two hotels nearby.

The lake is split between the provinces of Como and Lecco. The lake's tourism site lists its five-star hotels by town, a useful map for room blocks:

- **Como branch** (Relais Villa Vittoria, Villino Rubini, Villa Pliniana, Villa Làrio): Como's historic centre, Blevio, Brunate, Cernobbio, Moltrasio and Torno.
- **Western shore** (Villa Balbiano, Villa Sola Cabiati, Villa La Felice): Tremezzina, a string of small villages facing Bellagio and Isola Comacina, plus Cadenabbia, Sala Comacina and Menaggio.

## What drives the cost of an exclusive-use villa

None of the ten villas publishes an exclusive-use fee, so we have not printed one. Here is what moves the number.

- **Nights.** A minimum stay multiplies the fee. The three-night minimums at Villa Aura del Lago and Villino Rubini mean you are hiring a weekend, not a day.
- **Guest count.** At Relais Villa Vittoria, crossing 30 guests moves you from a wedding at the hotel to taking the whole villa and every room.
- **Season and date.** Villa Lario Resort's 2026 season runs from 2 April to 1 November. Ask how the fee changes between a Saturday in July and a weekday in early autumn.
- **What is in-house.** Ask which items (catering, music, staff) are included and which are billed on top.
- **Ceremony logistics.** A civil ceremony elsewhere adds transport and a town hall fee, which the Embassy notes is customary and varies by place.

For typical ranges across a whole wedding, read [what a Lake Como wedding costs](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost). Your Wedded Advisor costs the villas on your actual dates and quotes in AUD.

## Lake Como villa wedding FAQs

### Can you get legally married at a Lake Como villa?

Yes, at some. Villa Làrio says it is licensed for civil ceremonies with legal effect, and Relais Villa Vittoria serves as a municipal wedding location run with the Comune di Laglio. Varenna's tourism site also lists Villa Cipressi (page dated July 2024). Elsewhere, hold a symbolic ceremony at the villa and the legal one at a town hall. Smartraveller says a marriage valid where it took place is, in most cases, legally recognised in Australia. Your Wedded Advisor confirms current requirements.

### How many nights do exclusive-use villas on Lake Como require?

Most do not publish a minimum. Villa Aura del Lago states three nights for weddings, and Villino Rubini three nights for exclusive hire, all year. For every other villa, ask for the minimum stay on your dates before you commit.

### Is there a music curfew at Lake Como villas, and where does the party go after?

Of the ten, only Villa La Felice addresses it, saying there is no curfew; confirm in writing that this holds under local noise rules. For the others, ask each one. Relais Villa Vittoria's wine cellar becomes a private disco under whole-villa hire.

### Can all our guests stay at the villa?

Usually not. Those that publish a figure sleep from 12 (Villa Sola Cabiati and Villa La Felice) to about 40 (Villa Pliniana). Plan a nearby room block for everyone else.

### What happens if it rains on our Lake Como villa wedding?

Relais Villa Vittoria moves ceremony and dinner into the Salone, with exclusive hire. Villa Lario Resort uses its hall, veranda or marquee. At Villino Rubini, dinner for about 100 is outdoors under a tent, a warm-months format, and indoor rooms seat only small groups. Agree the rain plan, and when you will call it, at booking.

### Can you book Villa d'Este or Villa del Balbianello for exclusive use?

Villa d'Este is an operating hotel, and its official events page does not describe a whole-property buyout. Villa del Balbianello is a FAI property that hosts weddings of up to 130 guests but has no overnight rooms.
