# Booking checklist: Lake Como villa wedding, exclusive use

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the villa for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers left; the points the person chose to clear from the copy (2026-10-22) are listed under "Confirm before quoting" below.

## Confirm before quoting

Removed from the article as markers on 2026-10-22 (the wording in the copy already carries the caveat). Check these before quoting a couple or naming terms:

- Villa Sola Cabiati: wedding terms in writing from Grand Hotel Tremezzo (the villa's own pages do not mention weddings; Passalacqua says it suits events)
- Villa La Felice: whether "no curfew" meets local noise rules, and the comune (Pianello del Lario or near Menaggio)
- Relais Villa Vittoria: western-shore placement
- Villa Pliniana: 17 or 18 bedrooms, sleeping 34 to about 40 guests (Sereno's pages differ)
- Villa Làrio: eastern-shore placement
- Villa Aura del Lago: that the whole estate is exclusive during a wedding (floors are also offered separately)
- Villa Cipressi: civil-ceremony status is current (source page last updated July 2024)
- Villa d'Este (not on the list): whether a wedding buyout is possible

## General

- Minimum nights, check-in and check-out times, and set-up and breakdown windows at each villa
- Curfew times and outdoor or amplified music rules at each villa on the couple's dates
- In-house catering or approved-supplier rules, and terms for outside suppliers, at each villa
- Coach transfer times from Malpensa, Linate and Bergamo to each villa on the wedding date
- Step-free access, lifts, ground-floor or accessible rooms, and level boat boarding at each shortlisted villa
- Hotel-to-villa transfer times from each room-block town

## Villa Balbiano, Ossuccio

- Total guests sleeping on site, and whether other buildings add rooms beyond the six suites
- Seated and standing capacity
- Minimum nights
- Music curfew
- Civil or symbolic ceremony on site
- Indoor space for dinner and ceremony (wet-weather plan)

## Villa Sola Cabiati, Tremezzo (with Grand Hotel Tremezzo)

- Seated and standing capacity
- Minimum nights
- Music curfew
- Civil or symbolic ceremony on site
- Whether the indoor rooms (including the Sala degli Stucchi) serve as the rain plan, and for how many

## Villa La Felice, Pianello del Lario

- Minimum nights for a wedding booking (three-night package is not stated as a rule)
- Private dock for boat arrivals, or only the lakeside gate
- Street address and drive time from Menaggio
- Maximum wedding guest numbers (12 sleeping, about 30 ceremony, 14 dining) and the Celebration Weekend package guest count

## Relais Villa Vittoria, Laglio

- Room count and beds
- Standing capacity
- Minimum nights
- Music curfew

## Villino Rubini, Brunate

- Whether a civil (legally binding) ceremony can be held on site, or only a symbolic garden ceremony
- Music curfew
- Standing capacity
- Whether the tent for about 100 guests is included or hired separately
- Coach access up the Brunate road for a guest shuttle

## Villa Pliniana, Torno

- Minimum nights
- Music curfew
- Civil or symbolic ceremony on site
- Indoor ceremony option

## Villa Làrio, Pognana Lario

- Room count and guests sleeping on site
- Seated and standing capacity

## Villa Aura del Lago, Limonta
