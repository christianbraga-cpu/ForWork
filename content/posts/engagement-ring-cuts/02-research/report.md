## Cross-region summary
- Pricing shown: jeweller collection pages show AUD/USD ranges in snippets; brides.com snippet ranks shapes by cost. We show none (brief).
- Top concern: which shape suits me, which looks biggest, sparkle versus clarity, durability of corners.
- Format: Google mixes 'styles' (halo, solitaire) with 'shapes'. Results are jewellers, Pinterest and two magazines (Brides, Cosmopolitan). No wedding or travel publisher ranks. weddedwonderland.com is not in any top 10.
- Gap we can own: body-neutral, sourced hand guidance (only GIA-backed 'flatters' lines), a try-on routine (photo from above, arm's length, daylight, with the wedding band) and how each shape reads in sunlit outdoor ceremony photos. The pages we read say things like 'universally flattering' with no source and use gendered framing ('your bride-to-be').
- Asscher appears in all three returned AI overviews, but AU demand is low (140 vs cushion 720). Keep the default 8 and mention Asscher inside the emerald section, with an FAQ.

## Answers to the brief
1. **8 cuts:** confirmed (round, oval, emerald, cushion, princess, pear, marquise, radiant). No swap: cushion (720) beats Asscher (140). Radiant volume was not checked in this run. Asscher gets an H3 under emerald plus an FAQ.
2. **'diamond shapes' (3,600, KD 6) and 'engagement ring shapes' (880, KD 2):** this post should own both. The intent is visual shape comparison, not grading. The diamond guide keeps the 4Cs. Recommendation for the orchestrator: retitle the diamond guide's H2 'Diamond shapes and how each changes the 4Cs' to 'How shape changes the 4Cs' and link it here with the anchor 'engagement ring cuts and diamond shapes'.
3. **Regional scope:** global. SERPs are near identical in Sydney and Melbourne. NY, Dubai and Singapore show the same intent from US, UK and local jewellers. Use Australian English with no AU-only facts.
