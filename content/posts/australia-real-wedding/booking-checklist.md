# Booking checklist: Australian venue questions

A question sheet for couples weighing a venue in Australia, whether you live here or are coming home to marry. Take it to every site visit and to your first conversation with your Wedded Advisor. These are questions only; the answers belong in writing from the venue (or your celebrant) before you sign. This sheet doesn't block publishing.

Built from "What these weddings share" and "Planning a wedding in Australia from here" in `11-body.md`.

## Step 2: deposits, payments and date changes

- What does the deposit cover?
- When does each payment fall due?
- What happens if the date moves?

## Step 3: guests travelling from interstate and overseas

- How do guests reach the venue from the nearest airport, and how long is the trip?
- Where can guests stay nearby, and is there accommodation on the property?
- Where do coaches and cars set guests down, and how far is that from the ceremony?
- Can older guests, or anyone using a walking frame or wheelchair, get from there to the ceremony, to dinner and to the bathrooms without stairs?
- Can the venue host a welcome drink the night before or a long lunch the day after?

## Step 4: an outdoor ceremony and a covered plan

- Where is the covered option, and can every guest be seated there for the ceremony and for dinner?
- Who decides to move under cover, and how many hours before the ceremony?
- Is that space on the same property, and can we see it set up during our visit?
- Who is our named contact at the venue on the day, and how do we reach them?

## Step 5: styling and set up

- What can we bring in ourselves, and what has to come from the venue's own suppliers?
- What time can our stylist or our family start setting up, and when must everything be cleared?
- Can anything be hung from trees, beams or a marquee frame?
- Are candles or other open flames allowed inside and outdoors?

## Step 6: a small guest list

- Is there a minimum guest number for the space we want?
- Can a smaller room or a corner of the grounds be set aside so a small group does not feel lost?
- Is the venue ours alone for the whole stay, or will other events or guests be nearby?
- Can our guests' arrivals be kept away from the public?
- Can the ceremony and the reception take place in different spaces on the same property?

## Step 7: seasons, weather and sun

- Which months does the venue suggest for an outdoor ceremony, and what is the fallback in the wettest ones?
- What is the season like at this venue, rather than across the country?
- What shade covers the ceremony seating at the hour we plan to marry, and can a sunshade or tent be added?
- Who watches the forecast in the days before, and who tells us and our guests if the plan changes?
- Can we set out water and sunscreen for guests near the ceremony?

## Step 8: the legal steps (for your celebrant)

- Are you an authorised marriage celebrant, and which type?
- Can we see your entry on the Attorney-General's Department register of Commonwealth-registered celebrants (if that applies to you)?
- When do you need our Notice of Intended Marriage, and can we complete it from overseas?
- If our date is close, what are our options?
- Where in the running order should the signing of the certificates sit, and what do our witnesses need to know?
- When will you submit our paperwork to the registry in the state or territory where we marry, and how do we get our official certificate?

## Step 9: guests who fly in

- Which guests are travelling from overseas or interstate, and when do they need the date and venue to book?
- If we live overseas, who on the ground can visit the venue for us, and how will we see the covered option before we sign?
