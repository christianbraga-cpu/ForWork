- **Legal source:** everything about the process comes from Immigration and Civil Status Seychelles (ics.gov.sc): the "Getting Married" page and the "Marriage Registrar" FAQ, which I read myself. Key points for two foreigners:
  - documents: birth certificates, any divorce or death papers, passport copies and the application form; overseas documents translated into English or French and apostilled;
  - both of you register in person at the Civil Status office; 11 days of banns, or a special marriage licence normally issued within two days (a fee applies);
  - you can arrange it directly with the office, or through a local wedding agent or your hotel;
  - the certificate is valid worldwide; before you leave, it needs an Apostille from the Supreme Court and an authentication letter from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;
  - fees apply for foreigners (amounts left out by your rule).

The final copy answers from the official wording: you can register your marriage "at home", meaning somewhere other than the office, with at least a week's notice, and a fee applies; the Australian High Commission's page (credited) says ceremonies may be held around the hotel with permission.
