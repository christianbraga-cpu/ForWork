When to get married in Greece has no single answer, and any guide that names one month for every couple is guessing. The months that work for you depend on four things: the island, how well your guests cope with heat, whether you want a church ceremony, and how long everyone will stay.

The weather is the easy part to pin down. Official station averages show a dry, hot core from June to August and rain returning in October, with real differences between the Cyclades, Crete, Rhodes and Corfu. Venues set their own season windows, and the Orthodox calendar closes some dates entirely. Here is the picture month by month, April to October, so you can choose for your own wedding.

## How to read the weather figures

Every temperature and rain figure in this guide comes from the [Hellenic National Meteorological Service climate data](http://oldportal.emy.gr/emy/en/climatology/climatology_city?perifereia=South%20Aegean&poli=Naxos), and each one carries the name of the station it was measured at.

These are long-term monthly averages, not forecasts. The Rodos and Heraklion figures cover 1955 to 2010, Chania Souda covers 1958 to 2010 and Kerkyra covers 1955 to 2010. <!-- fact_11 --> <!-- fact_19 --> <!-- fact_22 --> <!-- fact_01 --> The Naxos page does not state its data period. <!-- fact_10 -->

Three station notes matter for planning:

- **The Cyclades.** HNMS has no station on Santorini, Mykonos or Paros. Naxos, at 3 metres above sea level, is the official station for the group, so figures here are labelled Naxos. <!-- fact_04 --> <!-- fact_05 --> <!-- fact_06 --> <!-- fact_10 -->
- **Crete.** Heraklion sits on the north coast at 39 metres, and Chania Souda in the west at 148 metres. <!-- fact_18 --> <!-- fact_22 -->
- **Corfu.** The Kerkyra figures in this guide run from May to October only, so April is left blank for Corfu. <!-- fact_01 --> <!-- fact_02 -->

Read the "rain days" column as the average number of days in the month with rain. A figure of 0.4 means a wet day in July is rare, not impossible.

### April

April is the coolest month in this guide. Naxos averages a maximum of 18.7°C and Heraklion 20.1°C, with six to eight rain days at the Cyclades, Crete and Rhodes stations. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_19 --> <!-- fact_20 --> <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_24 -->

Evenings are cool. Naxos averages a minimum of 12.5°C and Chania Souda 11.4°C, so an outdoor dinner needs wraps, heaters or a room to move into. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_23 -->

Smartraveller notes that dust storms from the Sahara are increasing in frequency across Greece, particularly in spring, reducing air quality and visibility. <!-- fact_30 --> For an April date, give the photographer a covered or indoor fallback as well as a rain plan.

Venue windows are the other check. At Angsana Corfu, the Oribu Garden runs from April to October, while the larger Vertigo Sky Bar does not open for weddings until mid-May. <!-- fact_31 --> <!-- fact_32 --> Minos Palace describes eastern Crete as pleasant from April to November, with spring at around 18 to 24°C. <!-- fact_33 -->

For an Orthodox ceremony, check any spring date against Great Lent, which runs until Easter Sunday and is closed to weddings, with dates that vary each year. <!-- fact_37 -->

### May

May is when the islands warm up properly. Maximums average 22.1°C at Naxos, 24.1°C at Rodos and 24.5°C at Chania Souda, and rain days fall to around four. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_22 --> <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_24 -->

Corfu runs a little wetter. Kerkyra averages a 24.0°C maximum but 7.7 rain days in May, so an outdoor Corfu ceremony this month needs a covered plan B. <!-- fact_01 --> <!-- fact_02 -->

Angsana Corfu opens Vertigo Sky Bar for ceremonies from mid-May and for receptions from the end of May, while Oribu Restaurant takes indoor and outdoor receptions in April, May, September and October. <!-- fact_31 --> <!-- fact_32 -->

### June

June is the first dry month across the Aegean stations. Naxos, Rodos, Heraklion and Chania Souda all average just over one rain day. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_20 --> <!-- fact_24 -->

The heat climbs fast. Chania Souda averages a 28.9°C maximum and Rodos 28.3°C, against 25.9°C at Naxos. <!-- fact_22 --> <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_10 -->

June is also the start of the wildfire period. Smartraveller says wildfires occur in Greece from June to September, including near cities and tourist locations; they can start with little warning and travel quickly, so avoid affected areas and follow local authorities' advice. <!-- fact_26 --> In practice, that means guests should know how to reach you if plans change on the day.

### July

July is the driest month in the table. Rain days average 0.4 at Naxos, 0.3 at Heraklion and 0.2 at Rodos. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_20 --> <!-- fact_15 -->

It is also hot. Kerkyra averages a 31.2°C maximum, Chania Souda 30.7°C and Rodos 30.4°C, and the nights stay warm: Naxos averages a minimum of 22.0°C. <!-- fact_01 --> <!-- fact_22 --> <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_10 -->

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) says heatwaves are frequent in Greece over the summer months and can cause heat exhaustion, heat stroke and death. It advises drinking water, wearing a hat and light clothing, and staying in air-conditioned buildings or shaded areas, and notes that many major tourist sites have little shade. <!-- fact_25 -->

For a July wedding, that shapes the day: an evening ceremony, shade for the aperitivo, water at every seat, and shorter excursions for older guests.

The Cyclades add wind. The Naxos station records a prevailing north wind in every month, and its wind power reading rises from 10.3 in June to 13.3 in July. <!-- fact_09 --> Smartraveller notes that ferry services can be delayed or cancelled in bad weather, mainly because of strong winds, so reconfirm departures. <!-- fact_28 -->

### August

August mirrors July, with the hottest averages in the table at two stations: Kerkyra at 31.5°C and Rodos at 30.7°C. <!-- fact_01 --> <!-- fact_12 --> Rain is close to absent on the Aegean side, with Rodos averaging 0.1 rain days. <!-- fact_15 -->

The Orthodox calendar matters more in August than in any other summer month. The Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of Australia lists 1 to 15 August (the Dormition of the Theotokos) and 29 August among the dates when marriages are not permitted. <!-- fact_37 -->

A civil or symbolic ceremony sits outside that calendar, but a church wedding does not. If yours is in the second half of the month, check the date with your priest before you send save-the-dates.

### September

September keeps summer's warmth with the edge taken off. Maximums average 25.1°C at Naxos, 26.6°C at Heraklion and 28.1°C at Rodos, and the Aegean stations average under three rain days. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_19 --> <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_20 -->

Two cautions apply. Smartraveller's wildfire period runs to the end of September, and the Naxos wind power reading stays high at 13.9. <!-- fact_26 --> <!-- fact_09 -->

Corfu turns earlier than the other islands. Kerkyra averages 7.4 rain days and 87.8 mm of rain in September, compared with 21.7 mm in August. <!-- fact_02 -->

On the church calendar, the Archdiocese lists 14 September as a closed date. <!-- fact_37 --> Angsana Corfu's Vertigo Sky Bar runs to the end of September, so it is the last month for that space. <!-- fact_31 --> <!-- fact_32 -->

### October

October is the month of trade-offs. Days are still mild, with maximums of 21.9°C at Naxos, 23.6°C at Heraklion and 24.5°C at Rodos. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_19 --> <!-- fact_12 -->

Rain returns. Naxos averages 5.5 rain days, Heraklion 7.5, Chania Souda 9.3 and Kerkyra 11.4, with 140.4 mm of rain at Kerkyra over the month. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_20 --> <!-- fact_24 --> <!-- fact_02 -->

The Naxos wind power reading peaks for the season at 14.5. <!-- fact_09 --> A clifftop ceremony in the Cyclades needs a sheltered alternative, and guests travelling by ferry need a buffer day.

Some venues keep going. Angsana Corfu's Oribu Garden and Oribu Restaurant both take weddings in October, and Minos Palace describes early autumn in eastern Crete at around 20 to 27°C. <!-- fact_31 --> <!-- fact_32 --> <!-- fact_33 -->

### Rhodes

Rhodes has the driest summer in the table. The Rodos station averages 0.2 rain days in July and 0.1 in August, with maximums of 30.4°C and 30.7°C. <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_12 --> Humidity is at its lowest in July, at 58.9 percent. <!-- fact_17 -->

## Dates to plan around

### Orthodox closed periods and Easter

If you plan a Greek Orthodox ceremony, the church calendar comes before the weather. The [Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of Australia](https://greekorthodox.org.au/services-and-sacraments/marriage/) lists these dates when marriages are not permitted: <!-- fact_37 -->

- 13 December until Christmas Day
- 5 January, the eve of Epiphany
- From Great Lent until Easter Sunday, with dates that vary each year
- 1 to 15 August, for the Dormition of the Theotokos
- 29 August
- 14 September

Those are the Archdiocese's dates in Australia. For a church wedding in Greece, ask your priest and the local Metropolis. Easter falls on a different date each year, so confirm the Great Lent window for your year before you hold a spring date. [[VERIFY: Orthodox Easter dates for 2027 and 2028]]

## Which months suit your wedding

There is no month that suits every wedding. These scenarios match common priorities to the figures above.

**A Greek Orthodox church ceremony.** Work from the closed dates first. June, July and the second half of September sit clear of the Archdiocese's August and 14 September closures, and spring dates need checking against Great Lent for your year. <!-- fact_37 --> Your priest in Greece has the final word.

**Older guests, or anyone who struggles in heat.** May and late September to October keep maximums in the low to mid 20s at most stations. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_19 --> In July and August, Smartraveller's heat advice applies: shade, water and air-conditioned rooms. <!-- fact_25 -->

**A sunset ceremony in the Cyclades.** The Naxos station shows the mildest summer maximums in this guide, at 25.9 to 27.1°C from June to August, and almost no rain. <!-- fact_10 --> Plan for the north wind, which strengthens from July. <!-- fact_09 -->

**A long guest stay with island hopping.** June and September give warm, mostly dry weeks either side of the hottest stretch. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_20 --> Leave a buffer day before the wedding, since Smartraveller says strong winds can delay or cancel ferries. <!-- fact_28 -->

**A green setting on Corfu or in Crete.** May and October bring cooler days, with more rain at Kerkyra and Chania Souda, so choose a venue with a covered space. <!-- fact_02 --> <!-- fact_24 --> <!-- fact_32 -->

**Swimming as part of the weekend.** July and August are the hottest and driest months at every station, with the trade-off of Smartraveller's heat and wildfire advice. <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_22 --> <!-- fact_25 --> <!-- fact_26 -->

## Frequently asked questions

### What month do most Greek venues open for weddings?

Each venue sets its own season. Angsana Corfu's Oribu Garden runs from April to October and its Vertigo Sky Bar from mid-May to the end of September. <!-- fact_31 --> <!-- fact_32 --> Minos Palace describes eastern Crete as pleasant from April to November, and Santo Wines on Santorini says its Wine Tourism Center is open all year round. <!-- fact_33 --> <!-- fact_34 -->

### How hot is Greece in July and August?

It depends on the island. Average maximums run from about 27°C at Naxos to 31.5°C at Kerkyra in August. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_01 --> As at November 2026, Smartraveller says heatwaves are frequent over the summer months and advises water, a hat, light clothing and shade or air-conditioning. <!-- fact_25 -->

### Can we marry in a Greek Orthodox church at any time of year?

No. The Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of Australia lists closed dates including Great Lent to Easter Sunday, 1 to 15 August, 29 August and 14 September. <!-- fact_37 --> For a church wedding in Greece, ask your priest and the local Metropolis.

### Is October too late for a Greek island wedding?

Not for many venues, but plan for rain. October maximums average 21.9°C at Naxos and 24.5°C at Rodos, with rain days rising to 5.5 and 6.5, and to 11.4 at Kerkyra. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_02 --> Angsana Corfu's Oribu Garden and Oribu Restaurant both take October weddings. <!-- fact_31 --> <!-- fact_32 -->

### When does the sea warm up for guests?

Ask your venue about water temperature for your month, since the figures in this guide cover air, not sea. On land, Naxos averages a minimum of 19.6°C in June and 22.2°C in August, so evenings by the water stay warm through summer. <!-- fact_10 -->
