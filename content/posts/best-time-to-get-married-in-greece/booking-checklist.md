# Booking checklist: best time to get married in Greece

These details sit with the couple, the venue, the church and the municipality, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms venue details in writing for the couple's dates. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (6 slots).

All items are as at November 2026. Reconfirm each one for the couple's date before relying on it. Weather figures in the article are long-term HNMS station averages, not forecasts.

## For every venue and month (ask the venue, in writing)

- The venue's own wedding season dates for the year, space by space (ceremony, drinks, reception), and whether the chosen month falls inside them.
- Rain plan: a covered or indoor alternative for the ceremony and dinner, how late the call can be made, and who makes it.
- Wind plan: a sheltered alternative for any clifftop or terrace ceremony, especially in the Cyclades, and what happens to florals, arches and sound.
- Heat plan: shade for the ceremony and pre-dinner drinks, water at every seat, the ceremony time, and air-conditioned rooms close by for older guests.
- Wildfire contingency: Smartraveller says wildfires occur in Greece from June to September and can start with little warning; ask how the venue would move or postpone, how guests are told on the day, and follow local authorities' advice.
- Exclusive use: whether the whole property can be held for the chosen month, and how far ahead.
- Step-free access to the ceremony spot, the reception and guest rooms, and how older guests reach them.

## Month by month

- **April:** cool evenings (wraps, heaters or an indoor room); a covered or indoor fallback for Saharan dust as well as rain (Smartraveller notes dust storms are increasing, particularly in spring); Great Lent for an Orthodox ceremony; which spaces are open yet (at Angsana Corfu, Oribu Garden takes ceremonies from April, Vertigo Sky Bar from mid-May).
- **May:** covered plan B on Corfu (Kerkyra averages 7.7 rain days); confirm Angsana Corfu's Vertigo Sky Bar ceremony and reception start dates for the year; Great Lent and Easter for your year if the date is early in the month.
- **June:** start of Smartraveller's wildfire period; heat plan for Crete and Rhodes.
- **July:** heat plan and evening ceremony time; wind plan in the Cyclades; ferry buffer day (Smartraveller says strong winds can delay or cancel ferries).
- **August:** heat plan; Orthodox closed dates 1 to 15 August and 29 August; ferry buffer day.
- **September:** wildfire period runs to the end of the month; wind plan in the Cyclades; rain plan on Corfu; Orthodox closed date 14 September; at Angsana Corfu, confirm Vertigo Sky Bar's end-of-September close.
- **October:** rain plan everywhere, most of all on Corfu; sheltered alternative and ferry buffer day in the Cyclades; confirm which spaces are still open (at Angsana Corfu, Oribu Garden and Oribu Restaurant).

## Named venues in the article

- **Santo Wines, Pyrgos, Santorini:** the Wine Tourism Center is open all year round, but that is the centre's opening, not a wedding season. Ask the winery which months it takes weddings, which spaces, and its wind and heat plans.
- **Angsana Corfu:** confirm each space's window for the year (Oribu Garden, Vertigo Sky Bar ceremonies and receptions, Oribu Restaurant indoor and outdoor) and the covered option for May, September and October.
- **Minos Palace, Lasithi, Crete:** its April to November description is about the climate, not a wedding season; ask which months and spaces it takes weddings.

## Orthodox dates (ask your priest and the local Metropolis)

- The Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of Australia lists these dates when marriages are not permitted: 13 December until Christmas Day; 5 January; from Great Lent until Easter Sunday; 1 to 15 August; 29 August; 14 September.
- These are the Archdiocese's dates in Australia. For a church wedding in Greece, confirm the closed dates, the Great Lent and Easter window for your year, and the church's own availability with your priest and the local Metropolis before holding a date.

## Public holidays (ask the venue and the municipality)

- Check Greek national and local public holidays for your date with the venue and the municipality before you hold it, including any effect on registrar days, suppliers, ferries and guest travel.
