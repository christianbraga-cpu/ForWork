## Key takeaways

- Guest count decides the region: Santorini's caldera terraces seat 15 to 60, while venues on the Athens Riviera and in Crete publish space for several hundred.
- Exclusive use can mean a chosen event space or the whole hotel, and only a few venues, such as Canaves, Katikies and Melenos, offer the whole hotel.
- Curfews, minimum nights and rain plans are rarely published, so have them confirmed in writing before you pay a deposit.
- A legal wedding means paperwork, which can include a Certificate of No Impediment, and a summer date means planning around heat, wildfire season and wind-delayed ferries.

## Greece wedding venues at a glance

Greece is not one wedding destination but several, and each region asks something different of your guests.

**Santorini and the Cyclades** give you the caldera and the whitewashed chapel, on small terraces. **Crete** and **Rhodes, Kos and the Dodecanese** pair chapels and beaches with venue teams that help with civil paperwork. **Corfu and the Ionian** is greener, with gardens, decks over the water and smaller settings. **Athens and the Athens Riviera** suit the largest guest lists and the shortest transfers. **The Peloponnese** and **Halkidiki** are resort country on the mainland, with space and privacy, and a drive or flight from Athens rather than a ferry.

The venues below run from the islands to the capital, then out to the mainland resorts. They are not ranked, and each entry uses the same fields in the same order.

## How to compare Greece wedding venues like for like

Start with the type of venue. Most venues on this list are hotels or resorts that host weddings in named spaces. A few let you take the whole hotel. One, Kokotos Estate outside Athens, lists no guest rooms. <!-- fact_222 -->

Then compare five things: ceremony type on site (chapel, beach, terrace, or a civil ceremony at the town hall), the largest published seated capacity, rooms on site, exclusive use, and how guests reach the venue.

Exclusive use is the field most often misread. At Grace Hotel, the wedding venue fee covers exclusive use of the chosen event spaces, not the whole hotel, with a resort buyout available on request. <!-- fact_03 --> Canaves, by contrast, sells full-property buyouts hotel by hotel. <!-- fact_08 --> Ask every venue which one is being quoted.

Then settle the house rules, because few venues publish them. Your Wedded Advisor asks each venue on your shortlist and confirms these in writing for your date: the curfew, minimum nights, music rules, set-up and pack-down times, and the rain plan for every outdoor space. Before you pay a deposit, ask which language and currency the venue contract is in and how the deposit is held.

If an older guest or anyone with limited mobility is coming, put the same care into access. Ask each venue:

- Is there step-free access from the guest rooms to the ceremony and reception spaces?
- Are there lifts, and do they reach every level you will use?
- On a terraced or clifftop site, how far is the walk, and how many steps are on the route?
- Can golf buggies or private transfers carry guests between spaces, or from the hotel to the venue?

When most guests fly long-haul, rooms on site matter more than usual. Housing them where you marry takes a transfer out of every day of the trip.

## Getting there from Australia: flights, ferries and transfers

Athens International Airport links to central Athens and the port of Piraeus by express bus, metro and rail, and domestic airlines fly on across Greece. <!-- fact_219 --> Plan the arrival as carefully as the wedding day: who meets each guest, how they reach the hotel, and where they rest before the first event.

Allow time at the airport. Since 30 March 2026 the EU Entry/Exit System has been fully deployed for non-EU travellers, passport control may involve long waits, and the airport advises arriving at least 2.5 hours before departure. <!-- fact_220 --> Do not schedule a welcome event too close to a long-haul landing.

Ferries and large catamarans carry most visitors to the islands. As at November 2026, services can be delayed or cancelled in bad weather, mainly because of strong winds, and passengers usually carry their own luggage up and down stairs. <!-- fact_214 --> Daily ferries connect Mykonos with Santorini, Paros and Naxos, and private yacht or speedboat transfers can be arranged. <!-- fact_17 -->

## When to marry in Greece: season, heat and the meltemi

Greece has a Mediterranean climate with plenty of sunshine and little rain, and in summer the dry, hot days are cooled by seasonal winds called the meltemi. <!-- fact_223 --> The wind that cools a terrace can also hold up a ferry, so plan both.

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller advice for Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) is to exercise normal safety precautions. <!-- fact_213 --> Wildfires occur from June to September, near cities and tourist areas on the mainland and the islands, and heatwaves are frequent through summer. <!-- fact_213 --> In the May to October tourist season, expect high temperatures and crowded seaports. <!-- fact_214 -->

In practice, that means an evening ceremony. Nana Princess on Crete recommends 6pm to 8pm from June to September, and from 4pm in April, May and October. <!-- fact_611 --> Ask every venue for its indoor plan: at One&Only Aesthesis, the chapel and rooftop run only from May to September, with the Agora Ballroom indoors. <!-- fact_221 -->

## Getting legally married in Greece as Australians, in brief

There are three routes: a civil ceremony, a religious ceremony such as a Greek Orthodox wedding, or a symbolic ceremony after a legal wedding at home. <!-- fact_224 --> <!-- fact_308 --> For the customs that surround the day, see our guide to [Greek wedding traditions](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/greek-wedding-traditions).

As at November 2026, some countries ask you to prove you are not already married, most commonly with a [Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) (CNI). <!-- fact_206 --> DFAT can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities only accept one issued in their own country. <!-- fact_206 -->

The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/Notarial.html) issues CNIs and witnesses notices of intended marriage, by appointment, and CNIs are ready for collection within two working days. <!-- fact_212 --> The Embassy does not give legal advice. <!-- fact_212 -->

A marriage overseas cannot be registered in Australia, but it is usually recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. <!-- fact_207 --> A foreign marriage certificate may not prove a change of name, and a lawyer can confirm validity. <!-- fact_207 -->

Before you book, put these questions to the municipality where you will marry and to the embassy:

- Which documents does the town hall need, and must they carry an apostille and an official Greek translation?
- Will it accept a CNI issued in Australia, or only one issued by the Embassy in Athens?
- How far ahead must the notice of marriage be lodged, and do both of you need to attend in person?
- Can a civil ceremony be held at the venue, or only at the town hall?
- Is a chapel ceremony at the venue legally binding for you, or symbolic?
- What fees apply, and to whom are they paid?

Whether a chapel ceremony is legally binding for non-Orthodox couples is confirmed couple by couple. <!-- fact_224 --> Your Wedded Advisor confirms current requirements.
