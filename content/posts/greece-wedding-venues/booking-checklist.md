# Booking checklist: Greece wedding venues

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers; each venue entry's "Not published" line is listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- One&Only Aesthesis: Agora Veranda seated capacity (the resort's pages give 250 and 300; the article says it varies by set-up)
- Grace Hotel: whether a resort buyout (offered on request) is available on the couple's dates

## General

- Curfew times and outdoor or amplified music rules at each venue on the couple's dates
- Minimum nights, check-in and check-out times, and set-up and pack-down windows at each venue
- Rain or wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space
- Whether exclusive use covers the chosen event spaces or the whole hotel, and which one is being quoted
- Contract language and currency, and how the deposit is held
- Step-free access, lifts, walking distance and steps between rooms and event spaces, and buggy or private transfers
- Private transfers from Athens or the nearest island airport, and who meets guests and carries luggage at the port or airport
- Legal ceremony: documents the municipality needs (apostille, Greek translation), whether an Australian-issued CNI is accepted, notice period, whether a civil ceremony can be held at the venue, whether a chapel ceremony is legally binding for the couple, and fees
