## How the guide is built
- Each venue: setting, ceremony options, capacity, exclusive use and guest rooms only where the venue's own site publishes them; "Not published" (three items at most) where it does not. Where a venue's own planner handles legal paperwork, that is attributed to the venue, never to the Wedded Advisor.
- A like-for-like comparison table of all 20; "How to compare" with the house rules to get in writing (curfew, minimum nights, music, set-up, rain plan), access questions and a contracts and deposits question; a region jump list.
- Getting there (Athens airport, arrival planning, private transfers, ferries that can be delayed by wind); season and heat (Smartraveller: wildfires June to September, heatwaves; the meltemi cools summer days); legal in brief for Australian couples (Smartraveller on certificates of no impediment and recognition; the Embassy in Athens; municipal steps as questions to ask, including any fees, as at November 2026). Smartraveller Greece: "Exercise normal safety precautions".
