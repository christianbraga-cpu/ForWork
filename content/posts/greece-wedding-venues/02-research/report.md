## Split recommendation (recommendation only; nothing on the live site changes)
SERPs for the head term are won by editorial list pages grouped by region (Thanasis Kaiafas, Jeff Brummett, Wedinspire, Love & Roll, The Knot); Google's AI overview in 3 of 5 cities is a region-by-region venue list. That is this guide's format, not the hub's. Recommend:
1. This guide takes "greece wedding venues" / "wedding venues in greece" plus regional variants (santorini, crete, athens, corfu, mykonos, greek island).
2. Retitle the hub to "Greece Destination Weddings: Venues, Packages & Concierge" targeting "destination wedding greece" (US 480) and "greece weddings" (US 2,400, hub already ranks 69) with packages intent, and remove "greece wedding venues" from its meta keywords.
3. Reciprocal links: guide links hub in first 100 words ("curated Greece collection"); hub links guide with anchor "Greece wedding venues by region".
4. Later: consolidate /7-must-see-santorini-destination-wedding-venues into a Santorini venues piece or 301 it to this guide (it ranks 58 to 82 for Santorini venue terms).

## Cross-region summary
- Guest counts: rarely stated; one result shows capacity ranges. Gap: capacity and rooms on site per venue, same fields.
- Pricing shown: AI overview gives EUR venue-hire and catering ranges; related searches push "with prices" and "packages". We show no prices (brief); route to Concierge.
- Top concern: which island/region suits guest count and travel; packages; affordability.
- Gap we can own: Australian lens (flights, ferries, transfers, wind and season, legal in brief for Australians from Smartraveller/Embassy), and only venues whose official site says they host weddings. No competitor page has an Australia angle except two AU vendor blogs.
- Page reads: skipped in fast mode (8-minute limit); SERP result URLs came back as Google redirects. Competitor wording not used.
