# Booking checklist: getting married in Italy as an Australian

Not published by the authorities, so not publishing blockers. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing for the couple's date and comune.

- Australian Embassy Rome / Consulate-General Milan: Nulla Osta appointment lead time and booking channel (not published); whether a non-Australian partner attends; current fee and card types accepted.
- Italian consulate for the couple's state: appointment lead time, current fee and payment method, witness ID rules, whether translations themselves must be apostilled.
- chosen comune: full document list for Australians, sworn declaration lead time (same day vs 3 days), approved external ceremony venues and hall fee, interpreter rules, date/room booking deadline, how many apostilled certificate copies can be issued on the day.
- Catholic (concordat) ceremony: parish and diocesan paperwork for foreign couples and who lodges the comune publications (not published by a government source).
- Prefettura in the wedding city: opening days and appointment rules for the Nulla Osta legalisation and the post-wedding Apostille in May to September.
