## 8. Brief corrections for planner-sme and writer
- ADD the Atto Notorio as a must-include. The Embassy PDF and the Italian consulates in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Adelaide all say Australians need two documents, the Atto Notorio and the Nulla Osta. The Atto Notorio is obtained first from the Italian consulate for your state (four unrelated witnesses present) or, failing that, in Italy (Tribunale with two witnesses per the Embassy, or the comune).
- ADD the Single Status Certificate (state BDM), DFAT-apostilled with a NAATI Italian translation, alongside the birth certificate (Consulate Sydney).
- Embassy: "Nulla Ostas sworn outside of Italy and Certificates of No Impediment are not valid for use in Italy". This sources the brief's no-CNI rule.
- Official figures (still [[VERIFY]] at writing; flag fees for Nic): Nulla Osta = euro equiv. of A$80 per person, card only, valid six months (Embassy PDF); marca da bollo EUR 16 (Embassy, "may change without notice"); Atto Notorio approx. AUD 45 (Consulate Melbourne); Atto Notorio valid six months, so it cannot be issued more than six months before the wedding (Consulate Sydney).
