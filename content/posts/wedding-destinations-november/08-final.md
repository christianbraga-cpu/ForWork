For couples weighing wedding destinations, November comes down to a choice of hemisphere. North of the equator it is late autumn, and southern Europe is winding down its season. South of it, spring is turning to early summer. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_116 -->

In the tropics, November opens the cyclone or monsoon season that Smartraveller describes for Fiji, Mauritius and the Seychelles, and falls inside the wet season it sets out for Bali and the winter rains the National Weather Service describes for Hawaii. New Zealand is the plain spring choice. <!-- fact_108 --> <!-- fact_115 --> <!-- fact_112 --> <!-- fact_110 --> <!-- fact_113 -->

## Key takeaways

- This list covers the Greek islands, Portugal, the Amalfi Coast, the French Riviera and Hawaii in the northern autumn, and New Zealand, Mauritius, the Seychelles, Fiji and Bali in the southern spring and early summer.
- Europe is at the close of its season, so first settle whether your venue is open on the date and how the evening works as the air cools. <!-- fact_002 --> <!-- fact_003 --> <!-- fact_004 --> <!-- fact_008 -->
- Fiji, Mauritius and the Seychelles begin their Smartraveller cyclone or monsoon seasons in November, and Bali and Hawaii are inside their wet windows, so book covered space and a buffer day. <!-- fact_108 --> <!-- fact_115 --> <!-- fact_112 --> <!-- fact_110 --> <!-- fact_113 -->
- Pick the November trade-off that suits your guests, whether that is a quieter European coast, a changeable New Zealand spring or a tropical island in its wet or cyclone season.

## Why November works for a destination wedding

November suits couples who like a place once the crowds have gone. The price of a quieter Mediterranean is a shorter day, cooler nights and venues that may be preparing for winter.

In the south the picture flips. New Zealand is in late spring, and the tropical islands are warm and in their wet or cyclone seasons, so a November date there means planning for weather rather than hoping against it.

For Australian guests, November also lands before the summer break at home, so ask early who can take leave.

### The Greek islands

**The November trade-off.** Quieter harbours and mild days, set against a prevailing north wind and the close of the island season. <!-- fact_008 -->

**November weather.** The [Hellenic National Meteorological Service's figures for Naxos](http://oldportal.emy.gr/emy/en/climatology/climatology_city?perifereia=South%20Aegean&poli=Naxos), for which HNMS publishes no base period, give a November minimum monthly temperature of 13.7°C, a monthly mean of 16.4°C and a maximum of 18.8°C. <!-- fact_008 -->

The same table shows 52.3 mm of precipitation over 8.8 precipitation days, with the wind prevailing from the north. <!-- fact_008 --> Naxos is one island station, not a reading for Santorini or Mykonos. To set November against the rest of the Greek year, see [when to marry in Greece, month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece).

**Questions for the venue.**
- Is the venue open in November, and will its kitchen and full team be working on our date?
- Which ferries still run that week, and what is the plan if a crossing is cancelled?

### The French Riviera

**The November trade-off.** A quieter promenade, with evenings cool enough to move the reception under a heated roof. <!-- fact_003 -->

**November weather.** Météo-France's [1971 to 2000 normals for Nice](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=902) record a November mean daily minimum of 8.8°C, a mean daily maximum of 16.3°C and 94.3 mm of precipitation over 6.6 days with at least 1 mm. <!-- fact_003 --> The station sits in the city, so it says little about the hill villages.

**Questions for the venue.**
- Is the venue open in November, or does its season end before our date?
- Which rooms are heated, and how many guests do they seat for dinner?

### Portugal

**The November trade-off.** Mild nights near Lisbon, paired with frequent rain days to work around. <!-- fact_004 -->

**November weather.** IPMA's [1961 to 1990 figures for Lisbon](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=2) record a November mean daily minimum of 11.2°C, a mean daily maximum of 17.8°C and 113.5 mm of rain over 14.0 rain days. <!-- fact_004 --> IPMA counts any day with 0.1 mm or more, so a damp morning qualifies. <!-- fact_004 --> These are older city normals, not a reading for Sintra.

**Questions for the venue.**
- Is the venue open in November, and is the whole property ours for the weekend?
- Can the ceremony move under cover at an hour's notice, and who decides?

### Hawaii

**The November trade-off.** An island wedding in the northern tropics, where November falls in the wetter winter season. <!-- fact_113 --> <!-- fact_114 -->

**November weather.** [National Weather Service Honolulu](https://www.weather.gov/hfo/climate_summary) describes two seasons for the state: summer from May to October and winter from October to April, the season of more frequent clouds and rainstorms. <!-- fact_113 -->

Its heaviest rains come from winter storms in that window. Windward coasts catch far more cloud and rain, while leeward areas, where the air descends, tend to be sunny and dry. <!-- fact_114 --> The description is statewide, so the side of the island matters.

**Questions for the venue.**
- Which side of the island is the venue on, leeward or windward, and where does the ceremony go in a winter storm?
- If a storm delays inter-island flights, what is the plan for guests still on their way?

### New Zealand

**Why November.** Late spring weather that can be settled one week and wild the next. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_102 --> <!-- fact_104 -->

<!-- IMAGE SLOT new-zealand: New Zealand lodge, vineyard or lakeside lawn (to match the H3 and our New Zealand guide), green vines or lawns, mountains or lake behind, clear light, no identifiable people. Month taken (October to December) recorded; 'spring' captioned only if the post's sources state it. Weather claims stay in the copy (Earth Sciences NZ November 2025 summary, one year only), never in the caption. -->

**November weather.** The [Earth Sciences New Zealand climate summary for November 2025](https://niwa.co.nz/climate-and-weather/monthly/climate-summary-november-2025) covers that single month, so read it as a snapshot of one year rather than a long-run pattern. That month was the country's warmest November on record, with a nationwide mean of 15.9°C, 2.2°C above the 1991 to 2020 November normal. <!-- fact_101 --> It opened with a settled week under high pressure. <!-- fact_101 -->

Lows drawing moisture from the Coral Sea also brought several periods of heavy rain to the upper and western North Island. <!-- fact_102 --> As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/new-zealand) notes that conditions can change quickly and severe weather can strand travellers in remote areas. <!-- fact_104 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- If the forecast turns in our wedding week, which covered space holds the ceremony and dinner?
- Is there accommodation on site, so guests avoid remote roads at night?

### Mauritius

**The November trade-off.** Hot days at the start of summer and of the cyclone season Smartraveller sets out. <!-- fact_116 --> <!-- fact_115 -->

**November weather.** Mauritius Meteorological Services' [1971 to 2000 normals for Port Louis](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=1219) record a November mean daily minimum of 21.8°C, a mean daily maximum of 30.2°C and 24.0 mm of precipitation over 3.0 days with at least 1 mm. <!-- fact_005 --> Port Louis is one station on the north-west coast. <!-- fact_005 -->

[Mauritius Meteorological Services](https://metservice.intnet.mu/climate-services/climate-of-mauritius.php) places November at the opening of a warm, humid summer that runs to April, straight after October, the driest month. <!-- fact_116 --> As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/mauritius) gives the cyclone season as November to May and warns that the airport may close during a cyclone. <!-- fact_115 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Where is the shaded or covered ceremony space, and is the dinner room cooled?
- Should a cyclone shut the airport in our wedding week, what happens to the date and the rooms?

**Our guide.** See [how Australians marry legally in Mauritius](/destination-weddings/mauritius-wedding).

### The Seychelles

**The November trade-off.** Lighter winds and pleasant conditions at sea, as the monsoon season Smartraveller describes begins. <!-- fact_111 --> <!-- fact_112 -->

**November weather.** [Victoria's climate normals for 1981 to 2010](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=253), supplied by the Seychelles Meteorological Authority, give a November mean daily minimum of 24.4°C, a mean daily maximum of 30.3°C and 192.5 mm of rain over 16.0 days with at least 0.1 mm. <!-- fact_006 -->

The [Seychelles Meteorological Authority's November guide](https://www.meteo.sc/services/climate/seychelles-climate-guide/) says the rains set in during November, though the winds drop and swing to the west and conditions at sea are quite pleasant. <!-- fact_111 --> As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/seychelles) warns of heavy rain, especially from November to March, its monsoon season, with flooding and landslides that can damage roads. <!-- fact_112 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Is there a sheltered spot on the property for the ceremony, and can it be dressed the day before?
- When guests travel to another island by boat, who calls off a rough crossing?

**Our guide.** Plan the islands with our guide to [getting married in the Seychelles](/destination-weddings/getting-married-in-seychelles).

### Fiji

**The November trade-off.** Island resorts at the start of the wet and cyclone season, as Fiji's met service described November 2025. <!-- fact_106 --> <!-- fact_108 -->

**November weather.** The [Fiji Meteorological Service's November 2025 summary](https://www.met.gov.fj/media/climate_product_files/Fiji_Climate_Summary_November_2025_rnIwkcN.pdf) covers that one year only. It describes warmer temperatures, high humidity and frequent rain, with 21 of 25 reporting rainfall stations above or well above their 1991 to 2020 normal. <!-- fact_106 --> <!-- fact_105 --> Troughs late in the month brought flash flooding that closed low-level crossings in the Central, Western and Northern Divisions. <!-- fact_106 -->

[Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/fiji), as at November 2026, gives Fiji's cyclone season as November to April, though cyclones can come outside it, and flights may be delayed or suspended. <!-- fact_108 --> It names downtown Nadi and the Coral Coast for common flooding, which can cut off the road to the airport. <!-- fact_109 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Will the resort put its wet weather and cyclone plans for the wedding in writing before we sign?
- If floodwater closes the road or a boat cannot run, how do guests reach the island?

### Wet and cyclone seasons in November

In the tropics, the rain plan belongs in the contract. Ask for a covered space that seats every guest, a set time for the call to move, and a buffer day between arrival and the ceremony. Ask the venue, in writing, what happens to deposits already paid if the weather moves the date, and check that travel insurance covers cyclone closures and flight delays, as Smartraveller suggests for Mauritius. <!-- fact_115 -->

### Venues at the end of the European season

Late in the season, opening and staffing vary from venue to venue. Ask each one, in writing, whether it is open on your date and which rooms are heated, and ask for the contract in English with the currency of each payment stated.

Plan around the light. As single-day readings, the sun set at 4.38pm in Naples on 25 November 2026 and at 5.18pm in Lisbon on 24 November 2026, so an early afternoon ceremony keeps the photographs in daylight. <!-- fact_010 -->

### Legal or symbolic ceremony

Rules differ by country. Check [where you can legally marry overseas](/destination-weddings/weddings-abroad-where-can-you-legally-get-married), then decide whether a [legal or symbolic ceremony](/expert-advice/symbolic-ceremony) suits your plans.

### Guests travelling from Australia

Ask each venue whether a local public holiday falls near your date, how guests who walk slowly manage wet steps, whether there is a route without steps to the ceremony and dinner, and how far away medical care is.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is November a good month for a destination wedding?

Yes, if you accept its trade-off: a quieter, cooler Europe at the end of its season, spring in New Zealand, or warm islands in their wet or cyclone season.

### Where is warm for a wedding in November?

The Indian Ocean islands. Mauritius records a November mean daily maximum of 30.2°C at Port Louis, <!-- fact_005 --> and the Seychelles 30.3°C at Victoria. <!-- fact_006 --> Both are entering their cyclone or monsoon season, so warmth comes with a rain plan. <!-- fact_115 --> <!-- fact_112 -->

### Is November cyclone season in Fiji and Mauritius?

Yes. As at November 2026, Smartraveller puts it at November to April in Fiji and November to May in Mauritius. <!-- fact_108 --> <!-- fact_115 --> The Fiji Meteorological Service called November 2025 the start of the country's wet and cyclone season. <!-- fact_106 -->

### Are European wedding venues open in November?

It varies from venue to venue, so ask each one first and get the answer in writing, with which rooms are heated and whether the full team works on your date.

### Can Australians marry legally in these November destinations?

Each country has its own requirements, and some couples marry legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony abroad. Our legal and symbolic ceremony guides, above, explain the choice.
