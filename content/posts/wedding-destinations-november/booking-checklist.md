# Booking checklist: November destination shortlist

A question sheet for couples weighing a November wedding abroad. Take these to the venue, your Wedded Advisor and the official sources for your own dates. These are questions only; the answers belong in writing from the venue or the official source. This sheet doesn't block publishing.

## Step 2: weather and season questions (for each destination)

- What does the national weather service say about November for the place closest to our venue, and which station and period is that reading from?
- How far is our venue from that station, and could the coast, cliffs, hills or island side make it read differently?
- What season is November in this destination, according to the national weather service?
- Is November inside, or at the start of, a wet, cyclone or monsoon season here, according to Smartraveller and the national weather service, and when does that season end?
- What does Smartraveller currently say about weather, flooding, landslides, road closures or airport disruption for this destination?
- How cool are November evenings here, and what will guests need to wear after dark?
- How hot and humid is the middle of the day here in November, and what time of day is cooler for the ceremony?
- What time does the sun set on our date, and does the ceremony time keep the photographs in daylight?
- What is the forecast for our wedding week, and when will we check it again closer to the day?

## Step 3: questions for every venue (ask in writing)

- Is the venue open, and fully staffed, on our November date? Does its season end before our date, or is it preparing for winter?
- Will the kitchen and the full team be working on our date?
- What is the covered or indoor ceremony option, does it seat the whole guest list, and can it be dressed in advance?
- How late on the day can we decide to move under cover, and who makes the call?
- Which rooms are heated for dinner and dancing, or is the reception space cooled?
- Is there a buffer day between guests arriving and the ceremony?
- What happens to deposits already paid if weather moves the date?
- Will the venue put its wet weather (and, where relevant, cyclone) plan in writing before we sign?
- Is the contract in English, and is the currency of each payment stated?
- Can the whole property be ours for exclusive use?
- Does a local public holiday fall near our date, and does it change how the venue or the town works that week?
- Is there a route without steps between the guest rooms, the ceremony, dinner and the bathrooms?
- How do guests who walk slowly manage wet steps or paths?
- How far away is medical care?
- What does the venue not publish (curfew, minimum nights, set-up times, inclusions), and will our Wedded Advisor confirm each one in writing before we commit?

## Step 4: destination questions from the post

- **Greek islands:** Is the venue open in November, and will its kitchen and full team be working on our date? Which ferries still run that week, and what is the plan if a crossing is cancelled?
- **Amalfi Coast:** Is the venue open in November, which indoor room seats every guest, and can the property be closed to outside guests for our stay? How do guests reach the terrace from the road in heavy rain, and is there a covered route?
- **French Riviera:** Is the venue open in November, or does its season end before our date? Which rooms are heated, and how many guests do they seat for dinner?
- **Portugal:** Is the venue open in November, and is the whole property ours for the weekend? Can the ceremony move under cover at an hour's notice, and who decides?
- **Hawaii:** Which side of the island is the venue on, leeward or windward, and where does the ceremony go in a winter storm? If a storm delays inter-island flights, what is the plan for guests still on their way?
- **New Zealand:** If the forecast turns in our wedding week, which covered space holds the ceremony and dinner? Is there accommodation on site, so guests avoid remote roads at night?
- **Mauritius:** Where is the shaded or covered ceremony space, and is the dinner room cooled? Should a cyclone shut the airport in our wedding week, what happens to the date and the rooms?
- **Seychelles:** Is there a sheltered spot on the property for the ceremony, and can it be dressed the day before? When guests travel to another island by boat, who calls off a rough crossing?
- **Bali:** Is there a roofed pavilion for every guest, and what is the latest hour the ceremony can move there? If heavy rain floods the roads, how long does the drive from the airport or the guests' hotel take?
- **Fiji:** Will the resort put its wet weather and cyclone plans for the wedding in writing before we sign? If floodwater closes the road or a boat cannot run, how do guests reach the island?

## Step 5: guest travel questions (guests travelling from Australia)

- Who on our guest list can take leave in November, and have we asked them early?
- Does our date clash with school holidays or public holidays in the states our guests live in?
- Have guests checked Smartraveller for the destination close to the dates, and subscribed to its updates?
- Does each guest's travel insurance cover cyclone, storm or flood disruption, airport closures, and flight delays or cancellations?
- If flights are delayed or the airport closes, where do guests stay, and who tells them?
- If guests need a ferry, boat, inter-island flight or long road transfer, what is the back-up if it is cancelled or the road is closed?
- Have we told guests what to pack for November at this destination (a layer for cool evenings, an umbrella for rain, sun protection for hot days)?
- Which guests will need a hand on the journey, and who looks after them on travel days?
- Do guests have copies of their flight, accommodation and event bookings, and have they shared their itinerary with family or friends?

## Step 6: legal or symbolic

- Can we marry legally in this country as Australians, and what does that require? (Read where you can legally marry overseas.)
- Would a legal marriage at home and a symbolic ceremony abroad suit us better?
- How long does the legal process take, and does it fit our November date?
