## Sources
- **Greek law (official, read live 12 November 2026):** Law 5089/2024 "Equality in civil marriage" (Government Gazette FEK A' 27/16.02.2024, via e-EFKA's copy), Ministry of Interior Circular 240 of 4 March 2024, and the Hellenic Parliament record. Voted 15 February 2024; in force on publication, 16 February 2024; Civil Code article 1350(1) now begins "between two persons of different or the same sex" (our translation from the Greek). The law leaves churches free to set their own rules; adoption applies through marriage; assisted reproduction is unchanged.
