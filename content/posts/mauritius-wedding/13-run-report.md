  - apply to the Central Civil Status Office in Port Louis at least one month ahead (documents 6 to 8 weeks before);
  - both of you call at the office on arrival so the originals can be checked;
  - birth certificates in English or French, issued within the last 3 months, plus passport copies and any divorce, death or name-change papers;
  - a Certificate of Authority is issued after the documents are verified;
