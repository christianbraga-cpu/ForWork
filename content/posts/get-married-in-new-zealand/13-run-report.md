## Please note
- **The legal steps, all from New Zealand government pages (govt.nz, as at November 2026):**
  - You do not need to be a New Zealand citizen; visitors can marry there.
  - Couples living outside New Zealand apply with the overseas notice form, sign the declaration in front of an authorised person, then visit a registry office on arrival to sign a declaration before the ceremony.
  - The licence must be arranged at least 3 working days before the wedding and lasts 3 months. A fee applies (linked to the official page, no amount).
  - The venue, and any back-up venue, must be named on the licence. Only registered celebrants can marry you, with 2 witnesses present in person.
  - The "Copy of Particulars" signed on the day is not the marriage certificate; the celebrant registers the marriage within 10 days, and the certificate is ordered separately.
  - **For Australia:** Australia accepts an Apostille from the Department of Internal Affairs Authentication Unit, on paper or as an e-apostille, within 15 working days (an urgent 5-day service costs extra).
