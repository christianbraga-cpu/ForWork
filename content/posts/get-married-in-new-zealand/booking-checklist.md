# New Zealand marriage checklist for Australians

The documents and steps from "How to Get Married in New Zealand as an Australian", in order, plus questions to put to Births, Deaths and Marriages (Te Tari Taiwhenua), your celebrant and your venue. Requirements come from the New Zealand Government (govt.nz and Births, Deaths and Marriages), the Department of Internal Affairs Authentication Unit, the Department of Conservation and Smartraveller; check their pages before you rely on any step. Fees apply to the marriage licence, a registry ceremony, the marriage certificate and an Apostille, and celebrants set their own fees: ask each for the current amount in writing.

As at November 2026. This is general information, not legal advice.

## Documents

- [ ] Check both passports are valid and that most Australian citizens' visa-free entry and NZeTA exemption apply to you; anyone travelling on another passport checks their own entry rules early.
- [ ] Download the notice of intended marriage form for parties ordinarily resident outside New Zealand (BDM 58), or check the online application.
- [ ] Which identity documents and details does Births, Deaths and Marriages want with the application for each of us?
- [ ] If either of us was married or in a civil union before, what evidence of the divorce, dissolution or death does Births, Deaths and Marriages need?
- [ ] Complete the New Zealand Traveller Declaration (NZTD) before passport control, and send guests the same reminder.

## Licence steps from overseas

- [ ] Choose your celebrant before you lodge the application (essential for a registry ceremony).
- [ ] Fill in the BDM 58 form, except the statutory declaration (only one of you needs to complete the form).
- [ ] Sign the statutory declaration in front of one of the authorised people listed on the form.
- [ ] Send the form and the fee to Births, Deaths and Marriages.
- [ ] Time the application so the licence is in place at least 3 working days before the wedding and does not expire (licences last 3 months) before the date.
- [ ] Ask Births, Deaths and Marriages: how long is processing taking for our dates, and where will the licence and copies be sent?

## Venue and back-up venue on the licence

- [ ] Confirm your ceremony venue before you apply: the ceremony can only take place at the places on the application.
- [ ] Name a back-up venue (your wet-weather plan in legal form).
- [ ] Ask Births, Deaths and Marriages: what do we do if the place has to change because of unforeseen circumstances? (It must still be within New Zealand.)
- [ ] For a beach, park or conservation land: who gives permission, is a Department of Conservation concession needed, who applies for it and how long before the date?

## Registry visit on arrival

- [ ] Find the registry office address on the application form and book or plan the visit for soon after you land.
- [ ] Both sign the declaration at the registry office before the ceremony (this is not the ceremony itself).
- [ ] Ask the registry office: which documents do we bring to the visit, and do we both need to attend?
- [ ] For a personalised ceremony, collect the licence and 2 copies of the Copy of Particulars of Marriage and keep them with your passports.

## Celebrant

- [ ] Are you registered to perform marriages in New Zealand?
- [ ] Personalised ceremony or registry ceremony (weekday business hours, standard vows, never on a public holiday)?
- [ ] What does your fee include, and when is it paid?
- [ ] How do you work with couples planning from Australia, and will you meet us before the day, in person or by video?
- [ ] Can you review our vows so each of us says words to the effect of "I [name] take you [name] to be my legal wife, husband or partner"?
- [ ] Hand the licence and both copies of the Copy of Particulars to the celebrant before the wedding.
- [ ] How do you decide on the day whether an outdoor ceremony goes ahead, and when do we move to the back-up venue?

## Two witnesses

- [ ] Choose 2 witnesses who understand what is happening, can clearly identify you both and are satisfied you both consent.
- [ ] Confirm both will be physically present with you and the celebrant (no virtual ceremonies).
- [ ] If a witness speaks a different language, arrange an interpreter who signs a declaration before the ceremony.
- [ ] For an elopement, ask the celebrant how witnesses are usually arranged and whether your photographer can stand in as one.

## Copy of Particulars and registration

- [ ] After the ceremony, you and your witnesses sign both copies of the Copy of Particulars.
- [ ] Keep your copy; the celebrant sends the other to Births, Deaths and Marriages within 10 days.
- [ ] Remember the Copy of Particulars is not a marriage certificate.

## Certificate order

- [ ] Once the celebrant confirms registration, order the marriage certificate online (it is posted to you).
- [ ] Order a second copy if you will send one away for an Apostille and keep one at home.
- [ ] Is a recently issued certificate needed by the Australian organisation that will receive it?

## Apostille

- [ ] Ask the Australian organisation receiving the certificate whether it wants an Apostille, and paper or e-apostille.
- [ ] Apply to the Department of Internal Affairs Authentication Unit (standard processing within 15 working days; urgent within 5 working days at an extra cost).
- [ ] Does the Authentication Unit need the original certificate or a notarised copy?
- [ ] Do not try to register the marriage in Australia: overseas marriages cannot be registered here, but are generally recognised if valid where they took place and under Australian law.

## Questions for the venue

### Deposits and contracts
- [ ] Whose name is on the contract, and in which currency are payments made?
- [ ] When do deposits become non-refundable?
- [ ] If an alert, closure or severe weather stops the wedding going ahead, what happens to our deposits and our date?
- [ ] Will you set the quote out in writing, line by line?

### Access
- [ ] Which ceremony, dining and guest spaces have level access?
- [ ] Can we take the whole property for the nights we need, with no other guests or events alongside?
- [ ] How do you handle other guests, drones and outside photographers, and how would your security work alongside any security we bring?

### Evacuation
- [ ] What is your emergency and evacuation plan for guests (earthquake, tsunami, volcanic alert, severe weather)?
- [ ] What is the indoor or covered plan, and can it be named as the back-up venue on our licence?

### Road closures
- [ ] How do guests reach you from the nearest airport?
- [ ] How do guests leave if roads close, and what happens to arrivals if ice, snow, fog or high winds close the route?
- [ ] Which MetService forecasts and warnings do you watch, and how early do you decide?

## Before you travel

- [ ] Read Smartraveller's New Zealand advice and subscribe to updates for your dates.
- [ ] Note the Australian High Commission in Wellington and the Consulate-General in Auckland.
- [ ] Remind guests to carry a passport for licensed premises; an Australian driver's licence is not approved ID there.
