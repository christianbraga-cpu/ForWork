## Cross-region summary
- **What ranks:** jeweller "get the look" sales pages and valuation-led editorial lists. Pinterest is in the top 3 in 4 of 5 cities, so the intent is strongly visual.
- **Pricing shown:** every editorial page lists dollar valuations. The jeweller pages upsell lab-grown stones.
- **Top concern:** how to get the look and which style it is. The answers are buried under price talk.
- **Gap we can own:** a no-price, no-gossip roundup that sorts rings into style families, says who each look suits, links to our styles guide, and covers both historic royals and 2025 engagements. No page we read does all of this.
- **recommended_scope:** global.
