Most lists of celebrity engagement rings stop at the photograph and a carat count. This one reads each ring the way a jeweller would: the cut, the setting and the style family it belongs to, then who that look suits on an ordinary Tuesday and on the flight to a wedding abroad.

The 25 rings below run from a serpent ring given in 1839 to the old mine cut diamond announced in 2025. Each entry ends with a "Get the look" line that names the style, so you can take it straight to our guide to [engagement ring styles](/style-advice/engagement-ring-styles) and build a shortlist of your own.

## Key takeaways

- Every celebrity ring here belongs to a style family you can ask a jeweller for by name, from the emerald-cut solitaire to the toi et moi.
- Royal rings lean on meaning more than size, using heirloom diamonds, birthstones and national colours.
- Three-stone and side-stone settings are more common in this list than they first appear, because tapered baguettes are easy to miss in photographs.
- For a destination wedding, a low or bezel setting travels better than a tall claw setting, and pearls should come off before the beach.

## How to read this list

Each entry gives the year, the stone, the cut and, where a reputable outlet has named one, the maker, written as an attributed fact rather than a recommendation. Carat weights appear only where the source pack supports them, and always as "reported as about X carats".

Where outlets disagree on a detail, we have left it out rather than choose between them. You will notice there are no prices or valuations. That is deliberate: the figure tells you nothing about whether a style will suit the hand that wears it.

The "Get the look" line names the style family and who it tends to suit. Every style mentioned is explained in more depth in the engagement ring styles guide linked above.

## Historic and royal engagement rings

The first 11 rings belong to queens, princesses and the women of old Hollywood. As a group, they show how often royal rings carry a story: diamonds taken from a family tiara, a birthstone, the colours of a flag.

They are also a reminder that a ring can be worn, reset and passed on. Several of the rings in this section were altered after the engagement, and one has been worn by two princesses.

## How celebrity rings shape engagement ring trends

The effect of a famous ring can be immediate. After the November 2010 royal engagement, US jewellers reported a wave of requests for replicas of the oval sapphire and diamond ring, and one New York sapphire retailer's website crashed after the first photographs appeared. <!-- fact_01 --> The influence is not new: serpent jewellery was at its most fashionable in the decade after Albert's ring for Victoria. <!-- fact_02 -->

Reading this list as a whole, a few patterns stand out. Step cuts recur across the modern entries, with Beyonce, Amal Clooney, Sofia Richie Grainge and Jennifer Lawrence all associated with emerald-cut or elongated stones. <!-- fact_200 --> <!-- fact_205 --> <!-- fact_232 --> <!-- fact_246 --> Colour arrives in both eras, from Diana's sapphire to Blake Lively's pink diamond and Lady Gaga's pink accents. <!-- fact_25 --> <!-- fact_209 --> <!-- fact_229 --> And yellow gold carries three rings that span 2017 to 2025: Meghan's, Eugenie's and Taylor Swift's. <!-- fact_38 --> <!-- fact_41 --> <!-- fact_236 -->

Heirloom stones are the other royal thread. ## Wearing and photographing a statement ring at a destination wedding

A ring chosen at home meets a different set of conditions abroad: sun, sand, sunscreen, salt water and a long flight either side. None of this should change the style you love, but it may change the setting, and the habits you bring with you.

### Sun, salt water and long-haul flights

Fingers swell in heat and on long-haul flights, so have the fit checked before you travel to a summer wedding. <!-- fact_26 --> If a ring needs adjusting, it can sometimes be fitted without cutting the band, as the platinum beads added inside Diana's ring for Catherine show. <!-- fact_26 -->

Settings matter more than stones on a beach. A bezel, like Taylor Swift's, wraps the stone's edge and copes better with active days than tall claws. <!-- fact_236 --> Pearls, like the Akoya at the centre of Emma Stone's ring, are softer than diamonds, so take a pearl ring off for swimming, sunscreen and salt water. <!-- fact_217 --> Eternity bands are difficult to resize because the stones run the full circle, which is one more reason to confirm the size before you fly. <!-- fact_18 -->

Before you leave, check that the ring is covered by your insurance while travelling overseas, and keep a recent photograph of it with your travel documents. Carry it on your hand or in your cabin bag, never in checked luggage.

For more on choosing a setting for a life spent outdoors, see our guide to [engagement ring settings that suit an active lifestyle](/style-advice/engagement-ring-setting-for-active-lifestyle).

### Ceremony close-ups

The ring shots that last are usually the quiet ones: hands at the signing table, the exchange of bands, a detail frame with the invitation suite. Brief your photographer on which way the stone is set, especially for an east-west or elongated stone, so the angle shows its shape rather than its edge.

Coloured stones photograph differently in harsh midday sun than in late afternoon light. If your ring has a sapphire, padparadscha or pink diamond, plan the detail shots for the softer hours.

If your engagement ring came with matching bands, as Princess Beatrice's did, photograph the set together on the morning of the wedding, before the rings are separated for the ceremony. <!-- fact_46 -->

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the most famous engagement ring in the world?

There is no official ranking, but the oval sapphire and diamond ring worn first by Diana and then by Catherine, Princess of Wales, has a strong claim. After the 2010 engagement, US jewellers reported a wave of requests for replicas within days. <!-- fact_01 -->

### Why do royals often choose coloured gemstone engagement rings?

Often for meaning. Queen Victoria's emerald was reported as her birthstone, and Queen Mary of Denmark's rubies were reported as chosen to echo the Danish flag. <!-- fact_07 --> <!-- fact_33 --> Coloured stones also make a ring personal in a way that size alone does not.

### What engagement ring style is most popular with celebrities right now?

In this list, the recent rings are varied: an old mine cut in a bezel, an east-west cushion, a marquise and several ovals. <!-- fact_235 --> <!-- fact_239 --> <!-- fact_226 --> <!-- fact_229 --> The common thread is an antique cut or an elongated shape rather than a single dominant style.

### Do celebrity engagement rings usually use yellow gold?

Not as a rule. In this list, Meghan, Princess Eugenie and Taylor Swift all wear yellow gold, while Queen Elizabeth II, Grace Kelly and Amal Clooney have platinum. <!-- fact_38 --> <!-- fact_41 --> <!-- fact_236 --> <!-- fact_12 --> <!-- fact_14 --> <!-- fact_205 -->

### How can you get a celebrity engagement ring look in your own style?

Name the style family, not the celebrity. Ask a jeweller for "an emerald-cut solitaire" or "a three-stone with tapered baguettes", then adjust the size, metal and details to your hand and your life. The engagement ring styles guide explains each family.

### Which celebrity engagement rings have an Australian connection?

Two in this list. Queen Mary of Denmark was born in Hobart [[VERIFY: Queen Mary of Denmark Australian-born (Hobart); cite kongehuset.dk or a major outlet]], and Australian-born model Miranda Kerr wears a round three-stone ring. <!-- fact_242 --> <!-- fact_243 -->
