## How the topic is handled
- 12 customs in the order families hold them, listed near the top: nontoni, lamaran, peningsetan, pasang tarub, siraman, midodareni, the akad, panggih, sungkeman, paes ageng, batik and attire, gamelan.
- General practice from the Girikerto village culture record (Sleman, Yogyakarta), an official local government page; royal practice from the Kraton Yogyakarta pages, always framed as the palace's account (seven springs, the Masjid Panepen akad, pondongan, sungkeman to the Sultan).
- UNESCO: Indonesian Batik (2009) linked to marriage; UNESCO Gamelan named only as music couples choose (its text does not mention weddings); kebaya not linked to weddings.
- Custom or folklore only: sindur binayang, kacar-kucur, dulangan, the "lands first" reading, the Solo bridal styles, sido mukti, beskap and blangkon, weton, the peningsetan gift items.
- Akad: the ijab qabul only; wali, witnesses and mahar not listed; readers sent to the officiant or the KUA.
- Legal (5 steps, from the Embassy page and Smartraveller): same religion or one converts; a certificate of no impediment from the Embassy in Jakarta for Yogyakarta, the Consulate-General in Surabaya for Solo and Central Java, Bali for Bali; not normally accepted after 3 months; KUA for Muslim couples, Civil Registry with 10 days' notice for others; generally recognised in Australia but cannot be registered there. Step 5 is labelled as our planning tip. Smartraveller level 2 (Papua Pegunungan and Papua Tengah level 3), the complaint-based criminal code and emergency numbers.
