## Cross-region summary
- Same jeweller-blog set everywhere (Reve top 2 in 4 of 5 cities; Brilliant Earth top 2 in 3). No editorial or gemmological authority ranks. Every top page is a retailer selling rings.
- Pricing shown: most product-led pages show USD prices. We show none (brief rule).
- Top concern: losing or chipping the stone (snag, knock), then comfort and metal wear.
- Common angles: bezel first, then prong count, channel over pave, platinum, rounder shapes, thick band.
- Gaps we can own: (1) one consistent test (profile height, snag risk, stone protection, upkeep) applied to all 9 settings; (2) flush, bar, half-bezel and basket vs cathedral are missing from most pages; (3) activity-by-activity guidance (dive, surf, ski gloves, climbing, gym) and travel care (flights, hotel safes, claw check before a destination wedding, overseas insurance cover) is absent everywhere; (4) gender-neutral framing; (5) no one is selling, so we can be honest with "skip it if" lines.
- Regional: AU jewellers (qreport, adc, thediamondclub, kavalri, shjewellery) appear in AU, Dubai and Singapore SERPs, so AU authority travels. Intent is global.
