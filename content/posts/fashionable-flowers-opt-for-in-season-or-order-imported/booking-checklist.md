# Booking checklist: wedding flowers in season

Take this to your first meeting with your florist, once your date and venue are fixed. Tick each item as it is answered. No amounts are listed: get every quote, deposit and payment schedule in writing.

## Questions for your florist

**Local, imported and backup**

- [ ] Which stems in the design are grown in Australia and in season for our date?
- [ ] Which stems will be imported, and when do they need to be ordered?
- [ ] If an imported or late stem is not available, what goes in its place, and in what colour and shape?

**Essentials and substitutes**

- [ ] Which flowers are essential to us, and which may you substitute? Please put both lists in writing.
- [ ] Can the proposal note which stems could be swapped, and what with?
- [ ] When will we review the substitute list again as the date approaches?

**Quote**

- [ ] Can the quote be itemised, with imported stems, labour and set-up shown as separate lines?
- [ ] Are likely substitutions noted on the quote?
- [ ] Does a peak week near our date (for example Valentine's Day or Christmas) affect supply or your team?

**Overseas florist (destination wedding)**

- [ ] What is grown near the venue, and what is brought in from further away?
- [ ] What is the backup if a stem is not available?
- [ ] Can we have the quote in writing, itemised, with substitutions noted?
- [ ] Which currency is the deposit paid in?

**Scale and set-up for a large guest list**

- [ ] How many spaces need dressing across the ceremony, reception and any other events?
- [ ] How will you staff the set-up of each space, and how close to the start can you set up?
- [ ] Can ceremony pieces be moved to the reception?
- [ ] Is there water and shade at the venue for buckets during the day?

## 12 month planner

Australia's seasons run opposite to the northern hemisphere's. Each season starts on the first of the month. In the northern tropics, plan around the wet season (October to April) and the dry season (May to September) instead. For a wedding overseas, use the destination's season and ask a florist there.
