# Booking checklist: seating display planning and venue questions

Take this to your venue and stationer once your guest list and table plan are settled. Tick each item as it is answered, and ask for the venue's answers in writing.

## Plan first

- [ ] **Finish the table plan before choosing a display.** Build it in [Wonderland Studio](https://weddedwonderland.com/wonderland-studio), our planning app for seating charts and RSVPs, so the list you send to the stationer or the screen is the same list your RSVPs produced.
- [ ] **Lock a date for final names.** Agree with your stationer when the list must be final for printing or writing.
- [ ] **Keep one master list.** Every later change goes into it first, then onto the display.

## Display style

- [ ] **Choose by what guests will do:** take something (a sweet, a glass, a favour, a key), find something (a wall, a map, a mirror) or do something (a screen, a code, a photo spot).
- [ ] **Match the style to your guest count.** A wall or a screen holds a long list; handwritten photos and messages suit a smaller wedding.
- [ ] **Edible or floral pieces:** agree with your caterer how close to arrival they can be set out, and where they stay out of direct sun.
- [ ] **Champagne or welcome drinks:** arrange pouring and top-ups with the bar team, and include something without alcohol.
- [ ] **Take-home favours:** small enough for a carry-on if guests are flying home.

## Readability and order

- [ ] Generous type size, with names and table numbers in the same position on every card.
- [ ] Text that stands out clearly from its background.
- [ ] Checked at the hour guests will see it, with a lamp beside it if it will be dusk.
- [ ] Mirror or glass tested under the actual lighting for glare.
- [ ] **Order:** alphabetical by surname for a large wedding; by table is fine for a small one.
- [ ] Each table group has a clear heading (walls and maps).
- [ ] A map display has a plain alphabetical list beside it.

## Placement and flow

- [ ] Where guests arrive, but not in a doorway or right beside the bar.
- [ ] Access from more than one side, with room for several guests at once.
- [ ] A photo spot display placed so a queue for photos does not block guests who only need their table.

## Accessibility

- [ ] The display is never the only way to find a seat: a printed list kept at a seated reading height.
- [ ] Readable at every height, including for guests using wheelchairs, children and taller guests; tested with people in front of it before the day.
- [ ] A clear, flat path to the display and on to the tables, with nothing in the way.
- [ ] A host or a member of the bridal party stands nearby while guests arrive.
- [ ] Guests with additional needs can be seated early, with someone walking them to their places.
- [ ] Any reserved area carries neutral wording, such as reserved seating.

## Outdoor weather backup

- [ ] Every card weighted or clipped against wind.
- [ ] Edible and floral elements kept in the shade until the last moment.
- [ ] A covered backup spot agreed with the venue in advance.
- [ ] A printed list ready to move indoors if it rains.

## Late changes on the day

- [ ] Spare blank cards printed in the same style.
- [ ] A pen that matches the lettering.
- [ ] One named person, not either of you, holds the master list on the day, and every change goes through them.
- [ ] For a screen or code lookup, that person knows how to update it.

## Guest list privacy for a QR code or screen

- [ ] Decide who can see the full guest list.
- [ ] Decide whether a guest's lookup shows only their own table or the whole list.
- [ ] Decide what a screen at the entrance shows while nobody is using it.
- [ ] A printed list and a person who knows the plan, for guests without a phone or with a flat battery.

## Questions for the venue

**Space and setup**

- [ ] Where can the seating display stand?
- [ ] How early can it be set up?
- [ ] Can anything be fixed to walls, beams or trees?
- [ ] Who sets up the display, and who packs it down?

**Outdoor rules**

- [ ] What are the rules for outdoor displays in wind or rain?
- [ ] Where is the covered backup?

**Power and signal**

- [ ] Is power available at the entrance for a screen or lighting?
- [ ] How reliable is mobile signal or Wi-Fi at the entrance, if guests will scan a code?

**Deliveries**

- [ ] Can printed or fragile pieces be delivered ahead?
- [ ] Where will they be stored until setup?

**Food, drink and access**

- [ ] Can staff pour and top up welcome drinks at the display?
- [ ] Is there a step-free route from the arrival point to the display and the tables?

**Guests**

- [ ] Should the display carry a second language for international guests?

Setup windows, outdoor display rules and power access are details the venue has to confirm directly. If a venue does not publish them, mark them "not published" here and ask. Your Wedded Advisor confirms inclusions in writing before you commit.
