# Booking checklist: Greece wedding packages

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers; its "Not published" lines and the source pack's booking checklist lines are listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- Abaton Island Resort & Spa: the weddings page still links the 2025-2026 brochure. For a 2027 date, ask for the current brochure and confirm the package terms (accommodation not included, minimum 10 rooms for two nights, at least 20 reception guests, items not interchangeable) and each venue fee still apply.
- Grand Resort Lagonissi: the package brochure it links dates from 2022. Ask for the current package contents before comparing it with any other quote.
- Melenos: confirm whether the government and administrative fees its planner attends to sit inside or outside its costing sheet.
- Angsana Corfu: confirm whether a legal ceremony can be held on site (the 2026 brochure lists its spaces as symbolic wedding ceremony venues), and the minimum food and beverage spend the wedding offer is tied to.
- Four Seasons Astir Palace: confirm which ceremony types it hosts (civil, Greek Orthodox or symbolic) and where.
- Nana Princess: ask for the full inclusions and conditions of each package (Deluxe Dream, Ultimate Passion, Essential Elegance and the bespoke option); the weddings page shows highlights only.

## General

- Transfers: which transfers are included and for whom, whether airport and private transfers are included or arranged on request, and who meets guests and carries luggage (not published in any of these packages)
- Access for older guests and anyone with limited mobility at every venue: steps between guest rooms, the ceremony and the reception, level or step-free paths and lifts, walking distances across the grounds, and buggy or private transfers
- Closure and exclusive use: whether the property or the chosen space can be closed to other guests, whether exclusive use covers the chosen spaces or the whole hotel, and which one is being quoted
- Finishing times, curfews, overtime terms, and set-up and pack-down windows (not published for any of these packages)
- Outdoor and amplified music rules on the couple's dates
- Rain or wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space
- Minimum guests, minimum nights and room blocks, and whether the couple's room upgrade is confirmed or only subject to availability
- Outside supplier policy: planner requirements, and whether the couple may bring their own florist, photographer or band
- Currency of the quote, which items are paid locally and separately, deposit amount and timing, refund timetable and cancellation terms
- Legal ceremony: documents the municipality or church needs (apostille, Greek translation), whether an Australian-issued CNI is accepted, notice period, registration within 40 days, and which municipal or Metropolis fees sit outside the package
