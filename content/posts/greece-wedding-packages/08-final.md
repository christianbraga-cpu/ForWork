Two package documents from two Greek resorts can share a title and describe quite different weddings. Greece wedding packages usually bundle the ceremony, its set-up, a coordinator and some help with the paperwork. Guest rooms, exclusive use of a space, transfers and outside suppliers are where they part ways, and each venue draws that line in its own place.

This guide sets out what each kind of package covers, venue by venue, and how to read two quotes side by side. For the venues themselves, start with [our guide to Greece wedding venues](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues), or browse [our curated Greece wedding collection](https://weddedwonderland.com/destinations/europe/greece).

## Key takeaways

- A Greek venue package usually covers the ceremony, its set-up and a coordinator, and the rest depends on the venue.
- Civil, Greek Orthodox and symbolic ceremonies can be packaged separately, so check which one a package is built for.
- Exclusive use, guest rooms, transfers and outside suppliers are where packages differ most, so ask about each in writing.
- Compare quotes on the same guest count, spaces, hours, nights and currency before paying any deposit.

The first question for any Greek package is which ceremony it is built for. The Elounda Collection publishes separate civil, Orthodox and symbolic packages, each with its own documents and conditions. <!-- fact_08 -->

Nana Princess holds civil weddings on its private beach, Greek Orthodox weddings in the Chapel of St. George in its grounds, and symbolic weddings led by its event coordinator. <!-- fact_04 --> Melenos coordinators arrange civil, symbolic or traditional Orthodox ceremonies, and Atrium Prestige offers its private Tsambika chapel, across a bridge, for a more traditional ceremony. <!-- fact_100 --> <!-- fact_111 -->

For an Orthodox package, the Elounda Collection's document list adds church requirements: certificates of marital status, a Certificate of No Impediment from the church parish and baptism certificates. <!-- fact_12 --> One partner must be Christian Orthodox, both must be baptised Orthodox, Roman Catholic or Protestant, and the wedding is announced in a local newspaper at least eight days before, with approval from the Metropolis. <!-- fact_12 -->

Small details belong to the ceremony too. Melenos coordinators look after touches for Orthodox ceremonies such as Jordan almonds on a silver tray, one of many [Greek wedding traditions](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/greek-wedding-traditions) a package can carry. <!-- fact_103 -->

The Elounda Collection's symbolic and vow renewal package includes a bouquet and boutonniere, two witnesses if needed, the hotel's official representative, sparkling wine, a single-tier cake and breakfast in bed. <!-- fact_13 --> Its document is direct: "Symbolic weddings /renewal of vows are not legally recognized from the Greek constitution." <!-- fact_13 -->

Euphoria's three options are written for an informal wedding or a renewal of vows, and each includes a certificate of informal marriage from the hotel. <!-- fact_16 --> Angsana Corfu's 2026 brochure lists its ceremony spaces under "Symbolic Wedding Ceremony Venues": Vertigo Sky Bar for up to 250 and Oribu Garden for up to 30. <!-- fact_105 --> If you plan to marry legally at home and celebrate in Greece, these packages are written for informal or symbolic ceremonies.

On the legal side, as at November 2026, some countries ask you to prove you are not already married, most often with a [Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) (CNI). <!-- fact_114 --> DFAT can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only one issued in their own country, so ask the town hall in Greece whether it accepts a CNI issued in Australia or needs one from the Australian Embassy in Athens. <!-- fact_114 -->

The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/Notarial.html) issues CNIs and witnesses notices of intended marriage by appointment, with CNI documents also accepted by post, and it does not give legal advice. <!-- fact_113 --> A marriage made overseas cannot be registered in Australia, but it is usually recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. <!-- fact_115 -->

Read the package's document list against that. The Elounda Collection sets out these steps: <!-- fact_10 -->

- Each document Apostille-certified, one by one. <!-- fact_10 -->
- Each document translated into Greek. <!-- fact_10 -->
- Documents emailed for approval at least four weeks before the wedding. <!-- fact_10 -->
- Documents couriered to the hotel at least three weeks before. <!-- fact_10 -->
- All documents issued within three months of the wedding date. <!-- fact_10 -->
- The marriage registered at the Registry Office within 40 days after the ceremony. <!-- fact_11 -->

In the Elounda Collection's words: "The marriage has to be registered by the couple at the Registry Office in Aghios Nikolaos within 40 days after the ceremony." <!-- fact_11 --> A Friday or weekend wedding is registered the following Monday. <!-- fact_11 -->

### Exclusive use of the space

Is the space yours alone, and which space is it? At Grace Hotel, the venue fee for more than 50 guests covers exclusive use of the event spaces you select, with furniture, bars, tableware, set-up and breakdown and event staff. <!-- fact_14 --> For 50 guests or fewer it covers the chosen spaces, tables and chairs, tableware and a wedding arbour. <!-- fact_14 -->

At Nana Princess, exclusive use of the chosen room or venue appears among the symbolic wedding decoration schemes. <!-- fact_04 --> Four Seasons Astir Palace offers exclusive use of one of four restaurants for private dining, and opens select public spaces to wedding parties subject to availability. <!-- fact_123 -->

Is the venue itself charged on top? Abaton's brochure gives each named venue its own venue fee. <!-- fact_23 --> And if you want the whole hotel, Canaves Collection takes buyout requests per property, from Canaves Sunday with 10 rooms for 22 guests to Canaves Epitome with 53 rooms for up to 170, with no buyout inclusions published. <!-- fact_26 -->

### Guest rooms and minimum nights

Do you have to book rooms to get the package? The Elounda Collection's packages are valid only if the couple stays a minimum of three nights in one of its hotels. <!-- fact_10 --> Abaton's 2025-2026 terms say packages do not include accommodation and are valid with a minimum booking of 10 rooms for two nights and at least 20 reception guests. <!-- fact_24 -->

Others take a lighter line. Grace Hotel lets guests book individually and sets no minimum number of rooms, with a room block or buyout priced by its sales manager on request. <!-- fact_15 --> Melenos suggests reserving the whole hotel for at least three nights, as a suggestion rather than a condition, with more guests in its village apartments a 10 minute walk away. <!-- fact_102 --> Elounda Beach's page publishes no minimum guest, room or night condition. <!-- fact_03 -->

Is the upgrade promised? Nana Princess, Euphoria and Elounda Beach make the couple's room upgrade subject to availability, and the Elounda Collection does the same for early check-in and late check-out. <!-- fact_27 --> Four Seasons includes one night, the wedding night, for the couple. <!-- fact_122 -->

### Transfers

Who moves your guests, and which guests? Grace Hotel's fee for more than 50 guests includes transport between the hotel and the event site for guests staying at Grace Hotel. <!-- fact_14 --> At Four Seasons Astir Palace, local transport for the couple or guests can be arranged on request. <!-- fact_123 -->

The Elounda Collection's package covers the couple's transfer within the hotel grounds to the ceremony, and lists boat transfers in its wedding services section. <!-- fact_09 --> <!-- fact_08 -->

Not published: airport transfers for guests, in any of these packages.

In peak season, traffic congestion is severe in the main cities and on the islands, and only licensed taxis with the meter running should be used. <!-- fact_117 -->

Can you bring your own people? ## Venue package, planner or building your own

There are three ways to put a Greek wedding together, and the right one depends on how much you want to hold yourself.

A venue package keeps the ceremony, the coordinator and the set-up under one roof and one contract. It suits a couple who want the venue's own team, its own spaces and its own rhythm. The trade-off is flexibility: where items are not interchangeable, a change you want may not be a change you can make. <!-- fact_17 -->

A planner sits above the venue. Some venues expect one. Grace Hotel requires a planner for all weddings and supplies its own event manager for venue details. <!-- fact_15 --> The Myconian Collection offers to prepare the stage for your own team or provide the whole event itself. <!-- fact_25 --> A planner gives you one brief across several suppliers, and one more contract to read.

Building your own means contracting the venue, the photographer, the florist, the music and the transport one by one. It gives you the most say and the most paperwork. It also makes the like-for-like comparison below harder, because every quote is shaped differently.

## How to compare two quotes like for like

Two Greek quotes rarely line up on the page. Put them on the same footing first, then compare. Ask each venue to answer the same ten points in writing.

1. **Ceremony type.** Is the package built for a civil, Greek Orthodox or symbolic ceremony, and does it change if you switch?
2. **Guest count basis.** What guest number is the quote built on, and what changes above or below it?
3. **Spaces and hours.** Which ceremony and reception spaces are named, and from what time until what time, and are there step-free routes between rooms, ceremony and reception for older guests?
4. **Exclusive use.** Is the space yours alone, and is a venue fee charged on top of the package, and can the property be closed to other guests?
5. **Rooms and nights.** Is there a minimum stay or room block, and is the couple's room included?
6. **Transfers.** Which transfers are included, for whom, which are arranged on request, and are airport and private transfers among them?
7. **Supplier rules.** Must you use a planner, and may you bring your own florist, photographer or band?
8. **Documents.** Which legal or church steps does the venue handle, and which fees sit outside the package?
9. **Currency.** What currency is the quote in, and which items are quoted separately?
10. **Deposit and cancellation terms.** What do you pay to hold the date, when, and what happens if plans change?

If one quote answers a point and the other is silent, the silent one is not cheaper. It is unfinished.

A Greek package priced abroad is paid from Australia, so the terms around the money matter as much as the inclusions.

Start with the currency. Is the quote in euro, and will the deposit and the balance be invoiced in the same currency? Which items, such as municipal or church fees, are paid locally and separately? Your Wedded Advisor quotes in AUD.

Then the deposit. How much holds the date, and when is the next payment due? What is refundable, and on what timetable? Abaton's packages are subject to availability, and Euphoria's offers are too, so ask when a quoted package becomes a confirmed one. <!-- fact_24 --> <!-- fact_17 -->

Read cancellation terms beside your travel insurance. The [travel insurance advice](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance) from Smartraveller, updated in August 2026, is to read the Product Disclosure Statement before you buy and to check whether the policy covers cancellation if the advice level rises after you have booked. <!-- fact_119 --> Basic medical-only policies usually don't cover cancellation costs, and credit card insurance often covers fewer situations and usually has to be activated. <!-- fact_119 -->

For Greece, take out comprehensive travel insurance that covers all medical costs, including evacuation, before you leave. [Smartraveller Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) is the page to check before you fly. <!-- fact_118 --> There is no agreement between Australia and Greece for free healthcare, so visitors pay for all medical care. <!-- fact_118 --> Every guest flying in should hold a policy that covers the whole trip, stopovers included. <!-- fact_118 -->

Wildfires occur in the extended summer from June to September, and heatwaves are frequent through the summer months. <!-- fact_116 --> Angsana Corfu's Vertigo Sky Bar is available for ceremonies from mid-May to the end of September, weather permitting, so ask every venue for its weather back-up in writing. <!-- fact_105 -->

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Greek wedding packages all inclusive?

Rarely in the sense most couples mean. Abaton's packages do not include accommodation, the Elounda Collection's exclude municipal or Metropolis fees, and Elounda Beach lists flowers for the location, photography and entertainment as additional services. <!-- fact_24 --> <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_03 --> Treat a package as the core of the day and ask for every extra in writing.

### Does a venue package include the legal paperwork?

Some include help with it. Elounda Beach lists legal and licence paperwork requirements, and the Elounda Collection lists documentation assistance and settlement of the licence with the Municipality Office. <!-- fact_01 --> <!-- fact_09 --> The Elounda Collection states that symbolic weddings and vow renewals are not legally recognised under the Greek constitution. <!-- fact_13 --> At the Elounda Collection, certifying, translating and couriering your documents still falls to you. <!-- fact_10 -->

### Can we bring our own planner or suppliers?

It depends on the venue. Grace Hotel requires a planner for every wedding, and Four Seasons Astir Palace works with your florist or consultant. <!-- fact_15 --> <!-- fact_123 --> Euphoria and Abaton sell complete packages whose items are not interchangeable. <!-- fact_17 --> <!-- fact_24 --> Neither publishes whether you may bring your own photographer or florist, so ask before you book.

### Do we have to book guest rooms to get a package?

At some venues, yes. The Elounda Collection asks for a minimum three-night stay, and Abaton's terms set a minimum of 10 rooms for two nights. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_24 --> Grace Hotel sets no minimum number of rooms. <!-- fact_15 -->

### Can Australians legally marry in Greece?

Yes. Venues such as the Elounda Collection offer civil and Orthodox packages, and the marriage is then registered at the local Registry Office. <!-- fact_08 --> <!-- fact_11 --> A marriage made overseas is usually recognised in Australia if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law, and a lawyer can confirm validity. <!-- fact_115 --> Prepare documents well ahead.
