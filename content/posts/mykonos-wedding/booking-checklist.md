# Booking checklist: Mykonos wedding

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue (or the authority named) for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy; the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences, which sit in the draft note, not here. Each venue's "Not published" items in the body, the source pack's booking checklist assumptions and the house-rule questions in "House rules to agree before you book" are listed below.

## Legal ceremony (check with the authorities, not the venue alone)

- Civil marriage with the Municipality of Mykonos: no official municipal page was read. Confirm the steps, documents, notice period or lead times, ceremony locations for foreign couples and any fees with the Municipality of Mykonos, and the documents and any fees with the Australian Embassy in Athens (notarial services by appointment, Monday to Friday, 9am to 12.30pm; a fee applies). Get the list in writing before the couple sets a date, and keep copies of every document sent.
- Whether Greece needs a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI), whether an Australian-issued CNI is accepted or it must be issued in Greece, and whether it needs an authentication or apostille (ask the Greek embassy or consulate).
- Which documents the Greek authority needs legalised, apostille or authentication certificate, and whether any need a translation and who may make it. No official page read says so; do not tell the couple a translation is required until confirmed.
- Legalisation timing: processing times vary and slow at the end of the school year and in peak holidays; lodge before the Australian summer holidays.
- Kivotos says its coordinators help with municipal paperwork and the marriage licence: confirm exactly what they handle. The Wedded Advisor does not handle legal, church or religious paperwork.
- Name change: whether the Greek certificate works as proof of a name change in the couple's state (check with the state registry of births, deaths and marriages).
- Same-sex couples: no official page on same-sex civil marriage in Greece was read. Do not state the legal position; confirm with the municipality and the embassy.
- Symbolic ceremony: confirm with the venue which ceremony types it hosts, and whether the couple marries legally in Australia first.

## Deposits, cancellation, exclusive use and music (every venue)

- Deposit amount and due date, the payment schedule, which payments are refundable, and the terms for moving or cancelling the date
- Whether exclusive use covers the chosen space, the venue or the whole property, whether it can be closed to other guests, for how many days (wedding day only, or the days either side), and what exclusive use covers
- Music: whether a DJ or live band can play, where, the time amplified music must stop, and where an after-party can move
- Contract language and currency, and how the deposit is held

## General (every venue)

- Ceremony types hosted (civil, religious, symbolic, vow renewal)
- Ceremony time: the times available on the date, with set-up and pack-down windows
- Weather: the covered or indoor plan for a strong north wind, a rare shower or a heatwave, and which covered space is held; whether ceremony spaces are open on the date
- Suppliers: whether outside photographers, florists and musicians are allowed, and the rules for access and loading
- Packages: which spaces, menus and services each package includes, and whether they are current for the couple's year
- Rooms: how many rooms guests can hold on the dates, room block or buyout, minimum stay
- Access: a step-free route between guest rooms, the getting-ready space, the ceremony and the reception; the nearest point a car can reach and how guests with limited mobility get from there to the ceremony
- Getting there: the drive from Mykonos Airport (no distance or drive time was sourced), and how guests travel between the venue and their accommodation in the evening and back from town after the reception
- Ferries: guests arriving by sea should reconfirm departures; some services lack suitable access for people with disabilities
