# Wedding RSVP reminder checklist

Your own planning sheet for the reminders you and your family send. Every step is timed from two dates you set: your reply date, and the date your venue and caterer need final numbers.

## Your two dates
- [ ] **Reply date:** ____________ (if you have not set one, see [how to manage wedding RSVPs from invitation to final numbers](/expert-advice/wedding-rsvp))
- [ ] **Final-numbers date from your venue and caterer:** ____________ (ask each supplier for theirs, in writing, and use the earlier one)
- [ ] **Last-call date:** ____________ (your firm new date, set before the final-numbers date so you can still give your caterer an answer)

## Reminder schedule template

Three steps at most, and each one goes only to guests who are still outstanding.

| Step | When | Who receives it | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Gentle reminder | Before your reply date, while there is still comfortable time to answer | Guests and households not yet replied | [ ] |
| Check your list | On your reply date: note who is outstanding, and let that evening's late replies settle | Nobody contacted | [ ] |
| 2. Personal follow-up | After your reply date | Each guest or household still outstanding, from someone they know | [ ] |
| 3. Last call | Only if a name is still blank close to your final-numbers date, with your last-call date | Guests still outstanding | [ ] |
| Decide and tell your caterer | After your last-call date, before the final-numbers date | Your caterer and venue | [ ] |

- [ ] The earlier your venue and caterer need numbers, the earlier every step moves.
- [ ] Take the website banner down once your reply date has passed.
- [ ] For a guest with no answer after the last call: decide as a couple whether to count them as attending or declined, and tell your caterer which way you went.
- [ ] Destination or multi-event weekend: ask for a reply for each event (welcome event, ceremony and dinner, farewell event), plus any dietary, mobility or access needs.

## Channel checklist per guest

Copy one block per guest or household, or keep these columns in your one guest list.

**Guest or household:** ____________ **Who sends:** ____________ (partner, or each set of parents for their own side)

- [ ] **Text message:** for friends and guests who already message you. One person or household per message, with the reply link or method included again.
- [ ] **Email:** for a formal suite or when guests need the details to plan (reply link, events, anything to book). Addressed to this household only.
- [ ] **Printed card:** for a formal suite, older relatives, or guests who replied by post before. Repeat the reply method exactly as on the reply card; if it carries a QR code, print the web address beside it.
- [ ] **Call or in person:** for older relatives, guests who have not answered a message, and the last few names. Check the guest's time zone before calling overseas, keep your guest list open and note the answer straight away.
- [ ] **Plus-one:** if the invitation included one, ask for their name in the same message; if it did not, say nothing about one.
- [ ] **Reply recorded:** yes / no / per event, plus-one name, dietary or access note, date received.

## Privacy checklist

General information as at November 2026, not legal advice.

- [ ] Send every reminder to one guest or household only.
- [ ] No group texts, group chats or shared email lists that show other guests' names, numbers or addresses.
- [ ] Keep the guest list somewhere you trust, and share it only with the people helping you.
- [ ] Give each helper only the names and details for the guests they are contacting.
- [ ] Helpers do not forward or screenshot the list, and pass every answer back to you so your one list stays accurate.
- [ ] Ask for dietary, mobility or access needs only as needed for planning, and keep those notes with the same care as contact details.
- [ ] Any card, message or banner with a QR code also shows the RSVP web address in full, so guests can check where it leads.
- [ ] Remind only guests who have not replied; filter your list before each step.
