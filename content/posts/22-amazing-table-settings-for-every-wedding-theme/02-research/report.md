## Cross-region summary
- Intent: visual inspiration plus "what goes where". Image and shopping features dominate every city; editorial guides are thin and dated (2017 to 2021 in AU).
- Guest counts / pricing shown: none of the ranking guides gives prices; shopping packs show hire pack prices (not used, no prices in this post).
- Top concern (PAA): the correct place setting, classic/basic setups, table spacing, themes.
- Gap we can own: a luxury, destination-aware guide that explains the place setting piece by piece (charger, plates, cutlery order, glassware, napkin, name card), then shows how each theme changes those same pieces. No ranking AU page does both.
- Regional: AU results lean to hire companies; US to shop guides and formal etiquette; Singapore surfaces solemnisation tables; Dubai is Pinterest-heavy.
- recommended_scope: global (single page, AU English, regional notes only where hire or service differs).

## Cannibalisation and split
- /style-advice/wedding-table-decor targets "wedding table decor" and already covers the place setting in step 9. Risk: both pages answering "how to set a wedding table".
- Proposed split: decor page owns decor and the room (table shape, linen, centrepieces, candles, hire quote). This page owns "wedding table settings", "wedding place settings", "formal place setting" and theme looks built from the place setting. This page links to the decor page as the hub for decor; the decor page's step 9 links here for the full piece-by-piece setting.
- Trend posts (2024 trends, King's Table, exhibition) are trend/news intent; link out to them, do not retarget.
- Do not create a new /wedding-table-settings URL.
