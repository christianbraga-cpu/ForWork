# Booking checklist: wedding table settings

Take this to your venue or stylist once your menu, service style and rough guest count are set. Tick each item as it is answered, and ask for the answers in writing.

## The place setting, piece by piece

Settle the menu first, because it decides the cutlery and glassware. Only set what your guests will use.

- [ ] **Charger:** yes or no, and which finish. It is optional, and suits a plated dinner and a classic theme more than a shared table.
- [ ] **Plates:** dinner plate, entree plate and bread plate (upper left, above the forks, with the butter knife across it, blade facing inward).
- [ ] **Cutlery by course:** laid in order of use from the outside in. Forks to the left; knives (blades facing inward) and spoons to the right.
    - [ ] Entree
    - [ ] Soup or sorbet, if on the menu
    - [ ] Main
    - [ ] Dessert: above the plate for the whole meal (fork handle pointing left, spoon above it with handle pointing right), or brought out with dessert
- [ ] **Glassware:** water glass above the knife, then wine glasses to its right in the order they are used; a coupe or flute for the toasts, set from the start or brought out with the speeches.
- [ ] **Napkin:** to the left of the forks or in the centre of the charger; fold and fabric chosen to suit the theme.
- [ ] **Name card:** at the top of the setting or on the napkin. Follows the final seating plan.
- [ ] **Menu:** on the charger, tucked under the napkin, or standing beside the name card. Follows the final menu.

## Questions for the venue

**Tableware the venue holds**

- [ ] What tableware, glassware and linen do you hold, and for how many guests?
- [ ] Do you have chargers, and in which finishes?
- [ ] Can we see the actual pieces, or photos of them laid on your tables?

**What is hired in**

- [ ] Which pieces would need to be hired in for our theme and guest count?
- [ ] What would that add to the quote? Please itemise it in writing.

**Service**

- [ ] Who sets the tables, and who clears them between courses?
- [ ] Will the dessert cutlery be set from the start or brought out with dessert?

**Access**

- [ ] Is there step-free access to the tables?
- [ ] Is there room between tables for a guest using a walking frame or wheelchair?

**The trial table**

- [ ] Can we see a full mock-up of one table, laid exactly as on the day, before we commit?
- [ ] If we cannot be there, can you send clear photos or a video walk-through?

**Other limits**

- [ ] Are there limits on open flame, or on bringing in outside suppliers?

## Trial table checks

Go through each check in person, or while you watch the photos or video walk-through.

- [ ] **The full setting with the centrepiece in place.** Glasses, cutlery and florals do not crowd each other.
- [ ] **Sightlines.** Sit down: can you see and speak to the guest opposite?
- [ ] **The light.** Look at the table at the hour the meal will be served, in daylight or by candlelight.
- [ ] **Stationery proofs.** Lay the real name cards and menus, and read them from a seated guest's view.
- [ ] **Spacing between places.** Ask what each guest needs for your chosen setting and chairs, and confirm it against your guest count.
- [ ] **Access.** Walk the route from the door to the tables, checking it is step-free with room for a walking frame or wheelchair.

## Order of decisions

1. Service style and menu
2. Guest count and table layout
3. Plates, cutlery and glassware (start with what the venue holds)
4. Napkins and linen
5. Stationery: menus, then name cards from the seating plan
