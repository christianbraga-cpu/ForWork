Santorini is the choice, or very nearly. What you need now is the order in which to decide things. Planning a Santorini wedding comes down to a short run of decisions: legal or symbolic, then the season, the setting, the guest list, how everyone arrives, the documents, the house rules and the weekend around the day. Take them in that order and each one narrows the next.

This guide walks through all nine steps, with a booking checklist at the end. For the wider picture of [getting married in Greece](https://weddedwonderland.com/destinations/europe/greece), and for venues across the islands, keep [our guide to Greece wedding venues](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues) open alongside it.

## Key takeaways

- Decide first whether your Santorini ceremony will be legal or symbolic, because that choice sets your documents, your timeline and the venues that suit you.
- Choose your season from official data: at Naxos, the nearest official station, June to August are the driest months, May and September run cooler than July and August, and October is wetter, so set the ceremony time around the summer heat and the north wind.
- Match the setting to your guest list, because caldera terraces, hotels, wineries and private villas differ widely in capacity and guest rooms.
- Put every house rule in writing before you sign, including music times, exclusive use, ceremony times and the plan if the weather turns.

## Why couples choose Santorini for their wedding

Santorini, known in ancient times as Thera, sits at the southernmost point of the Cyclades. <!-- fact_200 --> It is not one island but a cluster: Thera, Thirasia, Aspronisi, Palea Kameni and Nea Kameni. <!-- fact_200 --> Its fame rests on an active volcano, and its villages spread along the rim of the caldera, where you can stay right on the edge. <!-- fact_200 -->

The beaches are black, red and white sand, multicoloured pebbles and steep cliffs. <!-- fact_201 --> Inland, the island is arid but fertile, growing mainly vines, yellow split peas and tomatoes. <!-- fact_201 --> That is why a Santorini wedding can mean a cliff-edge terrace in Oia one evening and a winery in Pyrgos the next. <!-- fact_08 --> <!-- fact_300 -->

Everything else follows from this decision. A legal marriage in Greece brings documents, appointments and lead times. A symbolic ceremony on the island, after a legal step at home, keeps the paperwork in Australia and frees the day itself.

Some venues are set up for both. Canaves Collection in Oia lists religious, civil, symbolic and vow renewal ceremonies on its wedding enquiry form. <!-- fact_09 --> Ask every venue on your shortlist which ceremony types it hosts before you fall for a terrace.

### Marrying legally in Greece

As at November 2026, an overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid under the law of the country where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. <!-- fact_101 --> Australia does not recognise an overseas marriage where either person is already married, is not old enough to marry, is too closely related to the other, or is forced to marry. <!-- fact_102 -->

A foreign marriage certificate is usually accepted as proof of the marriage, but it may not work as proof of a name change. <!-- fact_101 --> If either of you plans to change your name, check with your state registry of births, deaths and marriages before you fly. A lawyer can confirm the certificate is valid in Australia. <!-- fact_101 -->

For a civil marriage on the island, check the steps with the Municipality of Thira and the [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html), including any fees. Step 6 covers the documents in detail, and [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) sets out the Australian side.

### A symbolic ceremony after a legal step at home

You can sign the legal paperwork quietly in Australia, then hold the ceremony everyone travels for in Santorini. An overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, though in most cases it is recognised here. <!-- fact_101 -->

A symbolic ceremony also opens the day to touches a registry office never would. Grace Hotel in Imerovigli offers a unity ceremony in which the two of you combine black and white volcanic sand collected from Santorini beaches. <!-- fact_03 -->

### Weather by month from official station data

The [Hellenic National Meteorological Service](http://oldportal.emy.gr/emy/en/climatology/climatology_city?perifereia=South%20Aegean&poli=Naxos) has no station on Santorini itself. <!-- fact_204 --> The figures below are for Naxos, the nearest official station, so read them as a guide to the neighbourhood rather than a forecast for the caldera. <!-- fact_204 -->

|---|---|---|---|

| May | 22.1 | 10.6 | 3.6 |
| June | 25.9 | 2.4 | 1.1 |
| July | 27.1 | 0.9 | 0.4 |
| August | 27.0 | 1.6 | 0.3 |
| September | 25.1 | 7.7 | 1.9 |
| October | 21.9 | 38.6 | 5.5 |

<!-- fact_205 --> <!-- fact_206 -->

A few patterns matter for a wedding. From June to August, rain all but disappears at Naxos, with under one rain day a month in July and August. <!-- fact_206 --> October is a different month: rain rises to 38.6 mm across 5.5 days, and April sees 6.0 rain days. <!-- fact_206 --> May and September sit between, with mean maximums of 22.1°C and 25.1°C. <!-- fact_205 -->

The wind deserves as much attention as the temperature. At Naxos the prevailing wind blows from the north in every month, and the station's mean wind readings run higher from July to October than in May and June. <!-- fact_207 -->

### Heat, wind and wildfire advice for travellers

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller's advice for Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) is to exercise normal safety precautions. <!-- fact_208 --> Within that, several points shape a Santorini wedding date.

**Heat.** Heatwaves are frequent over the summer months. <!-- fact_209 --> Plan water, light fabrics, shade and air-conditioned rest for guests, and set a summer ceremony later in the day.

**Wildfire.** Wildfires occur in the extended summer period from June to September, including near tourist areas, and can start with little warning and travel quickly. <!-- fact_209 --> Expect some travel disruption in affected areas, and keep a covered or indoor fallback in your plan. <!-- fact_209 -->

**Wind and ferries.** Ferry services can be delayed or cancelled in bad weather, mainly because of strong winds. <!-- fact_211 --> Guests arriving by sea should check the forecast and reconfirm departures. <!-- fact_211 -->

**Swimming.** Lifeguards operate at many Greek beaches from June to September, and sea conditions can change quickly. <!-- fact_210 --> Point guests to patrolled beaches, and away from cliffs, rock faces and boats. <!-- fact_210 -->

**The ground itself.** Greece is in an active seismic zone, with earthquakes and volcanic activity, including near Santorini. <!-- fact_104 --> Saharan dust storms also occur, particularly in spring, and can reduce air quality and visibility. <!-- fact_212 --> Keep travel insurance in place for everyone travelling, and check the advice again before you fly.

Whichever month you choose, confirm that your venue and its ceremony spaces are open on your date.

Guests flying from Australia arrive tired, and some will be older or less steady on stairs. Build the travel plan around them.

### Flying into Santorini

The flight from Athens International Airport to Santorini takes about 50 minutes, and from Thessaloniki about 1 hour 30 minutes. <!-- fact_202 --> [Santorini Airport](https://www.jtr-airport.gr/en) is served by Greek domestic airlines as well as several European and Gulf carriers, and has separate Schengen and non-Schengen departure areas. <!-- fact_203 --> From the airport, guests can travel on by public bus or taxi. <!-- fact_203 -->

The airport also has an Accessible Travel section for passengers with reduced mobility, which is worth sending to any guest who needs help on arrival. <!-- fact_203 -->

### Arriving by ferry

Some guests will want the sea crossing. As at November 2026, expect high temperatures and large crowds at busy seaports during the May to October tourist season. <!-- fact_211 --> In most cases you carry your own luggage up and down stairs on ferries. <!-- fact_211 --> Services can be delayed or cancelled when the wind is strong, so guests should reconfirm departures. <!-- fact_211 -->

On Santorini, smaller boats or tenders carry cruise ship passengers to and from shore, and passengers using tenders may have difficulty with stairs. <!-- fact_211 -->

**Entry.** Greece is in the Schengen area, and Australians can make short trips without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. <!-- fact_106 --> On arrival, Australian passport holders register fingerprints and a photo under the European Entry/Exit System, which is free but may slow the border. <!-- fact_106 --> Count the wedding week inside the 90 days if you are adding a longer European trip.

### Access and transfers to ask about

Caldera views come with steps. Ask each venue on your shortlist:

- Is there a step-free route between guest rooms, the getting-ready space, the ceremony and the reception?
- How far is the nearest point a car can reach, and how do guests with limited mobility get from there to the terrace?
- How long is the drive from the airport, and how do guests usually travel between the venue and their accommodation in the evening?

## Step 6: Gather your documents

If you are marrying legally in Australia and holding a symbolic ceremony on the island, skip to [Step 7](#step-7-lock-in-the-house-rules-in-writing). If you chose a legal ceremony in Greece in Step 1, start this step now rather than in the final months. The requirements below are as at November 2026.

### Certificate of No Impediment

Some countries ask you to prove you are free to marry, most commonly with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) and certificates from a registry of births, deaths and marriages. <!-- fact_100 --> Those registry certificates can be a statement of no record of a previous marriage, a Single Status certificate or a No Record Result certificate. <!-- fact_100 --> Local authorities may also ask for divorce papers, a late spouse's death certificate, and your original birth certificate and passport. <!-- fact_100 -->

The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only CNIs issued in their own country. <!-- fact_100 --> Ask the Greek embassy or consulate:

- whether you need a CNI
- whether you can get it in Australia
- whether it also needs an authentication or apostille. <!-- fact_100 -->

In Greece, the Australian Embassy in Athens witnesses CNI applications, issues CNIs and witnesses notices of intended marriage. <!-- fact_103 --> All notarial services need an appointment, Monday to Friday, 9am to 12.30pm, though CNI and apostille documents can be sent by post. <!-- fact_103 --> They are ready for collection within two working days unless same-day collection is arranged in advance, or can be returned by mail. <!-- fact_103 --> The Embassy gives no legal advice, honorary consuls cannot provide notarial services, and a fee applies. <!-- fact_103 -->

### Apostille and translation

An apostille or authentication legalises a signature or seal on a document so it can be used overseas. <!-- fact_103 --> Ask the Greek authority which of your documents it needs and which must be legalised, and whether that means an apostille or an authentication certificate. <!-- fact_320 --> Ask too whether any document needs a translation, and who may make it.

The apostille or authentication certificate is applied directly to the document. <!-- fact_321 --> If you do not want your originals marked, have a notarised copy legalised instead, and check the Greek authority accepts it. <!-- fact_321 --> Documents that are not original Australian public documents must first be notarised by an Australian Notary Public. <!-- fact_321 -->

Requests go on the Document Legalisation Request Form, signed in front of an authorised witness. <!-- fact_322 --> You can lodge it by appointment at an Australian Passport Office, in person at many Australian embassies and consulates overseas, or by registered mail to the Authentications Section in Melbourne or Sydney, and a fee applies. <!-- fact_322 --> [How to get your documents legalised](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services/how-get-your-documents-legalised) has the details.

Processing times vary, with delays at the end of the school year and in peak holiday periods, and for incomplete requests. <!-- fact_323 --> An Australian-issued apostille can be verified online. <!-- fact_323 --> If your wedding falls after the Australian summer holidays, lodge well before them.

### Check with the municipality and the embassy

For a civil marriage on the island, check the steps, documents and lead times with the Municipality of Thira and the Australian Embassy in Athens, including any fees. Confirm the list in writing before you set your date. The full picture for Australians marrying in Greece is in [our Greece wedding venues guide](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues#getting-legally-married-in-greece-as-australians-in-brief).

## Step 7: Lock in the house rules in writing

A venue's rules shape the day as much as its view, and every one is easier to settle before you sign. Send each venue these questions.

**The planner.** Does the venue require an outside wedding planner, and from how far out? Grace Hotel requires a wedding planner for every wedding, involved for at least 3 months beforehand, and gives you a dedicated event manager for the venue itself. <!-- fact_05 --> Santo Wines assigns an Event Manager to each wedding. <!-- fact_303 -->

**Getting ready.** Where does the wedding party get ready, and from what time? At Grace Hotel, a private lounge with a full-length mirror is available from 8am on the wedding day. <!-- fact_07 -->

**Music.** Can a DJ or live band play, and at what time must amplified music stop? Grace Hotel allows a DJ or live band at the reception. <!-- fact_07 -->

**Exclusive use.** Will the space, the venue or the whole property be closed to other guests, for how many days, and what does exclusive use cover?

**Ceremony time.** What times are available on your date, and how long do you have the space for set-up and pack-down?

**Suppliers.** May you bring your own photographer, florist and musicians, and what are the rules on access and loading?

**Weather.** What is the plan for a strong north wind, a rare shower or a heatwave, and which covered space is held for you? <!-- fact_207 --> <!-- fact_209 -->

**Deposits and cancellation.** How much is the deposit and when is it due, which payments are refundable, and what happens if you need to move or cancel the date?

Get the answers in the contract or a written confirmation, not a chat thread.

## What shapes the cost of a Santorini wedding

The cost of a Santorini wedding moves with decisions you make in the steps above.

**Season.** The May to October tourist season brings heat and crowds to the busy seaports. <!-- fact_211 --> Where your date falls in the season, and whether it is a weekday, changes what is available and on what terms.
