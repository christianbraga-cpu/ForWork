# Booking checklist: Santorini wedding

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue (or the authority named) for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy; the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences, which sit in the draft note, not here. Each venue's "Not published" lines in the body, the source pack's booking checklist assumptions and the house-rule questions in Step 7 are listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- Mystique, Oia: ceremony capacity, ceremony types and exclusive-use terms are not published. Confirm all three before naming it for any guest number or ceremony type.
- Santo Wines, Pyrgos: ceremony types, capacity (seated and standing) and exclusive-use terms are not published, and the Wine Tourism Center is open all year to visitors. Confirm whether the terraces are closed to tasting visitors on the day.
- Venetsanos Winery: exclusive-use terms are not published. The village (Megalochori) came from the brief's lead, not a page read, so the article says "Santorini"; confirm the address before naming the village.
- Grace Hotel, Canaves and Katikies: total room numbers are not published. Confirm how many rooms can be held as a block on the couple's dates.
- Grace Hotel: the flat venue fee covers exclusive use of the chosen event spaces, not the hotel. Confirm what a full buyout covers and costs (quoted on request).

## Legal ceremony (check with the authorities, not the venue alone)

- Civil marriage with the Municipality of Thira: no official municipal page was read. Confirm the steps, documents, notice period or lead times, ceremony locations for foreign couples and any fees with the municipality, and the documents and any fees with the Australian Embassy in Athens (notarial services by appointment, Monday to Friday, 9am to 12.30pm). Get the list in writing before the couple sets a date.
- Whether Greece needs a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI), whether an Australian-issued CNI is accepted or it must be issued in Greece, and whether it needs an authentication or apostille (ask the Greek embassy or consulate).
- Which documents the Greek authority needs legalised, apostille or authentication certificate, and whether any need a Greek translation and who may make it. No official page read says so; do not tell the couple a translation is required until confirmed.
- Name change: whether the Greek certificate works as proof of a name change in the couple's state (check with the state registry of births, deaths and marriages).
- Same-sex couples: no official page on same-sex civil marriage in Greece was read. Do not state the legal position; confirm with the municipality and the embassy.
- Symbolic ceremony: confirm with the venue which ceremony types it hosts, and whether the couple marries legally in Australia first.

## Deposits, cancellation and exclusive use (every venue)

- Deposit amount and due date, the payment schedule, which payments are refundable, and the terms for moving or cancelling the date
- Whether exclusive use covers the chosen event spaces, the venue or the whole property, whether the property can be closed to other guests, for how many days (wedding day only, or the days either side), and what exclusive use covers
- Contract language and currency, and how the deposit is held

## General (every venue)

- Ceremony types hosted (civil, religious, symbolic, vow renewal)
- Seated capacity for the ceremony and reception spaces on the couple's layout
- Rooms: how many rooms the guests can hold, room block or buyout, minimum stay
- Planner: whether an outside wedding planner is required and from how far ahead
- Getting ready: where the wedding party gets ready and from what time
- Music: whether a DJ or live band can play, the time amplified music must stop, and where an after-party can move
- Ceremony time: the times available on the date, with set-up and pack-down windows
- Suppliers: whether outside photographers, florists and musicians are allowed, and the rules for access and loading
- Weather: the covered or indoor plan for a strong north wind, a rare shower or a heatwave, and which covered space is held
- Access: a step-free route between guest rooms, the getting-ready space, the ceremony and the reception; the nearest point a car can reach and how guests with limited mobility get from there to the terrace (no official page on cliffside paths was read)
- Getting there: the drive from Santorini Airport (no distance or drive time was sourced), and how guests travel between the venue and their accommodation in the evening
- Ferries: crossing times and Athinios port details were not sourced; guests arriving by sea should reconfirm departures
