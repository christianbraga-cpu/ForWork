## Legal (Steps 1 and 6)
- The Municipality of Thira has no readable civil marriage page for foreign couples. The copy tells couples to check with the municipality and the Australian Embassy in Athens, including any fees.
- Australian steps (certificate of no impediment, notarial services at the Embassy in Athens, apostilles via Smartraveller's legalisation page, recognition in Australia) are from Smartraveller and the Embassy, read live and dated "as at November 2026".
- No official source states whether Greece requires an apostille or translation, or the position on same-sex civil marriage, so the copy states neither: it tells couples to ask the Greek authority which documents need an apostille.
- A skip line at the top of Step 6 sends couples who marry legally at home to Step 7.
- Nothing implies the Wedded Advisor handles legal, church or religious paperwork.
