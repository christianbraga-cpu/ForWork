Corfu wedding venues with a sea view sit in five parts of the island: the Kommeno peninsula, Corfu Town and Kanoni, Kalami in the north-east, the south-east coast around Benitses, and Ermones on the west coast. The right area depends on how many guests you are inviting, the kind of ceremony you want and the view you want behind you when you say your vows.

Every venue here hosts weddings and has the Ionian Sea in view. ## Key takeaways

- Nine of these ten venues sit on Corfu's east side, from Kalami in the north-east to the south-east coast, and one, Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo, overlooks Ermones Beach on the west.
- Guest count narrows the list quickly, because published ceremony spaces run from the couple and eight guests at Atlantica's Secret Garden to 250 at Angsana Corfu's Vertigo Sky Bar.

## How to compare Corfu venues like for like

Every venue on this list hosts weddings and looks out to the sea. Three questions then separate them.

### Resort, historic venue or private villa

Most Corfu venues here are resorts and hotels that host weddings in named spaces: a waterfront restaurant, a garden, a sky bar, a ballroom. That suits a guest list that wants to stay in one place and treat the wedding week as a holiday.

Two work the other way. Villa Medusa is a private Corfiot mansion that Grecotel offers for intimate celebrations, and The Courti Estate is a gated estate available for private hire for weddings. You trade a resort's facilities for privacy and a smaller, more personal guest list.

Corfu Holiday Palace and Corfu Palace Hotel sit in between: hotels in Corfu Town and Kanoni with ballrooms or banquet terms, handy for the days around the wedding in town.

### Ceremony setting and sea view

Read each venue's view as it describes it. Some face open water. Others put a landmark in the frame: Corfu Imperial names "the Old Town's Venetian silhouette", and Rodostamo frames its weddings against the Church of Ipapanti on Kommeno Bay.

Then check which ceremony each space hosts. Angsana Corfu lists its ceremony spaces as symbolic wedding venues. If you choose a symbolic venue, ask it where and when couples usually hold the legal ceremony. Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo's enquiry form lists legal, symbolic, renewal of vows and same-sex ceremonies. Most of the others say they host weddings without naming a ceremony type, so ask before you fall for a terrace.

If the legal marriage matters to you, the full steps are in the legal section below.

### Capacity, rooms and exclusive use

Published ceremony capacity runs from the couple and up to eight guests in Atlantica's Secret Garden to 250 at Angsana Corfu's Vertigo Sky Bar. For a large reception in town, Corfu Holiday Palace lists banquet seating for 300 and 350 in its two ballrooms. Many venues publish no figure, which is a reason to ask, not to rule them out.

Rooms matter more than usual when most guests fly long-haul. Angsana Corfu lists 196 accommodations and Eva Palace 225 rooms, bungalows and villas, while The Courti Estate has nine bedrooms.

Exclusive use needs a careful read. At The Courti Estate it means private hire of a gated estate. At Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo it means the resort hosts only one wedding on your chosen day, not that the resort closes to other guests. Ask every venue which of the two it is quoting.

Add three questions to every list: is there a step-free route between rooms, the ceremony and the reception for guests with limited mobility; can the venue organise transfers for guests who would rather not drive; and can the whole property be closed to other guests on your dates?

Stay rules can shape the whole trip. Atlantica asks couples marrying there to arrive at least three working days before the wedding and stay two working days after. Ask each venue on your shortlist the same question.

If you are weighing a resort package against a venue on its own terms, our guide to [Greece wedding packages](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-packages) sets out how they compare.

## Choosing an area: east or west coast

Most of this list sits on Corfu's east side, which looks across the water to the mainland. One venue is on the west. The difference is less about which coast is better and more about the setting you want and how your guests will move around.

**The east, from Kommeno to the south-east.** Four venues sit on or beside the Kommeno peninsula, with views back to the sea and, at Corfu Imperial, the Old Town. Rodostamo puts Kommeno Bay at 11 km from Corfu Airport and 10 km from Corfu Town. Corfu Palace Hotel looks over Garitsa Bay to the mainland. San Antonio sits on Kalami Bay in the north-east, and Angsana Corfu and The Courti Estate are south of town on the south-east coast.

**The west.** Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo overlooks Ermones Beach in western Corfu, with cliffside views of the Ionian. It puts itself about 25 minutes from Corfu airport and town.

A practical point on weather: the prevailing wind in Corfu is westerly in June, July and August and southerly in May, September and October. Ask any venue with an exposed ceremony spot what its plan is on a windy afternoon.

A simple rule: if your guests want to be near Corfu Town and the airport, look at Kommeno and Corfu Town. If you want a resort that runs the whole week, look south-east or west. If you want the place to yourselves, start with the two private houses.

## Getting to Corfu for your wedding

Guests fly into Corfu Airport (CFU), which you can reach by car, by a direct public bus service or by taxi. See the [airport's travel information](https://www.cfu-airport.gr/en).

Rodostamo, on Kommeno Bay, is 11 km from the airport and 10 km from Corfu Town, and Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo on the west coast is about 25 minutes from the airport and town. San Antonio, Angsana Corfu and The Courti Estate do not publish a distance from the airport, so ask each for its transfer time before guests book.

Two things help long-haul guests. First, give them the venue's own directions with the invitation, so nobody is hunting for a turn-off after a day of flying. Second, build a rest day in before the wedding. Atlantica's stay rule, at least three working days before a wedding, shows how early some venues expect you on the island.

Check [Smartraveller's advice for Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) before you book and again before you fly. As at November 2026, the overall advice level is "Exercise normal safety precautions", and it warns guests to take care swimming because of boat traffic, strong currents and marine life, and to swim at patrolled beaches where possible.

## When to marry in Corfu

Corfu's wedding season follows its weather, and the venues set their own seasons inside it. Averages from the [Hellenic National Meteorological Service](http://newportal.hnms.gr/emy/en/climatology/climatology_city?perifereia=Ionian%20Islands&poli=Kerkyra) for the Kerkyra station tell the story month by month.

| Month | Average high | Average rain days |
|---|---|---|
| May | 24.0°C | 7.7 |
| June | 28.2°C | 4.8 |
| July | 31.2°C | 3.3 |
| August | 31.5°C | 3.3 |
| September | 27.5°C | 7.4 |
| October | 23.2°C | 11.4 |

**Summer, July and August.** The fewest rain days, about three each, and the hottest, with average highs above 31°C. Evenings stay warm too: the average low is 18.9°C in July and 19.3°C in August.

**Early and late summer, June and September.** June averages a high of 28.2°C and under five rain days. September is a similar temperature, but rainfall climbs sharply, with an average of 87.8 mm against 21.7 mm in August.

**Shoulder season, May and October.** Highs of 24.0°C and 23.2°C, with more rain days, especially in October. Humidity also rises from summer into September and October.

Venue seasons add a second layer. At Angsana Corfu, Vertigo Sky Bar hosts ceremonies from mid-May to the end of September, while Oribu Garden runs from April to October. Ask every venue which of its spaces are open on your date.

**Heat and wildfire.** As at November 2026, Smartraveller says heatwaves are frequent in Greece over the summer months and can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke; it advises drinking water, wearing a hat and light clothing, and staying in shade or air-conditioning. Wildfires occur during the extended summer from June to September, can start with little warning and travel quickly, so follow local authorities' advice.

For a summer date, that means a shaded ceremony spot, a later start, water for guests and an indoor or covered fallback agreed with the venue in advance.

## Getting married legally in Corfu

Decide early whether the ceremony in Corfu will be your legal marriage or a symbolic one. Some venues say which they host: Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo lists legal and symbolic ceremonies, while Angsana Corfu lists its ceremony venues as symbolic. If the legal marriage matters to you, plan the paperwork before you choose the terrace.

**Proving you are free to marry.** As at November 2026, Smartraveller says some countries ask you to prove you are free to marry, most often with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) and registry certificates. The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only ones issued in their own country. Ask the destination's embassy or consulate:

- whether you need one
- where to get it
- whether it needs an authentication or apostille.

**In Athens.** The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) witnesses CNI applications, issues CNIs, witnesses notices of intended marriage and legalises documents by authentication or apostille. All notarial services need an appointment, though apostille and CNI documents can be sent by post. The Embassy gives no legal advice, and fees apply.

**Back home.** An overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. A foreign marriage certificate is usually accepted as proof of marriage but may not work as proof of a name change. Read [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which part of Corfu is best for a wedding?

It depends on your guest list and the setting you want. Kommeno and Corfu Town keep guests close to the Old Town and the airport, with Rodostamo 11 km from the airport. The south-east has Angsana Corfu's published spaces and seasons and a private estate. The west coast has cliffside views at Ermones.

### Are there beach wedding venues in Corfu?

Yes. Eva Palace offers "an intimate beach ceremony" on Kommeno Bay, and Angsana Corfu names Emerald Beach among its wedding venues. Ask each venue whether a ceremony on the sand is possible on your date.

### Can we book a Corfu villa for exclusive use for our wedding?

Yes. The Courti Estate is available for private hire of its gated estate, with nine bedrooms. Grecotel offers Villa Medusa, a six-bedroom private mansion on the Kommeno peninsula, for intimate celebrations. Neither publishes an event guest count, so ask before you plan the list.

### Which airport do guests fly into for a Corfu wedding?

Corfu Airport (CFU). Guests can reach it by car, direct public bus or taxi. Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo on the west coast is about 25 minutes away.

### When is the best time to get married in Corfu?

That depends on how you weigh heat against rain. July and August average about three rain days and highs above 31°C, while June averages 28.2°C with under five rain days. Wildfires occur from June to September. Check your venue's own season too: Angsana's largest ceremony space runs from mid-May to the end of September.

### Can Australians legally get married in Corfu?

Yes. Some venues, such as Atlantica Grand Mediterraneo, list legal ceremonies. As at November 2026, Smartraveller says you may need a Certificate of No Impediment, and a marriage valid in Greece that would also be valid under Australian law is in most cases recognised in Australia.
