# Booking checklist: Corfu wedding venues

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article's three `[[VERIFY]]` markers (Villa Medusa twice, the Corfu Holiday Palace fresco room) are publishing blockers and sit in the draft note, not here; the related booking questions are repeated below so they are asked of the venue too. Each venue entry's "Not published" line and the source pack's booking checklist lines are listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- Corfu Palace Hotel: the only published wedding terms are the 2023 wedding proposals. Ask for the current wedding offer and terms (reception times, outdoor music cut-off at 23:30, indoor party until 04:00, the 200-guest hall as wet-weather backup, the 100-person menu minimum, no fireworks) and confirm they still apply. Confirm the sea-view wording ("view to Garitsa Bay and the mainland") is current and applies to the reception spaces.
- Villa Medusa (Grecotel): confirm which Kommeno villa Grecotel offers as Villa Medusa for weddings, and that its listing states "Direct Sea View" with private steps to the beach.
- Corfu Holiday Palace: confirm that the Nafsika Hall with the fresco by Eirini Daniolou Neophytou is the Nausica Ballroom.
- Angsana Corfu: the 2026 brochure lists its ceremony venues as symbolic. Confirm whether a legal civil ceremony can be held on site, or where and when couples usually hold the legal ceremony.
- Airport transfer times: San Antonio Corfu Resort, Angsana Corfu and The Courti Estate do not publish a distance or time from Corfu Airport (CFU). Ask each for the transfer time before guests book.

## General

- Curfew times and outdoor or amplified music rules at each venue on the couple's dates
- Minimum nights, room blocks, check-in and check-out times, and set-up and pack-down windows at each venue
- Rain or wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space (the prevailing wind is westerly in June to August and southerly in May, September and October)
- Whether exclusive use covers the chosen event spaces, one wedding per day, or the whole property, and whether the whole property can be closed to other guests on the couple's dates
- Step-free access for older guests and anyone with limited mobility at every venue: the number of steps between guest rooms, the ceremony and the reception, whether there are level or step-free paths and lifts, walking distances across the grounds (see each venue below)
- Transfers: whether the venue can organise transfers for guests who would rather not drive, from Corfu Airport (CFU) and Corfu Town, and who meets guests and carries luggage
- Venue seasons: which ceremony and reception spaces are open on the couple's date
- Contract language and currency, and how the deposit is held
- Legal ceremony: whether each venue hosts a legal or a symbolic ceremony, and the documents the municipality needs (apostille, Greek translation), whether an Australian-issued CNI is accepted, notice period and fees
