## Cross-region summary
- Every SERP is led by a Google AI overview listing the same 6 to 8 styles: solitaire, halo and hidden halo, three-stone, toi et moi, vintage and art deco, pave/side stone, bezel or modern. Then stone shapes and metals.
- Ranking pages are almost all jewellers (shop pages or brand-led guides). Only Vogue (Dubai) and Cosmopolitan are editorial. Pricing appears only on shop pages and shopping units (AUD, SGD, USD); guides show none.
- Top concern (PAA): what the types are, which shape flatters, current trends, and budget (PAA '$30,000' in Sydney and NY; we do not answer with figures, link the budget post).
- Gaps we can own: (1) no page covers more than about 8 styles; (2) none judges every style on the same lenses; (3) no 'think twice' line per style; (4) no travel or destination wedding angle anywhere; (5) hand and finger advice is thin and often gendered ('for women', 'her'); (6) east-west, split shank, eternity, bypass and coloured gemstone centres are missing from the top guides; (7) none is a neutral, non-selling voice.
- Regional: AU uses 'trilogy' and 'jewellery'; Singapore shows a products carousel; Dubai's top result is Vogue; NY includes GIA 4Cs (trends post, 2017). Related search 'engagement ring styles for women' and 'chart' appear everywhere; 'quiz' in 4 of 5.

## Answer to the strategist's open question
- Cathedral and tension: not expected as standalone styles. No AI overview lists cathedral as a style; Singapore's mentions it only as one way to hold a solitaire, and Vanessa Nicole itself says cathedral is 'not a separate visual style'. Tension appears only inside 'modern and architectural' (NY) and as a solitaire option (Austen & Blake). Keep the brief's 15. Mention cathedral and tension in one line each (solitaire and bypass/contemporary) and link the sister settings post.
- Secondaries: add 'types of engagement rings' (880, KD 0), plus halo, three-stone, toi et moi, east-west and vintage engagement rings (all measured above). 'Engagement ring styles for your hand' was not measured; use the PAA wording 'most flattering engagement ring style for my hand' as an FAQ instead.
