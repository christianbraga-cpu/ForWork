# 4pm wedding day checklist

Use this alongside the wedding timeline for a 4pm ceremony. Fill in the day timeline once you have a venue and a date, then take the venue questions to your venue before you lock in the ceremony time. Every time below is yours to write in. Nothing here is a rule.

## 1. Find your sunset first

- [ ] Open [Geoscience Australia's sunrise, sunset and twilight calculator](https://geodesyapps.ga.gov.au/sunrise).
- [ ] Search for the venue itself (or enter its latitude and longitude), not the nearest city.
- [ ] Choose the time zone the venue uses on your wedding date.
- [ ] Look up the single day of your wedding.
- [ ] Write down three times: sunset, end of civil twilight and end of nautical twilight.
- [ ] For a venue outside Australia, cross-check with [the US Naval Observatory's one-day calculator](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/data/RS_OneDay).
- [ ] Ask the venue which way the ceremony site faces, and whether hills, trees or buildings to the west hide the sun before the listed time.
- [ ] Ask your photographer when the light actually leaves the spot you want for portraits.

## 3. Questions for the venue

### Curfew and noise rules
- [ ] What time must amplified music stop, and does it stop earlier outdoors?
- [ ] When must guests leave the grounds?
- [ ] Are there noise limits for the ceremony or drinks if they are outdoors?
- [ ] Does the welcome dinner the night before share the same curfew?

### Older and less mobile guests
- [ ] How far is the walk to the ceremony spot?
- [ ] Is there a route without steps between the ceremony, drinks and dinner spaces?
- [ ] Is that route lit after dark?
- [ ] Where can they sit in shade, or out of the cold, while photos are taken?
- [ ] Can seats near the front and near the exits be held for them?

### Livestream access and privacy
- [ ] Can the venue's connection carry a livestream from the ceremony spot?
- [ ] Is there a wired connection or a dedicated network, and who at the venue can help if it drops?
- [ ] How will access be kept private (a private link or a password shared only with invited family)?
- [ ] Are there any venue rules on filming other guests or staff?
- [ ] What time will the ceremony be for the family watching from home, in their time zone?

### Seating time for a large guest list
- [ ] How long does it take to seat our number of guests for the ceremony?
- [ ] How long does it take to seat them for the reception?
- [ ] Does that change the arrival time we print on the invitation?
- [ ] How long will guests need to travel from where they are staying?

### Fixed family or religious timings
- [ ] Which parts of the day are fixed by family or religious custom, and at what times?
- [ ] Does the venue allow any rituals we have planned (for example candles, music or a set prayer time)?
- [ ] Has the celebrant or officiant confirmed how long their part takes?
- [ ] Have we told the venue these times early, so the kitchen and staff plan around them?

### Other details the venue may not publish
- [ ] What time do local guests and vendors expect dinner to begin, and does the kitchen staff for a late dinner?
- [ ] What time can guests check in on the day?
- [ ] Is there lighting at the ceremony site if sunset falls before or during the ceremony?
- [ ] Where does the shade fall at the ceremony time we are considering?
- [ ] Is there a quiet fallback if a flight lands late?

Get the answers in writing before you lock in the ceremony time.
