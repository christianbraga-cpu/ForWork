# Booking checklist: Mykonos wedding venues

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy; the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences, which sit in the draft note, not here. Each venue entry's "Not published" lines, the source pack's booking checklist lines and the source pack's verify items for the 10 venues are listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- Mykonos Grand Hotel & Resort: the hotel's rule is that both partners must be Christian Orthodox for a religious wedding on the premises. For any couple where one or both partners are not Christian Orthodox, confirm which ceremony (civil or symbolic) the hotel can hold at the chapel or outdoors with the chapel as backdrop.
- Katikies Mykonos: children above 13 are welcome and younger children are directed to Katikies Villas Mykonos at Elia beach. Confirm how this applies to younger wedding guests, and the terms for a whole-hotel take-over (listed among Katikies Hotels' event enquiry types).
- Mykonos Blu, Grecotel: confirm the 2027 season reopening date with Grecotel, and which ceremony types (civil, religious or symbolic) are held on the Ocean Balcony.
- Petasos Beach Resort & Spa: confirm which space the resort uses for ceremonies and which ceremony types it holds.
- Myconian Ambassador: confirm where the small chapel is (on site or nearby), how many guests it holds, and the ceremony types.
- Grecotel Mykonos Lolita: confirm chapel and reception capacity, and how a civil ceremony at the chapel is registered for an Australian couple (who officiates, and the municipality's documents and notice).
- Royal Myconian and Myconian Imperial (Myconian Collection): confirm which Elia hotel's chapel and which hotel's Horizons Ballroom would be used for the couple's wedding.
- Saint John Hotel Villas & Spa: confirm the room count and which spaces (Theatraki, 1001 Nights, Ilios, Thalatta) reach the published 800 guests.
- ZeNIT Events Center: confirm seated and standing capacity for the couple's layout, and whether the central indoor hall holds the full guest list as the rain or wind plan.
- Santa Marina: request the 2026 wedding brochure and event menus (the brochure has not been read), and confirm capacity, room count, ceremony types and exclusive use from it.

## General

- Curfew times and outdoor or amplified music rules at each venue on the couple's dates
- Minimum nights, room blocks, check-in and check-out times, and set-up and pack-down windows at each venue
- Rain or wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space (chapels, terraces, balconies, beach receptions)
- Whether exclusive use covers the chosen event spaces, one wedding per day, or the whole property, and whether the whole property can be closed to other guests on the couple's dates
- Step-free access for older guests and anyone with limited mobility at every venue: the number of steps between guest rooms, the ceremony and the reception, whether there are level or step-free paths, and access to hilltop chapels and beach spaces
- Transfers: whether the venue can organise transfers for guests from Mykonos Airport (JMK), the ports and Mykonos Town, and who meets guests and carries luggage
- Contract language and currency, and how the deposit is held
- Legal ceremony: whether each venue hosts a legal or a symbolic ceremony, and the documents the municipality needs (apostille, Greek translation), whether an Australian-issued Certificate of No Impediment is accepted, notice period and fees
