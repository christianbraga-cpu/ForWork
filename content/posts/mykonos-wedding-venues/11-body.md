Mykonos wedding venues fall into five parts of the island: the west-coast bays of Agios Ioannis and Ornos, near Mykonos Town; the south-coast beaches at Psarou and Platis Gialos; Elia further east; the north coast at Agios Sostis; and a hilltop above Paradise beach. The right one depends on how many guests you are inviting, the ceremony you want and how your guests will move around the island.

Every venue here hosts weddings, and each is described on the same terms so you can compare them fairly. For the wider picture, read [our guide to Greece wedding venues](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues), or browse [our curated Greece wedding collection](https://weddedwonderland.com/destinations/europe/greece).

## Key takeaways

- Start on the west coast if guests want to be near Mykonos Town, look south for beach receptions, and look north for privacy and a chapel.
- Guest count narrows the list quickly, because published capacity ranges from a chapel for 25 guests at Mykonos Grand to weddings of up to 800 at Saint John.
- Several venues have a chapel of their own, but the rules differ, from religious, civil and symbolic weddings at Grecotel Mykonos Lolita to religious weddings at Mykonos Grand only when both partners are Christian Orthodox.
- Greek summers bring frequent heatwaves and a wildfire season from June to September, so plan shade, a later ceremony and a covered fallback for a summer date.

## How to compare Mykonos venues like for like

Every venue on this list hosts weddings. Three questions then separate them: what kind of place it is, where the ceremony happens, and how many people it holds and houses.

### Resort, boutique hotel or event venue

Most venues here are resorts and hotels that host weddings in named spaces: a chapel, a balcony above the sand, a ballroom, a pool deck. That suits a guest list that wants to stay together and treat the wedding as a week away.

The scale varies widely. Mykonos Blu lists 103 guest rooms on the Psarou waterfront, while Grecotel Mykonos Lolita calls itself "a little hotel on a secluded private beach after Agios Sostis". Katikies Mykonos welcomes children above 13, which matters if your guest list includes young families.

One venue works differently. ZeNIT Events Center is a hilltop event venue with no rooms, so guests stay elsewhere and come together for the day. You trade a resort's convenience for a space built only for events.

If a venue offers its own wedding package, our guide to [Greece wedding packages](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-packages) sets out how to compare it with booking the venue on its own terms.

### Chapel, terrace or beach ceremony

Several venues pair a chapel ceremony with a beach or sea-view reception at the same property; [Where the chapels are](#where-the-chapels-are) sets out all six.

The ceremony type is the detail to check first. Grecotel Mykonos Lolita offers religious, civil and symbolic weddings at its chapel. The Myconian Collection assists in officiating ceremonies across all denominations. At Mykonos Grand, a religious wedding on the hotel premises requires both partners to be Christian Orthodox.

Others offer a terrace rather than a chapel. Mykonos Blu holds its ceremonies on the Ocean Balcony above Psarou beach, Katikies Mykonos uses its Upper deck, and ZeNIT uses its pool terrace, two terraces and central hall. Where a venue does not name a ceremony type, ask before you fall for the view.

### Capacity, rooms and exclusive use

Published ceremony capacity runs from 25 guests inside the Mykonos Grand chapel to 60 on the Mykonos Blu Ocean Balcony and 100 outdoors at Mykonos Grand. For a larger reception, Petasos lists banquet seating for 280 at its pool, the Myconian Collection's Horizons Ballroom seats 270 at round tables, and Saint John organises weddings of up to 800 guests. For a guest list in the hundreds, the published options are Saint John (up to 800), Petasos (up to 500 for cocktails at its pool) and the Horizons Ballroom in Elia (up to 550 for cocktails).

Rooms matter when most guests fly long-haul. Mykonos Blu is the only venue here that publishes a room count. The others list rooms, suites and villas without a total, so ask how many your guests can hold on your dates.

Few venues on this list publish exclusive use. Katikies Hotels lists a hotel take-over among the event types it takes enquiries for, across its Santorini and Mykonos hotels. For every other venue here, ask whether the wedding spaces, or the whole property, can be closed to other guests.

Add four questions to every list: is there a step-free route between rooms, ceremony and reception; can the venue organise transfers for guests who would rather not drive; what is the plan on a windy afternoon; and what time must the music stop?

Anything this guide marks "Not published", from curfew to minimum nights, needs an answer in writing before you commit, so [talk to a Wedded Advisor about your shortlist](https://weddedwonderland.com/wonderland-concierge?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=mykonos-wedding-venues&utm_content=secondary).

## Where the chapels are

Six venues on this list have a chapel in the wedding plan. Here is where each sits and what it holds, where the venue says.

**Agios Ioannis and Ornos.** Santa Marina has a traditional whitewashed stone chapel on its peninsula at the southern tip of Ornos Bay. Mykonos Grand's chapel, dedicated to the Virgin Mary, is perched on a hill looking out over Agios Ioannis beach; it holds up to 25 guests inside and frames outdoor ceremonies for up to 100. Saint John offers the Agia Fotini Chapel as one of three ceremony settings.

**Platis Gialos.** The Myconian Ambassador pairs a small chapel with a reception on the beach or its entertainment deck. The hotel does not say where the chapel sits, so ask whether it is on site or a short walk away.

**Elia.** The Myconian Collection offers vows in a small whitewashed chapel, followed by a reception on the beach or in a restaurant overlooking the Aegean and Mykonos Town.

**Agios Sostis.** Grecotel Mykonos Lolita has a private chapel above the Aegean and holds religious, civil and symbolic weddings there.

No chapel publishes a guest count except Mykonos Grand's. If your guest list is larger than a chapel holds, keep the ceremony small and gather everyone for the reception that follows.

## Choosing an area of the island

Mykonos is "the island of the winds", in the heart of the Cyclades, and the five areas on this list each answer a different question about your wedding. See [Visit Greece](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/islands/cyclades/mykonos) for the island overview.

**The west coast: Agios Ioannis and Ornos.** These bays sit near Mykonos Town. Santa Marina describes itself as "just minutes from Mykonos Town", and Katikies Mykonos puts itself about 4 km away. Saint John calls Agios Ioannis one of Mykonos's sunset spots, and Mykonos Grand looks across to the island of Delos. Choose this coast if guests want evenings in town between wedding events.

**The south coast: Psarou and Platis Gialos.** Two neighbouring beaches with three venues between them. The Myconian Ambassador sits above Platis Gialos, 5 km from Mykonos Town, with Psarou within walking distance. Petasos sits on a private peninsula between the two. Choose this coast for beach receptions, with Psarou within walking distance of Platis Gialos.

**Elia.** Further east, the Myconian Collection's Elia hotels look over a "long stretch of golden sand" beneath "rugged Cycladic hills". Choose Elia for the largest published ballroom on this list.

**The north coast: Agios Sostis.** Grecotel Mykonos Lolita sits on a secluded private beach, off the beaten path, near Agios Sostis and Panormos beaches. Choose the north for a small wedding on a secluded beach.

**Above Paradise beach.** ZeNIT stands on a hilltop above Paradise beach, with views over the Aegean to Delos. Choose it when you want an event venue for the whole celebration and are happy for guests to stay elsewhere.

A simple rule: if guests want to be near town, start on the west coast. If you want the beach in every photograph, look south. If you want privacy and a chapel, look north.

[Santa Marina, a Luxury Collection Resort](https://www.santa-marina.gr/weddings-celebrations/) is a beachfront retreat on a private peninsula at the southern tip of Ornos Bay, and is marking 40 years. Its team of wedding planners works on everything from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, including elopement parties.

- **Setting:** Rooms, suites and villas with Aegean views, infinity pools and a private sandy beach, "just minutes from Mykonos Town".
- **Ceremony:** A traditional whitewashed stone chapel, and venues with panoramic views of the Aegean Sea. Ceremony types are not published.
- **Capacity:** Not published.
- **Rooms:** Rooms, suites and villas. No total room count is published.
- **Exclusive use:** Not published.
- **Standout:** A 2026 wedding brochure and event menus are available to download, so you can read the options before you enquire. The resort also has the Elais Restaurant, private dining and the Ginkgo Spa on site.

A chapel, a private beach and a planning team on one peninsula make Santa Marina a natural fit for a guest list that wants to stay in one place.

### 2. Katikies Mykonos, Agios Ioannis

[Katikies Mykonos](https://www.katikies.com/events/) is a five-star hotel above Agios Ioannis beach. The Katikies group offers micro weddings in Mykonos, small ceremonies "featuring classic Cycladic architecture".

- **Setting:** About 4 km from Mykonos Town, roughly a 10-minute drive. Every room and suite looks to Agios Ioannis beach, and most have sea views to Delos.
- **Ceremony:** The Upper deck is the space marked for wedding ceremonies, as well as private dinners and cocktail receptions. Ceremony types are not published.
- **Capacity:** The Upper deck seats 50 and holds 100 standing. Two dining spaces each seat 50, and the Private Balcony seats 20.
- **Rooms:** Rooms and suites; no total room count is published. Children above 13 are welcome, and younger children are directed to the sister property, Katikies Villas Mykonos at Elia beach.
- **Exclusive use:** Katikies Hotels lists a hotel take-over among its event enquiry types. Ask whether it applies to Katikies Mykonos on your dates.
- **Standout:** Four named event spaces at one small hotel, so the ceremony, dinner and a private balcony gathering can each have their own setting.

The published figures top out at 50 seated, so Katikies Mykonos suits a micro wedding or a small celebration.

### 3. Mykonos Grand Hotel & Resort, Agios Ioannis

[Mykonos Grand](https://www.mykonosgrand.gr/romance/weddings/) is a five-star beachfront hotel on Agios Ioannis beach, opposite the island of Delos, with "Cycladic charm" and a private beach section for hotel guests.

- **Setting:** On Agios Ioannis beach, facing Delos.
- **Ceremony:** A whitewashed chapel dedicated to the Virgin Mary, "perched on a hill looking out over Ayios Yiannis Beach". The hotel's rule is that both bride and groom must be Christian Orthodox for a religious wedding on the hotel premises.
- **Capacity:** Up to 25 guests inside the chapel; outdoor ceremonies with the chapel as backdrop for up to 100.
- **Rooms:** Rooms, suites with private pools and the Mykonos Grand Villa. No total room count is published.
- **Exclusive use:** Not published.
- **Standout:** An open-air amphitheatre, and an evening shuttle to Mykonos Town for guests who want a night in town. The hotel liaises with your wedding planner and arranges music, video and photography, beauty and flowers.
- **Not published:** reception capacity.

If one or both of you is not Christian Orthodox, ask the hotel which ceremony it can hold at the chapel for you before you plan around it.

### 4. Saint John Hotel Villas & Spa, Agios Ioannis

[Saint John](https://www.saintjohn.gr/mykonos_weddings/) offers a range of wedding venues and event spaces in Agios Ioannis, which it counts among Mykonos's sunset spots.

- **Setting:** Every venue has full sea views and panoramic views of the Aegean.
- **Ceremony:** Vows at the Agia Fotini Chapel, the sunset balcony or the deck. Ceremony types are not published.
- **Receptions:** Theatraki, 1001 Nights, Ilios or Thalatta, with Ilios and Thalatta suited to indoor celebrations.
- **Capacity:** From intimate gatherings to weddings of up to 800 guests, with welcome cocktails through to five-course gala dinners.
- **Rooms:** Not published.
- **Exclusive use:** Not published.
- **Standout:** The scale. Up to 800 guests is the largest published figure on this list. The hotel works with wedding planners for flowers, candles, DJs, photography, video and fireworks, at additional charge.

With two indoor reception spaces, Saint John also has a ready answer for an evening when the wind picks up.

## Getting to Mykonos for your wedding

Guests fly into Mykonos Airport (JMK). Flights from Athens International Airport take about 45 minutes, and from Thessaloniki about 1 hour 10 minutes.

From the airport, a bus runs to Mykonos Town (Chora) and other destinations from a stop in front of the terminal in the Arrivals area, with tickets bought from the driver. A taxi stand sits outside the Arrivals terminal. See the [airport's travel information](https://www.jmk-airport.gr/en).

Distances to town vary by area. Katikies Mykonos is about 4 km from Mykonos Town and the Myconian Ambassador 5 km. Most venues do not publish a distance from the airport, so ask each for its transfer time before guests book, and how long the trip takes for older guests.

## When to marry in Mykonos

The wedding date shapes everything from the dress fabric to the ceremony time. Ask every venue which of its spaces are open on your date before you settle on one.

**Heat.** As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) says heatwaves are frequent in Greece over the summer months and can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke, and that many major tourist sites have little shade. For a summer date, that means a shaded ceremony spot, a later start and water for every guest.

**Wildfire.** Wildfires occur in Greece during the extended summer, from June to September, including near tourist areas, and can start with little warning and travel quickly. Agree a covered or indoor fallback with your venue in advance; Saint John, the Myconian Collection and ZeNIT all publish indoor or covered spaces.

**Swimming.** Smartraveller also advises care when swimming because of boat traffic, coastal hazards, strong currents and marine life, and suggests patrolled beaches where possible.

**Wind.** On "the island of the winds", an exposed ceremony spot needs a plan for a breezy afternoon. Ask how each venue handles it.

A date outside the summer months sits outside the heatwave and wildfire season. Confirm that your chosen venue and its ceremony spaces are open on that date.

## Getting married legally in Mykonos

Decide early whether the ceremony in Mykonos will be your legal marriage or a symbolic one. Some venues say which they host: Grecotel Mykonos Lolita lists religious, civil and symbolic weddings at its chapel. If the legal marriage matters to you, plan the paperwork before you choose the terrace.

**Proving you are free to marry.** As at November 2026, Smartraveller says some countries ask you to prove you are free to marry, most often with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) and registry certificates. The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only ones issued in their own country. Ask the destination's embassy or consulate:

- whether you need one
- whether you can get it in Australia
- whether it needs an authentication or apostille.

**In Athens.** The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) witnesses CNI applications, issues CNIs, witnesses notices of intended marriage and legalises documents by authentication or apostille. All notarial services need an appointment, though apostille and CNI documents can be sent by post, and legalised documents and CNIs are ready within two working days. The Embassy gives no legal advice, and fees apply.

**Back home.** An overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. A foreign marriage certificate is usually accepted as proof of marriage but may not work as proof of a name change. Read [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which part of Mykonos suits a wedding?

It depends on your guest list and the setting you want. The west coast at Agios Ioannis and Ornos keeps guests close to Mykonos Town, with Katikies Mykonos about 4 km away. The south coast at Psarou and Platis Gialos puts the beach at the centre, Elia has the largest published ballroom, and Agios Sostis on the north coast is the secluded choice.

### Are there wedding chapels at Mykonos venues?

Yes. Santa Marina, Mykonos Grand, Saint John, the Myconian Ambassador, the Myconian Collection's Elia hotels and Grecotel Mykonos Lolita all describe a chapel. Mykonos Grand's holds 25 guests inside, and a religious wedding there requires both partners to be Christian Orthodox.

### Can we book a Mykonos venue for exclusive use?

Ask directly, because few venues publish it. Katikies Hotels lists a hotel take-over among its event enquiry types. For the others, exclusive use is not published, so confirm with each venue whether the wedding spaces or the whole property can be reserved for you.

### Can we have a beach wedding in Mykonos?

Several venues hold the ceremony or reception right by the sand. Mykonos Blu's Ocean Balcony sits just above Psarou beach, and the Myconian Collection and Myconian Ambassador both offer receptions on the beach. Ask each venue whether a ceremony on the sand itself is possible on your date.

### How do guests get to Mykonos?

They fly into Mykonos Airport (JMK), about 45 minutes from Athens and 1 hour 10 minutes from Thessaloniki. From the airport, a bus runs to Mykonos Town and a taxi stand sits outside Arrivals.

### Can Australians legally get married in Mykonos?

Plan the legal steps first. As at November 2026, Smartraveller says you may need a Certificate of No Impediment, and a marriage valid in Greece that would also be valid under Australian law is in most cases recognised in Australia.
