# Booking checklist: All-inclusive Greece wedding packages

These details are confirmed in writing with each venue, for the couple's dates, before anyone commits. They don't block publishing: the article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers and prints no prices. The only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (6 slots).

## Confirm before quoting

- **Published prices not used.** Several venues publish prices or money terms (Santorini Gem rental fees in euros and its venue policy, Canaves deposit terms, the Abaton and Elounda SA brochures). None are in the article. Any figure added later needs Nic's sign-off and is shown "From AUD".
- **Currency of every quote.** Confirm the quote currency, whether deposit and balance are invoiced in the same currency, card or bank fees, who carries exchange-rate movement, and how the Wedded Advisor's AUD quote converts.
- **"All inclusive" wording.** Where a resort sells an all-inclusive stay, confirm in writing which wedding items sit inside that stay and which are charged on top.

## Ask every venue, in writing

- Inclusions: the full list, item by item (date, ceremony set-up, coordinator, flowers, cake, toast, music, room touches, documents help)
- What is charged on top or quoted separately: reception food and drink, open bar, venue or space fees, extra hours, earlier starts, extra guests, décor, officiant, outside suppliers, service and tips
- Minimum rooms or nights: how many, for how long, at which hotel, and whether guests pay the venue directly
- Required planner: whether an outside wedding planner is required, and from when
- Deposit: amount, when due, what it secures, and whether it is refundable
- Cancellation and postponement terms, including heat, wildfire or other alerts that move the date
- Payment timings: balance and food and beverage due dates
- Music limits: decibel limit, when music drops and stops, bar closing time, DJ or band allowed
- Exclusive use: one space, the wedding spaces or the whole property, from what time, for how many hours and how many days
- Legal status of any certificate the venue issues (civil, religious or symbolic)
- Contract language, and quote and payment currency
- Level access: a route without steps from arrival to ceremony, dinner, rooms and an accessible bathroom
- Insurance: the venue's public liability cover, and what the couple's travel or event insurance must cover (including deposits)
- Weather plan: covered or indoor alternative, held for you or offered on the day

## Elounda SA Hotels & Resorts, Elounda, Crete

- Inclusions of the Lavish (civil, Orthodox) and Symbolic / Renewal of Vows packages; tasting for up to two without drinks; spa use

- Minimum: couple stays at least three nights in one of the group's hotels
- Documents help and licence settlement with the Municipality Office: what is done, what the couple supplies
- Symbolic ceremony: confirmed not legally recognised in Greece

## Canaves Collection, Oia, Santorini

- Package inclusions per property, space and guest band; elopement package

- Deposit: non-refundable; balance and food and drink one month before; venue payments non-refundable

## Santorini Gem, Santorini

- Inclusions per package (morning terrace hire; three evening packages: venue only, villa day use, villa overnight)
- On top: gazebo, earlier start by the hour, per-guest fee beyond the base group, set menu or buffet, open bar for all adults if chosen, wedding cake outside the set menu

- Menu minimums: set menu for 20 to 80 adults, buffet above 30

- Music up to 80 dB, lowered at 11pm, off 15 minutes before the end time; set-up from 1pm, no overnight storage
- Accessible facilities: confirm the route without steps

## Civil marriage: municipality and Australian Embassy in Athens

- With the municipality: the steps in order, documents required, official translations, two witnesses of legal age, lead times, and current Municipal fee (and Metropolis fee for an Orthodox wedding)
- With the Australian Embassy in Athens: Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage and apostilles, by appointment; current fees and processing times
- Confirm the marriage is valid in Greece and would be valid under Australian law; it cannot be registered in Australia
- Check Smartraveller's advice for Greece at booking time
