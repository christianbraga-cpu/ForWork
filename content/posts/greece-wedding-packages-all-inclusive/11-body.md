Search for Greece wedding packages all inclusive and you will meet a dozen resorts using the same words for very different things. A Greek venue package usually bundles the ceremony set-up, a coordinator and a set list of touches for the day. Food and drink for the reception, exclusive use, guest rooms, extra hours and outside suppliers are where packages part ways, and most venues quote them separately.

This guide puts ten Greek venue packages side by side by what each one includes. For the wider picture, read [what Greece wedding packages include](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-packages) and our guide to [all inclusive destination wedding packages](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-wedding-packages).

## Key takeaways

- A Greek venue wedding package usually covers the ceremony set-up, a coordinator and a short list of touches, with the reception menu and drinks quoted separately.
- Where a Greek resort calls itself all inclusive, the phrase almost always describes the holiday stay rather than the wedding package.
- Guest room minimums, exclusive use, music finish times and outside suppliers are where Greek packages differ most, so ask for each in writing.
- Compare two quotes only on the same guest count, spaces, hours, nights and currency, with every inclusion listed.

## What all inclusive means at a Greek wedding venue

None of the ten venues in this comparison sells its wedding package as all inclusive. The phrase belongs to the holiday, not the wedding.

Ikos Resorts describes its Greek resorts, Ikos Aria on Kos among them, as stays where everything is included, yet its wedding package is a list of named items. At Grecotel Amirandes near Heraklion, all inclusive appears only as a hotel meal plan option, and the events page names no wedding package. Atrium Prestige in south Rhodes sells its premium all inclusive offer as a holiday, not a wedding package.

So the honest reading of "all inclusive" in Greece is this: your stay may be inclusive, and your wedding package covers what its list names. The reception, the bar and the extras usually sit beside it.

### What venue packages usually cover

The common core is built around the ceremony and the couple, rather than the guests.

- **The date and the ceremony set-up.** Elounda SA, Ikos Aria and Andronis each list the reservation or booking of the ceremony date and time. Set-ups range from a table with a seasonal flower display at Elounda SA to lanterns, petals and covered chairs at Euphoria Resort.
- **A coordinator or planner.** Nana Princess has an event coordinator present throughout the stay on its Deluxe Dream package, Ikos Aria lists wedding planning and coordination, and Canaves includes event planning services in its packages.
- **Flowers for the couple.** A bridal bouquet and boutonniere appear at Elounda SA, Euphoria Resort and Andronis.
- **A cake and a toast.** Ikos Aria includes a cake to serve 10 people, Canaves a two-tier cake and a glass of sparkling wine for the toast, and Andronis a two-tier cake with a bottle of sparkling wine.
- **Touches for the couple's room.** Rose petals, flower decoration or a turndown appear at Ikos Aria, Elounda SA and Andronis.

### What they rarely cover

The reception is the largest piece that tends to sit outside. Elounda SA sets out reception food, beverage packages, open bars, dinner menus and entertainment as separate sections from its ceremony packages. Abaton books food and drink separately from its ceremony basics, and Euphoria Resort quotes receptions, beverage packages, open bars, custom cakes and entertainment separately on request.

Guest rooms are the second. Abaton's packages do not include accommodation, and several venues set a room or night minimum before a package applies. Some venues also require you to bring a wedding planner, which Santorini Gem makes a condition for every couple.

Fees set by others sit outside too. Elounda SA states that the Municipal fee for a civil wedding and the Metropolis fee for an Orthodox wedding are not in the package rate and are charged on top.

**Elounda SA Hotels & Resorts, Elounda.** The group's [wedding packages brochure](https://www.eloundapeninsula.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Elounda-Wedding-Packages-.pdf) covers civil, Greek Orthodox and symbolic ceremonies, with the reception set out separately from the ceremony packages. The civil and Orthodox package is the Lavish Wedding Package, and the symbolic option is the Symbolic Ceremony / Renewal of Vows package.

Before the day, the Lavish package includes the reservation of your date and time, your choice of ceremony venue, spa use and a tasting of your wedding menu for up to two people, without drinks. It also includes documentation help and settlement of the wedding licence, worked through with you and the local Municipality Office.

On the day, it adds a ceremony table with seasonal flowers, bouquet and boutonniere, and flowers and petals in your room afterwards.

Two terms shape the whole picture. Every package applies only when the couple stays at least three nights in one of the group's hotels. The Municipal fee for a civil wedding and the Metropolis fee for an Orthodox one are charged on top of the package rate and can change. The reception itself, from food and drink to open bars and entertainment, sits in separate sections of the brochure.

**Nana Princess, Chersonissos.** [Nana Princess](https://www.nanaprincess.gr/pages/weddings) offers civil weddings on its private sandy beach, Greek Orthodox weddings in the Chapel of St George within the grounds, symbolic weddings led by one of its event coordinators, and vow renewals. It presents three packages, Deluxe Dream, Ultimate Passion and Essential Elegance, and will design a bespoke package to your brief.

**Abaton Island Resort & Spa.** [Abaton](https://www.abaton.gr/meet/weddings) packages the ceremony and books the reception separately, and invites couples to choose a package or create their own. **Euphoria Resort, Kolymvari.** [Euphoria Resort](https://www.euphoriaresort.gr/weddings/) offers three options, Elxi, Pathos and Eros, for an informal wedding or vow renewal, with a certificate of informal marriage from the hotel. ## How to compare two all-inclusive quotes like for like

Two quotes compare only when they describe the same wedding. Set the guest count, the spaces, the hours and the nights first, then put each venue's answers to the same questions in one place.

### Inclusions in writing

Ask for every inclusion as a written line, not a summary. Packages that look alike in a headline often split at the detail: one includes a cake for 10, another a two-tier cake, a third leaves the cake outside the set menu.

Ask too whether items can be swapped. Abaton and Euphoria Resort both state that their packages are complete and items are not interchangeable.

### Per guest or fixed

Ask which parts of each quote are fixed and which move with your guest list. Abaton prices its menus, beverage packages and open bars per person. Santorini Gem charges a fee for each guest beyond its base group.

Questions to ask each venue:

- Is the package price fixed, or per guest?
- At what guest number does the package change, and what changes with it?
- Are drinks priced by the hour, by the bottle or by package, and must every adult be included?

That last question matters. At Santorini Gem, an open bar package is optional, but once chosen it applies to all adults.

### Deposits and cancellation

Ask how much is non-refundable, when each payment falls due and what happens if you postpone. Canaves takes a non-refundable deposit to secure the venue, with the balance and food and drink cost due one month before, and venue payments are always non-refundable. Santorini Gem takes a non-refundable deposit, with the rental balance due nine months before the wedding, food and drink paid 40 days before, and postponement only to the following season.

### Exclusive use and how many days

Exclusive use can mean one space for a few hours or a whole property for several nights. Canaves includes private use of its ceremony and reception areas for up to 5 hours, while its buyouts run to a whole hotel.

Ask how many hours the space is yours, when set-up can begin and whether the rest of the resort stays open to other guests. At Santorini Gem, set-up starts from 1pm on the day and nothing can be stored overnight.

### Music finish times

Music limits are written into many Greek packages. Canaves allows music up to 60 dB until 11.30pm on its Oia Suites package, and up to 70 dB until 1am where the Elements Restaurant is added at Canaves Epitome. At Santorini Gem, music runs up to 80 dB, is lowered to listening level at 11pm and stops 15 minutes before the end time.

Ask what time the music stops, what volume is allowed and whether a DJ or band can play in the space you have chosen.

### Weather plan

Heat shapes the timing as much as rain. Nana Princess recommends evening ceremonies because high-summer temperatures can reach 35 degrees C, setting ceremonies between 6.30pm and 8pm in July and August.

Ask where the ceremony and dinner move if the weather turns, and whether that covered space is held for you or shared. Nana Princess, for one, lists its Conference Hall for a fully covered reception.

### Level access

Clifftop and terraced venues can mean steps between ceremony, dinner and rooms. Santorini Gem lists accessible facilities in both its ceremony hire and its evening packages. Ask every venue for a route without steps from arrival to ceremony, dinner and bathrooms, especially if older guests are travelling.

### Quote currency

Santorini Gem, for one, publishes its rental fees in euros. Ask which currency and language the contract is written in, which currency each payment is taken in, whether card or bank fees apply, and who carries any change in the exchange rate between the deposit and the balance. Put both quotes in the same currency before you compare them. ## When all inclusive suits you and when to build your own

A venue package suits couples who want one set of terms, one coordinator and a clear list of what the day includes. If your ceremony and reception sit in one resort, your guests stay there, and you like the look of the venue's own set-up, a package keeps the decisions few. It works especially well for smaller weddings and elopements. Canaves sells a package for the couple alone, and Euphoria Resort's options are scaled for up to 10, 20 or 30 guests.

Building your own suits couples who want full control of suppliers, a look that is theirs rather than the venue's, or a celebration spread across several places. Some venues are built for this. The Myconian Collection will prepare the stage for your own team, and Four Seasons Astir Palace works with your own florist.

A few honest trade-offs to weigh:

- **Simplicity versus flexibility.** A complete package can mean no swaps and no refund for items you do not use.
- **Rooms.** A package can tie you to a room or night minimum, which suits a guest list that wants to stay together and pinches one that does not.
- **The planner question.** Some venues require a wedding planner on top of the venue, so a package does not always remove that cost.

## The legal side in brief

The venues here host three kinds of ceremony. Elounda SA has a civil and Greek Orthodox package and a separate symbolic one, and Nana Princess offers civil weddings on its beach and Orthodox weddings in its chapel. Elounda SA's brochure states that symbolic weddings and vow renewals are not legally recognised in Greece.

A civil ceremony needs officially translated legal documents and two witnesses of legal age, and the Greek government sets the requirements. As at November 2026, Smartraveller notes that some countries ask for proof that nothing prevents you from marrying, most often with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage, and the Australian Embassy in Athens issues these and apostilles by appointment. A marriage valid in Greece is in most cases legally recognised in Australia if it would also have been valid under Australian law, though it cannot be registered here. Municipal and church fees can sit outside a venue package, so confirm them with the municipality before you book.

Read [Smartraveller's advice for Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) and contact the [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/) for current requirements. For the steps in order, read our guide to [the legal steps for marrying in Greece](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues#legal).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is included in an all-inclusive wedding package in Greece?

It depends on the venue. Most packages cover the date and ceremony set-up, a coordinator, flowers for the couple, a cake and a toast. The reception menu and drinks are usually quoted separately.

### Are there all-inclusive wedding resorts in Greece?

Yes, as holidays. Ikos Aria on Kos belongs to a resort brand where everything is included in the stay, and Grecotel Amirandes on Crete offers an all inclusive meal plan. The wedding package at each is a separate, named list.

### Do all-inclusive Greece wedding packages have a minimum guest number?

Some do. Abaton requires a reception of at least 20 guests, and Santorini Gem's set menus start at 20 adults. Canaves sells a package for the couple alone.

### Do all-inclusive packages in Greece include flights or guest rooms?

Not flights. Guest rooms are more often a requirement than an inclusion: Elounda SA asks the couple to stay three nights, and Abaton asks for at least 10 rooms for at least 2 nights. Four Seasons Astir Palace includes the couple's wedding night.

### Are there all-inclusive elopement packages in Greece?

There are elopement packages, though none are sold as all inclusive. Canaves has a Panorama Balcony elopement for the couple alone, with private use for up to 5 hours and a two-tier cake. Euphoria Resort's Elxi option scales from the couple up to 10 guests.

### Is the legal ceremony part of an all-inclusive package?

Sometimes help with the documents is, as each venue entry above sets out, while fees set by the municipality or church can sit outside. Check the requirements with the municipality and the Australian Embassy in Athens.
