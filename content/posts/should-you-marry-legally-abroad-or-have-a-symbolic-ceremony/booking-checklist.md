# Booking checklist: legal wedding, then a symbolic ceremony

Use this once you have settled on marrying legally in Australia and celebrating abroad with a symbolic ceremony. Tick each item as it is settled, and ask for the venue's and celebrant's answers in writing.

**General information as at November 2026, not legal advice.** Each legal step links to the official source. Check the source again before you rely on it, and talk your dates through with your authorised marriage celebrant.

## The legal wedding in Australia

**Route and dates**

- [ ] Choose the route and the dates: a legal ceremony in Australia and a symbolic ceremony at the destination, settled before deposits go down.
- [ ] Decide whether the legal ceremony happens before you fly or after you come home, and check the dates against the notice window with your celebrant.

**Notice of Intended Marriage (NOIM)**

- [ ] Give your Notice of Intended Marriage to your authorised marriage celebrant at least 1 month and no more than 18 months before the legal wedding. Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Get married](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married).
- [ ] If you complete the notice while overseas: it cannot be witnessed online from outside Australia, and the witness abroad cannot be a celebrant. Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Notice of intended marriage](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/publications/notice-intended-marriage).
- [ ] If there is less than 1 month to go, talk to your celebrant: a prescribed authority may approve a shorter time. Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Get married](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married).

**Authorised celebrant**

- [ ] Choose an authorised marriage celebrant for the legal ceremony. Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Get married](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married).
- [ ] Book the celebrant's separate, in-person meeting with each of you before the marriage (required since 12 June 2024). Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Get married](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married).
- [ ] Confirm the celebrant will be physically present with you both and 2 witnesses. Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Get married](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married).

**The legal ceremony**

- [ ] Arrange 2 witnesses over 18.
- [ ] The required words are said. Where the celebrant is not a minister of religion, the wording is in the Marriage Act 1961 [section 45(2)](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ma196185/s45.html) and [section 46(1)](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ma196185/s46.html) (AustLII), or words to that effect. The Act itself is on the [Federal Register of Legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1961A00012/latest/text).

**Certificates**

- [ ] 3 marriage certificates are signed by the two of you, the celebrant and 2 witnesses over 18. Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Get married](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married).
- [ ] You receive a ceremonial certificate from the celebrant.
- [ ] The celebrant lodges the paperwork with the registry of births, deaths and marriages in the state or territory where you married, within 14 days. Source: [Attorney-General's Department, Get married](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married).

## Then the symbolic ceremony abroad

- [ ] Choose who leads it: a professional celebrant, or a friend or family member (a symbolic role only).
- [ ] If a friend or family member leads it, give them a script, a rehearsal and a microphone.
- [ ] Write vows that read as promises. If the legal ceremony comes after the trip, ask your celebrant how to word the symbolic vows so they are not the legal words in the Act.
- [ ] Choose any rituals: sand ceremony, handfasting, unity candle, readings and music.
- [ ] Tell guests which ceremony is which, with a line on the invitation or wedding website.

## Questions for the venue

- [ ] Does the venue host symbolic ceremonies, and in which spaces?
- [ ] Privacy: can the ceremony space be closed to the public?
- [ ] What are the photography rules?
- [ ] Is any local permit or notice needed for a ceremony on site or on a public beach? (Not published; ask the venue.)
- [ ] What time does the ceremony start, is there a rehearsal, and what is the wet weather plan?
- [ ] Contract: whose name is on the contract?
- [ ] In which currency are payments made?
- [ ] Deposits: when do deposits become non-refundable?
- [ ] Which rituals and props are allowed: sand, candles, open flame, confetti? (For a unity candle outdoors, ask about open flame and a wind plan.)
- [ ] Access: is there level access to the ceremony space for older guests?
- [ ] What do we need to tell guests beforehand?

## Questions for the celebrant

- [ ] Who leads the ceremony, and in which language?
- [ ] Has the celebrant led symbolic ceremonies abroad before?
- [ ] Contract: whose name is on the contract, in which currency are payments made, and when do deposits become non-refundable?
- [ ] Is there a rehearsal, and what is the plan if the weather turns?

## Questions to ask yourselves

- [ ] Does it matter to us that our wedding date abroad is also our legal anniversary?
- [ ] Who do we want at the legal ceremony, and how private should it be?
- [ ] How much paperwork are we willing to manage from Australia, and how far ahead?
- [ ] Would a celebrant from home, or one at the destination, suit the ceremony we picture?
