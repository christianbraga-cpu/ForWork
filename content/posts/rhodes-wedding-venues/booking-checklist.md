# Booking checklist: Rhodes wedding venues

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (7 slots).

## Legal documents (as at November 2026)

The article states these from the Municipality of Rhodes and the Australian Embassy in Athens. Reconfirm them for the couple's date before relying on them.

- Confirm with the Municipality of Rhodes: the joint application, the marriage licence or certificate of no impediment naming the person to be married and valid for six months, passport copies and the details form; the current municipal fee.
- Confirm translation into Greek (by a Greek lawyer, the Greek Embassy abroad or the Greek Ministry of Foreign Affairs) and the Apostille on every Australian document.
- Confirm originals reach the registrar two days before the wedding, two adult witnesses over 18, Town Hall ceremony days (Wednesday and Friday) and the return to the registry office the next working day to sign.
- Confirm which regional registrar covers the chosen ceremony location (Rhodes City, Lindos, Ialysos, Kallithea and others), and whether it officiates at that location on the date.
- Confirm with the Australian Embassy in Athens: CNI, apostille or authentication, appointment or postal submission, and fees.
- Confirm with each venue whether it can host the legal civil ceremony or only a symbolic one, and what its planner or wedding specialist handles (Melenos and the Atrium hotels name one).
- Check Smartraveller and Schengen entry rules (90 days in any 180, Entry/Exit System) close to travel.

## General (ask every venue, in writing)

- Deposits: amount due to secure the date, when it is due, and whether it is refundable
- Cancellation and postponement terms, including heat, wildfire and strong-wind disruption
- Capacity: seated and standing for each ceremony and reception space
- Rooms: room count, room blocks and minimum nights
- Exclusive use: the wedding spaces only, one wedding per day, or the whole property; from what time
- Music: the time amplified music must stop outdoors and indoors (curfew), and any noise rules
- Step-free access: steps between rooms, ceremony and reception spaces, and whether a step-free route exists
- Rain or wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space
- Set-up and pack-down windows, outside suppliers allowed, and which inclusions a package covers
