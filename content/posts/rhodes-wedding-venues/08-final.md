Rhodes wedding venues sit in four parts of the island: Lindos and the south, Rhodes Town in the north, the east coast around Kallithea, and the west coast at Ixia. The right one depends on how many guests you are inviting, the ceremony you want and whether you picture a village, a resort beach or a historic town on your doorstep.

Every venue here hosts weddings, and each is set out on the same terms so you can compare them fairly. For the wider picture, browse [our curated Greece wedding collection](https://weddedwonderland.com/destinations/europe/greece).

### The Old Town as a setting

The Medieval Town of Rhodes is a [UNESCO World Heritage Site](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/islands/dodecanese/rhodes), an open-air museum where Byzantines, Knights of St John, Ottomans and Italians each left their mark. <!-- fact_400 --> Plan it as the backdrop for portraits and a walk with your guests between events, rather than as a reception venue.

## Getting to Rhodes for your wedding

Most guests arrive by air. [Rhodes Airport](https://www.rho-airport.gr/en) (RHO) publishes live arrivals and departures, with all times in Greek local time. <!-- fact_307 -->

Flights from Athens International Airport to Rhodes take about 1 hour, and from Thessaloniki about 1 hour 20 minutes. <!-- fact_312 -->

Some guests will island-hop. Ferries and large catamarans carry most visitors to the Greek islands, and services can be delayed or cancelled in bad weather, mainly due to strong winds. <!-- fact_320 --> Check the forecast and reconfirm departures. <!-- fact_320 -->

As at November 2026, Australians can visit the Schengen area, which includes Greece, for short trips without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. <!-- fact_321 --> The Entry/Exit System registers fingerprints and a photo on arrival and may cause border delays, so leave a buffer before the first event. <!-- fact_321 -->

## Getting legally married on Rhodes, in brief

The facts below are as at November 2026. For the country-wide view, read [the legal steps in our Greece venues guide](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues#getting-legally-married-in-greece-as-australians-in-brief).

**The documents.** The [Municipality of Rhodes](https://www.rhodes.gr/dikaiologitika-gia-ekdosi-adeias-telesi-gamou/) sets out what a civil marriage of foreign nationals needs. <!-- fact_600 --> That includes a joint application, a marriage licence or certificate of no impediment naming the person you will marry and valid for six months, a copy of each passport and a form with your details. <!-- fact_600 --> A municipal fee applies. <!-- fact_600 -->

**Translation and Apostille.** Documents issued by foreign authorities must be recent originals, officially translated into Greek by a Greek lawyer, the Greek Embassy abroad or the Greek Ministry of Foreign Affairs. <!-- fact_601 --> Documents from countries outside the European Union, Australia among them, need the Apostille stamp. <!-- fact_601 --> Details not shown on your documents can be recorded through a solemn declaration on the municipality's template. <!-- fact_602 -->

**On the day.** Originals must be with the registrar two days before the wedding. <!-- fact_504 --> Two adult witnesses over 18 must be present. <!-- fact_505 --> Ceremonies at the Town Hall of Rhodes take place every Wednesday and Friday, and the couple return to the registry office the next working day to sign and have the marriage certificate drawn up. <!-- fact_505 -->

**Where.** The municipality's list of [civil ceremony venues](https://www.rhodes.gr/tourist-guide/ceremony-venues/) runs well beyond the Town Hall. <!-- fact_500 --> For Lindos it names St Paul's chapel, a tiny chapel below a cliff in the shadow of the Acropolis of Lindos, beside the turquoise waters of St Paul's Bay. <!-- fact_502 --> It lists Filerimos for Ialysos, and Kallithea Springs & Spa, Faliraki Beach and Koskinou Beach for Kallithea, each region with its own registrar. <!-- fact_501 --> Beaches from Kolimbia to Prasonisi and villages such as Embonas and Soroni are on the list too. <!-- fact_503 -->

**From Australia.** The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) witnesses applications for and issues Certificates of No Impediment, and legalises original Australian public documents by authentication or apostille. <!-- fact_324 --> An appointment is required for all notarial services, though Apostille and CNI documents may be submitted by post, and a fee applies. <!-- fact_325 --> The embassy does not provide legal advice, and the onus is on you to present documents in the correct form. <!-- fact_326 --> Some venues name a planner or wedding specialist who assists with the documents, as Melenos and the Atrium hotels do. <!-- fact_05 --> <!-- fact_202 --> <!-- fact_205 --> Read [Smartraveller's Greece advice](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) before you travel; its overall level for Greece is "Exercise normal safety precautions". <!-- fact_316 -->

## Rhodes wedding venue questions, answered

### Which Rhodes wedding venues are on the beach?

Aquagrand on Navarone Bay, Atrium Palace on Kalathos beach and Atrium Prestige on Lachania private beach all offer beach ceremonies. <!-- fact_08 --> <!-- fact_14 --> <!-- fact_201 --> Grande Albergo delle Rose has an adjoining beach, and Elysium offers a reception setting by the beach on Kallithea bay. <!-- fact_100 --> <!-- fact_509 -->

### Can we have a wedding ceremony at St Paul's Bay?

Yes. St Paul's chapel, below the Acropolis of Lindos, is on the municipality's list of civil ceremony locations. <!-- fact_502 --> Aquagrand and Atrium Palace both offer it as a ceremony option, and Atrium Palace advises booking it as early as possible. <!-- fact_10 --> <!-- fact_15 -->

### Can we get married in Rhodes Old Town?

None of the venues on this list is inside the Old Town walls, so plan the Medieval Town as your portrait backdrop. The Medieval Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. <!-- fact_310 --> Civil ceremony locations in Rhodes City are set by the municipality and include Rodini park, Monte Smith and the Town Hall staircase. <!-- fact_500 --> Grande Albergo delle Rose sits within reach of the Medieval Town. <!-- fact_100 -->

### Can we book a Rhodes venue for exclusive use?

Melenos in Lindos suggests reserving the entire hotel for a minimum of three nights. <!-- fact_03 --> The other venues here do not publish exclusive use, so ask each one whether the wedding spaces or the whole property can be reserved for you.

### Do Rhodes venues offer wedding packages?

Some do. Rodos Palace offers set packages or a tailor-made one, and Aquagrand publishes its Aqua Pearl and Aqua Marine packages. <!-- fact_207 --> <!-- fact_09 --> To see how inclusions compare across the country, read [how Greece wedding package inclusions compare](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-packages-all-inclusive).

### Can Australians legally get married in Rhodes?

Yes, with the right documents. As at November 2026, a civil marriage of foreign nationals needs a certificate of no impediment valid for six months, officially translated into Greek, with an Apostille for documents from outside the European Union. <!-- fact_600 --> <!-- fact_601 --> The Australian Embassy in Athens issues CNIs and apostilles, by appointment or by post. <!-- fact_324 --> <!-- fact_325 -->
