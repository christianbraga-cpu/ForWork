A question sheet for couples weighing a March wedding abroad. Take these to the venue, your Wedded Advisor and the official sources for your own dates. These are questions only; the answers belong in writing from the venue or the official source. This sheet doesn't block publishing.

## Step 2: weather and season questions (for each destination)

- What does the national weather service say about March for the place closest to our venue, and which station and period is that reading from?
- How far is our venue from that station, and could the coast, lake, hills or island make it read differently?
- What season is March in this destination, according to the national weather service?
- Is March inside a wet, cyclone or monsoon season here, according to Smartraveller and the national weather service, and when does that season end?
- What does Smartraveller currently say about weather, flooding, road closures or airport disruption for this destination?
- How cool are March evenings here, and what will guests need to wear after dark?
- How hot is the middle of the day here in March, and what time of day is cooler for the ceremony?
- What is the forecast for our wedding week, and when will we check it again closer to the day?
- Is Easter near our date in the year we are marrying, and does it change how the venue or the town works that week?

## Step 3: questions for every venue (ask in writing)

- Is the venue open, and fully staffed, on our March date? When does its season open?
- What is the covered or indoor ceremony option, does it hold the whole guest list, and can it be dressed in advance?
- How late on the day can we decide to move under cover, and who makes the call?
- Which rooms are heated for dinner and dancing, or is the reception space cooled?
- Is there a buffer day we can hold in case weather moves the date?
- What happens to our deposit if weather moves the date?
- Can the whole property be ours for exclusive use?
- Is there a step-free route between the guest rooms, the ceremony, dinner and the bathrooms?
- How do older guests or guests with limited mobility reach the ceremony if paths or steps are wet?
- Are the paths and floors easy underfoot when wet?
- How far is the nearest doctor?
- What does the venue not publish (curfew, minimum nights, set-up times, inclusions), and will our Wedded Advisor confirm each one in writing before we commit?

## Step 4: destination questions from the post

- **Greek islands:** Is the venue open in March, and which spaces are sheltered from a north wind? If a ferry or sea crossing is cancelled, what is the plan for guests arriving that week?
- **Lake Como:** Is the villa open for weddings in March, can the whole property be ours for exclusive use, and which rooms are heated for dinner and dancing? Is there a step-free route from the guest rooms to the ceremony and the dining room?
- **Amalfi Coast:** Is there an indoor or covered space that holds the whole guest list if it rains? Which hotels and restaurants on our stretch of coast are open in March? How do guests who find stairs hard reach the ceremony and dinner?
- **French Riviera:** Is there heating, a marquee or an indoor room for the evening reception? When does the venue open its season, and is the full team working in March?
- **Portugal:** Which part of the ceremony can move under cover at short notice, and who makes the call? Are the paths and floors between ceremony, dinner and bathrooms easy underfoot when wet?
- **New Zealand:** What is the indoor or covered plan if the forecast turns in our wedding week? If roads close, how do guests reach the venue, and is there accommodation on site or nearby?
- **Mauritius:** What is the covered ceremony option, and is the reception space cooled? What happens to the wedding, and to guests' rooms, if a cyclone closes the airport that week? Where is the shade for older guests, and is drinking water on hand?
- **Seychelles:** Where does the ceremony move in a downpour, and can that space be dressed in advance? If guests cross between islands by boat, who decides when the sea is too rough? Is the ceremony shaded, sheltered and close to guests' rooms?
- **Bali:** Is there a covered pavilion or indoor ceremony space, and how late on the day can we decide to use it? How do older guests reach the ceremony if paths or steps are wet? If heavy rain floods roads, how do guests get to the venue?
- **Fiji:** What are the wet-weather and cyclone plans, and will the venue set them out in writing? If floods close the road from the airport, how do guests, especially older ones, reach the resort?

## Step 5: guest travel questions (guests travelling from Australia)

- Does our date clash with school holidays or public holidays in the states our guests live in?
- Have guests checked Smartraveller for the destination close to the dates, and subscribed to its updates?
- Does each guest's travel insurance cover cyclone, storm or flood disruption, and flight delays or cancellations?
- If flights are delayed or the airport closes, where do guests stay, and who tells them?
- If guests need a ferry, boat or long road transfer, what is the back-up if it is cancelled or the road is closed?
- Have we told guests what to pack for March at this destination (a jacket for cool evenings, an umbrella for rain)?
- Which guests will need a hand on the journey, and who looks after them on travel days?
- Do guests have copies of their flight, accommodation and event bookings, and have they shared their itinerary with family or friends?

## Step 6: legal or symbolic

- Can we marry legally in this country as Australians, and what does that require? (Read where you can legally marry overseas.)
- Would a legal marriage at home and a symbolic ceremony abroad suit us better?
- How long does the legal process take, and does it fit our March date?
