March is a shoulder month for a wedding abroad, so the question is which kind of shoulder you want. It is early spring in Europe and autumn in New Zealand, while Bali, Fiji, Mauritius and the Seychelles are still inside the wet, cyclone or monsoon season described for them by Smartraveller. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_105 --> <!-- fact_107 --> <!-- fact_110 --> <!-- fact_112 -->

The ten wedding destinations in March below are unranked and grouped by what the month means in each place, with a table at a glance after the entries. If you are still choosing a region, browse our [destination wedding locations by region](/destinations).

## Key takeaways

- The ten are the Greek islands, Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, the French Riviera and Portugal in Europe's early spring, and New Zealand, Mauritius, the Seychelles, Bali and Fiji in the southern autumn and late summer.
- In Europe, March nights are cool at every official station quoted here, so heating, wraps and an indoor room belong in the plan. <!-- fact_001 --> <!-- fact_002 --> <!-- fact_003 --> <!-- fact_004 --> <!-- fact_008 -->

## March is a shoulder month for a destination wedding

Plan for March as it is. In Europe that means cool nights and changeable skies. In the tropics it means rain, and in some places cyclones, with a covered option you would be happy to marry under. New Zealand's autumn sits between the two.

### Greek islands

**The March trade-off.** A northerly wind and regular rain days make an exposed clifftop ceremony harder to promise in March. <!-- fact_008 -->

**March weather.** The [Hellenic National Meteorological Service's climate data for Naxos](http://oldportal.emy.gr/emy/en/climatology/climatology_city?perifereia=South%20Aegean&poli=Naxos) (period not stated by HNMS) gives a March minimum temperature of 10.4°C, a mean of 13.4°C and a maximum of 15.8°C. <!-- fact_008 --> It gives a monthly mean of 45.7 mm of precipitation over 9.5 precipitation days, and a prevailing north wind. <!-- fact_008 --> The station is on Naxos, not Santorini or Mykonos. Our [Greek island weather month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece) sets March beside the rest of the year.

### Lake Como, Italy

**The March trade-off.** A villa garden on the water, with cold evenings that move dinner indoors. <!-- fact_001 -->

### Amalfi Coast, Italy

**The March trade-off.** Cliff towns and sea terraces, with cool, changeable days on a coast where many spaces are outdoors and reached by stairs. <!-- fact_002 -->

**March weather.** [Naples' climate normals for 1971 to 2000](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=604), from the Italian Air Force meteorological service, give a March mean daily minimum of 6.3°C, a mean daily maximum of 15.7°C and 77.9 mm of rain over 8.6 rain days. <!-- fact_002 --> These are city readings from Naples, not from the Amalfi Coast. <!-- fact_002 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Is there an indoor or covered space that holds the whole guest list if it rains?
- Which hotels and restaurants on your stretch of coast are open in March?

### French Riviera

**The March trade-off.** Sea light and terraced gardens, with evenings cool enough to plan dinner under a roof. <!-- fact_003 -->

**March weather.** [Nice's climate normals for 1971 to 2000](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=902), supplied by Météo-France, give a March mean daily minimum of 7.6°C, a mean daily maximum of 14.9°C and 60.9 mm of precipitation over 5.2 days with at least 1 mm. That precipitation includes both rain and snow. <!-- fact_003 --> Read them as Nice on the coast, not inland Provence. <!-- fact_003 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Is there heating, a marquee or an indoor room for the evening reception?
- When does the venue open its season, and is the full team working in March?

**Our guide.** For Riviera estates and châteaux further afield, see [France wedding venues and chateaux](/destinations/europe/france).

### Portugal

**The March trade-off.** Palaces and estates around Lisbon, with mild days and frequent rain days to plan around. <!-- fact_004 -->

**March weather.** [Lisbon's older climate normals for 1961 to 1990](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=2), supplied by IPMA, give a March mean daily minimum of 9.9°C, a mean daily maximum of 17.6°C and 68.9 mm of rain over 13.0 days. <!-- fact_004 --> Lisbon counts any day with at least 0.1 mm as a rain day. <!-- fact_004 --> It is a city reading; estates beyond Lisbon are not measured here. <!-- fact_004 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Which part of the ceremony can move under cover at short notice, and who makes the call?
- Are the paths and floors between ceremony, dinner and bathrooms easy underfoot when wet?

## Autumn and late summer in the southern hemisphere

South of the equator, March means autumn in New Zealand and, in the tropics, heat and the wet or cyclone season.

### New Zealand

**The March trade-off.** Settled autumn spells, but one storm can change a week, as it did in March 2026. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_102 -->

**March weather.** The [Earth Sciences New Zealand climate summary for March 2026](https://niwa.co.nz/climate-and-weather/monthly/climate-summary-march-2026) covers one year, not an average. It called the month a dry start to autumn for much of the country, mild and mostly settled, with rainfall below or well below normal in Central Otago, most of Wellington and Hawke's Bay. The month's highest temperature was 30.9°C at Alexandra on 7 March and its lowest minus 2.6°C at Pūkaki Airport on 3 March. <!-- fact_101 --> On 25 and 26 March a deep subtropical low brought heavy rain and flooding, with states of emergency and road closures from Northland to the Bay of Plenty. <!-- fact_102 --> As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/new-zealand) warns that weather can change quickly and that severe weather can leave travellers stranded, especially in remote areas. <!-- fact_103 -->

### Mauritius

**The March trade-off.** A warm, humid summer month, but one of the two wettest of the year and inside the cyclone season. <!-- fact_113 --> <!-- fact_114 --> <!-- fact_112 -->

**March weather.** [Port Louis' climate normals for 1971 to 2000](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=1219), supplied by Mauritius Meteorological Services, give a March mean daily minimum of 23.8°C, a mean daily maximum of 31.5°C and 83.0 mm of precipitation over 8.0 days with at least 1 mm. <!-- fact_005 --> [Mauritius Meteorological Services](https://metservice.intnet.mu/climate-services/climate-of-mauritius.php) names February and March as the wettest months of the year, in a warm, humid summer from November to April. <!-- fact_114 --> <!-- fact_113 --> As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/mauritius) puts cyclone season from November to May, though cyclones can occur throughout the year, and during a cyclone the airport may close and flights may be cancelled or delayed. <!-- fact_112 --> At a mean daily maximum above 31°C, older guests need shade, water and a cooler hour for the ceremony. <!-- fact_005 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- What is the covered ceremony option, and is the reception space cooled?
- What happens to the wedding, and to guests' rooms, if a cyclone closes the airport that week?

**Our guide.** Go deeper with our [Mauritius wedding planning guide](/destination-weddings/mauritius-wedding).

### Seychelles

**The March trade-off.** Island beaches and hot days, but March is the last month of the monsoon season Smartraveller describes. <!-- fact_110 -->

**March weather.** The [Seychelles Meteorological Authority's normals for Victoria, 1981 to 2010](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=253) give a March mean daily minimum of 25.3°C, a mean daily maximum of 31.2°C and 201.9 mm of rain over 15.0 days with at least 0.1 mm. <!-- fact_109 --> As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/seychelles) warns that heavy rain can occur, especially in the monsoon season from November to March, causing flooding and landslides that damage roads. <!-- fact_110 --> In this heat, older guests need a shaded, sheltered ceremony close to their rooms. <!-- fact_109 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Where does the ceremony move in a downpour, and can that space be dressed in advance?
- If guests cross between islands by boat, who decides when the sea is too rough?

### Bali

**The March trade-off.** Clifftop and garden ceremonies, but March sits inside Indonesia's usual wet season, so the plan needs a roof. <!-- fact_107 -->

**March weather.** [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia), as at November 2026, describes Indonesia's wet season as usually around October to April (it can differ in Eastern Indonesia), with weather that can change quickly. Heavy rain can cause significant flooding in urban areas including Bali, disrupting roads and transport. <!-- fact_107 --> To see March beside the other eleven months, read [when to marry in Bali, month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-bali).

### Fiji

**The March trade-off.** Lagoons and island resorts, but March is in Fiji's wet season and its cyclone season. <!-- fact_104 --> <!-- fact_105 -->

**March weather.** The [Fiji Meteorological Service's March 2026 summary](https://www.met.gov.fj/media/climate_product_files/Fiji_Climate_Summary_March_2026_wUpxcQh.pdf) covers one year only. It called March the wet season, with rainfall from well below to above average, most stations drier than usual, and the month's highest total of 640.3 mm at Rarawai Mill in Ba. <!-- fact_104 --> As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/fiji) states that November to April is cyclone season, though cyclones may occur at other times, and that flights can be delayed or suspended. <!-- fact_105 --> It adds that flooding is common, especially in downtown Nadi and on the Coral Coast, and can cut road access to the airport. <!-- fact_106 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- What are the wet-weather and cyclone plans, and will you set them out in writing?
- If floods close the road from the airport, how do guests, especially older ones, reach the resort?

## What to plan for a March wedding abroad

### Shoulder season weather and a plan B

In a shoulder month, the covered option is the real plan. In Europe, that means heated rooms and an indoor ceremony space. In the tropics, a pavilion big enough for everyone, a buffer day and travel insurance that covers cyclone disruption. <!-- fact_112 --> Before you commit, your Wedded Advisor confirms in writing what each venue includes.

### Seasonal openings and Easter

Ask each European venue whether it is open and fully staffed on your March date. Ask, too, whether Easter falls near your date that year and how the venue and town work around it.

### Legal or symbolic ceremony

Each country sets its own requirements for a legal marriage, so read [where you can legally marry overseas](/destination-weddings/weddings-abroad-where-can-you-legally-get-married) before you choose between that and a [symbolic ceremony](/expert-advice/symbolic-ceremony).

### Guests travelling from Australia

Tell guests honestly what to pack: a jacket for Europe, an umbrella for the islands. Check with each venue how guests with limited mobility get between spaces when paths are wet.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is March a good month to get married?

Yes, if you plan for a shoulder month: cool early spring in Europe, autumn in New Zealand, or warm, wet weeks in the tropics.

### Where is warm for a wedding in March?

Mauritius and the Seychelles. Port Louis in Mauritius has a March mean daily maximum of 31.5°C in its 1971 to 2000 normals, <!-- fact_005 --> and Victoria in the Seychelles has 31.2°C in its 1981 to 2010 normals. <!-- fact_109 --> Both are wet too, so plan for heat and rain together.

### Which European destinations suit a March wedding?

The Greek islands, Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, the French Riviera and Portugal all work, with cool evenings and indoor space in the plan.

### Is March cyclone season in Fiji?

Yes. As at November 2026, Smartraveller puts Fiji's cyclone season from November to April, and the Fiji Meteorological Service called March 2026 the wet season. <!-- fact_105 --> <!-- fact_104 -->

### Is March autumn in New Zealand?

Yes. Earth Sciences New Zealand described March 2026 as a dry start to autumn for much of the country, a single year's reading rather than an average. <!-- fact_101 -->
