# Questions to ask any honeymoon planner

These questions apply to any honeymoon planner, whoever you speak with. Ask them early, and ask for the answers in writing. They are prompts for a first conversation, not answers. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the publishing blockers are image credits and licences (5 slots), the `/honeymoon-ideas` folder and Honeymoon Ideas category existing in the CMS, and the two draft internal links.

As at November 2026. This is general information, not legal or financial advice.

## Before you enquire

- [ ] Dates: the earliest we can leave, the latest we must be home and any days that cannot move.
- [ ] Length of the trip, and how many stays it can comfortably hold.
- [ ] Style: still or moving, secluded or close to a little life in the evenings.
- [ ] Each of our one or two must-haves.
- [ ] How we would like to pay, and in what order, before the first deposit is due.

## Fee and inclusions

- [ ] What is your fee, how is it worked out, when is it payable, and what does it cover? Please put it in writing before any work begins.
- [ ] What is included in each booking (every stay, flight and experience)? Please confirm the inclusions in writing before we pay a deposit.
- [ ] Do you receive commissions from suppliers? If so, is it disclosed to us, and does it affect which suppliers you recommend?

## Terms and protection

- [ ] What are your terms, and what are the terms of each airline, hotel or operator? Please show us both sets before we book.
- [ ] If any terms are unclear or cannot be found, will you ask the provider to confirm them in writing?
- [ ] Do you hold insolvency insurance, and what happens to our money if you or a provider becomes insolvent?

## Travel insurance

- [ ] When should we take out travel insurance? The earlier, the more likely we are to be covered for something unexpected before the trip.
- [ ] Does the policy cover travel provider or agent insolvency? It is commonly excluded.
- [ ] Does the policy cover every country on the route, stopovers included?

## Money in other currencies

- [ ] What exchange rate sits behind each AUD quote, and is it guaranteed?
- [ ] What fees or margin apply, and does the receiver pay anything at the other end?

## Names and documents

- [ ] Is every ticket booked in the name on the passport we will travel on?
- [ ] If either of us changes our family name, will we travel on a new passport or the current one, and do the bookings match?
- [ ] If we marry overseas, have we checked with our registry of births, deaths and marriages about proof of a name change?

## The wedding and the honeymoon together

- [ ] Straight after the wedding, or later? Have we weighed the weather, how tired we will be and the leave we have?
- [ ] If we leave the morning after: goodbye time with the last guests, a later check-out, who packs and sends home the wedding clothes and keepsakes, the journey to the next stay and which documents go in hand luggage.
