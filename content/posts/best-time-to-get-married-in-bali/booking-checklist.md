# Booking checklist: best time to get married in Bali

Questions for the couple, the Wedded Advisor and the venue to settle for a chosen date. These don't block publishing. All items are as at November 2026; reconfirm each for the couple's date.

## Weather outlook for your year

- Have you checked Stasiun Klimatologi Bali (BMKG) for the seasonal outlook covering your year and your venue's season zone?
- When does the outlook forecast the rainy season to start (or end) in that zone, and has it changed since you shortlisted months?
- Will you recheck the outlook about three months before the date?

## 2027 holidays and collective leave to plan around

- Does your date fall on or near Nyepi (Monday 8 March 2027) or the Idul Fitri holidays and collective leave from 9 to 15 March?
- Has the ministerial decision confirming Idul Fitri and Idul Adha 2027 been issued, and did any date move?
- Does your date sit near another 2027 national holiday or collective leave day (January, February, late March, May, June, August or late December)?
- Do your suppliers and guests' flights work on the days either side?

## Questions for the venue

### Wet-season backup
- Which covered space is held for you on the day, in writing?
- Does it seat the full guest list for both ceremony and dinner?
- What changes in styling, sound and lighting if you move indoors?

### Weather call deadline
- By what time, on the day or the day before, is the call made between outdoor and covered?
- Who makes the call, and how are guests and suppliers told?

### Heat and shade for older guests
- Is the ceremony space shaded, with fans or air-conditioning nearby?
- What ceremony time does the venue suggest for comfort in your month?
- Is there seating and water for older guests from arrival?
- How far is the walk from the cars, and is there a step-free route?

### Nyepi and arrivals
- What happens at the resort on Nyepi and on the evening before?
- Which days around 8 March suit guest arrivals and departures, and do airport transfers run?
- Which services (dining, spa, transfers) run on the day?
- Will suppliers work on the days either side, and at what notice?
- How many nights should guests book to sit comfortably around Nyepi?

## Insurance timing and contract terms

- Should travel and wedding insurance start from the first deposit?
- Does cover include cancellation, weather, volcanic ash disruption and medical needs, including for older guests?
- What does the contract say in writing about date changes, force majeure and refunds?
- How do deposits carry across if you move indoors or change the date?
