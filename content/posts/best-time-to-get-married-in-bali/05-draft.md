## What are Bali's dry and wet seasons?

Bali has two seasons rather than four. As at November 2026, Smartraveller says Indonesia's wet season is usually around October to April, that it can differ in Eastern Indonesia, and that weather can change quickly while it lasts. <!-- fact_103 --> Heavy rain can cause significant flooding in urban areas including Bali, disrupting roads and transport. <!-- fact_103 -->

The months outside that window, May to September, form the drier half of the year.

The island does not change season on a fixed date. Indonesia's meteorological agency, BMKG, runs [Stasiun Klimatologi Bali](https://staklim-bali.bmkg.go.id/), the climate station for Bali Province, which publishes 10-day rainfall forecasts, a monthly bulletin with a three-month outlook, and a season bulletin forecasting when the rainy or dry season begins. <!-- fact_01 -->

Bali is also not one climate. The station divides the province into 20 season zones, each an area whose rainfall shows a clear difference between the dry-season and rainy-season periods. <!-- fact_02 --> A clifftop in the south and a valley in the highlands can sit in different zones, which is why the venue's location matters as much as the month.

## How to read BMKG's seasonal forecast for your year

Each year, Stasiun Klimatologi Bali publishes a forecast of when the rainy season will begin, and its home page currently links to the prediction for 2026 to 2027. <!-- fact_08 --> Read the one for your wedding year, and your venue's zone, once you have a shortlist of months. <!-- fact_02 -->

**Season starts come in 10-day blocks.** BMKG writes onset in dasarian: days 1 to 10, 11 to 20, and 21 to the end of the month. <!-- fact_03 --> "November I to November II", for example, means between 1 and 20 November. <!-- fact_03 --> A forecast for your zone tells you which third of the month the season is expected to turn.

**Normal means a 30-year average.** In the bulletins, "normal" monthly rainfall is the average for that month over 30 years, and "average" monthly rainfall uses at least 10 years. <!-- fact_02 --> A month described as Above Normal is wetter than its long-term pattern; Below Normal is drier.

**Rain intensity has set bands.** A day under 5 mm is very light rain, 5 to 20 mm light, 20 to 50 mm moderate, 50 to 100 mm heavy and over 100 mm very heavy. <!-- fact_04 --> Light rain is a short wait under cover. Heavy rain is when you move indoors and leave extra time for guest transfers.

**One year is not every year.** The monthly bulletins analyse the month just gone and look three months ahead. <!-- fact_01 --> Treat any single year's figures as that year's weather, not as a guide to yours, and check the outlook again three months before the wedding.

### Nyepi, the Day of Silence

Nyepi, the Hindu New Year (Saka 1949), falls on Monday 8 March 2027. <!-- fact_100 --> It sits directly beside the Idul Fitri holidays and collective leave from 9 to 15 March. <!-- fact_102 -->

If your preferred dates fall in early March, ask your venue these questions before you hold a date:

- What happens at the resort on Nyepi itself, and on the evening before?
- Can guests arrive or depart on 8 March, or should flights avoid that day?
- Which services, from dining to spa to transfers, run on the day?
- Will your suppliers work on the days either side, and at what notice?

## How do you plan a wet-season wedding?

### Chapels and covered spaces, as each venue describes them

- **AYANA Bali** lists indoor venues including Tresna Chapel, a white chapel above the Indian Ocean for 70 at the ceremony, and Astina Pavilion, glass-walled with 180-degree ocean views for 40. <!-- fact_107 --> Its Amara Ballroom seats 390 for a ceremony. <!-- fact_107 -->

- **Mulia Bali** names a floating glass chapel among its wedding spaces. <!-- fact_108 -->
- **The St. Regis Bali Resort** has the Cloud Nine Chapel on its beachfront, with Swarovski crystal chandeliers and ocean views, a pond and cascading steps, and a wooden terrace beyond for canapés and drinks. <!-- fact_111 -->
- **Raffles Bali** lists beachfront, garden and private villa venues, with indoor and sheltered options. <!-- fact_112 -->
- **The Seminyak Beach Resort & Spa** has Purnama Chapel, a pavilion on Seminyak Beach with ocean views, for up to 90 guests. <!-- fact_114 -->
- **Alila Seminyak** says its indoor venues, open-air terraces and event lawns hold up to 250 guests. <!-- fact_113 -->
- **COMO Uma Ubud** names its Yoga Studio, for up to 40 guests, as the wet weather option for events in Uma Bar. <!-- fact_110 -->

Notice the capacities. A chapel for 70 and a lawn for 250 suit very different guest lists, and the covered space needs to hold everyone, not only the ceremony.

### Questions to ask your venue

- **Covered backup.** Which covered space is held for you on the day, and does it seat your full guest list for both ceremony and dinner?
- **Weather call deadline.** By what time on the day, or the day before, do you decide between outdoor and covered, and who makes the call?
- **Deposits.** If you move indoors or change the date, how do deposits and payments carry across, and what does the contract say in writing?
- **Guest travel.** How long do transfers take from the airport and between hotels in heavy rain? Smartraveller notes flooding can disrupt roads and transport in Bali. <!-- fact_103 -->
- **Volcanic ash.** Smartraveller says volcanic activity can escalate with little or no notice and cause flight delays, cancellations or airport closures, with Mount Agung in Bali Province among the volcanoes to watch. <!-- fact_104 --> Ask how the venue handles guests who arrive late.
- **Beach safety.** Australians have drowned in rough seas and rip currents at popular beaches, including in Bali. <!-- fact_105 --> Ask which beach is safe for guests on the days around the wedding.
- **Health.** Smartraveller notes dengue occurs in Bali and advises rabies vaccination before travel. <!-- fact_106 --> Pass the [Smartraveller Indonesia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia) advice on to guests with the save-the-date.

### Is there a right or wrong month to marry in Bali?

No single month suits every wedding. Drier months favour outdoor ceremonies, wet-season months call for a chapel or covered space, and official dates such as Nyepi shape guest travel. <!-- fact_103 --> <!-- fact_100 --> Choose by your ceremony setting, guest list and the 2027 calendar.

### Is December a good time for a wedding in Bali during the wet season?

It can be, with a covered plan. December sits within the usual wet-season window, and collective leave on 24 December, Christmas Day and Isra Mikraj on 26 December fall together in 2027. <!-- fact_103 --> <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_102 --> A venue with a chapel, such as Cloud Nine Chapel at The St. Regis Bali Resort, gives the day a covered setting. <!-- fact_111 -->

### What happens if it rains on the wedding day?

You move to the covered space agreed in your contract. Agree in writing which space is held, whether it seats every guest, and the time by which the weather call is made.
