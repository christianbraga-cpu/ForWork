## What are Bali's dry and wet seasons?

Bali has two seasons rather than four. As at November 2026, Smartraveller says Indonesia's wet season is usually around October to April, that it can differ in Eastern Indonesia, and that weather can change quickly while it lasts. <!-- fact_103 --> Heavy rain can cause significant flooding in urban areas including Bali, disrupting roads and transport. <!-- fact_103 -->

The months outside that window run from May to September.

Bali is also not one climate. Its climate station divides the province into 20 season zones, each an area whose rainfall shows a clear difference between the dry-season and rainy-season periods. <!-- fact_02 --> A clifftop in the south and a valley in the highlands can sit in different zones, which is why the venue's location matters as much as the month.

Rainfall varies from year to year, and the season does not turn on a fixed date. [Stasiun Klimatologi Bali](https://staklim-bali.bmkg.go.id/) publishes a seasonal outlook each year, and it is worth checking for your dates. <!-- fact_08 --> <!-- fact_03 -->

### Heat, shade and comfort

Whatever the month, ask your venue how the ceremony will feel for every guest, not only how it will look.

- **Shade.** Is the ceremony space shaded, and are there fans or air-conditioning nearby?
- **Time of day.** What ceremony time does the venue suggest for comfort in your month?
- **Older guests.** Is there seating and water for older guests from the moment they arrive?
- **Distance.** How far is the walk from the cars to the ceremony space, and is there a step-free route?

### Nyepi, the Day of Silence

Nyepi, the Hindu New Year (Saka 1949), falls on Monday 8 March 2027. <!-- fact_100 --> It sits directly beside the Idul Fitri holidays and collective leave from 9 to 15 March. <!-- fact_102 -->

Nyepi is the Day of Silence that opens the Saka new year. <!-- fact_100 --> If your preferred dates fall in early March, ask these questions before you hold a date:

- What happens at the resort on Nyepi itself, and on the evening before?
- Which days around 8 March suit guest arrivals and departures?
- Which services, from dining to spa to transfers, run on the day?
- Will your suppliers work on the days either side, and at what notice?
- How many nights should guests book to sit comfortably around Nyepi?
- Ask your airline how its schedule runs on and around 8 March before guests book.

## How do you plan a wet-season wedding?

### Chapels and covered spaces, as each venue describes them

- **AYANA Bali** lists indoor venues including Tresna Chapel, a white chapel above the Indian Ocean for 70 at the ceremony, and Astina Pavilion, glass-walled with 180-degree ocean views for 40. <!-- fact_107 --> Its Amara Ballroom seats 390 for a ceremony. <!-- fact_107 -->

- **The St. Regis Bali Resort** has the Cloud Nine Chapel on its beachfront, with Swarovski crystal chandeliers and ocean views, a pond and cascading steps, and a wooden terrace beyond for canapés and drinks. <!-- fact_111 -->
- **Raffles Bali** lists beachfront, garden and private villa venues, with indoor and sheltered options. <!-- fact_112 -->
- **The Seminyak Beach Resort & Spa** has Purnama Chapel, a pavilion on Seminyak Beach with ocean views, for up to 90 guests. <!-- fact_114 -->
- **Alila Seminyak** says its indoor venues, open-air terraces and event lawns hold up to 250 guests. <!-- fact_113 -->
- **COMO Uma Ubud** names its Yoga Studio, for up to 40 guests, as the wet weather option for events in Uma Bar. <!-- fact_110 -->

Notice the capacities. A chapel for 70 and a lawn for 250 suit very different guest lists, and the covered space needs to hold everyone, not only the ceremony.

### Questions to ask your venue

- **Covered backup.** Which covered space is held for you on the day, and does it seat your full guest list for both ceremony and dinner?
- **Weather call deadline.** By what time on the day, or the day before, do you decide between outdoor and covered, and who makes the call?
- **Contract terms.** What does the contract say in writing about date changes, force majeure and refunds, and how do deposits carry across if you move indoors or change the date?
- **Insurance timing.** Ask your insurer whether cover for cancellation, weather and medical needs, including for older guests, should start from the first deposit.
- **Guest travel.** How long do transfers take from the airport and between hotels in heavy rain? Smartraveller notes flooding can disrupt roads and transport in Bali. <!-- fact_103 -->
- **Volcanic ash.** Smartraveller says volcanic activity can escalate with little or no notice and cause flight delays, cancellations or airport closures, with Mount Agung in Bali Province among the volcanoes to watch. <!-- fact_104 --> Ask how the venue handles guests who arrive late.
- **Beach safety.** Australians have drowned in rough seas and rip currents at popular beaches, including in Bali. <!-- fact_105 --> Ask which beach is safe for guests on the days around the wedding.
- **Health.** Smartraveller notes dengue occurs in Bali and advises rabies vaccination before travel. <!-- fact_106 --> Pass the [Smartraveller Indonesia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia) advice on to guests with the save-the-date.

Smartraveller places Indonesia's wet season usually around October to April, when weather can change quickly and heavy rain can flood roads in Bali. <!-- fact_103 --> The exact start varies by year and by zone, and BMKG's climate station forecasts it each year. <!-- fact_01 --> <!-- fact_02 -->

### Is there a right or wrong month to marry in Bali?

No single month suits every wedding. Months outside the usual wet-season window suit outdoor plans, wet-season months call for a chapel or covered space, and official dates such as Nyepi shape guest travel. <!-- fact_103 --> <!-- fact_100 --> Choose by your ceremony setting, guest list and the 2027 calendar.
