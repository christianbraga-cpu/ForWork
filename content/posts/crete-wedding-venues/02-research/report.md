## Cross-region summary
- **Guest counts:** snippets mention small packages (up to 10 people) and a 50 seated estate; forum threads ask about groups of 22 to 30. Nothing ranking speaks to 60 to 150 guests.
- **Pricing shown:** Tripadvisor and related searches push "prices", "budget", "all inclusive". The brief bans prices, so these are not targeted.
- **Top concern:** which venue to view or book, sea views, beach and pool, and small family groups (Facebook threads in 4 of 5 cities).
- **Gap we can own:** an area-first guide (Elounda, Heraklion coast, Rethymno, Chania) that gives every venue the same fields and says how guests get there. It also covers legal steps from Australian Government sources. No ranking page does this.

## Cannibalisation
- No weddedwonderland.com page ranks for any "crete" query (ranked_keywords AU, 0 results). The site is absent from the top 10 in all 5 cities.
- /destinations/europe/greece ranks only for broad Greece terms (AU "greece wedding" pos 52, "weddings in greece" pos 56). This is the hub, not a conflict.
- /destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues (draft) targets "greece wedding venues". Keep its Crete section brief and link down to this post.
- The traditions page (/8-greek-wedding-traditions-you-didnt-know-about, pos 19 AU) and /how-to-plan-a-greek-wedding serve other intents.
