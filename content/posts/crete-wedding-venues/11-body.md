## How to compare Crete venues like for like

Every venue on this list hosts weddings or vow renewals. That is the starting line. From there, four questions separate them.

### Resort, private villa or estate

Most Crete venues are resorts that host weddings in named spaces: a chapel, a beach, a restaurant terrace, a ballroom. That suits a guest list that wants to stay in one place and treat the wedding as a holiday.

Sea View by En Kipo works the other way. It is a hilltop garden with no guest rooms, so you bring your own caterer and suppliers and house guests nearby. It gives you a blank canvas and a longer to-do list.

Exclusive use needs a careful read. At Nana Princess it means the chosen room or venue for a symbolic wedding, not the whole resort. At Sea View, the whole garden is yours for the day. Ask every venue which of the two it is quoting.

### Ceremony on site

Crete gives you real variety here: chapels at Elounda Beach, St. Nicolas Bay, Nana Princess, Amirandes, Out of the Blue and Avra Imperial, beach ceremonies at Nana Princess and Amirandes, and garden or lawn ceremonies at Out of the Blue and Sea View.

What matters is which ceremony each space hosts. A chapel in the grounds does not by itself mean a Greek Orthodox wedding, and a beach does not by itself mean a legal one. Where a venue states its ceremony types, each entry below gives them in the venue's terms.

### Seated capacity and rooms

Published capacity ranges from packages built for 10 to 30 guests at Euphoria to an events hall for up to 680 at Avra Imperial. Many venues publish no figure at all, which is not a reason to rule them out, only a reason to ask.

Rooms matter more than usual when most guests fly long-haul from Australia. Housing them where you marry takes a transfer out of every day of the trip. Some venues also set minimums: Abaton's wedding packages ask for at least 10 rooms for at least two nights, depending on guest numbers.

### Nearest airport and transfer

East Crete venues state their drives from Heraklion Airport. Minos Beach Art Hotel and Minos Palace both put it at about 50 minutes, and both put Chania Airport at about three hours. Closer to the city, Amirandes is 18 km from Heraklion International Airport.

For the west, Chania Airport is the natural arrival point. More on both airports below.

## Choosing an area: east or west Crete

Crete is Greece's largest island, and it is usually spoken of in three parts: east, centre and west. Each suits a different kind of wedding.

**The east (Lasithi).** Agios Nikolaos sits on Mirabello Bay, and Elounda is the resort coast with views of the lagoon and Spinalonga. This is resort country, with chapels, bay views and villas with private pools. Guests fly into Heraklion: Minos Beach and Minos Palace both state about 50 minutes by car.

**The centre (Heraklion).** Heraklion is the largest urban centre, the gateway to Knossos, with resorts along the northern coast. Venues here are the quickest from the airport. Amirandes sits 18 km from it, a short drive from the Palace of Knossos.

**Rethymno.** Grouped with Chania as part of western Crete, Rethymno has a medieval town and the Fortezza. Caramel sits on the north coast minutes from the old town.

**The west (Chania).** Chania has its Venetian harbour and old town. The venues here run from the 680-person hall at Avra Imperial to Euphoria's packages for up to 30, with a hilltop garden for 120 in between, which makes the west the place for both the largest and the most personal weddings on this list.

A simple rule: if most guests arrive through Heraklion, look east or central. If you want Chania's old town for the welcome drinks, look west and book flights into Chania. Read more about the island on [Visit Greece](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/islands/crete).

[Sea View by En Kipo](https://enkipo-seaview.com/venue/) is a wedding venue in Chania: a hilltop garden with panoramic sea and sunset views, for weddings, vow renewals and proposals.

- **Setting:** 4,200 square metres of open space, planted with seasonal blooms and Mediterranean greenery cared for by the venue's own florists.
- **Ceremony and capacity:** Up to 120 guests for the ceremony and 120 for the reception.

## Getting to Crete for your wedding

The airport your guests land at in Crete shapes the whole trip, so settle it before anyone books a flight.

Australians entering the Schengen area for a short stay now have fingerprints and a photo taken under the European Entry/Exit System. It is free and needs nothing done before arrival, but border crossings can be slow, so allow plenty of time for connections.

### Heraklion airport

For the east and central venues, Heraklion is the arrival point. The venues state their own drives: about 50 minutes to Minos Beach Art Hotel and Minos Palace, and 18 km to Amirandes. Minos Beach can help arrange car transfers.

### Chania airport

Chania Airport, Ioannis Daskalogiannis (CHQ), serves the west, with a direct public bus, taxis and 24-hour parking on site. See the [airport's travel information](https://www.chq-airport.gr/en/flights--more/airport-information). Sea View by En Kipo lists Chania Airport, Heraklion Airport and Souda Port as ways to reach it.

Booking guests into the wrong airport adds hours to their trip. Minos Beach and Minos Palace both put Chania Airport about three hours away.

### Transfers for guests

In peak season, traffic on the islands can be heavily congested. Use licensed taxis only, and arrange coach or taxi pick-ups in advance. For a guest list, that means one booked coach per flight wave rather than a taxi queue. See [Smartraveller Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) for current advice.

## When to marry in Crete

Greece has a Mediterranean climate, with plenty of sunshine, mild temperatures and little rain. In summer, the dry hot days are cooled by the seasonal meltemi winds.

For eastern Crete, Minos Palace describes the weather as pleasant from April to November, with early autumn around 20 to 27°C.

Heat sets the timing of the ceremony itself. High-summer temperatures can reach 35°C, and Nana Princess recommends evening ceremonies to match the month:

| Month | Recommended ceremony time |
|---|---|
| April, May and October | 4pm to 6pm |
| June and September | 6pm to 8pm |
| July and August | 6.30pm to 8pm |

Summer also brings two risks to plan around. Smartraveller notes that wildfires occur in Greece in the extended summer period from June to September, including near tourist areas on the islands, and can prompt evacuations, and that heatwaves are frequent over the summer months. Greece is also in an active seismic zone, and earthquakes have occurred near Crete.

None of this rules out a summer wedding. It means shade for the ceremony, an indoor fallback for every outdoor space, and travel insurance that every guest has read.

## Getting married legally in Crete

Crete venues offer three kinds of ceremony: civil, Greek Orthodox and symbolic. Symbolic weddings and vow renewals are not legally recognised in Greece, so if the legal marriage matters to you, it has to be a civil or religious ceremony with the paperwork behind it.

**Civil ceremony.** Expect paperwork on both sides of the date. Elounda Peninsula asks for documents by email at least four weeks before the wedding and by courier at least three weeks before. It states that the marriage must then be registered at the Registry Office in Aghios Nikolaos within 40 days, and its civil package includes a round trip to the Registry Office. Ask your venue whether you need to be there in person, and keep a weekday free in the itinerary until you know.

**Proving you are free to marry.** Some countries ask for a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) and certificates from a registry of births, deaths and marriages. The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT) can issue a CNI in Australia. Some local authorities only accept CNIs issued in their own country, so ask the Greek embassy or consulate three things: whether you need one, whether you can get it in Australia, and whether it needs an authentication or apostille. As at November 2026, the Australian Embassy in Athens witnesses CNI applications, issues CNIs and witnesses notices of intended marriage, by appointment, with CNIs ready for collection within two working days. See the [Embassy's notarial services](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html).

**Back home.** An overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. A foreign marriage certificate may not be accepted as proof of a name change, so check with your state registry. Read [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas).

**Greek Orthodox.** Elounda Peninsula has a Greek Orthodox package, and Nana Princess holds Orthodox weddings in its Chapel of St. George. For what the ceremony involves, read our piece on [Greek wedding traditions](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/greek-wedding-traditions).

The full legal steps for Greece are in [our guide to Greece wedding venues](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues). Requirements change, so check the current rules with the Embassy before you book flights.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which part of Crete is best for a wedding?

It depends on your guests. The east, around Elounda and Agios Nikolaos, has resort chapels and bay views and is about 50 minutes from Heraklion Airport. The west, around Chania, has the old town and the widest range, from a hall for 680 to packages for up to 30.

### Can we book a Crete wedding venue for exclusive use?

Sometimes. Sea View by En Kipo is yours alone from 10am until midnight. Nana Princess gives exclusive use of the chosen space for symbolic weddings. Most resorts do not publish a whole-hotel option, so ask each one in writing.

### Which airport should our guests fly into for a Crete wedding?

Heraklion for the east and centre, Chania for the west. Minos Beach and Minos Palace are about 50 minutes from Heraklion and about three hours from Chania.

### Which months and ceremony times suit a Crete wedding?

April to November, with a later ceremony as summer heats up. Minos Palace describes the weather in eastern Crete as pleasant from April to November. In April, May and October, Nana Princess recommends ceremonies from 4pm to 6pm; in July and August, 6.30pm to 8pm, because of the heat. Wildfires occur from June to September, and heatwaves are frequent over summer.

### Can Australians legally get married in Crete?

Yes. Venues such as Elounda Peninsula offer civil and Greek Orthodox packages, and the documents can include a Certificate of No Impediment. A marriage valid in Greece is in most cases recognised in Australia.

### Are there beach wedding venues in Crete?

Yes. Nana Princess holds civil weddings on its private beach, Amirandes holds ceremonies on its VIP Beach for up to 80 guests, and Euphoria offers informal weddings with a choice of beach settings.
