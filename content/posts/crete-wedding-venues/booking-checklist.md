# Booking checklist: Crete wedding venues

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers; each venue entry's "Not published" line and the source pack's booking checklist lines are listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- Minos Beach Art Hotel: the hotel's own celebrations page says it plans weddings, vow renewals and celebrations, but a Marriott events page for the hotel says it does not provide wedding services. Confirm in writing that the hotel hosts weddings, and which ceremony types, before quoting it.
- Abaton Island Resort & Spa: the wedding brochure covers 2025 and 2026. For a 2027 date, confirm the venue capacities and package terms (reception of at least 20 and up to 300 guests, at least 10 rooms for at least two nights) still apply.
- Caramel, Grecotel: confirm whether its informal weddings and exchange-of-vows packages are symbolic (non-legal) only, and whether a legal civil ceremony can be held on site.
- Porto Elounda Golf & Spa Resort: the resort planned work on the main veranda, lobby and reception for 2026. Confirm which works are complete and whether any affect the couple's date.
- Minos Palace Resort: adults only. Confirm the policy for any child guests at the wedding (nieces, nephews, godchildren, flower girls, page boys) before shortlisting.
- Sea View by En Kipo: confirm the exact village or locality near Chania (the venue says only a hilltop in Chania) and the ceremony type (symbolic or civil, and who officiates).

## General

- Curfew times and outdoor or amplified music rules at each venue on the couple's dates
- Minimum nights, room blocks, check-in and check-out times, and set-up and pack-down windows at each venue
- Rain or wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space
- Whether exclusive use covers the chosen event spaces or the whole hotel, and which one is being quoted
- Contract language and currency, and how the deposit is held
- Access for older guests and anyone with limited mobility at every venue: the number of steps between guest rooms, the ceremony and the reception, whether there are level or step-free paths and lifts, walking distances across the grounds, and buggy or private transfers (see each venue below)
- Airport: Heraklion for the east and centre, Chania for the west; private transfers and who meets guests and carries luggage
- Legal ceremony: documents the municipality needs (apostille, Greek translation), whether an Australian-issued CNI is accepted, notice period, whether a civil ceremony can be held at the venue, whether a chapel ceremony is legally binding for the couple, registration within 40 days, and fees
