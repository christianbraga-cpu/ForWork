Two Crete wedding packages can carry the same name and share almost nothing on paper. Most bundle a ceremony set-up in a chosen space, and several add a coordinator. Guest rooms, sole use of a venue, the reception, extra hours and outside suppliers are where they part ways, and every resort draws that line in its own place.

This guide sets out what Crete venue packages include, what sits outside them and how to lay two quotes side by side. For the settings themselves, start with [our guide to Crete wedding venues](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/crete-wedding-venues), and for timing, seasons and the steps in order, read [planning a wedding in Crete](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/wedding-in-crete).

## Key takeaways

- A Crete venue package usually covers the ceremony set-up in a chosen space, sometimes with a coordinator, and everything else depends on the venue.
- Guest rooms, minimum stays, the reception and outside suppliers are where Crete packages differ most, so ask about each one in writing.
- A venue package, a venue fee and a planner each cover different ground, so check who is responsible for every part of the day.
- Compare quotes on the same guest count, spaces, hours, nights and currency before you pay any deposit.

## What a Crete wedding package covers

A Crete wedding package is a venue's own bundle of ceremony services, sold around a date, a space and a set of inclusions.

Most Crete resort packages take one of four forms:

- **A named package for one ceremony type**, such as the civil, Orthodox and symbolic packages at Out of the Blue Resort & Spa in Aghia Pelaghia.
- **Named tiers that add inclusions as they climb**, such as Elxi, Pathos and Eros at Euphoria Resort in Kolymvari.
- **A core package with a menu of extras and a fee for each venue**, as at Abaton Island Resort & Spa.
- **A list of wedding services quoted on request**, with no named package, as at St. Nicolas Bay Resort Hotel & Villas in Agios Nikolaos.

The guest count shapes all of it. Abaton's packages run from a minimum reception of 20 guests to a maximum of 300, Grecotel Amirandes near Heraklion hosts weddings for up to 300 guests, and Avra Imperial in Kolymvari has an events hall it says holds up to 680 people. At the other end, Euphoria's first tier is built around up to 10 guests. For a larger celebration, Avra Imperial offers both its chapel of Saint Paul and that hall, and Nana Princess gives symbolic weddings sole use of the chosen room or venue.

Large beach resorts on Crete tend to tie a package to a stay, smaller ceremonies and vow renewals often have their own package, and a few venues build each wedding from a list.

### Resort packages with a minimum stay

The most common condition on a Crete package is a stay. Elounda SA Hotels & Resorts, the group behind Elounda Peninsula, Porto Elounda and Elounda Mare, makes every one of its packages valid only when the couple stays at least three nights in one of its hotels.

Abaton sets its condition across the guest list rather than the couple. Its packages are valid with a minimum booking of 10 rooms for at least two nights, depending on guest numbers, and they do not include the accommodation itself.

Euphoria links its honeymoon package to a stay: it is included for couples who book any of its wedding packages and stay at the hotel. Ask any resort whether its package depends on rooms, and whose rooms: yours, or your guests' as well.

### Symbolic and vow renewal packages

Several Crete resorts publish a package for a ceremony that is not a legal marriage. Elounda SA's Symbolic Ceremony / Renewal of Vows Wedding Package sits beside its Lavish Wedding Package for civil and Orthodox weddings.

Euphoria's three options, Elxi, Pathos and Eros, are written for an informal wedding or renewal of vows, with a certificate of informal marriage from the hotel. Nana Princess in Chersonissos offers symbolic weddings officiated by one of its event coordinators, and vow renewals alongside them.

### Elopement and small wedding packages

A small guest list fits neatly into the lower tiers. Euphoria's Elxi tier, covered below, is built for up to 10 guests on the beach or poolside.

Elounda SA's symbolic package is built for the couple: a bouquet and boutonniere, two witnesses if needed and the hotel's official representative, a bottle of sparkling wine, a single-tier cake, room decoration with flowers and petals, spa thermal suite use and breakfast in bed the next day.

For two people alone, check the witness arrangement first. Elounda SA provides two witnesses if needed, and every Euphoria ceremony has two witnesses and the hotel's official representative.

### Elounda SA Hotels & Resorts, Elounda

The [Elounda SA wedding packages](https://www.eloundapeninsula.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Elounda-Wedding-Packages-.pdf) cover civil, Greek Orthodox and symbolic ceremonies. The Lavish Wedding Package, for civil and Orthodox weddings, reserves the date and time, offers a choice of ceremony venue and includes a tasting of the chosen wedding menu for up to two people, with beverages excluded.

Before the day, it adds a room upgrade and early check-in on arrival, subject to availability, and use of the Six Senses Spa thalassotherapy pool and thermal suite for the couple. As the resort's own offer, it also includes documentation assistance and settlement of the wedding licence, in coordination with the couple and the local Municipality Office.

On the day, it covers two witnesses if needed and a ceremony set-up with seasonal flowers and a pre-recorded playlist.

Porto Elounda and Elounda Mare list the same range of wedding services on their own pages: ceremony venues including secluded beaches and historic sites, planning help, flowers with preferred designers, custom menus and beverages, table styling and cakes by the in-house Pastry Chef. Porto Elounda says its team will work with the couple or their bridal consultant.

### Nana Princess, Chersonissos

[Nana Princess](https://www.nanaprincess.gr/pages/weddings) presents three wedding packages, Deluxe Dream, Ultimate Passion and Essential Elegance, and will design a package to a couple's own requirements. Deluxe Dream keeps an event coordinator with the couple throughout the stay.

Ultimate Passion adds a pre-wedding menu tasting dinner and a massage the day before, and Essential Elegance a private brunch for the couple and guests and a farewell dinner.

Symbolic weddings there include sole use of the chosen room or venue, decorated with fresh flowers. For receptions, the resort offers the Eternal Blue terrace, the poolside Meat-In-Point restaurant for up to 120 guests, or the Conference Hall for a fully covered celebration, with a served dinner or a buffet.

As its own offer, Nana Princess assigns a dedicated event coordinator once the date is booked, to guide couples through the local registry office requirements and other formalities.

### Abaton Island Resort & Spa

[Abaton](https://www.abaton.gr/meet/weddings) invites couples to choose one of its wedding packages or create their own, from the ceremony, music, flowers and decor to beauty, a cocktail bar, a gala dinner, photography and video. Its 2025 to 2026 brochure package, The Basics, lists the ceremony, coordination services, photography, videography, Chiavari chairs, chair covers, table decoration, bridal party flowers and a wedding arch, with a wedding coordinator preparing the details.

Around The Basics, the brochure lists additional services from hair and make-up to a DJ and fireworks, and a venue fee for each of its spaces, with the beach set-up fee to be confirmed. Food and drink, from gala dinners to cocktail receptions and open bars, are booked separately from the ceremony basics.

### Euphoria Resort, Kolymvari, Chania

[Euphoria Resort](https://www.euphoriaresort.gr/weddings/) stands on the seashore at Kolymvari, west of Chania. Its first tier, Elxi, is a beach or poolside ceremony set-up for up to 10 guests.

Pathos raises the guest count to 20 and adds the couple's own event coordinator.

Eros takes it to 30 guests, with a jazz musician at the ceremony and a beach dinner with live music.

### Out of the Blue, Minoa Palace and the Chania resorts

Out of the Blue Resort & Spa publishes three packages, Civil, Orthodox and Symbolic, in its [wedding packages brochure](https://www.outoftheblueresort.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Wedding_Packages_2024-Out-Of-The-Blue-Resort-Spa-new.pdf). The Civil package covers the couple's flowers, a wedding cake and koufeta (sugared almonds).

As the resort's own offer, the Civil package also includes documentation assistance, the marriage licence, the Heraklion Mayor or Vice-Mayor and registry office fees. The Orthodox package books the resort's private chapel of St. Athanasios, with floral decoration, a ceremony table with an olive-branch wreath, Mavrodafni red wine during the ceremony, koufeta and a wedding cake, and it includes the Orthodox priest and assistant.

Minoa Palace Resort in Platanias, near Chania, lists a [wedding package](https://www.minoapalace.gr/wedding-events) that takes in both the ceremony and the reception, indoors or out, with customised menus, flowers, bridal hair and make-up, and a DJ or live band. A decorated wedding cake and a Newlyweds Retreat after the wedding are included too.

Avra Imperial Hotel in Kolymvari holds ceremonies in its private chapel of Saint Paul, with flower and candle decoration by its own team. For the reception, it offers the Maistro events hall, which the hotel says holds up to 680 people, or a private space by its glass pool for a small celebration, with menus tailored with the Executive Chef.

### St. Nicolas Bay and Grecotel Amirandes

St. Nicolas Bay Resort Hotel & Villas in Agios Nikolaos holds ceremonies on the deck of its beachside chapel, with a guest service team for couples' requests. It publishes no named package, and asks couples to request a detailed quotation for the ceremony and optional amenities.

Its wedding amenities are listed as included: a massage and aromatherapy for two in a spa couples suite, room service breakfast and Champagne throughout the stay, and a four-course candlelit dinner, with beverages excluded.

Grecotel Amirandes, near Heraklion, hosts weddings for up to 300 guests, with vows at its VIP Beach or chapel, a choice of dining venues and dedicated wedding specialists. It publishes no named wedding package.

## Are Crete wedding packages all inclusive?

Rarely, in the sense most couples mean. A package includes what its list names, and the reception is the part most often quoted on its own.

The phrase "all inclusive" on a Crete resort usually describes the holiday, not the wedding. Euphoria describes itself as an Ultra All Inclusive family beach resort. Yet its wedding options are complete packages, and its buffet, seated and cocktail receptions, beverage packages, open bars, custom cakes and entertainment are quoted separately on request. At Grecotel Amirandes, "All Inclusive" appears as a hotel meal plan, not a wedding package.

Elsewhere, the reception sits plainly outside the ceremony package:

- Elounda SA lists reception food, beverage packages, open bars, dinner menus and entertainment in separate sections, outside its ceremony packages.
- Out of the Blue offers the wedding cocktail or reception at special rates, as an optional service at extra charge.
- Abaton books gala dinners, cocktail receptions, beverage packages and open bars separately from The Basics.

So treat a Crete package as the core of the day, and ask for every reception item as its own line. ## What's left out or charged on top

A package list is only half of the picture. The other half is what the venue leaves to you, charges separately or makes a condition of the booking. Ask each of the questions below, and ask for the answers in writing.

### Legal and church fees

Does the package price include the official fees, or are they extra? At Elounda SA, the package rate does not include the Municipal fee for a civil wedding or the Metropolis fee, the church fee for an Orthodox wedding; both are charged on top and are subject to change.

Out of the Blue settles registry office fees inside its Civil and Orthodox packages, so the same question has two different answers on one island.

Ask, too, which documents you must supply, by when, and in what form. Elounda SA asks for documents with an apostille and Greek translation, sent for approval at least four weeks before the wedding, and says the marriage must be registered at the Registry Office in Aghios Nikolaos within 40 days.

### Accommodation and minimum nights

Do you have to book rooms to secure the package, and for how many nights? Elounda SA requires the couple to stay at least three nights in one of the group's hotels. Abaton requires a minimum of 10 rooms for at least two nights, depending on guest numbers, and its packages do not include accommodation.

Ask whether guests book and pay the venue directly, whether a room block is held for you and until when, and what happens to unbooked rooms.

### Reception food, drinks and entertainment

Is the reception inside the package, a separate menu, or a separate quote? At most Crete resorts with published packages, it is separate. Elounda SA's menu tasting, for example, excludes beverages.

Ask which menus apply, whether there are minimum numbers, how drinks are charged and when the bar closes. Abaton publishes minimum numbers for some of its menus.

### Hair, make-up and extras

Which personal services sit inside the package? Minoa Palace includes bridal hair, make-up and manicure in its wedding package. Euphoria's Pathos includes a manicure and pedicure for the bride. Abaton lists hair, make-up and spa among its additional services.

Out of the Blue states that arch or gazebo construction is not included in any of its packages, and that a set-up or private hire fee may apply to its civil and Orthodox packages depending on the venue chosen. Abaton charges a venue fee for each of its spaces. Ask whether the space you want carries a fee, and whether the arch in the photographs is part of your package.

Finally, ask about swaps. Euphoria states that its packages are complete, its items are not interchangeable, and unused services carry no refund or alternative, though packages can be customised at extra cost. If a package item does not suit you, know before you sign whether you can trade it.

Most Crete packages are built around one kind of ceremony, so settle the type before you compare anything else. The three you will meet are civil, Greek Orthodox and symbolic.

**Civil.** A civil wedding is a legal marriage before the local authority. Nana Princess holds civil weddings on its private sandy beach.

**Greek Orthodox.** Several resorts keep their own chapel. Nana Princess holds Orthodox weddings in its Chapel of St George, Out of the Blue in its chapel of St. Athanasios, and Avra Imperial uses its chapel of Saint Paul for ceremonies. Elounda SA notes that the Metropolis fee, the church fee for an Orthodox wedding, sits outside its package rate. The customs around a Greek wedding, from koufeta to the ceremony itself, are covered in our piece on [Greek wedding traditions](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/greek-wedding-traditions).

**Symbolic.** A symbolic ceremony carries the words and the setting without the legal step. In Greece, a symbolic wedding or vow renewal has no legal standing, as the Elounda SA brochure makes clear. Euphoria's certificate is for an informal ceremony.

Whichever you choose, confirm with the venue that the package price matches the ceremony type, and what changes if you switch.

## Venue package, venue fee or a planner

Every Crete wedding has the same three moving parts: the venue, the services around it and someone to coordinate both. A package bundles some of each. A venue fee buys the space. A planner brings the rest together.

Crete venues lean in different directions. Euphoria and Elounda SA publish named packages, Abaton pairs a core package with venue fees, and St. Nicolas Bay quotes each wedding on request, arranging photography, music and flowers by its own florist as you ask.

Coordination varies too. Nana Princess puts an event coordinator with the couple throughout the stay on Deluxe Dream, Euphoria gives the couple their own event coordinator on its two upper tiers, and Porto Elounda's team will work alongside a couple's own bridal consultant.

The trade-offs are practical:

- **A venue package** is simple to book and quick to compare, but its inclusions are fixed and its suppliers are usually the venue's own.
- **A venue fee** gives you the space with room to shape everything else, but each part of the day then needs its own quote.
- **A planner** connects the venue, suppliers and guests into one plan and lets you shape the day beyond any one package, though it is one more agreement to read closely.

Whichever route you take, write down who is responsible for each part of the day. ## East or west Crete for a package wedding

The package you choose also chooses your corner of the island. Among resorts with published wedding offers, two clusters stand out.

In the east, Elounda SA's three hotels share one wedding brochure, and St. Nicolas Bay sits at Agios Nikolaos. Both sit in Lasithi, in eastern Crete, and Elounda Peninsula calls itself a gateway to the east of the island. Couples marrying legally through Elounda SA register at the Registry Office in Aghios Nikolaos.

In the west, Euphoria and Avra Imperial both stand at Kolymvari, west of Chania, and Minoa Palace is at Platanias, also near Chania. Euphoria and Minoa Palace belong to the same group, EM Resorts.

Between them, around Heraklion, sit Out of the Blue at Aghia Pelaghia, Nana Princess at Chersonissos and Grecotel Amirandes.

Think about where your guests will fly in, how long they will stay and whether one resort can hold everyone, then compare the packages within that corner.

Two Crete quotes rarely line up on first reading: one gives you a ceremony set-up, cake and coordinator, another a space, a venue fee and a list of extras. Put both on the same footing before you judge either.

Use this checklist for every quote:

- **Inclusions in writing.** Ask for a full list of what the package or fee covers, item by item, from set-up and flowers to cake, music and photographs.
- **Ceremony type.** Confirm whether the quote is for a civil, Orthodox or symbolic ceremony, and which official fees sit outside it.
- **Guest count basis.** Check the guest number the quote is built on, and what changes as it grows.
- **Deposits and cancellation.** Ask what the deposit secures, whether it is refundable, when the balance is due and what happens if you postpone.
- **Exclusive use, and for how many days.** Is it one beach or terrace for set hours, a whole venue for the evening, or more across your stay?
- **Rooms and nights.** Are rooms required, how many, and for how many nights?
- **Music times.** When does music start, when must it stop, and is live music allowed outdoors?
- **Ceremony time and weather plan.** Nana Princess recommends evening ceremonies, because high-summer temperatures can reach 35 degrees C, with ceremonies from 6.30pm to 8pm in July and August. Ask what the covered or indoor alternative is if the wind rises, and whether it is held for you.
- **Level access.** Can older guests reach the ceremony and reception without steps or sand, and is there an accessible bathroom nearby?
- **Supplier rules.** Which outside suppliers are allowed, and from what time can they set up?
- **Quote currency.** What currency is the quote in, and will the deposit and the balance be invoiced in the same one?
- **Contract language.** Is the contract in English, and which version applies if there is a Greek one too? ## The legal side in brief

As at November 2026, Smartraveller says some countries ask you to prove there is no impediment to your marriage, most commonly with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) and certificates from a registry of births, deaths and marriages.

The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only CNIs issued in their own country, so ask whether yours needs an apostille too. The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) issues CNIs, witnesses notices of intended marriage and legalises documents by authentication or apostille, by appointment. It gives no legal advice, and a fee applies.

Back home, an overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. A foreign marriage certificate is usually accepted as proof of marriage but may not work as proof of a name change. [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) has the detail.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Crete wedding packages all inclusive?

Rarely. A package covers what its list names. Euphoria quotes its receptions, beverage packages and open bars separately, and Elounda SA lists reception food, drinks and entertainment outside its ceremony packages. Treat a package as the core of the day and ask for every extra in writing.

### What is included in a Crete wedding package?

It depends on the venue. Abaton's The Basics covers the ceremony, coordination, photography, videography, bridal party flowers and a wedding arch, while Elounda SA's Lavish package includes a ceremony set-up, flowers and a menu tasting.

### Do Crete wedding packages include accommodation?

Usually not, and some require it. Abaton's packages exclude accommodation and need a minimum room booking, and Elounda SA's packages need a stay of at least three nights. At Euphoria, a honeymoon package is included for couples who book a wedding package and stay at the hotel.

### Is there a Crete elopement package for just the two of us?

Yes, at several resorts. Elounda SA's symbolic package is built for the couple, with witnesses if needed, sparkling wine, a single-tier cake and breakfast in bed the next day. Euphoria's Elxi option suits a couple with a handful of guests, up to 10.

### Does a venue package include the legal paperwork?

Some include the official fees and some do not. At Elounda SA, the Municipal and Metropolis fees are charged on top of the package rate. Ask each venue what is covered and which fees sit outside its price.

### Is a symbolic ceremony in Crete legally recognised?

No. Elounda SA's brochure is clear that a symbolic wedding or vow renewal is not a legal marriage in Greece. Couples who want a legal marriage can marry at home first and hold the symbolic ceremony in Crete.
