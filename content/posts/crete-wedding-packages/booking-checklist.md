# Booking checklist: Crete wedding packages

These details are confirmed in writing with each venue, for the couple's dates, before anyone commits. They don't block publishing: the article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers and prints no prices. The only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (6 slots).

## Confirm before quoting

- **Published prices not used.** Some venues publish prices or money terms in their brochures (for example Abaton and Elounda SA). None are in the article. Any figure added later needs Nic's sign-off and is shown "From AUD".
- **Currency of every quote.** Confirm the quote currency, whether deposit and balance are invoiced in the same currency, card or bank fees, who carries exchange-rate movement, and how the Wedded Advisor's AUD quote converts.
- **Dated sources.** Out of the Blue's wedding packages brochure is the 2024 edition, and Minoa Palace's wedding page dates from 2019. Ask both for their current packages and terms before quoting.

## Ask every venue, in writing

- Inclusions: the full list, item by item (date, ceremony set-up, coordinator, flowers, cake, toast, music, room touches, documents help)
- What is charged on top or quoted separately: reception food and drink, open bar, venue or space fees, set-up or private hire fees, arch or gazebo, extra hours, extra guests, officiant, outside suppliers
- Minimum rooms or nights: how many, for how long, at which hotel, and whether guests pay the venue directly
- Required planner: whether an outside wedding planner is required or allowed, and from when
- Deposit: amount, when due, what it secures, and whether it is refundable
- Cancellation and postponement terms, including heat, wind, wildfire or other alerts that move the date
- Payment timings: balance and food and beverage due dates
- Music limits: when music drops and stops, bar closing time, DJ or live band allowed outdoors
- Exclusive use: one space, the wedding spaces or the whole property, from what time, for how many hours and how many days
- Legal status of any certificate the venue issues (civil, religious, symbolic or informal)
- Contract language (English, and which version applies if there is a Greek one), and quote and payment currency
- Level access: a route without steps or sand from arrival to ceremony, dinner, rooms and an accessible bathroom
- Insurance: the venue's public liability cover, and what the couple's travel or event insurance must cover (including deposits)
- Weather plan: covered or indoor alternative, held for you or offered on the day

## Civil marriage: municipality and Australian Embassy in Athens

- With the municipality (for example Heraklion, or Aghios Nikolaos for Elounda): the steps in order, documents required, apostilles and official Greek translations, witnesses, lead times, registration deadline, and the current Municipal fee (and Metropolis fee for an Orthodox wedding)
- With the Australian Embassy in Athens: Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage, notice of intended marriage witnessing and apostilles or authentication, by appointment; current fees and processing times
- Whether the municipality accepts a CNI issued in Australia by DFAT or only one issued in Greece
- Confirm the marriage is valid in Greece and would be valid under Australian law; it cannot be registered in Australia, and the foreign certificate may not prove a name change
- Check Smartraveller's advice for Greece at booking time
