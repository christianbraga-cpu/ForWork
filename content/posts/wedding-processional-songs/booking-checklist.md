# Processional music booking checklist

Use this when you book ceremony musicians or plan recorded music. Get every answer in writing.

## Choose the songs
- [ ] Song for the wedding party (parents, grandparents, attendants)
- [ ] Song for the couple's entrance, clearly different so guests know to stand
- [ ] Separate entrance song for a groom or partner, if they walk in on their own
- [ ] Shortlist sent to the musicians or sound person, with the exact version you want

## Timing the walk
- [ ] Order of the processional confirmed with your officiant
- [ ] Walk timed at the rehearsal for the length of your aisle and the number of people walking
- [ ] Musicians know where to repeat a section and when to move to the ending
- [ ] For a recording, someone knows where to fade at a natural phrase

## Live or recorded
- [ ] Live, recorded, or live for the ceremony and recorded for the reception
- [ ] Can the musicians learn your pieces from sheet music or a recording?

## Questions for musicians and the venue
- [ ] What does the quote include: rehearsal, travel, set-up time and how long they play?
- [ ] What deposit is due, and when?
- [ ] What are the cancellation terms?
- [ ] Are the quote, deposit and cancellation terms all confirmed in writing?
- [ ] Who supplies the sound system at the ceremony?
- [ ] Who runs the sound and presses play on the day?
- [ ] Who on the venue side is responsible for the music on the day?

## Back-up plan
- [ ] What happens if a musician can't play?
- [ ] What happens if the power drops or the playback fails?
- [ ] Back-up copy of every recorded track on a second device
- [ ] If the ceremony moves indoors for weather, where do the musicians set up?

## Outdoor ceremonies
- [ ] Will acoustic instruments carry, or is amplification needed?
- [ ] Is there power at the ceremony spot for a keyboard, speakers or a microphone?
- [ ] Is a generator or battery system available, and who manages it?
- [ ] Is there a step-free route for older guests?

## Music permissions
- [ ] Does the venue have the permissions it needs for live or recorded music at your ceremony?
- [ ] Are there sound limits or curfews?

## Church ceremonies
- [ ] Check the church's music rules with your officiant or music director, including whether secular songs are allowed.
