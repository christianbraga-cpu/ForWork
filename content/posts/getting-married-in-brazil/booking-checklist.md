# Booking checklist: getting married in Brazil

Civil marriage checklist for foreign couples. Questions only: confirm every requirement with the cartório, your venue and the official sources, as at the time you book.

## 1. Start with the cartório

- [ ] Which Cartório de Registro Civil takes our application?
- [ ] If neither of us lives in Brazil, can we apply for the habilitação, and how?
- [ ] Can any step be started before we arrive, and how long should we allow in Brazil?

## 2. Documents, apostille and sworn translation

- [ ] Which documents does the cartório require from each of us, and how recent must they be?
- [ ] Which of our documents need an apostille in our home country before we travel?
- [ ] Which documents need a sworn Portuguese translation in Brazil, and which sworn translator do you recommend?
- [ ] How long does the sworn translation take per document?
- [ ] Do we need an interpreter at the cartório or at the ceremony if we do not speak Portuguese?

## 3. For Australians

- [ ] Does the cartório require a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI), or will a state or territory single status certificate do instead?
- [ ] Must the CNI be issued in Brazil by the Australian Embassy in Brasília or the Consulate-General in São Paulo, or obtained from DFAT in Australia and apostilled?
- [ ] Have we booked a notarial services appointment if we need one?
- [ ] Which Australian documents need an apostille from DFAT before we travel?

## 4. Habilitação and banns

- [ ] What does the habilitação process involve, and how long does it take?
- [ ] How and where are the banns (public notice) published, and for how long?
- [ ] When is the certificate of habilitação issued, and how long is it valid?

## 5. Ceremony day, time and place

- [ ] Which day, time and place can we request, and does the registrar confirm it?
- [ ] Will the presiding officer come to our venue, a beach or an outdoor location?
- [ ] Who gives permission to use that location?

## 6. Witnesses

- [ ] Do we need two witnesses, and what does the cartório require of them (age, identity documents, nationality)?
- [ ] Can our own guests act as witnesses?

## 7. Property regime

- [ ] Which property regime applies by default, and do we need to choose one before the habilitação?
- [ ] Do we need a prenuptial agreement made by public deed for any other regime?

## 8. Marriage certificate

- [ ] When and how do we receive the marriage certificate?
- [ ] How many copies should we order?

## 9. Religious ceremony with civil effect

- [ ] If we have a religious ceremony, have we obtained the certificate of habilitação first?
- [ ] Who registers the religious marriage within 30 days of the ceremony, and at which registry office?

## 10. Apostille for Australia

- [ ] Does our Brazilian marriage certificate need an apostille for use in Australia, and where is it issued?
- [ ] Does the Australian organisation receiving the certificate want a translation as well?

## 11. Venue questions (any couple)

- [ ] What deposit is required, when is it due, and is any of it refundable?
- [ ] What are the cancellation and date-change terms, in writing?
- [ ] In which currency are payments made?
- [ ] Can we take the whole property, and how are other guests handled?
- [ ] How do guests reach the venue, including those with limited mobility?
- [ ] What safe transfer options do you recommend for guests from the airport and between venues, and after dark?
- [ ] What security is on site during the wedding and overnight?
- [ ] How are drones and outside photographers handled?
- [ ] What is the covered plan if it rains?
