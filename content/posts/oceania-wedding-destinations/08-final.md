Oceania wedding destinations run from the islands of Melanesia and Polynesia to New Zealand, Hawaii and Australia's own reef islands. As at November 2026, the South Pacific islands share a cyclone season that Smartraveller dates for each country, while Hawaii has its own wetter winter and New Zealand sits outside the tropics. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_112 --> <!-- fact_007 -->

What separates them for a wedding is the calendar, the paperwork and how easily your guests get there. The ten below are unranked and grouped by region, each with its weather months, the legal position and questions for a venue. For venues across the region, start with our [Pacific and Australian destination weddings](/destinations/oceania).

## Key takeaways

- Ten places, unranked, in four groups: Melanesia (Fiji, Vanuatu, New Caledonia), Polynesia (Tahiti and French Polynesia, the Cook Islands, Samoa, Tonga, Hawaii), New Zealand and the Whitsundays.
- As at November 2026, Smartraveller dates Fiji's cyclone season from November to April, so a covered ceremony space and a buffer day belong in the plan for much of the tropical South Pacific. <!-- fact_101 -->
- The legal route differs by country, from a Ministry of Justice licence in the Cook Islands or a state licence in Hawaii to a symbolic ceremony in Fiji with the legal marriage at home. <!-- fact_136 --> <!-- fact_131 -->
- For season and legal route side by side, jump to [the at-a-glance table](#oceania-wedding-destinations-at-a-glance).

### Fiji

**The trade-off.** Flooding is common in downtown Nadi and on the Coral Coast, and it can cut road access to the airport, so plan guest arrivals with a day to spare. <!-- fact_102 -->

**When to go.** The [WMO page for Nadi](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=94), with 1961 to 1990 normals from the Fiji Meteorological Service, shows January to March as the wettest months, with March at 324.0 mm, and July the driest at 46.0 mm. <!-- fact_001 -->

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller Fiji](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/fiji) gives November to April as cyclone season, though cyclones can come at other times. It also reports a National State of Disaster over worsening El Niño conditions, with possible water rationing, so ask the venue how rationing would affect guests. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_103 -->

**Getting married there.** Check with Fiji's authorities, or plan a symbolic ceremony in Fiji and marry legally at home.

**Questions for the venue.**
- Can the covered ceremony space, the cyclone postponement terms and what happens to our deposits if a cyclone moves the date be written into our contract?
- If flooding cuts the airport road, how do guests reach the property?
- Can the whole property be closed to outside guests for the wedding days?

### Vanuatu

**The trade-off.** Vanuatu is a predominantly Christian country where standards of dress and behaviour are modest, and supermarkets cannot sell alcohol from midday Saturday to 7am Monday, though hotels, bars and clubs can. <!-- fact_107 -->

**When to go.** The [WMO page for Port Vila](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=673) carries 1961 to 1990 normals from the Vanuatu Meteorology and Geo-hazards Department. March brings 330.4 mm over 21 rain days, and September 87.2 mm over 11. <!-- fact_004 -->

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller Vanuatu](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/vanuatu) dates the cyclone season from November to April, with cyclones and tropical storms possible outside it. <!-- fact_105 -->

As at November 2026, Smartraveller's latest update adds that local authorities have barred aircraft with more than 30 passenger seats from Santo International Airport until its rescue and firefighting services are fully operational. <!-- fact_106 -->

**Getting married there.** Check with the destination's authorities, or plan a symbolic ceremony.

**Questions for the venue.**
- If guests need to reach Santo, which smaller aircraft can carry them?
- Can alcohol for a Saturday or Sunday reception be bought and stored before the weekend rule applies?

### New Caledonia

**The trade-off.** As at November 2026, [Smartraveller New Caledonia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/new-caledonia) sets the overall level at "Exercise a high degree of caution" and advises: "Exercise a high degree of caution in New Caledonia due to the risk of civil disorder and violence." <!-- fact_108 -->

Its latest update reads: "There's a high police and security presence in New Caledonia. Isolated security incidents may occur across the territory. Monitor local media and follow the advice of local authorities." <!-- fact_108 -->

Curfews and alcohol bans can come at short notice, flights may be suspended with little or no warning, and demonstrations may increase around 13 May, 24 September and elections. <!-- fact_109 -->

**When to go.** The [WMO page for Noumea](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=296), with 1961 to 1990 normals from Météo-France's regional service, records 134.6 mm in March, well under Nadi's 324.0 mm or Port Vila's 330.4 mm, and a July mean maximum of 22.6°C with 17.2°C nights, so plan wraps for an evening reception. <!-- fact_005 --> <!-- fact_001 --> <!-- fact_004 -->

As at November 2026, Smartraveller gives cyclone season as November to May, with cyclones and severe weather possible at other times. <!-- fact_110 -->

**Getting married there.** New Caledonia is an overseas territory of France, where a mix of French and local laws apply. <!-- fact_111 --> Check with the destination's authorities, or plan a symbolic ceremony.

**Questions for the venue.**
- If a curfew or an alcohol ban is announced in our wedding week, how does the reception run?
- Should flights be suspended before the wedding, how are the date and our deposits handled?

### Tahiti and French Polynesia

**Why it suits a wedding.** Lagoons and volcanic peaks for a wedding that runs into a honeymoon. The trade-off is paperwork in French, and no patrolled beaches. <!-- fact_113 --> <!-- fact_142 -->

**When to go.** The [WMO page for Tahiti](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=297), with 1961 to 1990 normals from Météo-France, records 317.0 mm in December and 315.2 mm in January. August and September fall to 48.0 mm and 46.3 mm. <!-- fact_002 -->

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller French Polynesia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/french-polynesia) gives cyclone season as November to April, adding that tropical storms and cyclones occur at any time of year. <!-- fact_112 -->

**Getting married there.** In this French overseas territory, the town hall (mairie) of the commune advises on requirements and documents and confirms eligibility, as the [Australian Consulate-General in Papeete](https://papeete.consulate.gov.au/fppafrancais/mariage.html) explains. <!-- fact_113 --> <!-- fact_142 -->

Expect a request for a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) or Certificat de Célibat and a Certificat de Coutume, and possibly apostilles on Australian documents. The Consulate-General does not issue CNIs, but once you hold one it can provide a letter that meets the Certificat de Coutume criteria. <!-- fact_142 --> DFAT can issue a CNI in Australia, so check with the destination's authorities, starting with the mairie, whether it accepts one. <!-- fact_123 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Which commune is the venue in, and has it hosted a legal ceremony with that mairie before?
- If a cyclone alert is raised in our wedding week, where does the ceremony move?

### The Cook Islands

**Why it suits a wedding.** A wedding with a legal route the Ministry of Justice sets out step by step. The trade-off is modest dress outside resort areas, and no lifeguards on the beaches. <!-- fact_115 -->

**When to go.** As at November 2026, [Smartraveller Cook Islands](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/cook-islands) gives cyclone season as November to April and notes that severe storms can happen any time. Know your accommodation's evacuation plan and nearest shelter. <!-- fact_114 -->

**Getting married there.** As at November 2026, the [Cook Islands Ministry of Justice](https://www.justice.gov.ck/marriage-license/) page says a legal marriage requires a licence. You both give notice in person at the Ministry by statutory declaration, with copies of your birth certificates and passports, plus divorce decrees or death certificates where relevant. <!-- fact_136 --> <!-- fact_138 -->

The Registrar of Marriages issues the licence three days after notice, unless a waiver is granted, and it expires after three months. The licence names the place of marriage, and the ceremony must happen there. <!-- fact_139 -->

A minister or celebrant registered with the Ministry marries you, or the Registrar can at the Ministry. <!-- fact_137 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Which exact site goes on the licence, and can a covered space be named as well?
- If a storm forces an evacuation, what is the plan for the wedding and guests' rooms?

### Samoa

**The trade-off.** A wedding shaped by Sunday, when activities are extremely limited, particularly in rural areas, and supermarkets cannot sell alcohol. <!-- fact_117 -->

**When to go.** The [WMO page for Apia](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=1230), with Samoa Meteorology Division normals for 1971 to 2000, shows 489.0 mm in January. Even its lowest listed months, June to August, record 113.2 to 120.8 mm, so a rain plan belongs in every month. <!-- fact_003 -->

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller Samoa](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/samoa) gives cyclone season as November to April, with storms year-round, and reports a dengue outbreak ongoing since 2025. <!-- fact_116 -->

**Getting married there.** The [Samoa Bureau of Statistics](https://www.sbs.gov.ws/faq-bdm/) registers marriages from a Marriage Register submitted by a licensed marriage officer (a minister, registrar or appointed celebrant), within 14 days of the ceremony. <!-- fact_141 --> For notice, licence and documents, check with the destination's authorities.

For the feel of the day, read about [a Samoan wedding celebration](/a-traditional-samoan-wedding-celebration). <!-- LINK: /expert-advice/samoan-wedding-traditions when live -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- What are your Sunday rules for music, alcohol service and guest transfers?
- Which licensed marriage officer do couples use here, and who submits the register?

### Tonga

**The trade-off.** A quiet kingdom that keeps the Sabbath. Sabbath laws strictly limit Sunday work, sport, loud music and fishing, many businesses close, apart from some resorts and restaurants, and tours and airports do not operate. <!-- fact_119 -->

**When to go.** As at November 2026, [Smartraveller Tonga](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/tonga) says Tonga is prone to cyclones between November and April, with severe storms and flash flooding possible year-round. The Tonga Meteorological Service has forecast more tropical cyclones than normal this coming season, some of which may be severe. <!-- fact_118 -->

**Getting married there.** Check with the destination's authorities, or plan a symbolic ceremony.

**Questions for the venue.**
- When must a Saturday reception wind down so the music stops before Sunday?
- With no flights on a Sunday, which day do guests leave, and do the inter-island ferries suit that day?

### Hawaii

**Why it suits a wedding.** A US state north of the equator, with clear licence rules and no residence or US citizenship requirement. The trade-off is distance. <!-- fact_134 -->

**When to go.** Across much of Hawaii there are two seasons, in the [National Weather Service Honolulu climate summary](https://www.weather.gov/hfo/climate_summary): summer from May to October, and a cloudier, stormier winter from October to April. <!-- fact_007 --> Windward slopes catch the cloud, while the leeward side, where the air sinks, stays sunnier and drier. <!-- fact_008 -->

**Getting married there.** As at November 2026, the [Hawaii State Department of Health](https://health.hawaii.gov/vitalrecords/marriage-licenses/) requires a licence from an authorised agent, whom you meet together in person no sooner than 30 days before the ceremony. <!-- fact_131 --> <!-- fact_132 --> There is no waiting period, the ceremony must follow within 30 days, and the officiant must hold a state licence. <!-- fact_133 --> <!-- fact_135 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Does the ceremony lawn sit on the windward or leeward coast, and what is the indoor fallback between October and April?
- Which state-licensed officiants have married couples at the venue?

### New Zealand

**Why it suits a wedding.** Visitors can marry legally and apply for the licence online before they fly. <!-- fact_126 --> <!-- fact_128 --> The trade-off is a licence that ties the ceremony to the places you name on the application. <!-- fact_130 -->

**When to go.** For November 2025 only, [Earth Sciences New Zealand's summary of that month](https://niwa.co.nz/climate-and-weather/monthly/climate-summary-november-2025) records a nationwide 15.9°C, a new November record and 2.2°C warmer than the 1991 to 2020 normal. Low pressure carrying Coral Sea moisture soaked the upper and western North Island more than once. <!-- fact_009 --> <!-- fact_010 -->

**Getting married there.** As at November 2026, [New Zealand's marriage licence rules](https://www.govt.nz/browse/family-and-whanau/getting-married/get-a-marriage-licence/) ask you to arrange the licence at least 3 working days before the wedding, and it expires after 3 months. <!-- fact_125 --> <!-- fact_127 -->

On arrival you sign a declaration at a registry office, so list a covered back-up venue on the application before you fly. <!-- fact_129 --> <!-- fact_130 -->

**Questions for the venue.**
- Which covered space should we list on the licence as our back-up?
- How late on the day can the ceremony move indoors, and who makes the call?

### Cyclone season cover and a buffer day

As at November 2026, Smartraveller dates cyclone season from November to April for the South Pacific islands here, and to May in New Caledonia. For a wedding in those months, write the weather into the contract: the covered space for the full guest list, who calls the move and by what hour, and the postponement terms if a cyclone suspends flights. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_110 -->

Give guests a buffer day either side of the wedding. During and after a cyclone, flights can be delayed or suspended, and the seats left fill quickly. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_105 -->

### Legal or symbolic ceremony

Our country guide to [where you can legally marry overseas](/destination-weddings/weddings-abroad-where-can-you-legally-get-married) is the place to begin, and our note on a [legal or symbolic ceremony](/expert-advice/symbolic-ceremony) helps you choose between them. This is general information as at November 2026, not legal advice.

An overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but it is [generally recognised here](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married) if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid here. <!-- fact_120 --> <!-- fact_124 -->

Each country sets its own document list, which may include a CNI. As at November 2026, [Smartraveller's advice for getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) is to ask that country's embassy or consulate. <!-- fact_122 -->

## Frequently asked questions

### Which places in Oceania suit a wedding abroad?

Fiji, Vanuatu, New Caledonia, Tahiti and French Polynesia, the Cook Islands, Samoa, Tonga, Hawaii, New Zealand and the Whitsundays, each with its own season and legal route.

### When is cyclone season in the South Pacific?

As at November 2026, Smartraveller dates it from November to April for Fiji, Vanuatu, French Polynesia, the Cook Islands, Samoa and Tonga, and November to May for New Caledonia, with storms possible outside those months. <!-- fact_101 --> <!-- fact_105 --> <!-- fact_112 --> <!-- fact_114 --> <!-- fact_116 --> <!-- fact_118 --> <!-- fact_110 -->

### Can we marry legally in the Cook Islands?

Yes, with a licence from the Ministry of Justice, issued three days after you both give notice in person unless a waiver is granted. The ceremony must take place at the site the licence names. <!-- fact_136 --> <!-- fact_138 --> <!-- fact_139 -->

### Is Hawaii part of Oceania?

Hawaii sits in Polynesia, so it belongs here, but it is a US state north of the equator, with a wetter winter from October to April. <!-- fact_007 -->

### Is a wedding in Tahiti legal or symbolic?

Either. For a legal marriage, the mairie of the commune advises on requirements and documents, which may include a CNI and a Certificat de Coutume. <!-- fact_142 -->
