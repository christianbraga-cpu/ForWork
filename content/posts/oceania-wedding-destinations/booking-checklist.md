# Booking checklist: Oceania destination shortlist

A question sheet for couples weighing a wedding in Oceania. Take these to the venue, your Wedded Advisor and the official sources for your own dates. These are questions only; the answers belong in writing from the venue or the official source. This sheet doesn't block publishing.

## Step 2: season and cyclone questions (for each destination)

- What does the national weather service say about our month for the place closest to our venue, and which station and period is that reading from?
- How far is our venue from that station, and could the coast, the island side, the hills or the windward or leeward slope make it read differently?
- Does our date fall inside the cyclone season Smartraveller currently gives for this destination, and when does that season start and end this year?
- What does Smartraveller say today about cyclones, severe storms, flooding, road closures or flight disruption here, and have we subscribed to its updates?
- What has the national meteorological service forecast for the coming cyclone season?
- Which months are wettest and driest at the nearest station, and how wet is our month?
- How warm are the evenings in our month, and will guests need a layer after dark?
- How hot and humid is the middle of the day, and which time of day is cooler for the ceremony?
- What time does the sun set on our date, and does the ceremony time keep the photographs in daylight?
- What is the forecast for our wedding week, and when will we check it again closer to the day?
- Where is the nearest cyclone shelter, and what is the accommodation's evacuation plan?

## Step 3: questions for every venue (ask in writing)

- Is the venue open, and fully staffed, on our date?
- What is the covered or indoor ceremony option, does it seat the whole guest list, and can it be dressed in advance?
- How late on the day can we decide to move under cover, and who makes the call?
- Can the covered space, the cyclone and wet weather plan and the postponement terms be written into our contract before we sign?
- What happens to deposits already paid if a cyclone, a storm or a flight suspension moves the date?
- Is there a buffer day between guests arriving and the ceremony?
- Can the whole property be closed to outside guests for the wedding days?
- Is the contract in English, and is the currency of each payment stated?
- Does a local public holiday, a Sunday rule or a weekend alcohol rule fall near our date, and does it change how the venue works that week?
- How do guests get from the airport to the property, and what is the plan if the road floods?
- Is there a route without steps between the guest rooms, the ceremony, dinner and the bathrooms, and how does a guest using a walking frame or wheelchair reach the ceremony across sand or by boat?
- Is the beach patrolled, and are there lifeguards?
- How far away is medical care?
- Is there water rationing, or could there be in our month, and how would it affect guests?
- What does the venue not publish (curfew, minimum nights, set-up times, inclusions), and will our Wedded Advisor confirm each one in writing before we commit?

## Step 4: destination questions from the post

- **Fiji:** Can the covered ceremony space, the cyclone postponement terms and what happens to our deposits if a cyclone moves the date be written into our contract? If flooding cuts the airport road, how do guests reach the property? Can the whole property be closed to outside guests for the wedding days? How would water rationing affect guests?
- **Vanuatu:** If guests need to reach Santo, which smaller aircraft can carry them? Can alcohol for a Saturday or Sunday reception be bought and stored before the weekend rule applies? What dress do guests need outside the resort?
- **New Caledonia:** If a curfew or an alcohol ban is announced in our wedding week, how does the reception run? Should flights be suspended before the wedding, how are the date and our deposits handled? Does our date fall near a date when demonstrations may increase, or near an election?
- **Tahiti and French Polynesia:** Which commune is the venue in, and has it hosted a legal ceremony with that mairie before? If a cyclone alert is raised in our wedding week, where does the ceremony move? Is the beach at the venue patrolled?
- **The Cook Islands:** Which exact site goes on the licence, and can a covered space be named as well? If a storm forces an evacuation, what is the plan for the wedding and guests' rooms? What dress do guests need outside the resort?
- **Samoa:** What are your Sunday rules for music, alcohol service and guest transfers? Which licensed marriage officer do couples use here, and who submits the register?
- **Tonga:** When must a Saturday reception wind down so the music stops before Sunday? With no flights on a Sunday, which day do guests leave, and do the inter-island ferries suit that day?
- **Hawaii:** Does the ceremony lawn sit on the windward or leeward coast, and what is the indoor fallback between October and April? Which state-licensed officiants have married couples at the venue?
- **New Zealand:** Which covered space should we list on the licence as our back-up? How late on the day can the ceremony move indoors, and who makes the call?
- **The Whitsundays:** Which covered room seats every guest if a January or February ceremony is rained out? If boats are held in port, how do guests on other islands reach the ceremony?

## Step 5: legal or symbolic (ask the destination's authorities)

General questions for every destination:

- Can we marry legally in this country as Australians, and what does that require? (Read where you can legally marry overseas.)
- Would a legal marriage at home and a symbolic ceremony abroad suit us better? (Read our note on a legal or symbolic ceremony.)
- Which documents does this country ask for, and does that list include a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI), apostilles or translations?
- Which embassy or consulate of this country should we ask, as Smartraveller suggests?
- How long does the legal process take, and does it fit our date and our arrival day?
- Would the marriage be valid where it takes place, and would it have been valid in Australia?

Destination by destination:

- **Fiji:** Which Fiji authority should we ask about a legal marriage, and what does it require of Australians? If we choose a symbolic ceremony, what do we arrange at home first?
- **Vanuatu:** Which Vanuatu authority handles marriages for visitors, and what are its steps and documents?
- **New Caledonia:** Which authority handles marriages for visitors, and which French and local laws apply to us?
- **Tahiti and French Polynesia:** Which commune's mairie do we contact, and what documents and eligibility checks does it ask for? Does it accept a CNI issued by DFAT in Australia? Do we need a Certificat de Célibat, a Certificat de Coutume or apostilles, and can the Australian Consulate-General in Papeete provide a letter for the Certificat de Coutume once we hold a CNI? Do documents need to be in French?
- **The Cook Islands:** Is the Cook Islands Ministry of Justice marriage licence page (justice.gov.ck/marriage-license) still current when we book, and when was it last updated? Will the Ministry confirm in writing that its steps are unchanged for our date: notice in person by both of us, the statutory declaration, the documents, the wait between notice and licence, whether a waiver applies to us, the licence expiry and the place named on the licence? Which registered minister or celebrant will marry us, and can a covered back-up site be named? How many working days before the wedding should we land?
- **Samoa:** What are the steps for notice, licence and documents, and which authority sets them? Which licensed marriage officer will submit the Marriage Register, and how do we confirm it was registered within the 14 days?
- **Tonga:** Which Tongan authority handles marriages for visitors, and what are its steps and documents?
- **Hawaii:** Are the Hawaii State Department of Health licence rules unchanged for our date? Where and when do we meet the authorised agent together in person, and does our ceremony date fall within 30 days of the licence? Is our officiant licensed by the state?
- **New Zealand:** Are the govt.nz marriage licence rules unchanged for our date? When must we apply so the licence is arranged at least 3 working days before the wedding, which places (including a covered back-up) do we name on it, and which registry office do we visit on arrival to sign the declaration?
- **The Whitsundays:** When must the notice of intended marriage be lodged, and who is our authorised celebrant?

## Step 6: guest travel questions (guests travelling from Australia)

- Who on our guest list can travel in our month, and have we asked them early?
- Does our date clash with school holidays or public holidays in the states our guests live in?
- Does each guest need a passport for this destination, and is it valid for long enough?
- What do guests need on arrival (visa, visitor permit or entry form) for this destination, according to Smartraveller and the country's own authorities?
- Have guests checked Smartraveller for the destination close to the dates, and subscribed to its updates?
- Does each guest's travel insurance cover cyclone, storm or flood disruption, airport closures, flight delays or cancellations, and their own health, particularly older guests?
- If flights are delayed, suspended or the airport closes, where do guests stay, and who tells them?
- Do flights, ferries, tours and shops run on a Sunday at this destination, and does a Sunday rule touch arrival day, the wedding day or departure day?
- Can guests buy alcohol on a Saturday afternoon or a Sunday, and does that matter for any pre-wedding or post-wedding event?
- What dress and behaviour standards should guests follow outside the resort, in villages and on a Sunday?
- If guests need a ferry, boat, inter-island flight or long road transfer, what is the back-up if it is cancelled or the road floods?
- When are inter-island ferry schedules published for our week?
- Which airports can our guests fly into, and are there aircraft size limits that affect their route?
- Are there current health alerts (for example, mosquito-borne illness) for this destination, and what should guests do about them?
- Which guests will need a hand on the journey, and who looks after them on travel days?
- Have we told guests what to pack for our month at this destination (a layer for evenings, an umbrella for rain, sun protection, modest clothes for outside the resort)?
- Do guests have copies of their flight, accommodation and event bookings, and have they shared their itinerary with family or friends?
