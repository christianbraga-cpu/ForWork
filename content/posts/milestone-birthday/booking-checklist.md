# Milestone birthday planning checklist

Use this when you plan a milestone birthday, at home or away. Ask each venue, supplier and council the same questions, and ask for the answers in writing rather than in a phone call. Anything not written down counts as not confirmed.

## The guest of honour
- [ ] Whose birthday is it, and which milestone? ____________
- [ ] Do they light up in a crowd, or around a small table?
- [ ] Would they rather be fussed over at home or taken somewhere new?
- [ ] How do they want to feel at the end of the night?
- [ ] Do they love surprises, dislike them, or prefer to know the date only?
- [ ] Are there customs, food requirements or timings that matter to them or their family?

## The guest list
- [ ] Who must be in the room? ____________
- [ ] How many guests, roughly? ____________
- [ ] Which generations are coming, from young children to older relatives?
- [ ] Who will need to travel, and from where?
- [ ] Which guests need step-free access, shorter journeys or rooms without stairs?
- [ ] Which guests have dietary needs or allergies?
- [ ] Who has young children or little leave, and needs early notice?

## Home or destination
- [ ] A party at home, a destination weekend, or a party at home followed by a few days away with the closest few?
- [ ] Can the guests who matter most take the time and leave to travel?
- [ ] What will you host, and what will guests pay for themselves (for example flights and rooms)?
- [ ] Have you said so plainly in the save the date and the invitation?
- [ ] Which two or three things matter most: the food, the setting, the music, or bringing guests to you?

## Questions for the venue
- [ ] Is the date available, and are any alternative dates open?
- [ ] Is it exclusive use, or are spaces shared with other guests?
- [ ] Can the whole property be closed to the public for our stay or our event?
- [ ] Can another event be held at the property on the same day?
- [ ] What deposit is needed, and does it secure the date?
- [ ] When is the balance due, and in which currency is each payment invoiced?
- [ ] What happens to the deposit and the balance if we cancel?
- [ ] What happens if the date moves, at our request or yours?
- [ ] When must final guest numbers be confirmed?
- [ ] When does music have to stop?
- [ ] What is the wet weather plan, and what shade is there for a daytime event?
- [ ] Who holds the liquor licence for our event?
- [ ] Who serves the drinks, and are bar staff trained in responsible service of alcohol?
- [ ] Is BYO allowed, and does any charge or condition apply?
- [ ] Are candles allowed, and are open flames permitted indoors and outdoors?
- [ ] Is there a route without steps to every space we will use, including bathrooms and rooms?
- [ ] How are dietary needs and allergies handled, and who on the night knows about them?
- [ ] Who is our contact on the day, and how do we reach them?
- [ ] Will the venue and its suppliers respect a request that nothing is shared on social media?
- [ ] What will you confirm in writing, and when?

## Questions for the local council (a park or beach)
- [ ] Does our gathering need a permit or a booking at this park or beach?
- [ ] Does the answer change with guest numbers, a marquee, music or catering?
- [ ] Is alcohol allowed at this location, and under what conditions?
- [ ] Are there rules on amplified music, open flames or barbecues?
- [ ] How far ahead must we apply, and what does the council need from us?
- [ ] Which fees apply, and is a booking refundable if the weather turns?
- [ ] Are toilets, parking and step-free access available nearby?

## Questions for the hotel or accommodation (destination weekend)
- [ ] Can you hold a group of rooms for our guests, and until when?
- [ ] When are unbooked rooms released?
- [ ] Is there a minimum stay?
- [ ] Do you have rooms without stairs?
- [ ] Can airport transfers be arranged, and who books and pays for them?
- [ ] How do guests reach the dinner venue and get back?

## Travel questions for guests
- [ ] Have you read the destination's travel advice on Smartraveller, for every country on the route?
- [ ] Have you subscribed to Smartraveller updates for each destination, including places you transit through?
- [ ] Is every guest's passport valid for the trip, and is there time to renew one if needed?
- [ ] Has each guest checked visa needs for their own passport?
- [ ] For guests travelling from the United States, have they worked through the US Department of State international travel checklist?
- [ ] Does each guest's travel insurance cover every country on the trip, including stopovers and transit points?
- [ ] What does the policy say about cancellation if a destination's advice level goes up after booking?
- [ ] Has each guest read the Product Disclosure Statement?
- [ ] Has each guest declared everything upfront, including pre-existing health conditions?
- [ ] When will guests buy their travel insurance?

## Hosting for someone else
- [ ] Who is the co-host, and can they check the guest of honour's diary without raising questions?
- [ ] Full surprise, partial surprise, or the date only?
- [ ] What exactly must stay secret, and has every guest been told?
- [ ] What is the one source of truth for guests (a single email or a private page)?
- [ ] Has everyone been asked to stay off social media until the day, or altogether?
- [ ] Who is giving a speech, and is the list short?
- [ ] Which guests will send a short memory ahead of time to read on the night?
- [ ] Who answers supplier and guest questions on the day, so the host can be a guest?
- [ ] Is the run sheet written and shared with that person?
