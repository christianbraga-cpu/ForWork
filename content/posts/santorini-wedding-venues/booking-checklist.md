# Booking checklist: Santorini wedding venues

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy; the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (7 slots).

## Confirm before quoting

- **Canaves Collection capacity conflict.** The Weddings page gives the Epitome Sunset Terrace 25 guests, but the Exclusives & Buyouts table gives it theatre 100, banquet 80 and reception 100. The buyout list caps Canaves Sunday at 22 guests, but the same table gives the Sunday Veranda theatre 40, banquet 40, reception 60 (the Weddings page says 40). The article uses the Weddings page figures (25 and 40). Confirm which figure applies to a wedding, with and without a buyout.
- **Le Ciel Santorini location.** Its pages do not name the village or area; the article lists it under "Elsewhere on the caldera" with the village "not published". Confirm the address and village before naming it anywhere.
- **Venetsanos Winery location.** The wedding page does not state the village; the article gives the area as "Not published". Confirm the address and village.
- **Andronis wedding property.** The group Weddings & Celebrations page does not say which Andronis property hosts ceremonies. Confirm whether weddings are held at Andronis Luxury Suites (Oia), and request the wedding brochure for spaces, capacity and ceremony types.
- **Katikies Santorini and Katikies Chromata:** children above 13 only; confirm how this applies to younger wedding guests.
- **Grace Hotel:** a wedding planner is required, engaged at least three months before the day; confirm the terms.

## Civil marriage with the Municipality of Thira

No official Municipality of Thira civil marriage page could be read, so the article says to check with the municipality and the embassy, including fees.

- Confirm with the Municipality of Thira: whether it performs civil marriages for non-residents, the documents and notice period, translation and apostille requirements, who officiates, where (town hall or venue), and its fees.
- Confirm with the Australian Embassy in Athens: CNI (issued in Australia or by the Embassy), notice of intended marriage, authentication or apostille, appointment booking and fees.
- Confirm with each venue whether it can host the legal civil ceremony or only a symbolic one, and which ceremony types it holds.

## General (ask every venue, in writing)

- Deposits: amount due to secure the date, when it is due, and whether it is refundable
- Cancellation and postponement terms, including emergencies (heat, wildfire, earthquake or volcanic alerts) that move the date
- Exclusive use: the wedding spaces only, one wedding per day, or the whole property; from what time; which days a buyout or take-over is available, and minimum nights
- Music: the time amplified music must stop outdoors and indoors, and any noise rules
- Step-free access: steps between the road, rooms, ceremony and reception spaces, and whether a step-free route exists
- Insurance: the venue's public liability cover, and what the couple's event or travel insurance must cover
- Rain or wind plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space
- Room blocks, check-in and check-out times, set-up and pack-down windows
- Outside suppliers allowed, and which inclusions a wedding package covers
