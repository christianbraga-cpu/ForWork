Santorini wedding venues with a caldera view sit in four parts of the island: Oia, Imerovigli, Pyrgos and the inland villages, and Akrotiri. The right one depends on how many guests you are inviting, the ceremony you want and the view you want behind you as you say your vows.

Every venue in this guide hosts weddings and describes its caldera outlook in words, and each is set out on the same terms so you can compare them fairly. ## Key takeaways

- Choose Oia or Imerovigli for a small terrace ceremony, and a winery when a long dinner matters as much as the cliff edge.

- Several venues publish full-property buyouts or a hotel take-over, while others publish no capacity at all, so ask every venue the same questions in writing.

Every venue on this list hosts weddings and describes a caldera view. Three questions then separate them: what kind of place it is, where the ceremony and reception happen, and how many people it holds and houses.

### Caldera terrace, hotel, winery or private venue

Most venues here are cliff-top hotels that host weddings in named spaces: a balcony, a rooftop, a lounge terrace, a restaurant deck. That suits a guest list that wants to stay together and treat the wedding as a week away.

Scale varies. Kapari Natural Resort has 17 rooms and suites, Andronis's Oia hotel has 39 suites and villas, and Canaves Epitome has 53 rooms. Katikies Santorini and Katikies Chromata welcome children above 13, which matters if your guest list includes young families.

Two venues are wineries. Santo Wines and Venetsanos Winery both host weddings on terraces facing the caldera, so the ceremony and a wine-led dinner can sit in one place. Le Ciel is a dedicated wedding and events venue with a restaurant and bar rather than a hotel, so guests stay elsewhere and gather for the day.

### Ceremony and reception in one place

Some venues name a ceremony space and a separate reception space at the same property. Canaves Epitome holds the ceremony on its Sunset Terrace and moves to the Sunset Pool Lounge for cocktails and dinner. Venetsanos Winery says its Upper Terrace can host the ceremony, the reception and a private dinner.

The ceremony type is the next detail to check. Canaves lists religious, civil, symbolic and vow renewal ceremonies on its wedding enquiry form. Tholos Resort describes a wedding at a whitewashed chapel facing the volcano, on the beach or at the resort itself. Where a venue names no ceremony type, ask before you fall for the view.

### Capacity, rooms and exclusive use

Published ceremony capacity on the cliff edge is intimate. Canaves lists 25 guests on the Epitome Sunset Terrace, 30 on the Oia Suites Panorama Balcony and 40 on the Sunday Veranda. Grace Hotel's 363 Lounge seats 40, and Kapari accepts events for up to approximately 30.

For a larger guest list, the published options are Grand Ambassador, with outdoor space for up to 250 guests, Venetsanos Winery's Upper Terrace for about 100 seated or 160 standing, and a full buyout of Canaves Epitome for up to 170 guests.

Exclusive use is published by only a few venues. Canaves lists full-property buyouts of Oia Suites, Sunday and Epitome. Katikies lists a hotel take-over among its event enquiry types. Grace Hotel's venue fee includes exclusive use of the selected event spaces, and the hotel quotes room blocks or a resort buyout. For every other venue, ask whether the wedding spaces, or the whole property, can be closed to other guests.

## Choosing an area of the island

Each area on this list answers a different question about your wedding.

**Oia.** The village holds four venues on this list, with hotels set into the cliffs. Katikies describes its Pelagos House as suspended on the cliffs in the heart of Oia. Choose Oia for a small ceremony on a terrace above the water, with a buyout if your guest list is larger.

**Imerovigli.** Katikies Chromata calls the village the "Balcony on the Aegean", situated on the caldera. Grace Hotel holds its unity ceremony 363 metres above sea level. Five venues sit here, most of them intimate. Choose Imerovigli for height and a wide view across the caldera.

**Fira and Firostefani.** Fira, the main town, sits on the caldera too. No venue on this list publishes a Fira or Firostefani address, but Katikies Chromata is about a five-minute drive from Fira.

**Pyrgos and the inland villages.** Santo Wines sits in Pyrgos village, and its Wine Tourism Center looks out over the volcano. Choose a winery when wine and a long dinner matter as much as the cliff edge.

**Akrotiri.** Grand Ambassador sits on the cliffs of Akrotiri, with the caldera and the volcano as its backdrop. Choose Akrotiri when the guest list runs into the hundreds and you still want the caldera behind you.

## Getting to Santorini for your wedding

Guests can fly in. [Visit Greece](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/islands/cyclades/santorini) lists flights of 50 minutes from Athens International Airport to Santorini Airport, and 1 hour and 30 minutes from Thessaloniki.

[Santorini Airport (JTR)](https://www.jtr-airport.gr/en) is served by Greek airlines alongside European and Gulf carriers. It has Schengen and non-Schengen departure areas and an accessible travel service for passengers with reduced mobility. For older guests, ask each venue how many steps lie between the road, the rooms and the ceremony space before anyone books.

**Entry.** As at November 2026, Smartraveller says Greece is in the Schengen area, and Australians can make short trips without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. On arrival, you register fingerprints and a photo under the European Entry/Exit System, which is free and may slow the border queue. Entry conditions can change at short notice, so guests should check with the nearest Greek embassy or consulate.

**Ferries.** Ferries can be delayed or cancelled in strong winds, so check the forecast and reconfirm departures.

Ask every venue which of its spaces are open on your date before you settle on one.

**Temperature and rain.** The [Hellenic National Meteorological Service](http://oldportal.emy.gr/emy/en/climatology/climatology_city?perifereia=South%20Aegean&poli=Naxos) has no station on Santorini; Naxos is the nearest official station, so read its figures as a guide to the neighbourhood. At Naxos, mean maximums run from 18.7°C in April to 22.1°C in May, about 27°C in July and August, 25.1°C in September and 21.9°C in October. Rain all but disappears from June to August, while October brings 38.6 mm over 5.5 days. May and October fall outside the June to September wildfire season, with October the wetter of the two.

**Wind.** At Naxos the prevailing wind blows from the north in every month, and mean wind readings run higher from July to October than in May and June. On an open caldera terrace, ask how each venue handles a breezy afternoon.

**Heat.** As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) says heatwaves are frequent in Greece over the summer months, and advises water, light clothing and shade. For a summer date, that means a shaded ceremony spot, a later start and water for every guest.

**Wildfire.** Wildfires occur during the extended summer from June to September, including near tourist areas, and can start with little warning and travel quickly. Agree a covered or indoor fallback with your venue in advance.

**Earthquakes and volcanic activity.** Greece is in an active seismic zone with earthquakes and volcanic activity, including near Santorini. Read the current advice before you book and again before you travel. Ask your travel insurer what your policy covers, and suggest your guests ask theirs the same question.

## Getting married legally in Santorini

Decide early whether the ceremony in Santorini will be your legal marriage or a symbolic one. Canaves, for one, lists civil, religious and symbolic ceremonies on its enquiry form. The full steps are in [our Santorini wedding planning guide](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/santorini-wedding), so here is the short version.

**Proving you are free to marry.** As at November 2026, Smartraveller says some countries ask you to prove you are free to marry, most often with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) and registry certificates. The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only ones issued in their own country. Ask the Greek embassy or consulate whether you need one, whether you can get it in Australia, and whether it needs an authentication or apostille.

**Legalising documents.** Ask the Greek authority which documents it needs and which must be legalised with an apostille or an authentication certificate. The certificate is applied to the document itself, so if you want to keep your originals unmarked, have a notarised copy legalised and check the authority accepts it.

**In Athens.** The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) witnesses CNI applications, issues CNIs, witnesses notices of intended marriage and legalises documents by authentication or apostille. Every notarial service needs an appointment, though apostille and CNI documents can be sent by post. The Embassy gives no legal advice, and fees apply.

**Back home.** An overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. A foreign marriage certificate is usually accepted as proof of marriage but may not work as proof of a name change. Read [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas).

Requirements change, so check with the Municipality of Thira and the Australian Embassy in Athens, including any fees, before you set the date. For the customs around the day itself, read our piece on [Greek wedding traditions](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/greek-wedding-traditions).

9. **Weather.** What is the covered or indoor fallback for heat, wind or a wildfire alert, and what happens to your booking if an emergency moves the date?

## Frequently asked questions

### Which part of Santorini suits a wedding?

It depends on your guest list and the setting you want. Oia's published ceremony spaces run from 25 guests on the Epitome Sunset Terrace to 60 seated at Botrini's, while Imerovigli, the "Balcony on the Aegean", has Katikies Chromata on its highest point. The wineries suit a ceremony and dinner in one place, and Grand Ambassador in Akrotiri publishes outdoor space for up to 250 guests.

### Which Santorini venues have a caldera view?

All 13 venues in this guide describe a caldera view. In Oia, Katikies Santorini's Gea House looks over the caldera and the volcano; in Imerovigli, every room at Kapari faces the caldera; and Venetsanos Winery's Upper Terrace overlooks it.

### Are there small wedding venues in Santorini for intimate groups?

Yes. Canaves lists 25 guests on the Epitome Sunset Terrace and 30 on the Oia Suites Panorama Balcony, Kapari takes events for up to approximately 30, and Grace's Villa seats 15. Katikies offers micro weddings for small groups.

### Which Santorini venues can host 100 guests?

Grand Ambassador has outdoor space for up to 250 guests, a Canaves Epitome buyout takes up to 170 and a Canaves Oia Suites buyout up to 120. Venetsanos Winery seats about 100 on its Upper Terrace, and Botrini's Restaurant at Katikies Santorini holds 100 standing.

### Can we get married at a winery in Santorini?

Yes. Santo Wines in Pyrgos hosts weddings with the caldera as the backdrop, and Venetsanos Winery's Upper Terrace hosts the ceremony and the reception.

### Can we book a Santorini venue for exclusive use?

Some venues publish it. Canaves lists full buyouts of three Oia hotels, Katikies lists a hotel take-over among its event enquiries, and Grace Hotel's venue fee includes exclusive use of the selected event spaces. For the others, confirm in writing.

### How do guests get to Santorini?

Most fly. Visit Greece lists a 50-minute flight from Athens and 1 hour and 30 minutes from Thessaloniki. Guests arriving by ferry should expect stairs with luggage and possible delays in strong winds.

### When should we get married in Santorini?

Each period brings something different. At Naxos, the nearest official station, June to August are the driest months and July and August the hottest, and summer heatwaves are frequent. May and October fall outside the June to September wildfire season, with mean maximums of 22.1°C and 21.9°C at Naxos, though October is wetter.

### Can Australians legally get married in Santorini?

Plan the legal steps first. As at November 2026, Smartraveller says you may need a Certificate of No Impediment, and a marriage valid in Greece that would also be valid under Australian law is in most cases recognised in Australia.
