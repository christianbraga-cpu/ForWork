## Cross-region summary

- **Page one is mostly insurer sales pages: confirmed.** In Sydney 6 of 8 organic results are insurer pages (5 from specialist jewellery insurers, 1 global insurer), plus a 3-insurer local pack; the AI overview names specialist insurers. Melbourne is 5 of 8 insurer pages plus 2 paid ads. Remaining slots are Reddit and a jeweller blog. New York, Dubai and Singapore are also dominated by insurers, jewellers selling an insurance partner, comparison sites and affiliate-style "best of" listicles.
- **No regulator or neutral source ranks anywhere** in the 5 cities. No page cites ASIC Moneysmart, Understand Insurance, the General Insurance Code of Practice or AFCA.
- **Pricing shown:** AU pages and the AU AI overview show monthly premium figures, excess amounts and contents jewellery caps; US/UK pages and AI overview quote percentage-of-value rules of thumb. Our brief bans all of these, which is itself a point of difference.
- **Top concern across regions:** "Is my ring covered by home/contents insurance?" and "does it cover loss (not just theft) and overseas travel?" ("engagement ring insurance for travel" appears as a related search in Dubai and Singapore; "engagement ring insurance overseas" in AU related keywords).
- **Gap we can own:** a neutral, sell-nothing Australian checklist built on regulator sources that follows the ring through purchase, a proposal abroad, the flight, the week at the destination, and a claim (including internal dispute resolution then AFCA). No ranking page covers the proposal-abroad gap, travel insurance valuables limits versus jewellery cover, or AFCA.
- **Guest counts:** not applicable to this topic.
