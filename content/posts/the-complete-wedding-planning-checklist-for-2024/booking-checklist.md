# Booking checklist: 12 month wedding checklist

Printable month by month checklist. Tick each item as it is done. No amounts are listed: get every quote, deposit and payment schedule in writing.

## 12 months out

**Core checklist**

- [ ] Spend and contributors
- [ ] guest count
- [ ] style
- [ ] dates
- [ ] venue
- [ ] planner
- [ ] celebrant

**Destination track**

- [ ] Check passports
- [ ] choose legal route
- [ ] ask embassy about a CNI
- [ ] insure when you book
- [ ] ask about step-free access on the site visit

## 10 to 11 months out

## Questions for the venue

- [ ] What are the deposit, payment schedule, and cancellation and postponement terms? (in writing, before signing)
- [ ] Is there a step-free route from arrival to the ceremony and reception?
- [ ] What seating and shade are there for older guests? Are there accessible rooms and transfers?
- [ ] Can a block of rooms be held close to the venue, with a release date agreed in writing? Who pays for unfilled rooms after that date?
- [ ] For a large guest list, is exclusive use of the whole property possible instead of a room block?
- [ ] What is the wet-weather plan, and what time is sunset on the date?

## Questions for every supplier

- [ ] What are the deposit, payment schedule, and cancellation and postponement terms? (in writing)
- [ ] What happens to our deposit if you cancel?
- [ ] Can we see full galleries from real weddings, not just highlights? (photographer, videographer)
- [ ] Do you offer tastings? (catering)
- [ ] What is your lead time to delivery, including alterations? (attire designer or boutique)
- [ ] What is your approach to confidentiality and posting on social media?

## Questions for the embassy, overseas authority and insurer

- [ ] Do we need to prove we are not already married, for example with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI)?
- [ ] Can we get the CNI in Australia, or must it be issued locally? Does it need an authentication or apostille?
- [ ] Which of our documents are needed, and which must be legalised (apostille or authentication certificate)?
- [ ] Insurer: what does the policy cover if a supplier or venue cancels? (declare all pre-existing health conditions)
