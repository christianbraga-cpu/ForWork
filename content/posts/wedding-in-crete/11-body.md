## Why couples choose Crete

Crete is the largest island in Greece, with more than 1,000 kilometres of coastline. It runs about 260 kilometres from end to end and is intensely mountainous, crossed west to east by the Lefka Ori, Psiloritis and Mount Dikti. This is not one backdrop but a string of quite different places.

In the west sit Chania, with its old town and the Venetian harbour watched over by the Egyptian lighthouse, and Rethymno, a medieval town under its Fortezza. Heraklion, in the centre, is the island's largest urban centre and the gateway to Knossos, with resorts along its northern coast. The east is Lasithi, where Agios Nikolaos looks over Mirabello Bay and Elounda faces its lagoon and the island of Spinalonga.

The history runs deep. In 2025 the Minoan Palatial Centres, Knossos, Phaistos, Malia, Zakros, Zominthos and Kydonia among them, were added to the UNESCO World Heritage List.

Couples who want the day to carry some of the island's customs can read our notes on [Greek wedding traditions](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/greek-wedding-traditions) before they talk to a venue. At Elounda Beach, traditional Cretan lyre music during the ceremony is one of the services couples can add.

Crete has a mild Mediterranean climate, dry and warm, with high sunshine through the year. Within that, the east runs warmer than the west, the south warmer than the north, and the mountains cooler than either coast. A few patterns stand out. From June to August, rain all but disappears: Heraklion averages under a millimetre in July. In the Chania area, the summer highs run a little above Heraklion's, with July and August averaging above 30 degrees. The wet months are winter ones, and the Chania area is the wetter of the two, with well over 100 millimetres on average in January.

May and late September sit between those extremes. Average highs in May are in the low to mid twenties, with only a handful of rain days, and September stays mostly dry, with under 3 rain days at Heraklion and highs a few degrees below August. October brings milder days again, but rain days roughly treble compared with September at Heraklion.

Spring on Crete runs at roughly 16 to 24 degrees, and summer averages about 25 to 30 degrees, with maximums that can reach 40. The meltemi winds ease the summer heat. At Heraklion the prevailing wind turns north-west from June to September and blows from the south from October to May. Plan for wind on an exposed terrace or beach, whatever the month.

These are long-term averages, not a forecast; any single week can run hotter, cooler or wetter.

Spring and autumn are the [ideal seasons for touring Crete](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/routes/in-the-footsteps-of-the-gods-a-mythical-road-trip-in-crete), when the weather is mild; in summer, see the sights early in the morning, when temperatures are more pleasant.

### Venue season windows and ceremony times

Venues set their own season. Minos Palace, in Lasithi, says eastern Crete enjoys pleasant weather from April to November, with spring at around 18 to 24 degrees, summer the warmest season, and early autumn at around 20 to 27 degrees.

Ceremony times move with the months. Nana Princess, at Chersonissos, notes that high-summer temperatures in Greece can reach 35 degrees, with strong sun and high UV, and recommends evening ceremonies. Its suggested times are:

- April, May and October: 16:00 to 18:00.
- June to September: 18:00 to 20:00.
- July and August: 18:30 to 20:00.

A late-afternoon ceremony in May leaves the evening open for a long dinner; a summer ceremony at half past six pushes the reception later into the night.

Greek official holidays include 25 March, 1 May, Easter Monday (which moves each year), 15 August, 28 October and 25 and 26 December. If a date you love falls on one of them, raise it with the venue early.

### Summer heat and wildfire season

As at November 2026, [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) advises that wildfires occur in Greece during the extended summer period from June to September, including near cities and tourist locations, and can start with little warning, prompting evacuations. Heatwaves are frequent over the summer months and can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke, and many major tourist sites have little shade. The overall advice for Greece is to exercise normal safety precautions.

Smartraveller also advises comprehensive travel insurance, bought before you leave, that covers all medical costs overseas including evacuation, and notes that Greece sits in an active seismic zone, with earthquakes including near Crete. Ask every guest to read the policy's Product Disclosure Statement before they buy.

For a summer date, that means shade for the ceremony, water on every chair and a plan for the hottest hours.

### East, centre or west

Crete falls into [three regions](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/islands/crete).

**The east.** Lasithi holds Agios Nikolaos and Mirabello Bay, and Elounda, a resort with views of the lagoon and Spinalonga. Its resorts suit couples who want the wedding party under one roof.

**The centre.** Heraklion is the largest urban centre and the gateway to Knossos, with resorts along the northern coast. A venue here keeps the drive from Heraklion airport short and puts the island's best-known archaeology close at hand.

**The west.** Chania, with its old town and Venetian harbour, and Rethymno, with its medieval centre and Fortezza, make up western Crete. Venues here range from beach resorts at Kolymvari to a hilltop garden in Chania.

### Types of venue

Crete wedding venues tend to fall into one of three settings.

**The resort.** A single property with a chapel or beach for the ceremony, restaurants and terraces for the reception, and rooms for the wedding party. Guest rooms and minimum stays shape its cost.

**The chapel.** Several resorts keep a private chapel within their grounds, used for civil, religious or symbolic ceremonies depending on the venue. Chapels give a sheltered, intimate ceremony, which is worth weighing against a beach in a windy month.

**The estate or garden.** A venue hired for the day, with no guest rooms on site, where you bring in your own caterer, furniture and suppliers. It suits couples who want a blank canvas and are happy to house guests nearby.

Two Crete quotes with the same guest list can land far apart. Much of the difference sits in a handful of terms: when you marry, how many rooms you commit to, whether the space is yours alone, what the package leaves out and who pays for the paperwork.

**Season and date.** Venues set their own season windows and ceremony times, as Minos Palace in Lasithi and Nana Princess at Chersonissos do. Ask how the rate changes across those months, and between a weekday and a weekend.

**Guest numbers, room blocks and minimum stays.** At some resorts the wedding is tied to accommodation. The Elounda Collection, which covers Elounda Peninsula, Porto Elounda and Elounda Mare, makes its civil, Orthodox and symbolic packages valid only when the couple stays at least three nights in one of its hotels. Abaton Island Resort & Spa, also at Chersonissos, ties every package to a minimum room booking for a minimum number of nights and a minimum reception size, and its packages do not include accommodation. Guest numbers decide which spaces you can use and how many rooms you are asked to hold.

**Exclusive use.** Sole use of a space is a cost line of its own. Nana Princess offers exclusive use of the couple's chosen room or venue for symbolic weddings and vow renewals. Sea View by En Kipo is the couple's alone from 10:00am to midnight, with the possibility of extending to 2:00am. Ask what sole use covers, for how long, and what an extension involves.

**Venue fee or package.** Some venues charge for the space, others sell a package. Abaton lists a venue fee for each of its wedding venues, with set-up items such as a stage, arch and flowers charged as a supplement. Euphoria Resort at Kolymvari sells complete packages whose items are not interchangeable: a service not used brings no refund or alternative, and amendments or custom packages cost extra. Abaton's packages are complete in the same way. Know which model you are buying before you compare two totals.

**Extras priced separately.** The package is rarely the whole bill. Elounda Beach lists bridal hair and make-up, flowers for the wedding location, Cretan lyre music, photography, video, cocktails, reception menus, entertainment, a firework show and guest gifts as additional services outside its indicative package. At Euphoria, buffet, seated and cocktail receptions, beverage packages, an open bar, custom cakes and entertainment are priced on request. At Abaton, hair, make-up, a DJ, fireworks, a photographer and video are listed as additional services.

**Your own suppliers.** A garden venue moves cost from the venue to your suppliers. At Sea View by En Kipo, couples rent the equipment, furniture and decor they want and handpick their own caterer, choosing preferred vendors to shape the ceremony. That means more control, and more quotes to compare.

**Guest travel to the venue.** A remote venue adds driving time for every guest who arrives at the far airport, and for every evening that ends away from where people sleep. Ask each venue how guests usually reach it, and what that means on the wedding day itself.

**Documents and fees on top.** For a legal wedding, paperwork carries its own costs. At the Elounda Collection, every document except passports needs its own Apostille stamp, the Apostilled documents need translating into Greek, and the originals are couriered to the hotel at the couple's own cost. Its civil package rate does not include the Municipal fees, and its Orthodox rate does not include the Metropolis fee, so those are charged on top.

Not published: whether Sea View's later finish carries a charge, and how most venues price a change of season or day of the week.

For the packages themselves, venue by venue, read [Greece wedding packages and what they include](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-packages).

## Getting married legally in Greece

As at November 2026, an overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. A foreign marriage certificate is usually accepted as proof of marriage, though it may not work as proof of a name change.

Some countries ask you to prove you are free to marry, most commonly with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI). DFAT can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only one issued in their own country, so ask whether you need one, where to get it and whether it needs an apostille. [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) sets out the detail.

The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) issues CNIs, witnesses notices of intended marriage and legalises documents by authentication or apostille. Every notarial service needs an appointment, Monday to Friday from 9:00am to 12:30pm, though apostille and CNI documents can be sent by post, and legalised documents and CNIs are ready within two working days. The Embassy gives no legal advice, and fees apply.

On Crete itself, couples of non-Greek nationality should contact the local municipality where the wedding will take place for the certificates it requires. Venues that host legal weddings set out the steps in their own terms. The Elounda Collection's civil documentation page is direct about the final one: "The marriage has to be registered by the couple at the Registry Office in Aghios Nikolaos within 40 days after the ceremony. If the wedding takes place on a Friday (or weekends), the registration of the wedding takes place the following Monday."

Not every Crete wedding is a legal one. Caramel's most popular ceremonies are informal weddings and vow exchanges, which need no paperwork. Euphoria Resort's options are informal weddings or vow renewals, with a certificate of informal marriage from the hotel. Some couples marry legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony on the island.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the best time to get married in Crete?

On long-term averages, May and September are milder than high summer with few rain days, and Minos Palace gives April to November as eastern Crete's pleasant window. Beyond that, it depends on the heat and rain you are comfortable with. Long-term averages show almost no rain from June to August, with average highs near or above 30 degrees in the Chania area in July and August. Spring and autumn are the island's mild seasons for touring.

### Is Crete good for a destination wedding?

For many Australian couples, yes. It is the largest island in Greece, with more than 1,000 kilometres of coastline, airports at Heraklion and Chania and a range of venues from resort chapels to hilltop gardens. The trade-offs are summer heat, with July and August highs above 30 degrees in the Chania area, wildfire season from June to September, and a drive of about three hours from Agios Nikolaos to Chania Airport.

### Which part of Crete is best for a wedding?

It comes down to the wedding you want. The east, around Elounda and Agios Nikolaos, is resort country, and Heraklion in the centre keeps its airport close. Chania and Rethymno in the west centre on their old towns, with venues from beach resorts to a hilltop garden. Choose the end of the island by where your guests will land.

### What affects the cost of a wedding in Crete?

Season and date, the rooms and nights a venue asks you to hold, exclusive use, whether you pay a venue fee or a package, extras priced separately and the cost of documents. At the Elounda Collection, official fees are also charged on top of the package rate.

### Can Australians legally get married in Crete?

Yes. Venues such as the Elounda Collection offer civil packages, and there the couple registers the marriage at the Registry Office in Aghios Nikolaos after the ceremony. Contact the local municipality where you plan to marry for the certificates it requires.

### How far ahead should we plan a wedding in Crete?

Start with the venue's season and work back.
