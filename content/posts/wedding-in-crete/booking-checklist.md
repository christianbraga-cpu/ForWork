# Booking checklist: wedding in Crete

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article's "Not published" lines and its one `[[VERIFY]]` item (the Elounda Collection lead times, which is a publishing blocker and also sits in the draft note) are listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- Elounda Collection (Elounda Peninsula, Porto Elounda, Elounda Mare): the document lead times come from the 2025 packages PDF (all documents issued within 3 months of the date, copies emailed for approval at least 4 weeks before, originals couriered at least 3 weeks before). Confirm they still apply for 2027 dates, and confirm the registration step for 2027: the couple registers the marriage at the Registry Office in Aghios Nikolaos within 40 days of the ceremony, with a Friday or weekend wedding registered the following Monday.
- Abaton Island Resort & Spa: the terms on file are from the 2025-2026 brochure. For a 2027 date, ask for the current brochure and confirm the package terms (accommodation not included, minimum room booking and nights, minimum reception size, items not interchangeable), each venue fee and which set-up items are charged as a supplement.
- Sea View by En Kipo: the venue is the couple's alone from 10:00am to midnight, with the possibility of extending to 2:00am. Confirm whether the later finish carries a charge, what it covers, and whether music and amplification may continue during the extension.

## The six example venues: not published

- Elounda Beach Hotel & Villas, Elounda: exclusive use or buyout, guest room count and minimum nights, music curfew, rain plan (Artemis's capacity is an outdoor figure)
- St. Nicolas Bay Resort Hotel & Villas, Agios Nikolaos: capacity (chapel deck and reception) and whether an exclusive-use option exists, plus legal ceremony types on site, minimum nights, curfew and rain plan
- Amirandes, Grecotel, Heraklion coast: ceremony types at the VIP Beach and in the chapel (legal civil, Greek Orthodox or symbolic), exclusive use of venues, room block, curfew, rain plan
- Caramel, Grecotel, Rethymno: capacity and whether a legal ceremony option exists (published ceremonies are informal weddings and vow exchanges), plus ceremony venues, room block, exclusive use and curfew
- Avra Imperial, Kolymvari: Saint Paul chapel capacity, reception capacity outside the Maistro hall, exclusive use or buyout, room block, minimum nights, curfew, rain plan
- Sea View by En Kipo, Chania: later finish and any charge (see "Confirm before quoting"), ceremony type and who officiates, rain plan, supplier set-up and access times, minimum spend and what the venue hire includes

## General (every venue)

- Transfers for guests who won't drive: what the venue offers or recommends from the airport and back to guests' beds at night, how late taxis can be booked, where guests can park, and who arranges and pays (the article offers no transfer arrangements)
- Step-free access for older guests and anyone with limited mobility: steps between guest rooms, the ceremony and the reception, level paths and lifts, walking distances across the grounds, and buggy or private transfers on site
- Whole-property closure: whether the property can be closed to other guests, whether exclusive use covers the chosen spaces or the whole hotel, and which one is being quoted
- Deposits and cancellation: currency of the quote, deposit amount and timing, which items are paid locally and separately, refund timetable, and the terms for moving or cancelling the date; which year's terms apply and when they were last updated
- Rates across the season and between weekdays and weekends (not published by most venues)
- Minimum guests, minimum nights and room blocks
- Outside supplier policy: whether the couple may bring their own caterer, photographer, florist or planner
- Official fees and document costs that sit outside the package (municipal or Metropolis fees, Apostilles, translation, courier)
- Heat and weather plan: shade and water for a summer ceremony, and where the ceremony moves if heat, wind or rain rules out the outdoor space
