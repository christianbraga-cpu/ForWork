# Booking checklist: Santorini wedding packages

These details are confirmed in writing with each venue, for the couple's dates, before anyone commits. They don't block publishing: the article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers and prints no prices. The only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (7 slots).

## Confirm before quoting

- **Santorini Gem villa bathroom count.** The villa page says four bathrooms; the Rental Packages 2026 PDF says five. The article does not state a bathroom count. Confirm before quoting the overnight package.
- **Published prices not used.** Santorini Gem (rental PDF, venue policy, 2027 discount), Canaves (deposit as a percentage of the venue cost) and Grace Hotel (menu choice charge) publish money terms. Any figure added later needs Nic's sign-off and is shown "From AUD".
- **Currency of every quote.** Confirm whether the quote is in euros, whether deposit and balance are invoiced in the same currency, and how the Wedded Advisor's AUD quote converts.

## Ask every venue, in writing

- Inclusions: the full list, item by item (space, set-up, staff, cake, toast, music, coordinator, bridal room)
- What is charged on top: food and drink, service and tips, extra hours, earlier starts, extra guests, gazebo or décor, suppliers
- Required planner: whether an outside wedding planner is required, and from when
- Room minimums: whether rooms are required, how many, for how many nights, and whether guests pay the venue directly
- Deposit: amount, when due, what it secures, and whether it is refundable
- Cancellation and postponement terms, including heat, wildfire, earthquake or volcanic alerts that move the date
- Payment timings: balance and food and beverage due dates
- Music limits: decibel limit, when music drops and when it stops, bar closing time
- Exclusive use: one terrace, the wedding spaces or the whole property, from what time, and for how many days
- Step-free access between road, rooms, ceremony and reception, and an accessible bathroom
- Insurance: the venue's public liability cover, and what the couple's event or travel insurance must cover
- Weather plan: covered or indoor alternative, held for you or offered on the day
- Currency of the quote, deposit and balance

## Grace Hotel, Imerovigli

- Flat venue fee inclusions above and at or below 50 guests; what is charged on top (menu, choice-of-mains charge for up to 30 guests)
- Required planner: involved at least 3 months before; confirm terms
- No room minimum: room block or resort buyout terms on your dates
- Exclusive use of the selected spaces: which spaces, for how many hours or days
- Deposit, cancellation and payment timings (not published)
- Music curfew and limits (DJ or live band allowed)

## Canaves Collection (Oia Suites, Sunday, Epitome), Oia

- Package inclusions per property and guest band; food and beverage menus and cost sit outside the package
- In-house planner: what it covers; documents preparation is an option to request
- Room minimums by guest band (two suites for three nights at Sunday; one to six rooms, half or full buyout at Epitome; recommended only at Oia Suites)
- Deposit: non-refundable, percentage of venue cost; balance one month before; venue payments non-refundable
- Music: 60 dB until 11pm or 11.30pm; Epitome larger package 70 dB until 1am
- Exclusive use: private use for up to 5 or 7 hours; buyout by proposal, and for how many days

## Santorini Gem

- Villa bathroom count (4 vs 5, above)
- Inclusions per package (morning terrace hire; three evening packages: venue only, villa day use, villa overnight)
- On top: gazebo, earlier start by the hour, per-guest fee beyond the base group, required set menu or buffet, open bar for all adults if chosen, tips
- Required planner: confirm
- Deposit (non-refundable), rental balance 9 months before, final food and beverage order 40 days before, postponement fee and following-season only
- Music up to 80 dB, lowered at 11pm, off 15 minutes before end; bar closes 30 minutes before end
- Exclusive use: hours and whether the whole property is closed
