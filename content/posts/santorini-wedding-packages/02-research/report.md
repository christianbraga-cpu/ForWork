## Cross-region summary

- Guest counts: "50 guests", "for two", "small" and "micro" dominate related searches; intimate to mid-size.
- Pricing shown: euro figures in snippets everywhere (FB answers, BeachBride, elopesantorini). We show none.
- Top concern: is it all inclusive, and what does it really cost.
- Gap we can own: inside versus outside the package from each venue's own pages, a like-for-like quote checklist, and an Australian angle (euros versus AUD, deposits, insurance as questions).
- recommended_scope: au_focus.
