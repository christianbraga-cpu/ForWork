Santorini wedding packages share a name and very little else. Most bundle a ceremony space, its set-up, a coordinator and a set number of hours in that space. Guest rooms, sole use of the venue, food and drink, late finishes and outside suppliers are where they part ways, and each venue draws that line in its own place.

This guide sets out what Santorini venue packages include, what sits outside them and how to put two quotes side by side. For the venues themselves, start with [our guide to Santorini wedding venues](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-weddings/santorini-wedding-venues), or browse [our curated Greece wedding collection](https://weddedwonderland.com/destinations/europe/greece).

## Key takeaways

- A Santorini venue package usually covers the ceremony space, its set-up, a coordinator and fixed hours, and everything else depends on the venue.
- Food and drink, guest rooms and room-night minimums are often priced or required outside the package itself, so ask for each in writing.
- Some Santorini venues require a wedding planner on top of the venue fee, which changes what a package is really worth to you.
- Compare quotes on the same guest count, spaces, hours, nights, music times and currency before paying a deposit that may not be refundable.

## What a Santorini wedding package covers

A package is a venue's own bundle of space, time and services, sold under one name. On Santorini that bundle can be as broad as a ceremony, dinner, cake and planning services for a set guest count, or as narrow as hire of a terrace for a morning ceremony with chairs and a sound system.

What a package is not, in most cases, is the whole wedding. Food and drink, rooms for your guests, a photographer, flowers beyond the basics and the legal paperwork can each sit inside or outside the bundle, venue by venue. The word "package" tells you a venue has grouped some things together. It does not tell you which.

There are three shapes you will meet most often:

- **A named package** with a fixed list of inclusions for a stated guest band, such as the Canaves Collection packages in Oia.
- **A venue fee** that covers spaces, furniture, staff and set-up, with a planner bringing the rest, as at Grace Hotel in Imerovigli.
- **Space hire by the session**, such as a morning terrace hire or an evening ceremony and reception package, as at Santorini Gem.

Each shape suits a different kind of wedding, and each leaves a different list of questions to ask.

Planning a larger wedding? Canaves Epitome has a package across its Sunset Terrace, pool area and Elements Restaurant for 45 to 100 guests, Grand Ambassador Santorini lists outdoor space for up to 250 guests, Vedema's wine cellar holds up to 150 for a reception, and Canaves quotes full buyouts by proposal, up to 170 guests at Canaves Epitome.

The Santorini venues that describe their packages in detail tend to cover the same core: a ceremony space for set hours, a basic set-up of tables, chairs and linen, someone to coordinate the day, and a toast. Beyond that core, the lists diverge quickly.

### Ceremony space, set-up and a coordinator

The Canaves Collection sets out its packages in a [wedding brochure](https://canaves.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/CANAVES-COLLECTION-WEDDING-BROCHURE-LR-4.pdf) across several of its Oia properties. The Panorama Balcony and Caldera Arches package at Canaves Oia Suites is a ceremony and dinner reception for up to 30 guests.

It includes private use of the ceremony and reception area for up to 5 hours, until 11.30pm, event planning services, recorded music of your choice, a glass of sparkling wine for the toast, a basic set-up of chairs, tables, white tablecloths and silverware, and a two-tier wedding cake. Music is capped at 60 dB until 11.30pm.

The Veranda package at Canaves Sunday follows the same pattern for up to 40 guests, with private use for up to 5 hours until 11pm. At Canaves Epitome, packages run from the Sunset Terrace alone to the terrace, pool area and Elements Restaurant together, each with event planning services, recorded music, a sparkling wine toast, basic set-up and a two-tier cake.

Santorini Gem, a caldera venue with a wedding villa, works differently. Its [Rental Packages 2026](https://santorinigem.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SANTORINI-GEM_RENTAL-PACKAGES_2026.pdf) list a morning Ceremony Wedding Terrace Exclusive Hire, with the event from 10.30am to 11.30am and the bridal room from 10.15am. That hire covers the ceremony set-up and a sound system, with accessible facilities and a bridal suite.

The same document lists three evening Ceremony and Reception packages on the upper terrace and pool area, with the event from 5pm to 1.30am. One is venue only, one adds day use of the villa from noon, and one adds an overnight stay in the villa until 10am the next morning. Each covers the ceremony and table set-up, a sound system and a bar, with accessible facilities and an air-conditioned bridal room.

Grace Hotel in Imerovigli sells space and service rather than a ceremony bundle. Its [flat venue fee](https://auberge.com/grace-hotel/events/weddings/) for celebrations over 50 people includes exclusive use of the selected event spaces, furniture, bars and tableware, set-up and breakdown, and event staffing. For 50 guests or fewer, the fee includes exclusive use of the selected spaces, tables, chairs and tableware, a wedding arbour, and set-up and breakdown.

The hotel provides a dedicated event manager for venue details. The wedding party has a private lounge with a full-length mirror from 8am.

Packages are often built around one kind of ceremony, so ask which one before you compare anything else. Canaves lists Religious, Civil, Symbolic and Vow Renewal as ceremony types, and its brochure says weddings there can be real or symbolic. Santorini Gem's booking form offers a civil or symbolic ceremony only, a ceremony and reception, or a ceremony, reception and villa overnight.

Andronis, in Oia, builds its package around a legal ceremony. The [Andronis Wedding Package](https://www.andronis.com/explore/weddingsandcelebrations) lists preparation of the documents for the marriage licence, a marriage certificate issued in Greek, and coordination and booking of the ceremony date and time with the Mayor, with two witnesses required. That paperwork is the venue's own offer, inside its own package.

Ask any venue which ceremony its package price is written for, and what changes if you switch.

### Reception, menus and drinks where the venue states them

Food and drink are where packages most often stop. At Canaves, food and beverage menus sit separately from the package, and the food and beverage cost is paid with the balance. At Santorini Gem, the evening packages require a set menu, for 20 to 80 adults, or a buffet for more than 30 guests, with poolside receptions served as buffet only.

Santorini Gem's set menus run to starter, salad, main and dessert, with the wedding cake not included. Open bar packages are optional, but once chosen they apply to every adult guest. Tips are not included in menu prices, and no outside food or drink is allowed.

At Grace Hotel, a minimum four-course menu applies, and for parties of up to 30 guests, offering a choice of three pre-selected main courses on the night comes at a charge. Andronis offers a curated degustation menu at Lycabettus, and its ceremony venues include two glasses of prosecco, koufeta and a jar of honey with walnuts.

### Packages with accommodation

Accommodation can be part of the package, a condition of it, or neither. Santorini Gem's overnight package includes the villa, with five bedrooms sleeping eight, a welcome sparkling wine and seasonal fruit, mineral water, a mini bar, linen, night security and breakfast for the couple and villa guests. Breakfast for more friends is at additional cost.

Grand Ambassador Santorini offers [Wedding and Honeymoon Packages](https://www.grandambassadorsantorini.gr/venues) that pair the wedding with honeymoon extras, and Vedema, in Megalochori, sells a separate Honeymoon Package, so ask whether any extras sit inside your wedding quote or are booked apart.

At Canaves, rooms are a condition rather than an inclusion, which the section on what packages leave out covers below.

## Are Santorini wedding packages all inclusive?

Rarely, in the sense most couples mean. A package includes what its list names and nothing more. Canaves keeps food and drink separate from its packages. Santorini Gem charges a gazebo at an extra cost and an earlier start by the hour. Andronis arranges an officiant, florals, photography sessions, fireworks and other bespoke services on request at an additional charge.

Grace Hotel's fee covers the spaces, furniture and staff, but every wedding there needs a planner as well.

The useful way to read any Santorini package is as the core of the day. Ask for every item outside it, priced and in writing, before you compare one venue with another.

Santorini suits a small wedding, and several venues size their packages for it, from a couple alone to around 50 guests.

### Elopement and couple-only packages

Canaves lists a Panorama Balcony "Elopement for couple only" package, with the same inclusions as its larger packages: up to 5 hours of private use until 11.30pm, event planning services, recorded music, a sparkling wine toast, a basic set-up, a two-tier cake and music up to 60 dB. The Andronis Wedding Package suits a couple well: a private cliffside ceremony area, flowers, cake, sparkling wine and a turndown that night. At the Lycabettus Restaurant cliff area, ceremonies run from 10am to 5pm, with the last at 4pm, and prosecco for up to two additional guests is charged separately.

Katikies lists micro weddings among its package ideas, with inclusions on request.

### Small wedding packages up to around 50 guests

Grace Hotel draws its own line at 50 guests: at or below it, the flat venue fee includes a wedding arbour and the core set-up, and above it, lounge furniture, bars and event staffing join the list. Its 363 Lounge seats 40 and holds 60 standing, and The Villa seats 15 and holds 60 standing.

At Canaves, the Caldera Arches dinner seats up to 25 at one long table or up to 30 across three tables, and the Panorama Balcony dinner reception is for up to 12. The Canaves Epitome package on the Sunset Terrace and Elements Restaurant runs for 26 to 45 guests, for 7 hours until 1am, with music up to 70 dB.

Kapari Natural Resort in Imerovigli accepts events for up to about 30 people, and provides a dedicated wedding planner who works with the hotel team. Andronis offers full exclusivity at Lycabettus for up to 40 guests and the cliff area for up to 20.

A small guest list does not always mean a small set of conditions. At Canaves, even a wedding of eight to 25 guests on the Sunset Terrace comes with a room requirement.

These are the gaps that matter most on Santorini. Turn each into a question for every venue on your shortlist.

### Exclusive use

Is the space yours alone, and for how long? Grace Hotel's fee includes exclusive use of the selected event spaces. Canaves packages give private use for a set number of hours, and a full buyout of a Canaves property is quoted separately through a buyout proposal. Those buyouts range from Canaves Sunday, with 10 rooms for up to 22 guests, to Canaves Epitome, with 53 rooms for up to 170.

Kivotos invites couples to combine the wedding with a hotel buyout, so family and friends stay on site. Katikies lists a hotel takeover among its event types. Ask whether exclusive use covers the whole property or one terrace, and for how many days.

### Guest rooms and minimum nights

Do you have to book rooms to secure the package? At Canaves, often yes. The Veranda package at Canaves Sunday asks up to 20 guests to book two suites for at least three nights, and 21 to 40 guests require a buyout of the property under special terms.

At Canaves Epitome, the room requirement grows with the guest list: one room for three nights for 8 to 25 guests, three rooms for 26 to 45, then four or six rooms, a half buyout of 25 rooms for 80 to 100 guests and a full buyout of 53 rooms above 100, each with a three-night minimum. At Canaves Oia Suites, a room booking is recommended but not required.

Grace Hotel sets no minimum number of rooms; guests may book individually, and the Sales Manager quotes a room block or resort buyout on your dates. Ask each venue whether rooms are required, how many, for how many nights, and whether guests pay the venue directly.

### Extra hours and late finishes

When must the music stop, and what does another hour cost? Canaves packages end at 11pm or 11.30pm with music at 60 dB, and the larger Epitome package runs until 1am at 70 dB.

At Santorini Gem, music runs up to 80 dB, is lowered to listening level at 11pm and switched off 15 minutes before the end time, and the bar closes 30 minutes before the end. An earlier start is charged by the hour. Ask for the finish time in writing, and what happens to music and the bar in the last hour.

### Outside suppliers

Can you bring your own photographer, florist or band, and what does the venue supply? Grace Hotel allows a DJ or live band and gives recommendations. At Vedema, a Celebration Consultant arranges local support services, a photographer and videographer, custom florals, the wedding cake and musical entertainment.

Santorini Gem sets clear rules: the venue provides its standard set-up, set-up starts from 1pm on the day, there is no storage, and confetti, plate smashing and flying lanterns are not permitted. Canaves lists extras such as a flower arch, bouquets, wedding crowns, a photographer, a videographer, fireworks, a DJ, live bands, a harpist and lighting as options to request. Ask for the supplier rules in writing before you book anyone.

### Legal documents and fees

Does the package include the paperwork, or only help with it? Vedema says the resort attends to all of the legal documents necessary to marry on Santorini. Canaves lists "Preparation of Wedding Documents" as an option on its enquiry form.

Ask whether government fees, translations and certification sit inside the venue's price or outside it.

## Venue package, venue fee or a planner

Every Santorini wedding has the same three moving parts: the venue, the services around it and someone to coordinate both. A package bundles some of each. A venue fee buys the space and staff. A planner brings everything else together.

Some venues make that choice for you. Grace Hotel requires a wedding planner for every wedding, involved for at least 3 months before the day, and at its 363 Lounge the décor and seating are looked after by the couple's wedding coordinator. Santorini Gem requires every couple to have a wedding planner.

Other venues keep the planning in house. Canaves has an in-house wedding planner for the details. Kapari provides a dedicated wedding planner who handles the details with the hotel team. At Vedema, the Celebration Consultant arranges suppliers, and the resort can provide flowers, music or a DJ, hair, make-up, spa treatments, photography and video, and customised food and wine menus.

Grand Ambassador Santorini lets couples work with their own wedding planner or with the hotel's concierge team. Mystique, in Oia, says its wedding specialists help organise the wedding and find the setting, with getting ready in a suite and treatments at its Elios Spa, and gives package details on request.

The trade-offs are practical:

- **A venue package** is simple to book and easy to compare, but its inclusions are fixed and its suppliers are usually the venue's own.
- **A venue fee** gives you space and staff with room to shape everything else, but you need a planner and separate costings for food, flowers and music.
- **A planner** connects the venue, suppliers and guests into one plan, and is the route some Santorini venues insist on, but it adds another party to brief and another fee to agree in writing.

Two Santorini quotes rarely line up on first reading. One might give you five hours of private use, a cake and a toast for 30 guests; another might give you a terrace and staff for the evening and leave food, flowers and music to your planner. Put both on the same footing before you judge either.

Use this checklist for every quote:

- **Inclusions in writing.** Ask for a full list of what the package or fee covers, item by item, including set-up, staff, cake, toast and music.
- **Ceremony type.** Confirm whether the price is for a civil, religious or symbolic ceremony, and what changes if you switch.
- **Guest count basis.** Check the guest band the quote is built on, and what an extra guest adds. Santorini Gem charges a per-guest fee beyond its base group, with children under 12 complimentary.
- **Deposits and cancellation terms.** Ask what the deposit secures, whether it is refundable, when the balance is due and what happens if you postpone. Canaves states that its deposit is non-refundable, that the balance is due one month before the event, and that venue payments are always non-refundable. Santorini Gem asks for the rental balance 9 months before the wedding and the final food and beverage order 40 days before, charges a postponement handling fee and allows postponement only to the following season.
- **Exclusive use, and for how many days.** Is it one terrace for set hours, the whole property for one evening, or a buyout across your stay?
- **Rooms and nights.** Are rooms required, how many, and for how many nights?
- **Music times.** When does music drop, when does it stop, and does the decibel limit allow dancing or only background music?
- **Food and drink.** Is catering inside the package, a separate menu, or a required set menu or buffet? Are tips and service included?
- **Weather plan.** What is the covered or indoor alternative if the wind rises, and is it held for you or offered on the day?
- **Step-free access.** Can older guests reach the ceremony and reception without stairs, and is there an accessible bathroom?
- **Supplier rules.** Which outside suppliers are allowed, and from what time can they set up?
- **Documents.** Does the venue prepare the paperwork, help with it or leave it to you?
- **Currency.** What currency is the quote in, and will the deposit and balance be invoiced in the same one?

Once both quotes answer the same questions, the real difference between them is usually obvious. ## The legal side in brief

As at November 2026, Smartraveller says some countries ask you to prove you are free to marry, most often with a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) and certificates from a registry of births, deaths and marriages. The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade can issue a CNI in Australia, but some local authorities accept only CNIs issued in their own country, so ask the destination's embassy or consulate which applies.

The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) issues CNIs, witnesses notices of intended marriage and legalises documents by authentication or apostille, by appointment, and a fee applies. Documents that are not original Australian public documents must first be notarised by an Australian Notary Public.

Back home, an overseas marriage cannot be registered in Australia, but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law. A foreign marriage certificate is usually accepted as proof of marriage but may not work as proof of a name change. Read [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) for the detail.

Requirements change, so check with the town hall on Santorini and the Australian Embassy in Athens, including any fees, before you set the date. - Is the quote in euros, and will the deposit and the balance be invoiced in the same currency?
- How much does the exchange rate move the total between the deposit and the balance, and who carries that risk?
- Is the deposit refundable, and on what terms if you need to postpone or cancel?
- Which items are paid locally on the island, and which through the venue?
- Does your travel insurance cover the wedding itself, and what does it exclude?

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Santorini wedding packages all inclusive?

Rarely. A package covers what its list names. Canaves keeps food and drink separate, and Andronis arranges extras such as an officiant and florals at an additional charge. Treat any package as the core of the day and ask for every extra in writing.

### What is included in a Santorini wedding package for two?

It depends on the venue. The Canaves elopement package for a couple includes up to 5 hours of private use, event planning services, recorded music, a sparkling wine toast, a basic set-up and a two-tier cake. The Andronis package adds a private cliffside ceremony area, flowers, cake, sparkling wine and a turndown.

### Do Santorini wedding packages include accommodation?

Some do, and some require it. Santorini Gem's overnight package includes its villa for the couple and villa guests. Canaves packages often require a set number of rooms for at least three nights. Grace Hotel sets no minimum number of rooms.

### Can a Santorini package work for around 50 guests?

Yes, at several venues. Grace Hotel sets its venue fee inclusions either side of 50 guests, and Canaves Epitome has a package for 26 to 45 guests on the Sunset Terrace and Elements Restaurant, and a larger one with the pool area added for 45 to 100. The rooms you must book there rise with each guest band, so ask which band your number falls in.

### Does a venue package include the legal paperwork?

Some venues include it in their package. At others, such as Canaves, preparation of the wedding documents is an option to request, so ask each venue what is covered and which fees sit outside its price.

### Can Australians legally marry in Santorini?

Yes. Canaves lists civil and religious ceremonies among its ceremony types, and a marriage valid where it took place, and valid under Australian law, is in most cases recognised in Australia. You may need a Certificate of No Impediment, so prepare documents well ahead.
