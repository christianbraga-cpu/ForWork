# Seminyak venue booking checklist

Confirm every item below **in writing** with each venue on your shortlist before you sign or pay a deposit. Anything the guide marks "Not published" belongs here. No amounts are given on purpose: ask each venue for its own written quote.

## For every venue

### Capacity and spaces
- What is the confirmed guest capacity for the ceremony space and the reception space, seated and standing?
- Which named spaces can be used for the ceremony and which for the reception?
- Can the ceremony and dinner happen on the same site, and how do guests move between them?

### Ceremony type and legal status
- Is the ceremony you are booking legal or symbolic (non-legal)?
- If symbolic, does the venue help with the separate legal marriage, or must you arrange it yourselves?
- Can you bring your own celebrant or religious officiant?

### Beach access
- Is the ceremony space on the venue's own grounds or on the public beach in front of it?
- How do guests reach the sand, and how is the space kept clear of the public?
- Are there tide, permit or time-of-day limits on a beach ceremony?

### Exclusive use and privacy
- Can your spaces, or the whole property, be closed to other guests? What exactly does private hire cover?
- For a pool club or venue open to the public: how does private hire work around public opening hours?
- Which areas are reserved for wedding guests only?

### Room blocks
- How many rooms, suites or villas can be held for your guests, and until what date?
- What happens to unbooked rooms in the block, and is that written into the contract?

### Deposits and getting them back
- How much deposit is due, when, and when does it become non-refundable?
- If the venue cancels, changes ownership, closes or cannot host on your date, how and when is your deposit returned? Is that written into the contract?
- Whose name is on the contract?

### Currency
- In what currency is the contract written and paid?
- Who carries exchange-rate risk between deposit and final payment?
- Are service charge and tax included in the quote?

### Cancellation
- What does the cancellation clause cover, including weather, flooding, volcanic disruption and flight cancellations?
- What are the cancellation and date-change terms on your side, and the deadlines for each?

### Step-free access
- Is there a step-free route from guest rooms and arrival points to the ceremony and reception?
- Are accessible rooms, bathrooms and transport available for guests who need them?

### Insurance timing
- When should travel insurance be in place relative to your first deposit, and does it cover the wedding itself?
- What does the venue's own insurance cover if the wedding is moved or cancelled?
- Do outside vendors need their own insurance to work on site?

### Security and privacy
- How is the ceremony screened from the public beach and passers-by?
- How loud are neighbouring beach clubs at your ceremony and dinner times?
- What are the rules on drones, outside photographers and guest social media posts?
- Can your own security or close-protection team work with the venue's staff, and who is the contact?

### Wet-season plan
- What is the covered or indoor fallback for each space, and is it held for you on the day?
- Who makes the weather call, and how far ahead?
- What happens to guest transport if roads flood in heavy rain?

### Noise and timing
- What are the music and noise curfews for beachfront and indoor spaces?

## Venue-specific items

- **W Bali Seminyak:** dedicated ceremony space; wedding capacity of the Great Room; room count; room blocks; exclusive use.
- **The Oberoi, Bali:** room or villa count; exclusive use; reception options beyond Bale Banjar.
- **Alila Seminyak:** named ceremony spaces; wedding (not general gathering) capacity; total room count; exclusive use.
- **The Seminyak Beach Resort & Spa:** reception capacity; total room count; exclusive use; packages are non-legal, so plan the legal marriage separately.
- **Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach:** which spaces take the ceremony and which the reception; room block terms; exclusive use; packaged celebrant ceremony is non-legal.
- **The Elysian:** guest capacity; villa count; exclusive use; what the Wedding Package includes.
- **Mrs Sippy Bali:** ceremony options; capacity; private hire around public opening hours; music curfew; exclusive use.

## Legal steps: questions for the Embassy and the civil registry

### Australian Embassy in Indonesia or the Consulate-General in Bali
- What do we need to apply for a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI), and how do we book an appointment?
- How long does the CNI take to issue, and how close to the wedding should we apply, given registries do not normally accept one issued more than three months before?
- Will our marriage be recognised in Australia, and is there anything we should do on return?

### Indonesian Embassy or Consulate-General
- Which religions are recognised for marriage, and what proof of religion do we need?
- Do both of us need to hold the same religion, and how is that confirmed?
- Do any documents need translation, legalisation or apostille?

### Civil Registry Office (Catatan Sipil) where we will marry
- Which documents do you require in this province (CNI, passports, birth certificates, proof any previous marriage has ended, photos), and in what format?
- How far ahead must we lodge the Notice of Intention to Marry?
- Does the religious ceremony need to happen first, and who must officiate?
- How and when is the marriage registered, and when do we receive the certificate?
- Do we need witnesses, and what documents must they bring?

### A lawyer
- Will our marriage be legally valid in Indonesia and recognised in Australia?
