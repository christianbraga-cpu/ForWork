## Cross-region summary
- Intent: head term is transactional everywhere; the informational sub-intent is 'is it real', 'is it worth it', 'downside', 'value', 'how to tell'.
- Pricing shown: almost every result shows prices or percentage savings. We show none (brief rule); that is also a differentiator.
- Top concern: is it real and does it hold value; secondary: what is it called, can someone tell, is it legal.
- Gap we can own: no top result is neutral. None cites the FTC naming rule or Australian Consumer Law; none qualifies 'sustainable/ethical' claims; none frames heirloom and sentiment as values. A non-seller 'worth it if / think twice if' page is unique in the AU SERP.
