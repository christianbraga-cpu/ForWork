Some couples want to speak for five minutes. Others would rather say something true, say it well and hand the moment back to the ceremony. Short wedding vows suit that instinct, and a few honest lines can carry as much weight as a long speech.

Below are 20 original examples, each written to be spoken in well under a minute, grouped by tone and occasion. One is three promises: "I will listen. I will laugh with you, often. And I will show up every day, especially the hard ones." There is a fill-in-the-blanks template, a few delivery notes, and the legal words you say alongside your own. For the full method, see our guide on [how to write your own wedding vows](/how-to-write-wedding-vows).

## Key takeaways

- Each example here reads in under a minute, and 1 to 2 minutes is the most we suggest for any vow, so read yours aloud and time it.
- In Australia, when your celebrant is not a minister of religion, it is sufficient for each of you to say the words in section 45(2) of the Marriage Act 1961, or words to that effect, in front of the celebrant and witnesses. <!-- fact_101 -->
- A strong short vow needs only a name, one specific detail, two or three promises and a closing line.
- If you marry legally overseas, the legal wording follows that country, so ask your celebrant or registrar early.

## How long should short wedding vows be?

<!-- IMAGE SLOT vow-cards: Still life of two handwritten vow cards or a small vow booklet on linen with rings and a pen, soft window light, no people, no legible real names, no identifiable venue. Preferred: licensed or own photography. -->

Our advice is to keep vows to 1 to 2 minutes each. That is long enough to say something personal and short enough that nobody, including you, loses the thread.

Every example in this guide runs well under a minute; treat 2 minutes as the ceiling, not the target.

Don't count words. Read your vows aloud at the pace you would use in front of guests, with pauses, and time yourself on your phone. Then ask your partner to do the same so the two of you land at a similar length.

## What are some good short wedding vows?

<!-- IMAGE SLOT destination-ceremony: Real, licensed photo of a couple saying vows outdoors at a named destination (clifftop, garden or beach), golden hour, guests seated, view in frame. Consenting couple and guests. Never AI. -->

Each of these is our own writing, and yours to adapt. Swap in names, change a detail, cut a line. Where you see [husband/wife/partner], choose the word that fits you. If your formal vows follow a faith or family tradition, ask your celebrant where personal lines fit.

### Classic and timeless

If you love the shape of [traditional wedding vows](/expert-advice/traditional-wedding-vows) but want words of your own, start here.

**1.** "[Name], I choose you today, and I will keep choosing you. I promise to be honest with you when it is easy and when it is hard, to stand beside you in every season, and to make our home a place where you are always welcome. Everything I have, I share with you. Everything I am, I offer you. From this day, I am yours."

**2.** "I take you as my partner and my closest friend. I promise to listen before I answer, to forgive quickly and to celebrate you out loud. I will hold your hand through the ordinary days as gladly as I hold it today. This is my promise, made in front of the people we love, and I will keep it for life."

**3.** "Today I give you my word. I will be faithful, patient and kind. I will care for you when you are unwell and cheer for you when you are winning. When we disagree, I will remember we are on the same side. I love you, and I promise to love you well."

### Romantic

**4.** "Before you, I thought I knew what home felt like. Then you made coffee in my kitchen one Sunday morning, and I understood. I promise to keep finding new reasons to fall for you, to notice the small things, and to say the words often, not only on days like this. I want every Sunday morning with you."

**5.** "I loved you first for your laugh, then for your courage, and now for a thousand reasons I could never fit into a minute. I promise to be gentle with your heart, brave with my own, and present for every chapter ahead. You are my favourite person, and today I get to call you my [husband/wife/partner]."

**6.** "I promise you the long view. Not only today, with everyone dressed up and the light just right, but the Tuesday nights, the airport queues and the years our hair turns grey. I will reach for your hand in all of them. I am so proud to marry you."

### Modern and simple

**7.** "[Name], you are the one I want beside me. I promise to tell you the truth, to share the last slice, and to make time for us when life gets loud. I will back your plans as if they were my own. I love you. Let's do this."

**8.** "Three promises. I will listen. I will laugh with you, often. And I will show up every day, especially the hard ones. That is all of it, and it is everything. I love you, [Name], and I am ready."

**9.** "I don't need a long speech to say this: I am all in. Your family is my family, your goals are my goals, and your bad days are mine to help carry. I know how lucky I am, and I promise never to take you for granted."

### Light and funny

**10.** "I promise to love you, to look after you, and to pretend I don't notice when you steal the covers. I will laugh at your jokes, even the third time. I will let you choose the restaurant, unless you can't decide, in which case I will choose and you will change your mind. In all seriousness, I can't wait for every year ahead with you."

**11.** "I vow to share the remote, to learn the name of every plant you bring home, and to never again say 'it's only a short drive' when it isn't. Most of all, I promise to make you laugh on the days you need it most. Life with you is the adventure I would choose every time."

**12.** "Our dog approved of you before I did, and she has excellent judgement. I promise to walk her when it's raining, to answer your messages with more than a thumbs up, and to keep dancing badly in the kitchen with you for as long as our knees allow. I love you."

### Short vows for him

**13.** "[Name], you are the steadiest person I know, and somehow also the silliest. I promise to be your partner in every plan, to notice the quiet ways you look after everyone, and to look after you just as well. I will cheer for you, argue fairly and love you without holding back. Today I am proud to call you my husband."

**14.** "You made room for me in your life without hesitating. I promise to do the same for you, every day: to be patient on the slow days, honest on the hard ones and grateful on all of them. I love who you are and who you are becoming. I choose you, now and always."

### Short vows for her

**15.** "[Name], you walk into a room and make people feel at home. I promise to give you that same feeling for the rest of our lives. I will support your ambitions, protect your peace and always tell you the truth. I will love you loudly and quietly. Today I am proud to call you my wife."

**16.** "You are the bravest person I know, and the kindest. I promise to stand beside you when you leap and hold you steady when you land. I will make you tea when you are tired and make room for you as you grow. I love you, and I always will."

### For a destination ceremony

Written for the couple who has flown everyone somewhere far from home. If that is your plan, speak with [Wedded Concierge](/wonderland-concierge?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=short-wedding-vows&utm_content=secondary) early, and keep the words for yourselves.

**17.** "We travelled a long way to stand here, and I would travel twice as far to stand anywhere with you. I promise that wherever we live and wherever we wander, home will be the two of us. Thank you to everyone who crossed oceans to be here. [Name], I am yours, in this place and every place after it."

**18.** "Far from home, with the people we love around us, I make you this promise: I will stay beside you, wherever we are. When we are lost, I will find the way with you. When we are tired, I will carry the bags. I will remember this view, but mostly I will remember your face today."

### Renewal

<!-- IMAGE SLOT renewal: Real, licensed photo of an established couple renewing vows, intimate setting, relaxed and joyful, small guest group or none, destination or garden. Consenting couple. Never AI. -->

For a renewal, the length of your marriage is the detail that does the work. Our [vow renewal ideas](/expert-advice/vow-renewal-ideas) cover the rest of the day.

**19.** "[Number] years ago, I promised you forever without knowing what it would ask of us. Now I know. It asked for patience, laughter and a great deal of forgiveness, and you gave all three. Knowing everything I know now, I would choose you again without a second thought. So I am. I choose you, again."

**20.** "We have moved house, raised [children, a dog, a business], and weathered more than either of us expected. Through all of it, you have been my constant. Today I renew my promise: to keep listening, keep laughing and keep holding your hand. The first time, I hoped. This time, I know."

## How do you write a wedding vow in under a minute?

Five parts are enough. Fill in the brackets, read it aloud, and cut anything that feels like padding.

> "[Partner's name], when we [a specific first moment or memory], I knew [what you realised about them]. Today I promise to [promise one], to [promise two], and to [promise three]. I love the way you [a small, specific habit of theirs]. Whatever comes, [a closing line that sounds like you]. I am yours."

A few rules keep it tight:

- **One memory, not a history.** A single detail, such as a road trip or a burnt dinner, says more than a timeline.
- **Promises you can keep.** "I will make the coffee on weekdays" is more convincing than a promise about the stars.
- **End on a short line.** The last words are the ones guests hear, so give them room.

## What words do you legally have to say when you marry in Australia?

As at November 2026, the form of the ceremony sits in section 45 of the Marriage Act 1961, and the celebrant's explanation sits in section 46. <!-- fact_01 -->

When your celebrant is not a minister of religion, it is sufficient for each of you to say to the other, in the presence of the celebrant and the witnesses, the words set out in [section 45(2)](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ma196185/s45.html) of the [Marriage Act 1961](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A07402/latest/text):

> "I call upon the persons here present to witness that I, A.B. (or C.D.), take thee, C.D. (or A.B.), to be my lawful wedded wife (or husband, or spouse)"; or words to that effect. <!-- fact_101 -->

Before the marriage is solemnised, a celebrant who is not a minister of religion of a recognised denomination says these words to you, in the presence of the witnesses, under [section 46(1)](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/cth/consol_act/ma196185/s46.html) of the [Act](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A07402/latest/text):

> "I am duly authorised by law to solemnise marriages according to law. Before you are joined in marriage in my presence and in the presence of these witnesses, I am to remind you of the solemn and binding nature of the relationship into which you are now about to enter. Marriage, according to law in Australia, is the union of 2 people to the exclusion of all others, voluntarily entered into for life."; or words to that effect. <!-- fact_102 -->

When a minister of religion marries you, the ceremony may follow any form and ceremony recognised as sufficient by their religious body or organisation. <!-- fact_100 --> See [who can perform a marriage](/expert-advice/who-can-perform-a-marriage) for the difference between celebrants.

The law allows these words "or words to that effect". Ask your celebrant how your own vows fit around the legal ones, and raise it when you lodge your [notice of intended marriage](/expert-advice/notice-of-intended-marriage).

### Marrying overseas

If you marry legally in another country, the legal wording follows that country. In New Zealand, [couples can write their own vows](https://www.govt.nz/organisations/births-deaths-and-marriages/information-for-marriage-celebrants/performing-a-marriage-or-civil-union-ceremony/) as long as each says words to the effect of "I [name] take you [name] to be my legal wife/husband/partner". <!-- fact_02 --> Our guide to [getting married in New Zealand](/destination-weddings/get-married-in-new-zealand) covers the rest of the process.

Decide early where you will marry legally, and let the celebrant or registrar there confirm the words; the place, the guests and the travel are the bigger puzzle. ## How do you say your vows with confidence?

**Cards or memory.** Write your vows on a small, sturdy card in large print. If you prefer to speak from memory, keep the card in a pocket or with your celebrant anyway.

**The order on the day.** Agree with your celebrant who speaks first, where the legal words sit and when the rings come in. Our guide to [what happens in a wedding ceremony](/expert-advice/what-happens-in-a-wedding-ceremony) covers the rest of the order of service. If you are also choosing [processional songs](/expert-advice/wedding-processional-songs), set the running order of music and words together at the rehearsal.

**A microphone outdoors.** Wind, waves and fountains swallow voices. Ask your venue whether a microphone is available for the ceremony, who sets it up, and whether you can test it at the rehearsal. For a large outdoor ceremony, ask too whether sound reaches every seat.

**Breathe.** Pause after your partner's name. Look up at the end of each promise. Under a minute feels long when you are standing there, and that is fine.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should wedding vows be?

Each example here reads in under a minute; our advice is 1 to 2 minutes at most for any vow. Read yours aloud and time it before the day.

### Can we write our own vows in Australia?

When your celebrant is not a minister of religion, the Marriage Act 1961 makes it sufficient for each of you to say the section 45(2) words "or words to that effect". <!-- fact_101 --> Ask your celebrant how your own vows fit around them.

### What are good short vows for him and for her?

See examples 13 to 16 above. Examples 13 and 15 end on "husband" or "wife", so change that word to suit you; 14 and 16 suit anyone as written.

### Do we both say the same vows?

You can say matching vows or write your own separately. If you write separately, agree on a similar length so neither of you feels outweighed, and share them with your celebrant before the day.

### What are the shortest wedding vows?

Example 8 is the shortest in this guide: three promises and a closing line. For the legal words in Australia, see the legal section above.
