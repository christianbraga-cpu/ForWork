## What ranks
- "wedding hashtag generator" is tool intent: page one in all three cities is mostly tools, Pinterest, tool listicles and video; only 2 or 3 of 9 results are ideas or how-to articles. AI overview on every query.
- "wedding hashtag ideas" and "funny wedding hashtags": mostly Pinterest, forums and social.
- "how to make a wedding hashtag": editorial how-to pages; the realistic win for an ideas page without a tool.
- "destination wedding hashtags": no page owns it.

**Expectation:** without a tool, weaker for "generator" itself; realistic targets are the how-to queries (already #17 to #24 US), destination and ideas queries.
