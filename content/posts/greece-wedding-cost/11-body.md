The Greece wedding cost you will be quoted depends on decisions only you can make: which island, what kind of venue, which month and day, how many guests fly in, and how much of their travel and accommodation you cover. A single figure blends all of those together and says very little about your own wedding, so this guide gives no headline figure: the total depends on those drivers, and one written brief sent to several venues gets you a real number.

Below is what shapes the total, what a package bundles, how paying in euro from Australia works and what to ask before you request a number. For the wider picture, start with our [Greece wedding venues and destinations](/destinations/europe/greece).

## Key takeaways

- The total for a wedding in Greece is set by island, venue type, season, guest numbers and how much guest travel you cover, so ask every venue to quote against the same written brief.
- A civil wedding has its own paperwork: as at November 2026, Rhodes asks for documents officially translated into Greek, an Apostille on Australian documents and fees at several steps (general information, not legal advice).
- Ask each supplier which currency the quote, deposit and balance are invoiced in, when each payment falls due and what happens to money paid if plans change.
- As general information as at November 2026, an overseas business selling directly to Australian consumers must follow the Australian Consumer Law, but a remedy can be difficult to obtain in practice, so read the cancellation terms before you pay.

## Why there is no single Greece wedding cost

One couple marries at a town hall with a long lunch for close family. Another takes a clifftop venue for the weekend, hosts a welcome dinner and covers ferries for every guest. Same island, same month, and neither total helps the other.

Write down your wedding first: island, date, guest list, events and what you will pay for on your guests' behalf. Send that brief to every venue and planner, so the quotes describe the same wedding.

### Island and venue type

A caldera terrace, a beach club, an olive grove estate and a hotel ballroom are priced differently, and each island has its own mix. Compare quotes for the islands on your shortlist against the same brief.

Our guides on [how to plan a Santorini wedding](/destination-weddings/santorini-wedding), [our Mykonos wedding guide](/destination-weddings/mykonos-wedding) and [planning a wedding in Crete](/destination-weddings/wedding-in-crete) each set out what moves the cost on that island.

### Exclusive use

Some venues close to other guests for your wedding; others host your ceremony while the hotel carries on around you. Ask whether exclusive use, where your party is the only one on site, is offered, for how many nights, and whether it needs a minimum number of rooms or guests. Ask, too, whether another wedding can be held there the same day, and whether the property can stay closed to the public for the whole celebration.

### Season and day of the week

As at November 2026, Smartraveller says heatwaves are frequent in Greece over the summer months, and wildfires occur in the extended summer period from June to September. In the May to October tourist season, expect high temperatures and large crowds at popular seaports. Each carries a cost: shade, water for guests, a weather plan, a buffer day. Our guide to [when to marry in Greece, month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece) goes further.

Ask whether the venue's rates change between months, and between a Saturday and a weekday. The legal ceremony can set the day too: as general information as at November 2026, the town hall ceremony in Rhodes takes place every Wednesday and Friday.

For a Greek Orthodox wedding, settle the church date first. The Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of Australia does not permit weddings during several periods of the church year, including the first half of August and from Great Lent until Easter Sunday. Confirm the calendar with the parish in Greece.

### Guest numbers

Guest numbers touch catering, drinks, transfers and the space you need. Ask which items are charged per guest, whether there is a minimum spend for your date, and when the final number is locked. For a large guest list, ask what size of event the venue has hosted.

### The legal route and its fees

This is general information as at November 2026, not legal advice. Check the document list, notice period and ceremony days with the municipality where you will marry.

Rhodes is the worked example, because its municipality publishes the list. On another island, ask that municipality for its own.

- **The application:** a joint application, a certificate of no impediment to marriage naming the person you will marry, passport copies, a form with your details and a municipal fee.
- **Documents from abroad:** recent originals, officially translated into Greek, with an Apostille on those from outside the European Union.
- **Before the day:** originals go to the registrar two days before the wedding, and a fee applies at the registry office.

For the Australian documents:

- The [Australian Embassy in Athens](https://greece.embassy.gov.au/athn/notarial.html) issues certificates of no impediment and applies apostilles to original Australian public documents, for a fee.
- DFAT also legalises Australian documents for a fee, and processing is slower in peak holiday periods.

A Greek Orthodox wedding adds its own documents. The Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of Australia asks for a baptismal certificate, and the parish priest gives directions on documents and church fees, which vary by circumstances. For a church wedding in Greece, check the documents, dates and fees with the parish where you will marry.

For the steps in order, read [getting legally married in Greece as Australians](/destination-weddings/greece-wedding-venues#getting-legally-married-in-greece-as-australians-in-brief) and [Smartraveller's guide to getting married overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas).

### Getting there and getting around

Flights from Australia come first, then the islands. As at November 2026, Smartraveller says ferries and large catamarans carry visitors to the Greek islands, that services can be delayed or cancelled in bad weather, mainly due to strong winds, and that a coach or taxi should be arranged in advance. Ask early:

- Which airport or port is closest to the venue, and how do guests get between them?
- Who books and pays for transfers on the day, and for arrivals and departures?
- If guests are island hopping, who carries the cost of a cancelled ferry and an extra night?
- Can older or less mobile guests reach the ceremony, the reception and their rooms without steep steps?

### Accommodation for guests

Decide early who pays for guest rooms: the whole stay, the wedding night, or none. Ask how many rooms the venue has, whether a minimum stay applies, and when unbooked rooms are released. A civil ceremony can add nights for the couple: in Rhodes, as general information as at November 2026, you return to the registry office the next working day to sign and have the marriage certificate drawn up.

### Welcome and farewell events

A welcome dinner, a boat day, a farewell lunch: each has its own venue, catering and transport. Ask whether your wedding venue can host them under the same agreement.

### The core of a venue package

Ask whether each item is inside the package, an add-on or not offered:

- **Ceremony setup:** chairs, an arch or arbour, aisle styling and sound.
- **Reception venue hire:** the space, the hours and when music must stop.
- **Catering and drinks:** the menu, the drinks package and its duration.
- **Coordination on the day:** who runs the timeline, named in writing.
- **Paperwork support:** help with documents and the registrar, and who pays official fees.
- **Styling and florals:** what is included, and what can be changed.
- **Accommodation:** whether any rooms are part of the package.

### Items quoted separately

Ask about each of these before you compare totals:

- Photography and film.
- Music, from a ceremony musician to a band.
- Hair and make-up.
- Transfers for guests and the couple.
- Welcome and farewell events.
- Municipal, embassy or church fees, translations and Apostilles.
- Taxes and service charges, and whether they sit inside the quoted price.
- Overtime if the evening runs late.

### Euro quotes and the Australian dollar

Ask whether the quote, deposit and balance are invoiced in euro or AUD. If you pay in euro, each payment's cost in AUD depends on the exchange rate on the day it leaves your account, so ask your bank how it charges for international transfers and allow for the rate to move.

### Deposits and when payments fall due

Ask for the payment schedule in writing, with dates for the deposit, each instalment and the balance. Ask whether the deposit secures the date, whether it is refundable, and what happens to it if you postpone, or if the supplier cancels or stops trading.

### Contracts in English

Ask for every contract in English, with a copy you keep. If the binding version is in Greek, ask which version prevails if the two differ, and have anything unclear read before you sign.

### Written quotes

Ask for a written quote listing every inclusion, the guest number it assumes, the date and the payment terms. Only written quotes built on the same brief compare line by line.

### Your consumer rights

This is general information about Australian law as at November 2026, not legal advice. Under the [ACCC consumer guarantees](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees), services must be carried out with an acceptable level of care and skill and be fit for any particular purpose you tell the provider about. These basic rights can't be taken away by anything a business says or does.

When a service has a [major problem](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought/repair-replace-refund-cancel), you can choose to cancel and get a refund, or keep the contract and pay a lower price. A refund may not be in full, because you pay a reasonable amount for work already done. A change of mind gives no right to a refund under the consumer guarantees, though a business with a change of mind policy must follow it.

An overseas business selling directly to Australian consumers must follow the Australian Consumer Law, but a remedy can be hard to obtain in practice; the consumer protection agency where the seller is based may help.

[Smartraveller's advice for Greece](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/greece) is to buy comprehensive travel insurance before you leave and read the Product Disclosure Statement closely. Australia and Greece have no agreement covering medical care for tourists, so visitors pay for all medical care, including hospital stays and evacuation.

### Questions for the venue

- What is included at our guest number, and what changes if it rises or falls?
- What changes in peak season, or on a Saturday rather than a weekday?
- Which items are charged per guest, and which are fixed?
- Are taxes and service charges inside the quoted price?
- Which payments are in euro, and when does each fall due?
- What are the cancellation and postponement terms?
- Who is our contact if something goes wrong, and how do we reach them out of hours?

### Questions for the planner

- Which suppliers sit inside your quote, and which do we contract directly?
- How is your fee set, and is it agreed in writing before work begins?
- Who arranges the legal paperwork, and who pays the official fees?
- What happens if a ferry is cancelled or the weather turns on the day?

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it expensive to have a wedding in Greece?

It can be either. A town hall ceremony with lunch for close family and a clifftop weekend with ferries for every guest sit at very different ends. Island, venue, season, guest numbers and guest travel decide where yours lands.

### How much does a wedding in Greece cost?

This guide gives no headline figure, because no single number holds until your island, venue, date, guest list, legal route and guest travel are set. For a real one, send each venue the same written brief, then compare the written quotes line by line.

### What affects the cost of a Santorini or Mykonos wedding?

The same drivers apply on every island: venue, exclusive use, date, guests, transfers and accommodation. Compare quotes for each island against one brief.

### What sits outside a Greek wedding package?

Ask about photography, music, hair and make-up, transfers, extra events, official fees, taxes and overtime. Treat anything not written into the package as not included.

### How much does it cost to marry legally in Greece?

As general information as at November 2026, fees apply at several steps, and each body sets its own. In Rhodes, a fee applies at the registry office, the Australian Embassy in Athens charges for notarial services, and DFAT charges to legalise Australian documents. Confirm each with the authority.

### Can foreigners get married in Greece?

Yes. As general information as at November 2026, the Municipality of Rhodes, for example, publishes the documents foreign nationals need for a civil marriage. Smartraveller says an overseas marriage can't be registered in Australia, but it is generally recognised here if it was valid under the law of the country where it took place and would have been valid under Australian law had it taken place in Australia.
