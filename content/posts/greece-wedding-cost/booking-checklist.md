# Greece wedding budget checklist

Use this when you write your brief and compare quotes for a wedding in Greece. Send every venue and planner the same written brief, and ask for each answer in the written quote rather than in a phone call. Fill in each line from the written quotes. Anything not written down counts as not included.

## Your brief, before you ask for quotes
- [ ] Island or islands on the shortlist: ____________
- [ ] Venue type (terrace, beach, estate, hotel, other): ____________
- [ ] Date, and any alternative dates: ____________
- [ ] Day of the week: ____________
- [ ] Guest numbers: ____________
- [ ] Legal route (civil, Greek Orthodox church, other): ____________
- [ ] Events (welcome, wedding day, farewell, other): ____________
- [ ] What you will pay for on your guests' behalf (travel, transfers, rooms, none): ____________

## Questions for the venue
- [ ] What is included at our guest number, and what changes if it rises or falls?
- [ ] What changes in peak season, or on a Saturday rather than a weekday?
- [ ] Which items are charged per guest, and which are fixed?
- [ ] Is there a minimum spend for our date, and when is the final guest number locked?
- [ ] What size of event have you hosted?
- [ ] Is exclusive use offered, for how many nights, and does it need a minimum number of rooms or guests?
- [ ] Can another wedding be held here the same day, and can the property stay closed to the public for the whole celebration?
- [ ] How many rooms do you have, does a minimum stay apply, and when are unbooked rooms released?
- [ ] Can you host a welcome or farewell event under the same agreement?
- [ ] Which airport or port is closest, and how do guests get between them and the venue?
- [ ] Who books and pays for transfers on the day, and for arrivals and departures?
- [ ] Can older or less mobile guests reach the ceremony, the reception and their rooms without steep steps?
- [ ] What is the weather plan for heat or wind?
- [ ] Are taxes and service charges inside the quoted price?
- [ ] Which payments are in euro, and when does each fall due?
- [ ] Does the deposit secure the date, is it refundable, and what happens to it if we postpone, or if you cancel or stop trading?
- [ ] What are the cancellation and postponement terms?
- [ ] Is the contract in English, with a copy we keep? If the binding version is in Greek, which version prevails if the two differ?
- [ ] Who is our contact if something goes wrong, and how do we reach them out of hours?

## Questions for the planner
- [ ] Which suppliers sit inside your quote, and which do we contract directly?
- [ ] How is your fee set, and is it agreed in writing before work begins?
- [ ] Who arranges the legal paperwork, and who pays the official fees?
- [ ] If guests are island hopping, who carries the cost of a cancelled ferry and an extra night?
- [ ] What happens if a ferry is cancelled or the weather turns on the day?
- [ ] Which currency is your quote, deposit and balance invoiced in?
- [ ] Who is our contact on the day, and who is accountable if a supplier lets us down?

## Questions for the municipality where you will marry
- [ ] What is the document list for a civil marriage of foreign nationals?
- [ ] Which documents must be officially translated into Greek, and which need an Apostille?
- [ ] How recent must the original documents be?
- [ ] What is the notice period, and when must the originals reach the registrar?
- [ ] On which days are civil ceremonies held?
- [ ] Which fees apply, at which steps, and how are they paid?
- [ ] When do we return to sign and have the marriage certificate drawn up?

## Questions for the parish in Greece (Greek Orthodox wedding)
- [ ] Which documents does the parish need, including a baptismal certificate?
- [ ] Which dates are available, and which periods of the church year are closed to weddings?
- [ ] Which church fees apply, and how are they paid?
- [ ] Does the parish priest need anything from our parish in Australia?

## Questions for the Australian side
- [ ] Australian Embassy in Athens: which notarial services do we need, and what does each require?
- [ ] DFAT: which documents need an Apostille, and how long does processing take in peak holiday periods?
- [ ] Bank: how are international transfers charged?
- [ ] Insurer: what does the policy cover, and what does the Product Disclosure Statement exclude?
