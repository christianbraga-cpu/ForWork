## Cross-region summary
- Guest counts: snippets and the New York AI Overview frame weddings around 30 to 50 guests; nothing at a larger luxury scale.
- Pricing shown: USD ranges everywhere, all unsourced and conflicting. We publish no figures (series rule).
- Top concern: "how much" (PAA in all 5 cities), then "how hard / requirements" and legal-for-foreigners.
- Gap we can own: no result in any city links TSE / Registro Civil or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and none is written for Australians (apostille on Australian documents, Spanish translation, recognition at home, consular help from Mexico City per Smartraveller). Top AU results are 2020 to 2023 and thin.
