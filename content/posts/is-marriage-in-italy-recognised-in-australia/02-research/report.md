## Cross-region summary
- Guest counts: none of the ranking pages discuss guest numbers. This is a legal query.
- Pricing shown: only Floe (comune EUR 600 to 7,000+, admin about EUR 500) and celebrant or divorce fees. Per the brief, we write "a fee applies" and show no figures.
- Top concern: "Will it count at home, and what do we do when we get back?" The Facebook threads that rank first in 8 of 10 locations are couples asking exactly this. One title reads "Has anyone from Australia got legally married in Italy? ... was it fairly straightforward to have it recognised back".
- Gap we can own: no page connects the Italian documents (certificate, multilingual extract, apostille, NAATI translation) to what Australian agencies accept. No page states the Passport Office rule: if you were born in Australia and married overseas, the Passport Office always needs an Australian name change certificate. No page puts the NSW and Victoria surname rule in an Italy context. No page explains the legal effect of a later Australian ceremony or what Australia does with an Italian unione civile. The top results are either Italian process guides or generic law-firm explainers. None combines both sides.
- Regional notes: Australian SERPs mix official sources with Italy planners. Other English SERPs are led by Facebook and generic Australian law-firm pages, which means Australians abroad and mixed-nationality couples. Arabic SERPs have migration intent.
- AI overview is present in every location. Answer in the first 60 words, with both government sources named.
