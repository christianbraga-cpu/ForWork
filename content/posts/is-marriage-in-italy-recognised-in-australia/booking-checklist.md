# Booking checklist: Italian marriage recognition in Australia

Not published by the authorities, so not publishing blockers. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing for the couple's dates.

- Prefettura apostille appointment for the province of marriage (processing time not published; peak May to September per the Rome embassy).
- Comune of marriage: confirm when the full marriage certificate is issued after the ceremony, and arrange a translator-interpreter if either partner is not fluent in Italian (see sister pack).
- Translation: book a NAATI-certified translator in Australia, or ask the Australian embassy in Rome for its list of approved translators.
