# Questions to ask about any planner's fee

These questions apply to any honeymoon planner, whoever you speak with. Ask them early, and ask for the answers in writing. They are prompts for a first conversation, not answers. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the publishing blockers are image credits and licences (5 slots), the `/honeymoon-ideas` folder and Honeymoon Ideas category existing in the CMS, and the two draft internal links.

As at November 2026. This is general information, not legal or financial advice.

## How the fee is charged

- [ ] Do you charge a flat fee for planning?
- [ ] Is any part of your fee a percentage of the cost of the trip?
- [ ] Is your fee a mix of these, and when is each part payable?
- [ ] If you are also planning our wedding, is the honeymoon quoted with the wedding planning or separately?
- [ ] Will you show your fee and the cost of flights, stays and experiences separately?

## What the fee covers

- [ ] What is the scope of the planning, and where does it end?
- [ ] What will be booked on our behalf?
- [ ] Are changes after booking part of the fee, and is anything charged separately?
- [ ] Who do we contact while we are travelling?

## Commissions and mark-ups

- [ ] Do you receive commissions from the suppliers you book, and will you tell us which?
- [ ] Does any supplier price you quote include a mark-up, and can we see the supplier's own price?
- [ ] Is every commission and mark-up disclosed to us in writing?

## Inclusions in writing

- [ ] What is included in each booking (every stay, flight and experience)? Please confirm the inclusions in writing before we pay anything.

## Terms, payments and cancellation

- [ ] What are your terms, and what are the terms of each airline, hotel or operator? Please show us both sets before we book.
- [ ] If any terms are unclear or missing, will you ask the provider to confirm them in writing, and do different terms apply to different travel periods?
- [ ] When does each payment fall due, to you and to each supplier?
- [ ] What happens to your fee if we change our plans, or cancel, and is any part of it refundable?
- [ ] What are the change and cancellation terms for every supplier?

## Currency and exchange rates

- [ ] Is the quote in Australian dollars?
- [ ] If a supplier is paid in another currency, what exchange rate sits behind the AUD quote, is it the rate we actually get, and is it guaranteed?
- [ ] What transfer fees or margin apply, and does the receiver pay fees too?
- [ ] Is the money transfer service registered with AUSTRAC?

## Insurance and protection

- [ ] When should we take out travel insurance? The earlier we buy, the more likely we are to be covered if something happens before we leave.
- [ ] Have we read the Product Disclosure Statement, so we know what is covered?
- [ ] Does the policy cover travel provider or agent insolvency? It is commonly left out of cancellation cover.
- [ ] Do you hold insolvency insurance, and what happens to our money if you or a provider becomes insolvent?
- [ ] Do the contract terms ask us to give up chargeback rights? If so, ask why before agreeing.
