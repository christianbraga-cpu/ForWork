# Questions to put to any planner or agent

These questions apply to any honeymoon planner or travel agent, whatever title the business uses. Put the same questions to everyone you are considering, ask for the answers in writing and set them side by side. They are prompts for a first conversation, not answers. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the publishing blockers are image credits and licences, the `/honeymoon-ideas` folder and Honeymoon Ideas category existing in the CMS, and the three draft internal links.

As at November 2026. This is general information, not legal or financial advice.

## Before you speak to anyone

- [ ] How much of the planning do we want to hand over: design the trip, book what we have chosen, or check our plan and take on the parts we would rather not?
- [ ] Is the honeymoon straight after the wedding, or a separate trip later?
- [ ] How many stops, flights, time zones and linked bookings does the trip have?

## What they will actually do

- [ ] Will you design the trip, or book what we choose? Will you tell us when something on our list does not suit the season or the route?
- [ ] Who books the flights, who checks that arrivals line up with check-in, and who arranges the days between stays? If only some of it, which parts stay with us?
- [ ] Can you book any hotel or experience we find, or do you work with particular suppliers?
- [ ] Who will we deal with from first call to return?
- [ ] What will you book, and in whose name?
- [ ] Who do we contact while we are travelling, and in which time zone?

## How they are paid

- [ ] Is there a planning fee, and what does it cover?
- [ ] Is it a set amount, a share of the trip, or something else?
- [ ] How is your fee worked out, and is it agreed in writing before work begins?
- [ ] Do you receive anything from the suppliers you book or recommend, and is that disclosed to us?
- [ ] Does any supplier price you quote include a mark-up, and can we see the supplier's own price?
- [ ] When are deposits and the fee payable, who holds the deposits, and is any of it refundable if our plans change?
- [ ] Will the quote be in Australian dollars, with your fee shown separately from the trip?
- [ ] If any payment goes overseas, is the advertised rate the rate we get, is it guaranteed, and what fee or margin is charged on top?

## Terms and what is confirmed in writing

- [ ] Whose terms apply to each booking, and will you show us both sets before we pay?
- [ ] What is confirmed in writing before we commit?
- [ ] Can we see each supplier's terms before we pay, and will you confirm anything unclear in writing?

## When plans change

- [ ] If a provider changes or cancels something, who tells us, and who asks for the replacement or refund?
- [ ] What do your terms say if we cancel or change plans ourselves?
- [ ] While we are away, what is the contact name, contact method and hours, and do we call you, the airline or the hotel first?
- [ ] Have we allowed extra time between connections where timing matters?

## Travel insurance

- [ ] Are we buying the policy early, so we are more likely to be covered if something happens before we leave?
- [ ] Is anything already known before we buy that could affect our travel? It will not be covered.
- [ ] Does the policy cover a travel provider or agent going out of business? It is commonly excluded.
- [ ] Do you hold insolvency insurance?

## When the honeymoon follows a destination wedding

- [ ] Can you plan the honeymoon together with our wedding travel, or only one of them?
- [ ] If only one, who checks that the dates and flights line up?
- [ ] Whose name goes on the honeymoon tickets? Book travel in the name on your passport.
