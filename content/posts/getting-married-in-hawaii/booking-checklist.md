# Hawaii marriage licence checklist

The documents and steps from "How to Get Married in Hawaii Step by Step", in order, plus questions to put to your licence agent, officiant and venue. Requirements come from the Hawaii State Department of Health, the Department of Land and Natural Resources (DLNR), US Customs and Border Protection and Smartraveller; check their pages before you rely on any step. A fee applies to the licence and to certified copies: the Department of Health lists the current amounts. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off).

As at November 2026. This is general information, not legal advice.

## Before you leave Australia

- [ ] Decide on a legal ceremony in Hawaii, or a symbolic ceremony there with the legal marriage at home.
- [ ] Each of you has a valid e-Passport.
- [ ] Check ESTA eligibility under the Visa Waiver Program (stays under 90 days); if not eligible, apply for a visa. Apply before you buy airline tickets.
- [ ] Remind every guest travelling under the Visa Waiver Program to apply for their own ESTA (send it with the save-the-dates).
- [ ] Subscribe to Smartraveller's United States updates for your travel dates.
- [ ] Complete the online marriage licence application together at emrs.ehawaii.gov and pay the fee. Names and dates must match your passports.
- [ ] Write down or print the Authorization Code (Locator ID).
- [ ] Book a licence agent appointment: the Honolulu office (Oahu, Monday to Friday, excluding State holidays, online or by email), or an agent on Hawaii Island, Kauai, Maui or Molokai by phone, or a private agent listed by the Department.
- [ ] Confirm your officiant holds a State of Hawaii performer licence. A celebrant from Australia registers at marriage.ehawaii.gov first.
- [ ] Beach ceremony on State shoreline: apply for a DLNR right-of-entry permit through Wiki Permits, with a Certificate of Liability Insurance.

## Documents to bring to the agent

- [ ] Valid government-issued photo ID for both of you, such as your passports.
- [ ] Proof of age (the agent may ask for a certified birth certificate).
- [ ] Certified divorce decree or death certificate, if a previous marriage ended less than 30 days before you pick up the licence.
- [ ] Certified translation of any document not in English.
- [ ] Certified copy of a legal name change, where it applies.
- [ ] Anything extra the agent asks for at their discretion (for example, a registry statement that there is no record of an earlier marriage).

## In Hawaii

- [ ] Meet the agent together, in person (no proxies), no sooner than 30 days before the ceremony. Plan it for the first days after you land, away from early inter-island flights.
- [ ] Marry within 30 days of picking up the licence, in the State of Hawaii only, or the licence is void. There is no waiting period.
- [ ] Hold the ceremony with your state-licensed officiant.
- [ ] Ask your officiant how and when the marriage is registered with the State.

## After the wedding

- [ ] Order certified copies of the marriage certificate (online, by mail or in person; online is the fast route; government-issued ID needed). Either of you can order.
- [ ] Order more than one copy, and allow up to four to six weeks.
- [ ] Ask whoever will receive the certificate whether they need an Apostille. Hawaii apostille requests are mail-in only.
- [ ] Before using the certificate for a name change, check with your state or territory registry of births, deaths and marriages.
- [ ] Nothing to register in Australia. A recommitment ceremony at home is possible; a second marriage is not.

## Questions for the licence agent

- [ ] Which proof of age do you need from us: is a passport enough, or a certified birth certificate?
- [ ] Do you need any other documents for our circumstances?
- [ ] What should we bring to the appointment besides our IDs and the Authorization Code?
- [ ] How long does the appointment usually take?

## Questions for the officiant

- [ ] Can we see your current State of Hawaii performer licence before we pay a deposit?
- [ ] What does the ceremony itself require on the day, including witnesses?
- [ ] How and when is the marriage registered with the State, and when can we order certified copies?
- [ ] If someone from Australia is marrying us, have they registered at marriage.ehawaii.gov and been licensed?

## Questions for the venue

- [ ] Does your lawn, terrace or beach area need any permit, and who holds it?
- [ ] If the ceremony is on the beach, is it State shoreline (DLNR) or managed by another agency such as the county?
- [ ] Can the ceremony space be closed to the public or booked for exclusive use?
- [ ] How does your security work alongside a private team of our own?
- [ ] How do guests reach the ceremony spot, is there step-free access, and where can someone sit out of the sun?
- [ ] Which inclusions will you confirm in writing before we commit?
