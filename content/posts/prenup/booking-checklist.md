# Prenup checklist: questions to take to your lawyer

These questions sit with the couple and each partner's own family lawyer, so they don't block publishing. They are prompts for a first meeting, not answers. The Wedded Advisor plans the celebration; the agreement and all legal and financial advice stay with you and your lawyers. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy and no prices (nothing for Nic to sign off); the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences (5 slots) and a family lawyer's review of the draft.

As at November 2026. This is general information, not legal advice. Each partner needs their own independent legal advice from an Australian legal practitioner.

## Timing around the wedding

- [ ] When should we start, given our wedding date?
- [ ] Is it better to sign before the wedding, or during the marriage?
- [ ] How long does drafting, review and signing usually take with two separate lawyers?

## What to cover

- [ ] Should our agreement cover all of our financial relationship, or only some issues?
- [ ] How should it deal with property, super, savings, debts and any business?
- [ ] How should it deal with an expected inheritance, or property for children from a previous relationship?
- [ ] Does it need to say anything about spousal maintenance?
- [ ] What should it say about what happens if one of us dies, and how does it sit with our wills?

## Independent advice for each partner

- [ ] Each of us books our own family lawyer (not the same firm).
- [ ] What financial information do we each need to disclose, and in what form?
- [ ] What does the signed statement of advice cover, and when is it given?
- [ ] Should either of us also get financial advice before signing?

## Overseas assets or marrying overseas

- [ ] We plan to sign in Australia and marry overseas: does the overseas ceremony change anything about our agreement?
- [ ] One of us has assets or family in another country: how does the agreement deal with them?
- [ ] If either of us has signed an agreement overseas, does it meet the Australian formal requirements?

## Reviewing after marriage

- [ ] When should we review the agreement (for example after children, a property purchase or an inheritance)?
- [ ] How do we change or end it if our circumstances change?
- [ ] Where are the signed originals kept, and who holds copies?

## Before publishing (Wedded Wonderland)

- [ ] A family lawyer reviews the draft.
- [ ] Planner SME reconfirms the FCFCOA, Legal Aid NSW, Victoria Legal Aid and Moneysmart pages live.
