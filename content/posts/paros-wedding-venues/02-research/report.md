## Cross-region summary

- **Guest counts:** the only figures shown come from a directory quoted in the AI overview. Not used; each venue's own site is the source.
- **Pricing shown:** Tripadvisor titles promise prices, and related searches ask about cost. This post shows no prices, and those searches are not targeted.
- **Top concern:** choosing a setting (villa, beach hotel or estate with a chapel) and an area (the AI overview names Naousa and Golden Beach), then how to marry legally.
- **Gap we can own:** venues grouped by area with the same fields for each, plus a legal section from official sources written for Australians. Nothing ranking does either.
- **recommended_scope:** global. The SERP has the same shape in every city, and the Australian legal steps are what sets this post apart.
