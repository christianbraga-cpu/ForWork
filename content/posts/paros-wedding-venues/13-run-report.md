## How to get married there
- The Municipality of Paros has a civil marriage and registry office but publishes no steps for foreigners. The copy tells couples to check with the municipality and the Greek embassy or consulate, including any fees.
- Churches and chapels named on the municipality's own tourism site are listed.
- Australian steps (certificate of no impediment, notarial services at the Embassy in Athens, recognition in Australia) are from Smartraveller and the Embassy, re-checked live and dated "as at November 2026".
- Nothing implies the Wedded Advisor handles legal, church or religious paperwork. The pack's "Wedded Advisor confirms current requirements" lines were not used.
