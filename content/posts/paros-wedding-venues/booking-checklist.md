# Booking checklist: Paros wedding venues

These details are not published by the venues, so they don't block publishing. The Wedded Advisor confirms each one in writing with the venue (or the authority named) for the couple's dates before anyone commits. The article has no `[[VERIFY]]` markers in the copy; the only publishing blockers are image credits and licences, which sit in the draft note, not here. Each venue entry's "Not published" lines, the source pack's booking checklist lines and the source pack's verify items for the 8 venues are listed below.

## Confirm before quoting

- Astir of Paros: the weddings page was last modified in 2018. Confirm the current wedding terms and inclusions, wedding capacity, reception spaces, and whether ceremonies at the St. Nicholas chapel (and the Panagia Angelochairetisti chapel) are still offered, and of which types.
- Pyrgaki Hotel: the wedding page was last modified in September 2020. Confirm that weddings are still offered, the current terms and inclusions, guest capacity and room count, and that ceremonies at the Hundred Doors church (Panagia Ekatontapiliani) in Parikia can currently be arranged through the hotel, as only the hotel page states it.
- The Secret View at Fladakia Estate: its own Estate page gives the Terrace as 110 for a ceremony and 120 for dinner, but also "up to 250 guests in a seated layout". Confirm the seated maximum in writing before quoting any figure above 120.
- Monastiri: the only published figure, 800, is amphitheatre seating for corporate events, not a wedding figure. Confirm wedding capacity for the beach, restaurant, lounge and deck, and read the wedding brochure.
- Petra Farm: only a bridal suite is stated. Confirm whether there is any guest accommodation, and the overall seated maximum for one wedding across the chapel (120), poolside dinner (80) and event space (150).
- Villa Mira Paros: no wedding guest capacity is published and the events page has not been read. Confirm capacity, ceremony spots and how many guests the four villas sleep.
- Anemoi Paros: confirm which ceremony types (religious, civil or symbolic) the Chapel of St. John holds, chapel and poolside capacity, and the number of apartments and studios (eight units vs eight listed room types).
- Poseidon of Paros: confirm chapel and reception capacity (pool and beach), the total room count, and how many rooms can be held as a block.

## Legal ceremony (check with the authorities, not the venue alone)

- Civil marriage with the Municipality of Paros: no official municipal page sets out the steps, documents, ceremony location, lead times or fees for foreign couples (paros.gr lists only a Civil Marriages section within its Registry Office). Confirm all of these with the municipality, and the documents and any fees with the Australian Embassy in Athens.
- Whether Greece needs a Certificate of No Impediment (CNI) from the couple, whether an Australian-issued CNI is accepted or it must be issued in Greece, and whether it needs an authentication or apostille (ask the Greek embassy or consulate)
- Greek Orthodox ceremony: no official source read states the requirements or eligibility on Paros. Confirm with the church itself; the Advisor does not handle church or religious paperwork
- Symbolic ceremony: no official source read states requirements. Confirm with the venue (The Secret View offers one), and whether the couple marries legally elsewhere first
- Whether each venue's chapel hosts a legal or a symbolic ceremony, and the documents the municipality needs (apostille, Greek translation), notice period and fees

## General

- Curfew times and outdoor or amplified music rules at each venue on the couple's dates (The Secret View publishes 2am for loud music)
- Minimum nights, room blocks, check-in and check-out times, and set-up and pack-down windows at each venue
- Rain, wind or heat plan for every outdoor ceremony and reception space (chapels, courtyards, terraces, beach receptions)
- Whether exclusive use covers the chosen event spaces, one wedding per day, or the whole property, and whether the whole property can be closed to other guests on the couple's dates
- Step-free access for older guests and anyone with limited mobility at every venue: the number of steps between guest rooms, the ceremony and the reception, whether there are level or step-free paths, and access to hillside chapels, terraces and beach spaces
- Transfers and drive times: the drive time from Paros Airport (PAS) and Parikia port to each venue, whether the venue can organise transfers for guests, who meets guests and carries luggage, and return transport at the end of the night
- Where guests stay: for Monastiri, The Secret View and Petra Farm (no guest rooms published), and for small properties (Anemoi, Villa Mira), which nearby hotels hold the guest list, and the shuttle The Secret View says it is happy to organise
- Contract language and currency, and how the deposit is held
- Supplier rules: whether outside planners, caterers and vendors are allowed, and set-up and pack-down times
