## Cross-region summary
- Guest counts: not discussed by any top page (party size drives the morning, not guest count).
- Pricing shown: none.
- Top concern: hair and makeup running over; "how early do bridesmaids get ready" (PAA in 3 of 5 cities).
- Intent split: Google's AI Overview treats the head term as "morning ceremony wedding"; organic results are "getting ready hours". Our title targets the getting-ready hours; add one short section for a morning ceremony so the page also satisfies the AI Overview reading.
- Gap we can own: (1) the maths of the chair (people x time per service / artists = start time), worked back from any ceremony time; (2) chair order and who goes last; (3) a destination morning: hotel suite access, heat and humidity, local artists, time zones, travel to the ceremony; (4) partner's morning in parallel; (5) buffer placement rather than "add buffer". No top page does all of these, none is AU or ME specific.
