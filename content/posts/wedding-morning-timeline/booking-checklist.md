# Wedding morning checklist

Use this alongside the wedding morning timeline. Fill in the schedule from the bottom up, starting with the ceremony, once your stylist and artist have given you their timings in writing. Then take the questions below to your stylist and artist, your photographer and your venue. Every time here is yours to write in. Nothing here is a rule.

## 1. Fill in your getting-ready schedule, worked back from the ceremony

## 2. Questions for the hair stylist and makeup artist

- [ ] At the trial, how long did my hair and my makeup take? Will you allow the same on the day, or more?
- [ ] How long will you allow for each person in the wedding party, for hair, for makeup and for both? Please confirm these times in writing.
- [ ] How many chairs can you run at once?
- [ ] Working back from our dressed-by time, what time does the first chair need to start?
- [ ] If that start is earlier than we would like, can you bring a second stylist or artist so more chairs run at once?
- [ ] For a large wedding party or several family members, would a second room with its own stylist and artist keep the morning on time?
- [ ] Which order do you prefer for the chairs, and do you put the person getting married last or second to last?
- [ ] Does a veil, hairpiece, lashes or skin preparation add time for anyone?
- [ ] What time will you arrive to set up, and what do you need in the room (chairs, power points, a table, light)?
- [ ] Do you stay for touch-ups, or leave a small kit? Who should hold the lipstick and powder?
- [ ] For a destination wedding: will the heat or humidity change the products or the time you allow?

## 3. Questions for the photographer

- [ ] What time will you arrive on the wedding morning, and does that leave time for details before anyone is dressed?
- [ ] Which details do you want ready on arrival (rings, invitation suite, shoes, fragrance, florals)?
- [ ] Do you want to be in the room for dressing?
- [ ] With one photographer, which side do you photograph first, and when do you move to the other?
- [ ] With a first look, where and when should it happen, and how long do you need for portraits afterwards?
- [ ] Which spot in the suite has the best light for getting-ready photos?

## 4. Questions for the venue

### The suite or villa
- [ ] What time can we get into the suite or villa on the wedding morning?
- [ ] Does it have a large window with soft daylight where the chairs can go?
- [ ] Is there room for two or more chairs, the kit, a rail for outfits and the photographer?
- [ ] Will it be cooled from the first chair, and where can we wait once dressed?
- [ ] Can breakfast and something later in the morning be brought to the suite?

### Privacy
- [ ] Is the suite private, or will staff or other guests pass through?
- [ ] Can we keep the couple out of sight as guests arrive? Which route avoids them?
- [ ] Are there any rules on who may photograph or post from the suite or grounds?

### Distance and route to the ceremony
- [ ] How far is the ceremony site from the suite, and how long is the walk or drive?
- [ ] Does the route involve stairs, sand, gravel or a slope, and is there a smoother route for a long gown?
- [ ] Can older relatives, or anyone using a walking frame or wheelchair, make the same trip? Is there a lift, a ramp or a buggy?
- [ ] Where can they be dropped off, and how close to their seats?

### Shade and waiting
- [ ] Where does the shade fall at the ceremony site at our ceremony time?
- [ ] Is there a seat in the shade for older guests while they wait?
- [ ] Is there water at the ceremony site for guests who arrive early?

Get the answers in writing before you fix the morning.
