# Wedding photo app set-up checklist

Use this while you choose and set up a way to collect your guests' photos and videos. Ask each provider, your venue and your photographer for the answers in writing, and keep them with your wedding paperwork.

## 1. Choose the type of tool
- [ ] Shared album app: guests join your event in an app, upload and browse everyone's photos. Suits app-confident guests and a weekend of events; some guests will not install it.
- [ ] QR code upload page with no install: guests scan a code and upload in the phone's browser, with no account. Suits a mixed or international guest list; browsing can be more basic.
- [ ] Disposable camera style app: a limited number of shots, with photos hidden until a later reveal. Playful, but you will receive fewer photos.
- [ ] Live slideshow display: uploads appear on a screen at the reception. Lively, but only choose one where you can review uploads before they reach the screen.
- [ ] Your photographer's own gallery: guest uploads alongside the professional images, if your photographer offers it.
- [ ] If you choose an app, also offer a browser link, so guests who would rather not download anything can still contribute.

## 2. What to look for before you choose
Ask every provider the same questions, and compare the written answers side by side.

### Cost and inclusions
- [ ] What is the total cost for our wedding, and exactly what does it include?
- [ ] Do full resolution downloads, video, a slideshow, extra events or longer storage cost extra?
- [ ] Is the cost a one-off payment or ongoing, and what happens to the album if we stop paying?
- [ ] Anything not written down counts as not included.

### Uploading
- [ ] Can a guest upload from the phone's browser, with no app and no account? If not, what does a guest have to share to sign up?
- [ ] Can photos taken with no signal send automatically once the phone reconnects?
- [ ] Are photos kept at original resolution, or compressed on upload?
- [ ] Is video supported, at what clip length and in what quality?

### Moderation
- [ ] Can we hide or delete a single photo quickly?
- [ ] Can we review uploads before they appear in the album or on a slideshow?

### Downloading and storage
- [ ] Can we download the full collection in one go, at full resolution, with videos included?
- [ ] Does the download keep the name of the guest who uploaded each file?
- [ ] How long does the album stay online, and what happens to the photos afterwards?
- [ ] Can uploads be sorted into a separate album for each event (welcome dinner, wedding, farewell lunch)?

## 3. Privacy settings and who can view
- [ ] Who can view the album: people with the link, guests we approve, or anyone?
- [ ] Can the link be forwarded, and can we stop that?
- [ ] Can we stop guests downloading other people's photos?
- [ ] Is the album hidden from search engines and public listings?
- [ ] Set the album to private, invite-only, before the first guest uploads.
- [ ] Agree a short photo policy between the two of you (ask before sharing anyone else's photo publicly; we remove any picture on request).
- [ ] Check with parents before any image of their children is shared, and keep those images inside the private album.
- [ ] Decide who on your side handles take-down requests during and after the wedding, and remove any photo a guest asks you to take down.
- [ ] Before you print, frame or post a guest's photo, ask the guest who took it.

Privacy and copyright points here are general information as at November 2026, not legal advice. The guide links the official eSafety, OAIC and Copyright Act sources at each point.

## 4. QR cards and signage
- [ ] A small QR card on every table
- [ ] A larger sign at the entrance, beside the seating chart, where every guest pauses
- [ ] Cards and signs matched to your stationery
- [ ] Every code tested on several phones (different makes and older models) before printing, and again on the day
- [ ] If the venue Wi-Fi has a password, add it to the cards (only if the venue agrees)
- [ ] For a weekend of events, label the cards by day or event
- [ ] A line on the invitation and a section on your wedding website telling guests how to share

Sample wording for cards and signs (choose one):
- [ ] Share your photos: "Scan to add your photos and videos to our private album."
- [ ] Ask before sharing: "Please ask before sharing anyone else's photo publicly. We will remove any picture on request."
- [ ] No posting: "Please share your photos with us here, and keep today off social media. Thank you for helping us keep it private."

## 5. Unplugged ceremony
- [ ] Decide whether you want an unplugged ceremony (phones away during the ceremony)
- [ ] Ask your celebrant to make the announcement before you walk in
- [ ] Add a line to the welcome sign at the ceremony
- [ ] Tell your photographer, so they know the aisle will be clear
- [ ] Save the upload invitation for the reception, and ask your MC to point guests to the QR cards

## 6. After the wedding: download and back up
- [ ] Leave the album open for a few weeks for late uploads, including guests flying home to other time zones
- [ ] Send guests a reminder link after the wedding
- [ ] Download everything at full resolution, videos included, well before the album closes
- [ ] Keep two copies in different places
- [ ] Remove anything a guest asked you to take down before you share a selection
- [ ] Share a curated selection with guests
- [ ] Close or archive the album when you are done

## Questions for the venue

### Internet and data
- [ ] Does guest Wi-Fi reach every space we are using, from the ceremony site to the garden and the reception?
- [ ] Will it cope with many guests uploading photos and videos at once?
- [ ] Can the Wi-Fi password go on our table cards and signs?
- [ ] How does mobile signal hold up across the grounds, including any terrace, beach, boat or clifftop we are using?
- [ ] If signal is patchy in some spaces, where is the nearest spot with a reliable connection?
- [ ] If there is a live slideshow, is there a screen, power and a connection for it, and who sets it up?

### Staff briefing
- [ ] Will venue staff and suppliers be briefed on our photo policy (unplugged ceremony, no posting, or ask before sharing)?
- [ ] Who on the venue team is our contact on the day if a guest or staff member has a photo concern?
- [ ] Can staff place the QR cards on tables and set up the entrance sign?

## Questions for the photographer
- [ ] Does your delivered gallery have room for guest uploads, and is that included in our package?
- [ ] If it is, do guest uploads sit in the same gallery as your images or in a separate album, and do they download separately?
- [ ] How long does the gallery stay open, and can guests view or download from it?
- [ ] Are you happy to work with an unplugged ceremony, and would you like us to ask guests to keep the aisle clear?
- [ ] Does a live slideshow or guest uploads change anything about how you work on the day?

### The contract
- [ ] Read the usage and copyright clause in your photographer's contract before you sign.
- [ ] Who owns the copyright in the professional images, and what may each of you do with them?
- [ ] Does the contract say anything about guest photos or guest uploads to your gallery?
- [ ] May the photographer use our images, or any guest uploads in their gallery, in a portfolio or on social media without our written consent? If you would rather they didn't, write that limit into the contract.
