# Bali Villa Wedding Packages and What They Include

[IMAGE: bali-garden-lawn-long-table-golden-hour-01.webp]

Bali villa wedding packages sell a private house and garden for your group, for the dates and hours the contract sets. What each package includes varies by villa, so the only safe way to read one is line by line. This guide is for couples planning a wedding week for 60 to 150 guests and weighing a private villa against a resort.

One thing first, so you know where we stand. Wedded Wonderland does not list private villas in Bali: the venues in [our Bali destination guide](/destinations/asia/bali) are resorts and hotels, some with villa-style rooms. This is a buyer's guide to villa packages in general, and the closest alternative we can show is a resort you can take over in full.

Sources current as at November 2026.

## What Bali villa wedding packages include

A Bali villa wedding package may bundle use of a private villa for a set time, its in-house staff, a set number of bedrooms for a set number of nights, and a planner's core set-up. What it covers varies by villa and by planner, so check each one against the list below before you compare.

Ask which of these a villa package covers:

- An event fee for the villa and its grounds
- Villa staff
- Included bedrooms and nights
- Ceremony and reception set-up
- Basic styling
- On-day coordination
- Catering, and whether the villa names the caterer

The gap between a villa and a resort shows in what sits behind that list. Our destination venues guide puts it plainly: "A private villa or estate hands you the keys and nothing else: total freedom over vendors, menus, timings and days, and total responsibility for sourcing all of it." A villa package narrows that gap without closing it. For the categories any Bali package covers, see [our guide to what Bali packages cover in general](/destination-weddings/bali-wedding-packages).

## Bali villa wedding or resort: which suits your wedding week

A Bali villa wedding gives you privacy and the freedom to choose your own suppliers. Your closest people may stay under one roof, and you have far more say over the food, the drinks and the shape of each day. Against that, a villa has no resort back-of-house: no events team down the corridor. It also comes with a fixed number of beds, house rules and neighbours, and leaves much more for you to arrange.

Before anything else, ask whether a package is exclusive use or event use. Our venues guide puts the test simply: "What exactly is exclusive to us: which spaces, for which hours, and who else is on the property that day?"

[IMAGE: bali-resort-villas-pavilion-garden-01.webp]

Neither choice is the safe one. In the words of our venues guide, "a resort brings its own vendors, menus and coordinator; a villa brings you freedom and a blank slate you must staff. Neither is cheaper by default." A resort suits couples who want one team. Our Bali page lists resorts such as [AYANA Bali](/venue/ayana-bali) and [Alila Villas Uluwatu](/venue/alila-villas-uluwatu), whose listing says it provides wedding services, dining and accommodation for guests. A villa suits couples who want privacy above all and are ready for more coordination.

There is a middle path: taking over a whole resort. [Nirjhara Bali Waterfall Weddings](/venue/nirjhara-bali-waterfall-weddings), in Tabanan, describes "25 standalone suites and villas and the option to reserve the entire resort." Its Resort Exclusivity package reads, as our listing words it: "Up to 60 guests. Two-night buyout of all 25 units, up to 2-day venue use, flowers and reception lighting." A buyout also changes the music rule: "No music curfew applies with an exclusive resort buyout." It is a resort of suites and villas, not a private villa, so ask for current pricing when you enquire.

## What a villa package usually leaves out

This is where the real work of a villa wedding sits. For each line below, ask whether your package covers it and, if not, who arranges it.

| Villa item | What to ask and pin down |
|---|---|
| Power for the event | Whether the villa's supply can carry lighting, sound and a catering kitchen at once, or a generator is needed, and who supplies, fuels and runs it |
| Guest toilets | How many the villa has for guests, whether portable units are needed, where they go and who services them |
| A working kitchen for your caterer | Whether the caterer can use the villa kitchen or must bring a field kitchen |
| Tables, chairs and lighting | What the villa owns, and what has to be hired in |
| Wet-weather cover | A named covered space or a marquee, who installs it, and the time the call is made |
| Beds for everyone else | Which guests sleep elsewhere, and who books those rooms |
| Access | Where coaches, vendor trucks and guest cars can stop, and how far guests walk from there |
| Neighbours | Shared driveways, and whether neighbouring houses overlook the event space |
| Staff on the night | Which villa staff work your events, until when, and what overtime costs |
| Outside supplier fees and corkage | Whether the villa charges for suppliers or drinks you bring in |

[IMAGE: bali-lawn-marquee-setup-crew-01.webp]

Wet-weather cover deserves its own line in the contract. [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia) says conditions can change quickly in the wet season, usually around October to April, so on a villa lawn, book and price the marquee or covered space before the day.

Costs gather in the same places. [Our Bali wedding budget guide](/expert-advice/bali-wedding-cost-the-complete-2026-guide) lists banjar (local community) fees, generator hire, overtime, corkage, wet-weather marquees, government taxes and service charges among the hidden costs. Even on our own packages page, "Specific inclusions vary by resort and are sent through after you enquire", so ask for an itemised quote; see [how our destination wedding packages are built](/destination-wedding-packages).

## How many guests a Bali villa can hold

The number on a villa package is rarely the whole answer. Ask for four numbers:

1. **Standing capacity** for the ceremony or drinks
2. **Seated capacity** for dinner, at tables you would actually use
3. **Sleeping capacity:** beds, not bedrooms
4. **Parking and drop-off space** for coaches, shuttles and vendor trucks

The first two can differ more than you expect. Our Nirjhara listing shows 150 seated across the resort overall, but its spaces vary: The Garden holds 100 seated or 150 standing, and Ambu holds 65 seated or 80 standing. The listing also notes that "Ceremony and dinner venues depend on weather and guest numbers." A villa works the same way, so ask which space each part of the day uses.

[IMAGE: bali-terrace-reception-dusk-festoon-01.webp]

If the ceremony, dinner and dancing share one lawn, someone has to reset it between them. Ask who does the turnaround, how long it takes and where guests wait meanwhile.

For 60 to 150 guests, plan on housing part of the group elsewhere unless the villa's beds match your list. That means nearby villas or a hotel room block, plus transfers each evening timed to the run sheet. Start with [wedding and guest travel planning](/travel) early, because guest rooms shape the whole week.

## Who holds the vendor list

Villas run their supplier lists in one of three ways, and each changes who answers for what.

- **Mandatory suppliers.** The villa names its caterer or other suppliers, and you use them. Some resorts work this way: our Alila Villas Uluwatu listing shows catering as "In-house only".
- **A preferred list, outside suppliers allowed.** You can bring your own, sometimes for a fee. Ask what that fee covers.
- **An open villa.** You or your planner contract every supplier directly.

For each supplier, write down who holds the contract, who carries the insurance, and who answers on the night if the band is late or the generator fails. In an open villa, that can be nobody unless you name someone. A paying parent will want each quote itemised, with deposits and cancellation terms side by side.

## Questions to ask the villa manager

These 10 are specific to the villa.

1. Is this exclusive use or event use, and from what time on which day to what time on which day?
2. Which licence does the villa hold today, and does it cover events as well as stays?
3. Which permits does the villa arrange for an event of our size, and which fall to us or our planner?
4. Does the local banjar need to be told or paid for our event, who speaks to it, and is any contribution in the quote?
5. What time must amplified music stop at this property, and is that a villa rule or a local one?
6. What power is on site, and do we need a generator for lighting, sound and the kitchen?
7. How many guest toilets are there, and how many guests are they meant for?
8. Where do coaches and vendor trucks stop, and do neighbours share the access or overlook the event space?
9. Which villa staff are on duty during each event, until when, and who manages them on the night?
10. Is there a damage deposit separate from the event fee, and what can be deducted from it?

On licensing, [a 2017 Badung regency regulation](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/61959/Perbup%20No.%202%20Tahun%202017.pdf) lists "Villa" among the accommodation businesses licensed through the regency's one-stop licensing service. It covers Badung only and says nothing about weddings or events, so ask what the villa holds today.

## The legal side, and when to bring in help

A villa ceremony can be symbolic or legal, depending on the route you take. The [Attorney-General's Department](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married) says an Australian-authorised celebrant cannot perform a marriage overseas, so a villa ceremony they lead is symbolic. A legal Indonesian wedding has its own rules, including a religion requirement under Law No. 1 of 1974 and registration with the Civil Registry Office, as the [Australian Embassy in Indonesia](https://indonesia.embassy.gov.au/jakt/MarriageInd.html) sets out. Confirm the requirements for your own passports with the Civil Registry Office where you marry, the Certificate of No Impediment (CNI) with the Australian Consulate-General in Bali and, if you marry at home first, your celebrant. For getting married in Bali legally, read [our guide to Bali packages and the two legal routes](/destination-weddings/bali-wedding-packages) and [where Australians can legally marry abroad](/expert-advice/weddings-abroad-where-can-you-legally-get-married); for a villa comparison elsewhere, see [how villa weddings work in Tuscany](/expert-advice/can-you-get-married-in-a-tuscan-villa).

A villa wedding usually needs more than a package when your week has:

- More than one event across several days
- A guest list larger than the villa's beds
- Suppliers you want to bring from Australia
- Cultural or multi-faith ceremonies
- An open villa with no supplier list at all

## FAQ

### Can you get married at a private villa in Bali?

You can hold your ceremony at a villa. Whether a villa ceremony is also your legal marriage depends on your route. If you marry legally in Indonesia, the Attorney-General's Department says an overseas marriage is generally recognised in Australia if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid here. Read [what to do with a marriage certificate issued overseas](/expert-advice/overseas-marriage-certificate).

### What is a banjar fee for a Bali villa wedding?

Our Bali cost guide lists banjar (local community) fees among the hidden costs of a Bali wedding, next to generator hire and corkage. We cannot give you a figure. Ask the villa manager whether one applies to your event, who arranges it and whether it sits inside the quote, and get the answer in writing.

### How much does a Bali villa wedding cost?

It depends on guest numbers, nights, events and how much the villa leaves for you to hire in, so we do not quote a villa price here. [Our guide to Bali wedding budgets](/expert-advice/bali-wedding-cost-the-complete-2026-guide) gives figures in USD and notes that suppliers usually take a 30 to 50% deposit, with the balance due 30 to 60 days before.

### Can all our guests stay at the wedding villa?

Only if its beds match your guest list, so ask for the number of beds rather than bedrooms. For a larger group, plan nearby villas or a hotel room block. On our own packages page, guest accommodation is listed as "Paid separately, usually at negotiated group rates", so budget for it as its own line.

### How early should we secure a Bali villa for an exclusive-use week?

Our Bali cost guide says premium venues book out 12 to 18 months ahead. For a villa, secure the exclusive-use dates and nearby guest rooms together. Marrying legally in Australia first? Your Notice of Intended Marriage must reach your celebrant at least one month and no more than 18 months before, says the Attorney-General's Department.
