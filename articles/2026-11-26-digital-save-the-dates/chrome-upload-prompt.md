## Article body

# Digital Save the Dates and the Best Sites to Send Them

[IMAGE: hero]

Digital save the dates are the first word about your wedding, sent by email, message or link instead of by post, asking guests to hold the date before the formal invitation arrives. For a wedding abroad, that early note matters more than the card itself. It reaches relatives in several countries on the same day, carries a link to your travel and accommodation details, and needs no postage. Below are the sites worth knowing: Paperless Post, Greenvelope and Bliss & Bone send and track it for you, while Canva, Greetings Island, Etsy and Zazzle give you a design or file to send yourself. Each works best when it points guests to one page where the details live.

Still settling on wording and design? Start with [our step-by-step guide to making a digital invitation](/wedding-tools/how-to-make-a-digital-wedding-invitation), browse [printed save the date inspiration](/save-the-date-ideas), or see our notes on [sending shower invitations online](/wedding-tools/online-wedding-shower-invitations).

## How we chose these sites

We read each site's own pages for how it delivers, whether it links to an RSVP or your wedding website, how much design control you get, whether it tracks opens or replies, and what is free or paid, without scoring or ranking them, since the right one depends on your guest list. Sources current as at November 2026, with prices in US dollars exactly as each site listed them.

## Start with your wedding website

Before you choose where to send a save the date, decide where it leads. The card holds the date and the place; the page behind it holds everything a travelling guest will ask next.

[Wonderland Studio](/wonderland-studio) is our free planning suite, and it is the page we would put on the card. It does not design or send save the dates. What it gives you is a wedding website "in minutes", with six templates rooted in wedding traditions, each with RSVP built in. Guests can RSVP straight from your public link without creating an account, and replies, plus-ones and dietary notes arrive straight into your guest list.

That guest list keeps RSVP status, meal preferences, dietary notes and travel details for every guest. Guests can self-register, and you can import or export the whole list as a CSV, which helps when two families are building it from two countries. The suite is free to use, with no credit card and no trial clock, and the [full set of free planning tools](/wedding-planning-tools) also covers budget and seating, plus a checklist that lists "Send save-the-dates" as a task.

For ideas on structure, see [website layouts that work for travelling guests](/destination-weddings/wedding-website-examples), and for the reply side, our guide to [collecting every reply through your website](/expert-advice/wedding-rsvp-website).

## Paperless Post for cards sent by email, text or link

[Paperless Post](https://www.paperlesspost.com/cards/group/save-the-dates/beach-tropical) lets you customise a save the date and share it by email, text or link, and its page description says you can track opens. Text delivery is for US phone numbers only, so for guests in Australia and elsewhere, send by email or paste the shareable link into WhatsApp or any message app.

Paperless Post says it has many free designs. Paid designs sit on a pay per event plan priced in the site's own Coins: you multiply a design's Coin cost by the number of recipients for your total send cost, and its pricing calculator shows 3 Coins as US$1.44. Paperless Post Plus, at 200 Coins, unlocks more customisation and guest management tools.

## Greenvelope for animated envelopes and WhatsApp delivery

[Greenvelope](https://www.greenvelope.com/wedding-save-the-dates) sends save the dates that open with an animated envelope, a personalised liner, a stamp and background music. You can send by email, by text in supported countries, by WhatsApp, by Facebook Messenger or as a shareable link, and guests never need an account. Every paid account includes a maximum of 500 text messages. Its page does not say whether Australian mobile numbers are a supported country for text, so plan on email, WhatsApp or the link for guests in Australia.

Two features suit a wedding abroad. You can add your wedding website link, and Greenvelope tracks opens and responses in real time and can collect guests' mailing addresses inside the save the date, ready for the paper invitations that follow. You can also upload your own design, and its page says it pairs with any wedding website builder. Pricing is a flat, one-time amount by guest count, with no subscription required. A single mailing was listed at US$19 for up to 20 people, US$99 for up to 100 and US$249 for up to 250. Each person counts as one, so a family of four counts as four.

## Bliss & Bone for open tracking and follow-ups

[Bliss & Bone](https://blissandbone.com/save-the-date-online) lets you design online save the dates for free, from hundreds of designs or your own photo, art and logos, then deliver by email, text or WhatsApp. Its page says email and WhatsApp work globally with no additional cost or postage, and that text delivery is supported in the US and Canada. For guests in Australia, that means email or WhatsApp.

From your account you can track opens, catch bounced addresses and send free follow-up reminders to guests who have not responded, which helps when guests read email across several time zones. A link to your wedding website sits inside the email, and RSVP collection is optional. You pay per recipient when you send: US$1.20 each for 1 to 9 recipients, US$0.90 each from 10, and US$0.70 each from 300, with all prices in USD.

## Canva for designing your own save the date

Canva, Etsy and Zazzle let you design your own or have a designer make one, and you send the finished file yourself. [Canva](https://www.canva.com/cards/templates/save-the-date) is a design tool rather than a sending service. Its save the date page offers free, customisable templates and showed 2,540 save the date templates in November 2026, so you can match the card to your wedding's colours and type. The Free plan is US$0 a month for one person and Pro is US$18 a month for one person, with prices excluding applicable tax.

Canva's save the date page names no email delivery, RSVP or open tracking. Plan to send the finished design yourself, and put your wedding website address, or a QR code that opens it, on the design so replies land somewhere you can see them.

## Greetings Island for free designs you can print or send

[Greetings Island](https://www.greetingsisland.com/announcements/save-the-date) lists 718 save the date designs, both free and Premium. Once you have personalised one, you can send it as an email, share it by SMS, WhatsApp or Facebook, download an image or PDF, print at home or order prints. That mix helps when one grandparent wants a card on the fridge and everyone else wants a link.

Premium is described as "a low monthly price" for more than 5,000 premium designs, fonts and stickers, with no ads, but the monthly figure is not given on its save the date page. The page also does not say whether save the dates track opens, so treat it as a design-and-send option and collect replies on your website.

## Etsy for templates from independent sellers

[Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013328108-How-to-Download-a-Digital-Item) is a marketplace of independent sellers rather than a single platform, so what you get depends on the listing, and each seller sets the price. Its help centre describes two kinds of digital purchase: instant downloads, which are ready-made files available once payment is confirmed, and made-to-order downloads, which the seller makes to your specifications and sends once complete. Files download from Purchases in a web browser, not the Etsy app.

Read the listing closely before you buy. Etsy says digital listings cannot be returned or cancelled, although Purchase Protection may cover you if the file is not as described or never arrives. Sending, replies and tracking are all up to you, so the file is the card and your website does the rest.

## Zazzle for a downloadable file

[Zazzle](https://www.zazzle.com/digital+save+the+dates) sells digital save the dates that you customise from a template or start from scratch, then download as a JPG, PNG or PDF. After purchase, it emails you a link to the editable template. Its page describes them as evites commonly sent by email, SMS or social media, which means you do the sending.

Most designs listed on zazzle.com showed a price of US$11.95 in November 2026. The page names no RSVP or open tracking, so, as with Canva and Etsy, put your website address on the design.

## What to include for a wedding abroad

A save the date for a wedding overseas does more work than one for a wedding at home. Keep it short, and make sure it carries:

- **The place in full.** Town and country, so guests can price flights the day it arrives.
- **The travel window.** The wedding date and any welcome or farewell events, so guests know how many nights to take.
- **Your wedding website link.** Where accommodation, transfers and the RSVP will live. Here is [what a page for travelling guests can hold](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-guest-guide).
- **A passport reminder.** One line asking guests to check their passport's expiry date now.

The passport line earns its place. Smartraveller says [some countries won't let you enter unless your passport is valid for six months after you plan to leave](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/passport-services), that some airlines impose the rule themselves, and that it can apply even when transiting. It also advises, where possible, not booking flights without a valid passport, and the Australian Passport Office says to [allow at least six weeks from lodging](https://www.passports.gov.au/help/processing-times) an application. Each guest should confirm the entry rules for their own passport with the embassy or consulate of the destination.

Send by email and WhatsApp rather than text alone, and for older relatives, use whichever channel they actually read. A quick call to say it is coming saves a save the date from sitting unopened. Send-and-track sites hold the names, emails and phone numbers you upload, so read each platform's privacy policy before you upload your list. If you send a file yourself, send it individually or by BCC rather than in one open group email.

## When to send save the dates to guests abroad

[Our invitation timeline](/expert-advice/when-to-send-wedding-invitations) says to "send save the dates 9 to 12 months ahead once any guest travels or the date falls in peak season, and about six months ahead otherwise." For overseas guests, it puts the save the date 9 to 12 months before the wedding, before they book annual leave or fares. Send theirs first, with a note saying why you are writing early, then tell close family at home in person. Guests who need a visa also need the date, venue and accommodation in writing before the six-month mark.

The same guide sets three conditions. Wait for the countersigned venue contract. Send one only to guests you are certain to invite, since it promises an invitation. And with under about four months to go, skip the save the date and send the invitation.

Have your wedding website live, with at least the date and place, before the save the dates go out. Then open the link on your own phone before you press send.

## Questions about digital save the dates

### What is an online save the date?

It is a note sent by email, message or link, rather than by post, asking guests to hold your date. Online, digital and electronic save the dates all mean the same thing.

### Are digital save the dates tacky for a luxury wedding?

Not when they look considered and lead somewhere useful. Greenvelope's animated envelope, liner and stamp feel close to opening real post, and a printed invitation can still follow. For a wedding abroad, a digital note also reaches guests in several countries at once, which matters when they need months to plan travel.

### Can a digital save the date collect RSVPs and addresses?

Some can. Bliss & Bone offers optional RSVP collection, and Greenvelope tracks responses. Greenvelope's page also says it collects mailing addresses directly through the save the date, ready for paper invitations. A file from Canva, Etsy or Zazzle collects nothing on its own, so link it to your wedding website, where Wonderland Studio lets guests self-register and keeps travel details on your guest list.

### Are there free digital save the dates?

Yes, with limits. Paperless Post has free designs, Canva's Free plan is US$0 a month, Greetings Island has free designs alongside Premium, and Bliss & Bone lets you design for free before you pay per recipient. Wonderland Studio, the website the card points to, is free with no credit card.
