## Article body

# Getting Married in Greece from the UK Step by Step

[IMAGE: getting-married-in-greece-from-uk-cycladic-terrace-couple-01.webp]

You have an island shortlist and a month in mind, and the paperwork, done in the right order, decides when your guests can book flights. This guide to getting married in Greece from the UK takes British passport holders through each step: the free-to-marry document, the FCDO apostille, Greek translations, the town hall, and recognition when you come home.

Sources current as at November 2026. Greek town halls apply national rules in their own way, so confirm every requirement for your own passports with the municipality where you will marry and, on the UK side, with the FCDO through gov.uk.

## Can British couples marry legally in Greece?

Yes. The legal side runs through the Greek municipality (the town hall) where you marry, and [gov.uk's guidance on marrying abroad](https://www.gov.uk/marriages-civil-partnerships-abroad) says whether you can marry abroad depends on local laws, so check the local process and which documents prove you are free to marry.

Greek law requires a licence from the mayor of each partner's last place of residence before a civil marriage or a marriage solemnised by the Eastern Orthodox Church, according to the [gov.gr civil licence entry](https://en.mitos.gov.gr/index.php/%CE%94%CE%94:Issue_of_a_civil_marriage_licence). For couples living abroad, a 4 January 2019 [Interior Ministry circular](https://www.ypes.gr/UserFiles/f0ff9297-f516-40ff-a70e-eca84e2ec9b9/1969-04012019.pdf) indicates that a foreign document showing you are free to marry counts as that licence when it also names your partner, so no Greek licence needs to be issued. Ask your municipality in writing whether it accepts yours. Municipalities such as [Chania](https://www.chania.gr/files/57/44617/civil_marriages_documents_for_foreigners_of_abroad_no5_%28en%29.pdf) and [Heraklion](https://www.heraklion.gr/en/citizen/civil-marriages/marriage-foreigners.html) publish document lists for foreigners who do not live in Greece.

Still choosing a country? Our guide to [how British citizens marry in Italy](/expert-advice/how-do-british-citizens-get-married-in-italy) sets out the Italian route.

## Residency and eligibility for British passport holders

Greece's licence route for residents is not the route for couples living in the UK. Chania's non-resident list is for foreigners living abroad and Heraklion's for foreigners "not residing permanently in Greece", and both start from a document issued by your own country.

[The 2019 circular](https://www.ypes.gr/UserFiles/f0ff9297-f516-40ff-a70e-eca84e2ec9b9/1969-04012019.pdf) makes proof that you are lawfully in Greece, such as a passport, visa or residence permit, an essential precondition. The [FCDO's Greece entry requirements](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/greece/entry-requirements) say your passport must be issued less than 10 years before you arrive and expire at least three months after you plan to leave the Schengen area. You can travel without a visa for up to 90 days in any 180-day period as a tourist or to visit family or friends, and the FCDO says to contact the Greek Embassy in the UK if you are unsure how the entry rules apply to you. Heraklion asks for "a residence permit or VISA (whose validity period has not expired)", so ask how that applies to a visa-free visit.

If your partner is a Greek citizen registered with a Greek municipality, you both apply for the licence at the Consular Office of the [Greek Embassy in London](https://www.mfa.gr/uk/en/services/citizen-services/registration-birth-marriage-divorce-death/issuance-of-marriage-license/), each completing an application, an authorisation and a Solemn Declaration of No Impediment to Marriage. The Embassy lists EUR 10 for the declaration and EUR 10 for a copy, or the sterling equivalent.

## Prefer to marry in the UK? Marry at home, celebrate in Greece

If the Greek paperwork feels like too much, [gov.uk's guidance on marriages registered abroad](https://www.gov.uk/marriages-civil-partnerships-abroad/register-your-marriage-or-civil-partnership-abroad) says that if you want a UK marriage certificate, you can choose a legal ceremony in the UK and a commitment ceremony abroad.

Check with the register office in the nation where you will marry (England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland) for its notice period and the documents it needs.

[IMAGE: uk-register-office-couple-signing-witnesses-01.webp]

Your guests in Greece see a commitment ceremony rather than the legal one, but your date no longer depends on a Greek town hall's diary. For the celebration, start with our [Greece destination page](/destinations/europe/greece) and see [how a celebration spread over two days can unfold](/a-two-day-wedding-celebration-in-greece).

## Documents for getting married in Greece from the UK

Lists differ between municipalities, so confirm the final list with yours before you order anything.

| Document | Issued by | Apostille | Greek translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proof you are free to marry (a certificate of no impediment or the equivalent Greece asks for) | FCDO, through the gov.uk online service. [Consular fee](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/consular-services-fees) GBP 50 per document (FCDO fee schedule, 9 April 2025) | Ask your municipality | Yes |
| Birth certificate | The register office where your birth was registered (check with it which certificate to order for an apostille) | Paper apostille from the FCDO Legalisation Office, GBP 45 per document (November 2026) | Yes |
| Proof a previous marriage ended, if either of you was married before | The court or register that issued it | Ask your municipality | Yes |
| Passport | HM Passport Office | Ask your municipality | Ask your municipality |

[gov.uk's page on documents for marrying abroad](https://www.gov.uk/marriages-civil-partnerships-abroad/get-documents-for-a-marriage-or-civil-partnership-abroad) says some countries ask for a certificate of no impediment or a marital status affirmation (see [what a certificate of no impediment is](/expert-advice/what-is-a-certificate-of-no-impediment-and-who-issues-it)), and its online service lets you check which documents you might need. Which document Greece needs from you, and the total the service charges, show only inside the application. Once started, it cannot be saved and times out after 30 minutes of inactivity, so set aside time and have your passport to hand.

Chania will not accept a home-country document that does not name the person you are marrying, so whether yours does is the first question for your municipality. Chania also asks for a "recent" birth certificate and for originals, not photocopies, so do not order too early.

## Apostilles from the FCDO and Greek translations

Greece accepts documents from Hague Convention countries such as the UK only with an apostille and "translated into Greek by a Greek translator", says the [gov.gr foreign-resident licence entry](https://en.mitos.gov.gr/index.php/%CE%94%CE%94:Issue_of_a_marriage_licence_for_foreign_residents_in_Greece).

In the UK, the [FCDO Legalisation Office](https://www.gov.uk/get-document-legalised) attaches the apostille to UK public documents. General Register Office certificates cannot get an e-Apostille, so they need the paper one, at GBP 45 per document. It usually takes up to 25 working days plus courier or postage time, so allow at least five working weeks plus postage.

Translation comes after the apostille. Since 1 September 2021, under a procedure from the [Greek Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.mfa.gr/en/announcement-regarding-the-simplification-of-procedure-for-the-official-translation-of-documents/), you choose a translator from the Electronic Register of Certified Translators at metafraseis.services.gov.gr and contact them directly. Certified translators set their own fees, so ask for a quote before you commit.

### Questions to ask the municipality

Write to your town hall and keep every reply; Chania, for example, accepts documents in person or by email. The Santorini (Thira) and Mykonos municipalities publish no wedding requirements we could confirm, so if you marry on either island, send these questions to that municipality in writing.

- Can the legal ceremony be held at our venue, which days, times and fees apply, and how many witnesses with what ID do we need?
- Will you accept the document from the gov.uk service in place of a Greek marriage licence, and must it name my partner?
- Which documents need an apostille and a Greek translation, how recent must each be, and how many working days before the ceremony do you need the originals?
- Do couples living abroad need a newspaper notice or a waiting period?
- What proof do you need if one of us was married before?
- After the ceremony, how and by when is the marriage registered, how do we order certificate copies, and which Greek authority adds an apostille?

## The Greek marriage licence and your timeline

For residents of Greece, the mayor waits one week after a complete application and newspaper notice for any objection; the civil licence entry lists eight days in all, and the licence is valid for six months.

For couples living abroad, a document from home can stand in for the Greek licence, and neither Chania's nor Heraklion's non-resident list includes a newspaper notice or a week's wait. If your municipality does run a Greek licence for you, count the week from when it accepts a complete file, not from when you land. For the residents' wait in more detail, see [how the Greek licence wait works](/destination-weddings/greece-marriage-licence).

This timeline is illustrative; your municipality's written answers set the real dates.

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| 12 months or more before | Choose your island and send that municipality the questions above. Hold non-refundable deposits until it confirms your date in writing. |
| Nine months before | Check both passports against the FCDO rules and start the gov.uk application to see which document Greece needs. |
| Six months before | Order birth certificates and any proof a previous marriage ended, then send them for paper apostilles (up to 25 working days plus postage). |
| Three months before | Arrange Greek translations from the register and send copies to the municipality if it accepts them by email. |
| Wedding week | Take your original documents, passports and witnesses to the town hall when it asks. |
| After the ceremony | Ask the municipality how and when the marriage is registered and how to order certificate copies, including from outside Greece. |

A Greek marriage certificate presupposes registration at the registry office of the municipality where the marriage took place, says the [gov.gr marriage certificate entry](https://mitos.gov.gr/index.php/%CE%94%CE%94:%CE%9B%CE%B7%CE%BE%CE%B9%CE%B1%CF%81%CF%87%CE%B9%CE%BA%CE%AE_%CF%80%CF%81%CE%AC%CE%BE%CE%B7_%CE%B3%CE%AC%CE%BC%CE%BF%CF%85).

## The civil ceremony, and religious or symbolic options

[IMAGE: greek-town-hall-civil-wedding-ceremony-01.webp]

[Heraklion holds](https://www.heraklion.gr/en/citizen/civil-marriages/marriage-foreigners.html) its civil ceremonies in the Municipal Council Hall and lists EUR 100 for Tuesday or Thursday at 12:00 and EUR 150 for Wednesday or Friday from 18:00, fees set in 2007, so confirm them with the town hall. Each person marrying there brings an adult witness with ID or a passport, and a relative may be one.

Law 5089/2024 provides for civil marriage between people of the same sex, and [gov.gr cites it](https://en.mitos.gov.gr/index.php/%CE%94%CE%94:Issue_of_a_civil_marriage_licence) as a legal basis for the civil licence; see [what changed for same-sex couples in 2024](/destination-weddings/same-sex-marriage-greece).

An Eastern Orthodox wedding also needs the mayor's licence, and the church sets its own conditions, so speak to the parish priest first. A symbolic ceremony carries no legal weight on its own, so pair it with a legal ceremony at home, the route gov.uk describes above.

## Recognition and name change back in the UK

[gov.uk says a foreign marriage is recognised](https://www.gov.uk/marriages-civil-partnerships-abroad/register-your-marriage-or-civil-partnership-abroad) in the UK if it was lawful in the country where it took place and would be allowed under UK law. gov.uk is plain: "You cannot get a UK marriage or civil partnership certificate if your marriage or civil partnership happened abroad. Only UK marriages and civil partnerships are registered in the UK." Your Greek certificate is your proof, so order more than one copy.

gov.uk adds that a translation can help when you use the certificate outside Greece, so ask any UK bank or lender that wants it whether it needs one. For an apostille on the Greek certificate, [the FCDO Legalisation Office cannot legalise](https://www.gov.uk/get-document-legalised) documents issued outside the UK; they must be legalised in the country that issued them, so ask your municipality which Greek authority adds the apostille and how long it takes.

[HM Passport Office](https://www.gov.uk/changing-passport-information/name-marriage-and-civil-partnership) says you can change your name before or after the ceremony; after it, you send your marriage certificate with the application (GBP 102 online, GBP 115.50 on paper). Book flights in the name on your current passport, because the two must match.

A post-dated passport is possible, but your old one is cancelled, so the simpler order is to marry in your current name and change it afterwards. Ask HM Passport Office whether it needs a translation of your Greek certificate.

## FAQ

### How do UK couples get married in Greece?

You apply through gov.uk for the FCDO document that shows you are free to marry, have your birth certificate apostilled by the FCDO Legalisation Office, and have both translated into Greek. You then lodge them with the municipality where you will marry, which sets the ceremony arrangements and holds the civil ceremony; ask it which documents, translations and dates apply to your own passports.

### Is a wedding in Greece legal in the UK?

Yes, if it was lawful in Greece and would be allowed under UK law, according to gov.uk. It is not registered in the UK, so keep your Greek certificate safe.

### Do British citizens need a certificate of no impediment to marry in Greece?

You need a document proving you are free to marry; gov.uk's online service shows whether that is a certificate of no impediment or another document. Chania also wants it to name your partner.

### How long do we need to be in Greece before the wedding?

Your municipality decides. The one-week wait belongs to the residents' licence and is not on Chania's or Heraklion's non-resident lists, so ask how many working days the town hall needs your originals.

### Is it different if we live in Scotland or Northern Ireland?

The FCDO and Greek steps are the same for every British passport holder, and gov.uk says only UK marriages and civil partnerships are registered in the UK. Check with the register office where your birth was registered which certificate to order for an apostille and, if you marry at home first, check with the register office where you will marry for its notice period and the documents it needs.
