## Article body

# Destination Wedding Cost per Guest Explained

[IMAGE: destination-wedding-cost-per-guest-coastal-terrace-long-table-01.webp]

Somewhere between the first venue brochure and the first deposit, the question changes. It stops being "what will the wedding cost?" and becomes "what does each person we invite actually cost us?" Abroad, that answer has more moving parts than at home: guests fly in, sleep somewhere, need getting to the venue and are often fed on more than one night.

This guide explains destination wedding cost per guest line by line: which costs rise with every name on your list, which stay fixed, who usually pays for what, and the per-guest figures our own cost guides publish. Each figure is quoted as its guide states it, in its own currency, with its guest count and date where the guide gives them, and linked to that guide. Sources current as at November 2026.

## What a per-guest cost is made of

Every quote for a wedding abroad splits into two kinds of line. Some rise with each guest you add: dinner, drinks, transfers priced per seat and any per-person levy. Others, such as sound, staging and basic decor, stay relatively flat whatever the head count. Knowing which is which tells you what one more name on the list really costs.

**Venue hire.** Venues charge for space in different ways. Some step the fee up in guest-count bands rather than per head: [our Lake Como budget guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost) lists FAI's 2026 rate for a two-hour ceremony after 6pm at Villa del Balbianello, for up to 50 guests, at €9,800 plus 22% VAT. Others double as accommodation. [Our Italy cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-wedding-in-italy-cost) notes that exclusive hire of a Tuscan borgo for 60 to 80 guests across three nights typically starts at around €27,000 to €30,000 in 2026, and can replace a separate hotel block. For worked examples, see [a 50-guest Tuscany budget, line by line](/real-weddings/wedding-in-italy-cost), and [venue hire priced by the hour in Santorini](/destination-weddings/santorini-wedding-cost).

**Catering and drinks.** These are the clearest per-guest lines. [Our Bali guide for 80 guests](/real-weddings/wedding-in-bali-cost) notes that food and beverage costs are usually calculated per guest, and suggests an itemised quotation that identifies the menu, beverage selection, service duration, minimum guest commitment and any additional per-person charges. [The wider Bali budget](/expert-advice/bali-wedding-cost-the-complete-2026-guide) has a guide of its own. Drinks can be priced by the hour or by the night; the table below gives our Italy figures.

**Minimum spends.** [Our Dubai cost guide](/destination-weddings/dubai-wedding-cost) defines a minimum spend as the least the venue will charge, however few guests you invite. It is a floor, not a per-head price, so it matters most when your list is short.

**Guest rooms and side events.** Accommodation usually sits outside the wedding quote. Our Lake Como budget guide lists guest rooms, flights, legal documents and welcome or farewell events as usually extra, and advises pricing side events at the start. [Our Santorini cost breakdown](/real-weddings/santorini-wedding-cost-breakdown) treats accommodation as a separate part of the budget, and says including welcome drinks, a rehearsal gathering or a farewell lunch in the initial budget gives a more realistic view.

**Transfers.** Where guests travel by water or air, getting there becomes a per-guest line. [Our Maldives cost guide](/real-weddings/maldives-wedding-cost) notes that seaplane and speedboat transfers are priced per person, so a guest list of 20 turns transfers into a line of its own. On the Amalfi Coast guests are usually moved by boat, and our Italy cost guide says to budget for water transfers from the start.

**Taxes and service charges.** A per-head menu price is rarely the final per-head cost. [Our guide to small weddings in Italy](/destination-weddings/small-wedding-in-italy-cost) puts VAT at 22 percent on most services and 10 percent on hotel stays and restaurant meals. In the Maldives, tax and service charge are listed at 27% (a 10% service charge plus 17% GST). In Dubai, one venue's published rate included a 7% municipality fee, a 10% service charge and 5% VAT, so ask whether a quoted rate includes those charges or adds them on top.

**Per-person levies.** Some destinations add a charge for each visitor. Our Maldives cost guide and our hidden-costs guide both list a green tax charged per person. Confirm the current rate and how it is charged with your resort before you budget it. That guide covers [the costs a headline quote tends to leave out](/destination-weddings/hidden-costs-destination-wedding) in depth.

## Who pays for guests at a destination wedding

The convention is simpler than it looks. Our guide to guest travel puts it this way: guests usually cover their own flights and most accommodation, while couples host the celebration. Our Fiji cost breakdown says the same of flights, and notes that guest arrangements sit outside its package estimates and should be calculated separately.

Beyond that, what you host is a choice, and the guide sets out [four levels of hosting](/travel-guides/who-pays-guest-travel): Essential (the events only), Considered (adding group transfers, a welcome dinner and a farewell brunch), Hosted (adding rooms for parents and the wedding party) and Fully hosted (adding rooms for every guest).

Some venues move a line for you. Our hidden-costs guide notes that Le Ciel in Santorini requires couples to arrange guest transfers, and our Amalfi Coast cost guide, budgeting a wedding for 60 to 80 guests, carries guest transport and shuttles as its own line at €2,000 to €6,000. Our who-pays guide notes that accommodation is often arranged by the couple and paid for by each guest, through a held room block that gives guests a defined place to book.

Whichever level you choose, tell guests early and in writing what is covered, so they can plan their own spend. The trade-off is plain: taking on more of the travel and accommodation costs may mean inviting fewer guests.

**If a parent is contributing.** Agree at the start which lines the contribution covers, such as the welcome dinner or guest transfers, rather than a share of an overall total. A named line is easier to follow when deposits fall due on different dates and in different currencies.

## How guest count changes the bill

Fewer guests lower the total, but they rarely lower the cost per head in step, because fixed lines and minimums are shared by fewer people. The small Italy guide shows this in its own figures: budget €800 to €1,600 per guest for a micro wedding of 10 to 20 guests, and €500 to €1,200 at 20 to 40 guests, before VAT.

Minimums sharpen the effect. Our Dubai cost guide puts it plainly: for a small wedding, a high minimum can cost more than the per-person rate suggests. Some venues set a minimum on numbers instead. Our hidden-costs guide notes that Le Ciel requires at least 20 guests over the age of 10 between 20 May and 30 September, and that couples below this number are charged an additional €190 for each missing guest. Check the venue's current terms before you sign.

Space can shape the list as much as budget. Our Santorini cost breakdown notes that guest lists at many caldera venues are capped at about 30, and says to check published menu prices for applicable taxes, service charges and minimum guest commitments.

Going the other way, each guest you add brings their own per-head lines. In Fiji that is the FJD $130 to $180 or more per person for each guest beyond the package; in the Maldives it is a separate transfer fare for every guest. So the most useful question for any venue is the cost of one more guest, and one fewer, in writing. Our guide to [how many invited guests usually attend](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-guest-attendance) can help you set realistic numbers.

Packages follow the same logic. Our guide to [how package prices work, line by line](/destination-weddings/all-inclusive-wedding-cost) describes a base fee plus a per-person rate, wrapped around a guest minimum: below the minimum, you pay for seats nobody fills. Guest travel is never in the package, and welcome events, recovery brunches and rehearsal dinners are add-ons almost everywhere. If you are weighing [our destination wedding packages](/destination-wedding-packages), ask for the same breakdown.

## Protecting deposits and guests

Deposits for a wedding abroad tend to go out months ahead, to several suppliers, on separate schedules. Our who-pays guide notes that accommodation deposits, room blocks and hosted experiences may each have separate payment schedules and cancellation conditions, and that properties in popular destinations may require a minimum number of consecutive nights in peak periods.

The ACCC's guidance on [paying for services you have not received](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/not-receiving-products-or-services-that-are-paid-for) says that under the Australian Consumer Law, businesses must not accept payment if they don't intend to supply, or if they know, or should know, that they won't be able to supply by the promised date or within a reasonable time. Where a third party prevents supply, such as government restrictions, your right to a refund or replacement will generally depend on the terms and conditions of the agreement. Its page on [refunds and cancelling a service](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought/repair-replace-refund-cancel) says that when a service has a major problem, you can cancel and get a refund, which may not be a full refund, but not if you simply changed your mind. This is general guidance, not legal advice, and it does not settle how an overseas contract treats a cancellation, so read the cancellation and postponement terms of every contract before you pay a deposit. If a contract is in Italian or another language, ask for an English version or have it translated before you sign.

Your guests carry their own risk. Smartraveller says [medical assistance is usually the highest unexpected cost](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance) for Australians overseas, that most countries won't give you free or subsidised care, and that it expects all Australians travelling overseas to take out appropriate insurance. It also says to always read the Product Disclosure Statement before buying. Put that in your guest travel guide, beside arrival times and transfer details.

Currency is the last risk. Figures here stay in the currency each guide uses, and we have not converted them. If your savings are in Australian dollars and your contracts are in euros, dirhams or Fijian dollars, the rate can move between deposit and final payment, so convert on the day you pay and keep a separate buffer for movement.

## Working out your own cost per guest

1. **List the per-head lines.** Catering, drinks, per-person transfers and any per-person levies.
2. **List the fixed lines.** Venue hire or its guest-count band, planning, photography, styling, sound and staging.
3. **List the guest-paid lines.** Flights and rooms, then mark any you have chosen to host.
4. **Ask for every quote in the same format.** Our Lake Como budget guide suggests guest count, hours and VAT on each line, so offers sit side by side.
5. **Ask for the cost of one extra guest, and one fewer.** Get it in writing, with taxes and service charges included.
6. **Add a buffer.** The small Italy guide suggests holding back 8 to 10 percent for the unexpected.
7. **Keep it in one place.** [Wonderland Studio](/wonderland-studio) is free, includes a multi-currency budget and needs no credit card.

## Questions about cost per guest, answered

### How much does a destination wedding cost per guest?

There is no single figure, because it depends on the destination and on which lines you host. As one starting point, our Italy cost guide puts catering at €150 to €220 per guest for a strong destination-wedding standard, before drinks, transfers and taxes. The table above sets out what our other guides publish, each in its own currency.

### Do you have to pay for guests at a destination wedding?

Not for everything. Guests usually cover their own flights and most accommodation, while couples host the celebration. Anything beyond that, such as group transfers, a welcome event or rooms for close family, is your choice, so tell guests early and in writing what is covered.

### Is a destination wedding cheaper per guest than a wedding at home?

Not reliably. A wedding abroad often adds lines a home wedding may not, such as guest rooms, boat or seaplane transfers and per-person levies. Our [side-by-side comparison of overseas and home budgets](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-vs-home-cost) sets the two out in full.

### Why is our cost per guest higher with fewer guests?

Because fixed lines, minimum spends and guest minimums are shared by fewer people. The section on guest count above shows the effect in our small Italy guide's own per-guest bands.

### What per-person taxes apply at a destination wedding?

It depends on the destination. Our Maldives cost guide and our hidden-costs guide both list a green tax charged per person, and venues may add service charges and local taxes on top of a menu price, as the taxes section above sets out. Confirm the current rate and how each charge applies with your venue or resort before you budget it.
