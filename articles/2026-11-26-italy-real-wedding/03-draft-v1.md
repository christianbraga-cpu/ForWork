## Lake Como real weddings

These three Lake Como villa weddings range from a weekend of Lebanese traditions to an intimate Hindu ceremony. Our [Lake Como wedding guide](/destinations/europe/italy/lake-como-wedding) covers venues, seasons and getting there, and our [Lake Como cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost) sets out the budget.

### Laura and Wahib at Villa Pizzo, Como

**Venue.** [Villa Pizzo](/venue/villa-pizzo), Como. **When.** September 2023. **Ceremony.** A blessing by a Lebanese Maronite priest based in Milan.

Laura and Wahib, both from Sydney, planned over 15 months and built a weekend around Lebanese traditions. A Laylieh, the pre-wedding dinner, was held at the Grand Imperiale Hotel, and the day before, the groom hosted a pool party and brunch while the bride took a boat cruise. The wedding closed with the cake cutting and fireworks.

"The ceremony, performed outdoors with hymns sung in Arabic, left their guests mesmerized," as our feature describes it.

### Josh and Isabela at Villa del Balbianello

**Venue.** Villa del Balbianello, Lake Como. **Ceremony.** Vows at the villa, after a civil wedding at Bambini Wine in Sydney.

Melbourne-born Isabela and Sydney-native Josh took their colours from her sister's love of pink and sunflowers, with mismatched bridesmaid looks and bouquets set against "the white, minimal backdrop of the venue". ### Kinjal and Herit at Villa Balbiano

**Venue.** [Villa Balbiano](/venue/villa-balbiano), Lake Como, with the reception the following night at Villa Passalacqua. **When.** June 2025. **Ceremony.** An intimate Gujarati Hindu ceremony.

## Italy real wedding questions answered

### Which Italy region suits a first destination wedding?

It depends on your guests and the day you picture. Lake Como and the Amalfi Coast suit a villa wedding with water views, while Tuscany and Puglia suit a long weekend on one estate, as Cristina and Brett's and Jenayde and Alfonso's weddings show.

### Can you marry legally at an Italian villa?

The pages featured here do not say which ceremonies were legal. Josh and Isabela held their civil wedding in Sydney, and Sana and Hani held a separate civil wedding. A symbolic ceremony has no legal standing in Italy, so confirm your steps with the marriage office of your chosen comune. Marrying in Italy itself? Smartraveller notes [you cannot register an overseas marriage in Australia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas), though in most cases it is recognised if valid where it happened and under Australian law.

### Can we marry in Italy outside the summer months?

The weddings on this page that state a date fall in June, September and autumn, and none confirms a winter date. Our Italy timing guide names late May to June and September to early October as the two sweet spots, and goes through the year month by month.

### How many guests travel to an Italy wedding?

It varies widely. These weddings range from Kinjal and Herit's intimate Gujarati Hindu ceremony to Sana and Hani's large celebration in Rome, and each full wedding page gives its own detail. Tiara's advice, as her page reports it, is that spreading events over four days took pressure off the wedding day.
