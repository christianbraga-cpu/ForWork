  - q: "Can you marry legally at an Italian villa?"
    a: "Our Italy legal guide explains that a symbolic ceremony has no legal standing in Italy. Confirm the requirements for your own passports with the marriage office (ufficio di stato civile) of your chosen comune or the Italian consulate. If you marry in Italy, Smartraveller notes you cannot register an overseas marriage in Australia, though in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it happened and would have been valid under Australian law had it occurred in Australia."
  - q: "Can we marry in Italy outside the summer months?"

# Italy Real Weddings to Inspire Your Celebration

Before you choose a region, it helps to see what an Italy real wedding looks like once the guests arrive. Here are 12 Italy weddings from our pages, chosen to show how each region feels, from lake villas to clifftop terraces. All were featured on Wedded Wonderland, and each card gives the venue, the month where the page states it, the ceremony and one detail worth borrowing. For couples beyond Italy, browse [all our real weddings](/blog/real-weddings). Sources current as at November 2026.

## Lake Como real weddings

These three villa weddings range from a weekend of Lebanese traditions to an intimate Hindu ceremony. Our [Lake Como destination page](/destinations/europe/italy/lake-como-wedding) covers venues, seasons and getting there.

### Laura and Wahib at Villa Pizzo, Como

**Venue.** [Villa Pizzo](/venue/villa-pizzo), Como. **When.** September 2023. **Ceremony.** A blessing by a Lebanese Maronite priest based in Milan.

[Laura and Wahib](/a-traditional-lebanese-destination-wedding-in-lake-como-italy), both from Sydney, married 15 months after their May 2022 engagement, with celebrations built around Lebanese traditions. A Laylieh, the pre-wedding dinner, was held at the Grand Imperiale Hotel, and the day before the wedding the groom hosted a pool party and brunch while the bride took a boat cruise.

"The ceremony, performed outdoors with hymns sung in Arabic, left their guests mesmerized," as our feature describes it.

*Photo and video: @saltatelier_wedding*

[IMAGE: lake_como_villa_pizzo]

### Josh and Isabela at Villa del Balbianello

**Venue.** Villa del Balbianello, Lake Como.

[Josh and Isabela's Lake Como wedding](/this-australian-couple-had-a-magical-destination-wedding-in-lake-como) began at home. Melbourne-born Isabela and Sydney-native Josh held their civil wedding at Bambini Wine in Sydney, with Josh's grandparents as witnesses, a gesture for family who could not travel. At the villa, the mother and daughter dance honoured Isabela's Colombian heritage, "guided by the rich voice of her abuela". Her sister's love of pink and sunflowers set the colours, against "the white, minimal backdrop of the venue".

Isabela's advice, as the page reports it: "rehearse with your father to avoid any unexpected 'take it off' moments".

*Wedding photographer: @lostinlove_photography*

### Kinjal and Herit at Villa Balbiano

**Venue.** [Villa Balbiano](/venue/villa-balbiano), Lake Como, with the reception the following night at Villa Passalacqua. **When.** June 2025. **Ceremony.** An intimate Gujarati Hindu ceremony.

[Kinjal and Herit's Lake Como wedding](/luxury-indian-wedding-in-lake-como) makes the honest case for a wet-weather plan, because the couple had their first dance in the rain, under lightning. Before you book a lake villa, ask where the dinner and the dancing move if the sky turns. Kinjal wore a magenta couture lehenga saree by Tarun Tahiliani with her great-grandmother's gold bangles.

"A wet bride is a lucky bride," a local nonna told Kinjal.

*Photography: @thehouseontheclouds*

## Amalfi Coast real weddings

These three show Positano's lanes and the villas of Ravello above the Tyrrhenian Sea, and our [Amalfi Coast wedding guide](/destinations/europe/italy/amalfi-coast-wedding) compares the towns.

### Tiara and Josh at Villa Oliviero, Positano

**Venue.** Villa Oliviero, Positano. **Ceremony.** Led by the bride's brother-in-law as celebrant.

[Tiara and Josh](/tiara-and-josh-had-a-picturesque-destination-wedding-in-positano-italy), from Sydney, spread their events across four days, and before the ceremony the bridal party strolled through Positano for drinks in the town square. Guests were greeted with lemon granita and fans hand-painted by the bride's Nonna, and Tiara wore three dresses from her parents' Sydney bridal store.

Tourists and locals called out "tanti auguri", as the page tells it.

*Photographer: @blaisebell*

[IMAGE: amalfi_positano_stroll]

### Loret and Franco at Villa Eva, Ravello

**Venue.** Villa Eva, Ravello. **Ceremony.** On the villa's balcony, looking out over the Amalfi Coast.

[Loret and Franco](/this-italian-and-macedonian-couple-from-australia-had-a-destination-wedding-in-ravello-italy), an Italian and Macedonian couple from Australia, married at the end of a rose petal aisle, to a harpist. Aperitif hour moved to the garden with a mandolin duo and giant chess, then dinner was served in a marquee under diamond chandeliers, with a DJ mixing Italian and Macedonian tunes. Rooftop fireworks came courtesy of a saint's name day celebration in town.

Loret's advice, in the page's words: "Don't settle, don't compete with others, and don't be afraid to do something different."

*Photographer: @mervetogan*

### Lara and Salah at Villa Cimbrone, Ravello

**Venue.** Villa Cimbrone, Ravello.

[Lara and Salah's Ravello wedding](/real-wedding-dream-wedding-in-italy) kept everything at one address above the Tyrrhenian Sea. Both the ceremony and the reception were held at Villa Cimbrone. Her structured Ashi Studio gown set a glamorous, vintage tone.

Our feature calls the father and daughter entrance "a heart-warming moment".

*Photographer: Patrick Sawaya and Imagine Weddings*

## Italy real wedding questions answered

### Which Italy region suits a first destination wedding?

It depends on the day you picture. Lake Como and the Amalfi Coast suit a villa wedding with water views, while Tuscany and Puglia suit a long weekend on one estate, as Cristina and Brett's and Jenayde and Alfonso's weddings show.

### Can you marry legally at an Italian villa?

Our [Italy legal guide](/expert-advice/getting-married-in-italy-the-complete-legal-guide-for-destination-couples) explains that a symbolic ceremony has no legal standing in Italy. Confirm the requirements for your own passports with the marriage office (ufficio di stato civile) of your chosen comune or the Italian consulate. If you marry in Italy, Smartraveller notes [you cannot register an overseas marriage in Australia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas), though in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it happened and would have been valid under Australian law had it occurred in Australia.

### Can we marry in Italy outside the summer months?

The weddings on this page that state a date fall in June, September and autumn, and none confirms a winter date. If you are considering a winter date, check with your venue and your region guide first.

### Do multi-day Italy weddings work for travelling guests?

They can. Cristina and Brett and Jenayde and Alfonso each held three days of events on one estate, from a welcome barbecue in Tuscany to a pool party in Puglia, and Tiara and Josh spread theirs over four days in Positano. Tiara's advice, as her page reports it, is that this took pressure off the wedding day.
