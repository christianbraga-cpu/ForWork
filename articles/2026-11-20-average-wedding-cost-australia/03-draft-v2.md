# Average Wedding Cost in Australia and What Drives It

[IMAGE: hero]

There is no official average wedding cost in Australia. The [ABS counts marriages, not spending](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/marriages-and-divorces-australia/latest-release) (118,804 were registered in 2025), and [Moneysmart's marriage and money guidance](https://moneysmart.gov.au/family-and-relationships/marriage-and-money) gives no average. The figures quoted online come from industry surveys with different samples, years and inclusions. Your cost is set by guest count, venue, date and what each quote includes.

You have a date in mind, a guest list that keeps growing and perhaps a parent asking what it will all cost. The natural first step is to search for the average wedding cost in Australia. You will find several confident numbers, and they rarely agree.

This guide shows why survey averages mislead and which decisions really move your total. Sources current as at November 2026.

## Why there is no official average wedding cost in Australia

We found no government source that publishes a current average wedding cost in Australia. The ABS release [Marriages and Divorces, Australia](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/marriages-and-divorces-australia/latest-release) records 118,804 marriages registered in 2025, 1.7% fewer than in 2024, and gives no figure for what they cost.

Moneysmart's [Marriage and money page](https://moneysmart.gov.au/family-and-relationships/marriage-and-money) gives no average either. It points couples to its budget planner instead, for "a good overview of the money that will be coming in and going out".

So the averages you see online come from industry surveys, not from an official source, each with its own sample, questions and year. We do not repeat them here, because none is current, independent and comparable.

## Why wedding averages mislead couples

An average sounds precise, but it hides four problems that matter more as your plans grow.

- **The mean is pulled upward.** A handful of very large celebrations lifts a mean. The median, the middle figure when weddings are lined up from smallest to largest, is steadier.
- **The sample chooses itself.** People who answer an online survey are not a random cross-section of everyone who married that year.
- **The inclusions differ.** One survey counts the engagement ring, the honeymoon and the attire. Another counts the reception alone.
- **The guest counts differ.** A 40-guest lunch and a 200-guest evening reception sit inside the same number.

Spending spreads out at both ends, so a single average describes few real weddings. If you are planning 150 guests at a harbourside venue on a Saturday in October, you are pricing one specific event, and only quotes can tell you what it costs. ## What actually drives the cost of an Australian wedding

Five decisions shape your total. Make them in roughly this order, because each one shapes the next.

**Guest count.** This is the multiplier. Every guest brings a meal, drinks, a chair, a place setting, stationery and often a share of transport. Add 20 names and every per-head line grows with them, so fix a low and a high count before you tour a single venue.

**City and venue type.** A harbourside function room, a winery, a private estate and a hotel ballroom are priced in different ways. A hotel may bundle the room, furniture, staff and catering into one package. A private estate may need furniture, power, lighting, amenities and sometimes a marquee brought in, each with its own supplier and delivery fee. Browsing [Sydney wedding venues](/au/venues-in-sydney) side by side is a quick way to see how differently packages are built.

[IMAGE: h2-3]

**Date, season and day of the week.** The ABS does not record prices, but it does show where demand gathers. Its [marriage figures for 2025](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/marriages-and-divorces-australia/latest-release) show the most marriages on Saturdays and in spring and autumn, and fewer in winter and over Easter and Christmas. When everyone asks for the same Saturdays, ask each venue how its pricing and minimum spend change by month and by day.

**Length of the celebration.** Welcome drinks and a recovery lunch are two more events, each with catering, staff and transport.

**Level of service and inclusions.** Two quotes for the same room can differ because one includes a coordinator, a longer drinks package or set-up and pack-down. Compare inclusions before totals.

## The costs couples forget to budget for

Ask about each of these early.

- **Wet-weather plan.** For an outdoor ceremony or reception, ask what the alternative is, whether it is held for your date, and the cost of a marquee hold. No one can promise the weather, but the plan can be priced.
- **Guest transfers.** Coaches or cars between ceremony, reception and hotels, including the late return.
- **Supplier meals.** Ask whether your photographer, planner and musicians need a meal, and at what per-head price.
- **Overtime.** Venue, band, photographer and bar staff each have a rate if the night runs late.
- **Surcharges.** Public holiday and card payment surcharges can add to every invoice.
- **GST.** Check whether each quote includes GST or adds it at the end.
- **Delivery, bump-in and bump-out fees.** Styling, hire furniture and florals may be charged separately for set-up and pack-down.

### Deposits, payment schedules and your rights

Read the payment schedule and the cancellation and postponement clauses before you pay a deposit. Under the Australian Consumer Law, a business [must not accept payment](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/not-receiving-products-or-services-that-are-paid-for) if it does not intend to supply, intends to supply something different from what was promised, or knows, or should know, that it cannot supply by the promised date or within a reasonable time.

If a service has [a major problem](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought/repair-replace-refund-cancel), you can cancel and get a refund, less a reasonable amount for work already done, or keep the contract at a lower price. Businesses cannot use terms and conditions to take these rights away. The consumer guarantees do not cover a change of mind, though, so if you cancel by choice, your position depends on the contract.

Where a third party prevents a business from supplying, for example government restrictions, the ACCC says refund rights [generally depend on the contract terms](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/not-receiving-products-or-services-that-are-paid-for).

These are Australian Consumer Law rules. Do not assume they cover a supplier based overseas: read that contract's cancellation, postponement and refund terms before you pay. This is general information, not legal advice. For your own contract, check with the ACCC or your state or territory consumer protection agency.

Keep a contingency as its own line, apart from the known extras above. Moneysmart's [free budget planner](https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/budget-planner) lets you create your own custom items and change the currency, so you can add wedding lines and a buffer next to your household budget.

## How to set a realistic budget for your own wedding

Your own number is built, not looked up. Work through it in this order.

1. **Fix a guest range.** Write down a low and a high count. Every later figure depends on it.
2. **Choose three priorities.** Decide what matters most, perhaps the venue, the food and the photography, and where you are happy to keep things simple.
3. **Agree who contributes what.** If parents are contributing, settle the amounts and what they cover before any deposit is paid. Moneysmart's [general money guidance for couples](https://moneysmart.gov.au/family-and-relationships/marriage-and-money) suggests talking about income, savings and what you want for the future, and notes that agreeing on shared goals helps you work as a team.
4. **Get real quotes.** Ask three shortlisted venues for itemised quotes at both ends of your guest range.
5. **Add the forgotten costs and a contingency.** Use the list above, and keep the contingency as its own line.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the average wedding cost in Australia?

There is no current official figure. The ABS records how many couples marry, not what they spend, and Moneysmart's marriage and money guidance gives no average wedding cost. The averages quoted online come from industry surveys with different samples, years and inclusions. Your own cost depends on your guest count, city and venue, date and what each quote includes.

### Why do websites quote different average wedding costs?

Because they measure different things. Surveys differ in who answers, which year they cover, whether they count the ring, honeymoon and attire, and whether they report the mean or the median. A few very large weddings pull a mean upward, so two honest surveys can publish very different numbers.

### What is the biggest wedding expense in Australia?

There is no current official breakdown, so treat any ranking with care. Venue and catering are the lines to watch, because catering and drinks are often quoted per guest and grow with every name you add. Fix your guest range before you compare venues.

### Is a destination wedding cheaper than a wedding in Australia?

Not reliably. It can cost less if your guest list is much smaller, but it can add costs a wedding at home may not carry, such as guest flights and rooms, extra days and currency movement. Compare like with like using [our side-by-side guide to overseas and home wedding budgets](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-vs-home-cost).

### How much contingency should we keep in a wedding budget?

There is no official percentage, and Moneysmart gives no wedding-specific rule. Agree the size with whoever is contributing, and keep it as its own line, separate from known extras such as surcharges, overtime and a wet-weather plan. Moneysmart's [free budget planner](https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/budget-planner) lets you add custom lines for this.

### What is the cheapest month to get married in Australia?

There is no official price data by month. [ABS figures](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/marriages-and-divorces-australia/latest-release) show fewer marriages in winter and over Easter and Christmas, and the most on Saturdays and in spring and autumn. Ask each venue whether its minimum spend is lower for a winter date or a weekday.
