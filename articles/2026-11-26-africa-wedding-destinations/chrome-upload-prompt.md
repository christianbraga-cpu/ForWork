## Article body

# Africa Wedding Destinations From Safari to Coast

[IMAGE: hero]

A wedding in Africa can mean four very different days, and the best Africa wedding destinations each lean towards one of them. A safari wedding puts the ceremony at a lodge or camp in the bush. The coast and islands run from Cape Town's Atlantic shore to Zanzibar, Seychelles and Mauritius in the Indian Ocean. The Cape Winelands offer estates where the vows, the dinner and often the guests share one property. And North Africa brings riads in Marrakech and the Nile in Egypt.

Here are our 12 picks for an African destination wedding, ranked, each with the season as official sources or our own guides word it, whether you can marry legally there, and the exact travel advice for your guests. Sources current as at November 2026.

## How we chose these 12

This is an editorial judgement, not a weather score or a popularity list. We weighed five things:

- **Setting.** What the place gives a wedding that another would not.
- **Season.** Only as Smartraveller or our own guides describe it.
- **Legal or symbolic.** Is there an official legal route for foreigners? Where there is not, confirm the legal route with that country's embassy before you plan.
- **Smartraveller level.** Quoted exactly, as at November 2026. Levels can change, so check again before you book and before guests fly.
- **Guest travel.** Entry steps, transfers and how far everyone has to go.

South Africa takes the top three places because a city coast, the Winelands and the safari reserves sit inside one country and one legal system. Still choosing a region? Our [destination wedding quiz](/destination-wedding-quiz) gives you a ranked top three.

## Our 12 Africa wedding destinations

### 1. Cape Town and the Atlantic coast, South Africa

**Why it works.** A city wedding with the ocean on one side and the mountain on the other. It is also a straightforward guest trip: one country, one set of entry steps, and the Winelands and the reserves in the same itinerary.

**Season.** Smartraveller's rainy-season line for South Africa is national, so check the Cape Town season with the South African Weather Service.

**Legal or symbolic.** The South African consulate's guidance says [foreigners can marry in South Africa under South African law](https://dirco.gov.za/newyork/getting-married-in-sa/), presenting original passports to the marriage officer on the day, with the ceremony booked by appointment. Our [South Africa legal guide](/destination-weddings/weddings-in-south-africa) adds that a celebrant who is not an authorised marriage officer cannot register a legal marriage. Confirm the current process with the Department of Home Affairs.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise a high degree of caution](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/south-africa)" and notes that crime and violence are serious issues across South Africa.

### 2. Cape Winelands, South Africa

**Why it works.** Stellenbosch, Franschhoek and Paarl suit estate weddings, where the ceremony, a long lunch or dinner and, at some properties, the guests' rooms share one address. For inspiration, see two real weddings we have featured, [a wedding at La Roche Estate in South Africa](/a-dreamy-wedding-at-la-roche-estate-south-africa) and [a glam winery wedding in the Cape Winelands (MolenVliet)](/glam-south-african-winery-makes-perfect-destination-wedding).

**Season.** As for Cape Town.

**Legal or symbolic.** South African law applies, as above. Our South Africa guide notes that marriages are in community of property unless an ante-nuptial contract is signed, so raise it with your own lawyer before you travel.

**Travel advice.** The South Africa level above applies here too.

[IMAGE: winelands_reception]

### 3. Sabi Sands and Greater Kruger, South Africa

**Why it works.** A safari wedding that sits under South African law. Guest numbers depend on how many each lodge sleeps, so the guest list and the lodge are chosen together.

**Season.** Our [safari and island honeymoon guide](/honeymoon-ideas/african-honeymoon) puts South Africa's reserves at their best from May to September. Smartraveller gives the [national rainy season as November to March](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/south-africa), with heavy rainfall and thunderstorms.

**Legal or symbolic.** The South African rules above apply. Confirm with the Department of Home Affairs where the legal ceremony itself can take place.

**Travel advice.** The South Africa level above applies here too.

### 4. Maasai Mara, Kenya

**Why it works.** The classic open-savannah ceremony, with game drives filling the days either side. It suits couples who want the wedding and the safari to be one trip.

**Season.** Smartraveller says [Kenya normally has two rainy seasons, March to June and October to November](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/kenya), and that flash flooding and mudslides are common. Our honeymoon guide puts safari at its best from May to October. Read together, that puts July to September outside both rainy seasons.

**Legal or symbolic.** Kenya's State Law Office says [a special licence applies where one or both parties are foreign nationals](https://statelaw.go.ke/services-to-the-public/getting-married-in-kenya/registration-of-marriage-by-special-license/), or where the marriage is outside a gazetted location, such as a garden or hotel. Both of you must be resident in Kenya for at least seven days before applying online through eCitizen, followed by an interview with original documents. Confirm the current process with the Registrar of Marriages.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise a high degree of caution](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/kenya)", with higher levels in some areas, including the regions bordering Somalia.

[IMAGE: bush_airstrip]

### 5. Serengeti and Ngorongoro, Tanzania

**Why it works.** Wide plains and the crater, and the natural first half of a safari and beach week. Our honeymoon guide suggests five to seven nights on safari, then three to four nights in Zanzibar.

**Season.** Smartraveller gives Tanzania's [rainy season as March to May and November to December](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/tanzania), when floods can block roads.

**Legal or symbolic.** Tanzania's High Commission in New Delhi says [foreigners marrying in Tanzania need a certificate of no impediment](https://www.in.tzembassy.go.tz/services/married-in-tanzania-abroad) from their home country. With less than 21 days for a notice of intention to marry, you apply to the Registrar of Marriages for a special marriage licence, and two witnesses are required. Confirm the current steps with the Registrar of Marriages.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise a high degree of caution](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/tanzania)" due to the risk of violent crime and civil unrest, and higher levels apply in some areas.

### 6. Zanzibar, Tanzania

**Why it works.** A beach ceremony after the safari, with Stone Town's old streets for a welcome dinner.

**Season.** Beyond Tanzania's rainy seasons, Smartraveller says [monsoons occur in coastal and island areas from July to October](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/tanzania), and that cyclones in coastal areas are also common.

**Legal or symbolic.** The same High Commission notes that [Zanzibar marriage certificates must be certified](https://www.in.tzembassy.go.tz/services/married-in-tanzania-abroad) by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dar es Salaam. For the island steps, confirm with the embassy and Zanzibar's civil registration authority, or marry at home first.

**Travel advice.** The Tanzania level above applies here too.

[IMAGE: zanzibar_dhow]

### 7. Okavango Delta, Botswana

**Why it works.** Water channels and small, private camps make this the setting for a micro wedding or an elopement rather than a crowd.

**Season.** Smartraveller says [Botswana's wet season is usually November to March](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/botswana), with flooding on low ground around rivers. Our honeymoon guide gives the dry season as May to October, with flood waters in the Delta peaking from June to August.

**Legal or symbolic.** Confirm with the embassy. Until then, plan the Delta ceremony as symbolic, after a legal wedding at home.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise normal safety precautions](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/botswana)".

### 8. Victoria Falls, Zambia and Zimbabwe

**Why it works.** The falls and the Zambezi river lodges, one destination shared by two countries. Choose your side first, because it sets your legal system and your travel advice.

**Season.** Our honeymoon guide says water levels are highest from March to June, and that from September to December the lower water allows swimming at Devil's Pool on the Zambian side.

**Legal or symbolic.** Confirm with the embassy of the side you marry on, or hold a symbolic ceremony after a legal wedding at home.

**Travel advice.** For Zambia, Smartraveller says "[Exercise normal safety precautions](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/zambia)", with higher levels in some areas. For Zimbabwe it says "[Exercise a high degree of caution](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/zimbabwe) in Zimbabwe due to the threat of crime and the risk of civil unrest."

### 9. Seychelles

**Why it works.** Small-island beaches for an intimate wedding, with a legal route set out clearly for two foreigners.

**Season.** Smartraveller says [heavy rain and flooding can occur, especially during the monsoon season from November to March](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/seychelles).

**Legal or symbolic.** Seychelles Immigration and Civil Status says [any person in accordance with the Laws of Seychelles can get married](https://ics.gov.sc/civil-status/getting-married). Its 2025 procedure for two foreigners sets out [banns published for 11 days before the marriage date](https://ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/notices), or a licence instead, with apostilled original documents and a certificate issued within 24 hours. Our [Seychelles step-by-step guide](/destination-weddings/getting-married-in-seychelles) covers the documents; confirm the details with Seychelles Civil Status.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise normal safety precautions](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/seychelles)".

[IMAGE: seychelles_beach]

### 10. Mauritius

**Why it works.** An Indian Ocean island nation suited to resort weddings, where the ceremony, the dinner and the guests can stay in one place.

**Season.** Smartraveller says [cyclone season is November to May, but cyclones can also occur at other times](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/mauritius). [Our Mauritius guide](/destination-weddings/mauritius-wedding) describes winter, June to September, as relatively cool and dry, with October the driest month.

**Legal or symbolic.** There is no official current page in our sources for two foreign nationals marrying in Mauritius. Confirm the legal route with the Mauritius embassy and the Civil Status Division before you plan; our Mauritius guide explains what to ask. Smartraveller notes that [same-sex marriage and civil unions are not legally recognised](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/mauritius) in Mauritius.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise normal safety precautions](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/mauritius)", and reports localised demonstrations and community unrest in October.

### 11. Marrakech and the Agafay desert, Morocco

**Why it works.** Riad courtyards, palaces and desert camps for a design-led celebration over several days. See [desert venues in Morocco and Egypt](/enchanting-desert-destinations-for-a-middle-eastern-wedding).

**Season.** Smartraveller says flash flooding can occur, with the risk higher [during the rainy season from November to March](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/morocco).

**Legal or symbolic.** Australia's embassy in Morocco says [marriages are not performed or registered at the embassy](https://morocco.embassy.gov.au/rbat/marriage_Morocco.html), that you may be asked for a Certificate of No Impediment, and that Moroccan requirements come from the Moroccan diplomatic mission. Confirm with the Moroccan embassy. Same-sex couples should read the local laws section of Smartraveller's Morocco page first.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise a high degree of caution](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/morocco)", with higher levels in some areas, and notes that protests may occur at short notice.

[IMAGE: marrakech_riad]

### 12. Egypt, the Nile and the Red Sea

**Why it works.** A Nile cruise or a Red Sea resort, with temples and desert as the backdrop to the celebration.

**Season.** Smartraveller says [sand and dust storms occur between March and May](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/egypt).

**Legal or symbolic.** Australia's embassy in Egypt explains that, under Egyptian law, [non-Egyptians must conduct a civil marriage at the Foreigners' Marriage Office at the Ministry of Justice](https://egypt.embassy.gov.au/caro/4.html), with a Certificate of No Impediment. The Nile or Red Sea ceremony is then the celebration, not the legal act. Confirm with the Egyptian Embassy in Canberra, and same-sex couples should read Smartraveller's local laws section first.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise a high degree of caution](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/egypt)", with higher levels in some areas, and warns that regional tensions could disrupt air travel.

## Planning a wedding in Africa from Australia

**Legal at home, celebration in Africa.** Smartraveller explains that [you cannot register an overseas marriage in Australia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas), but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it happened and would have been valid under Australian law. Where a destination's legal route is unclear, a legal ceremony at home and a symbolic one in Africa keeps the paperwork simple. Check any name change with your state or territory BDM registry.

**Guest entry steps.** Smartraveller lists [an online traveller declaration](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/south-africa) to enter or leave South Africa, [an ETA and mandatory inbound travel health insurance](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/kenya) for Kenya, and [mandatory inbound travel insurance from designated national insurers](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/tanzania) for Tanzania and Zanzibar. ## Africa wedding questions answered

### What is the best month for a wedding in Africa?

It depends on the destination. Our honeymoon guide puts safari at its best from May to October, while Smartraveller places the Seychelles monsoon from November to March and the Mauritius cyclone season from November to May. Match the month to the place first.

### Can foreigners marry legally in Africa?

In several places, yes. Official sources set out routes for foreigners in South Africa, Kenya, mainland Tanzania, Seychelles and Egypt, each with conditions. For Mauritius, Botswana, Victoria Falls, Zanzibar and Morocco, confirm with the embassy, or marry at home first.

### Can same-sex couples marry in Africa?

Smartraveller says same-sex marriage and civil unions are [not legally recognised in Mauritius](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/mauritius). For every other country on this list, read the local laws section of its Smartraveller page and confirm with the embassy before you choose a destination.

### How many guests can we bring to a safari wedding?

There is no single number. It depends on how many each lodge or camp sleeps, so the guest list and the venue are chosen together. Cape Town and the Winelands suit larger weddings, and the Okavango Delta suits micro weddings and elopements.

### Is Africa safe for our wedding guests?

Advice varies by country. As at November 2026, Smartraveller says "Exercise normal safety precautions" for Seychelles, Mauritius, Botswana and Zambia, and "Exercise a high degree of caution" for South Africa, Kenya, Tanzania, Zimbabwe, Morocco and Egypt. Check again before guests book.
