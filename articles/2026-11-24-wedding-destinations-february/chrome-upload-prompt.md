## Article body

# Wedding Destinations in February and Our 10 Best Picks

[IMAGE: hero]

Is February a good time for a wedding abroad? It is, once you choose the right hemisphere. February is summer in Queenstown and Tasmania, the cooler months in the Gulf and winter in the Swiss Alps, and it sits outside the monsoon and wet seasons in the Maldives and Thailand.

The best wedding destinations in February are spread across four very different settings. Here are our ten, ranked, each with one honest caveat, then the places we would wait on and the planning February asks of you. Sources current as at November 2026.

## How we ranked the ten

This is an editorial judgement, not a weather score. We weighed four things:

- **February conditions,** as Smartraveller, a national weather service or our own guide describes them.
- **Guest travel from Australia,** including the transfer at the far end.
- **What February gives you there** that another month would not.
- **What the calendar asks of you,** from Ramadan in the Gulf to Valentine's week.

No temperature or price appears unless an official source or our own guide publishes it, and travel advice levels are as at November 2026 and can change. Coming from [our January picks](/destination-weddings/wedding-destinations-january) or still choosing a region? Our [destination wedding quiz](/destination-wedding-quiz) gives you a ranked top three. Simply travelling that month? See [our February travel guide](/travel-guides/best-places-to-travel-in-february).

## Our 10 best wedding destinations in February

### 1. The Maldives

**Why February works.** Smartraveller says [the Maldives monsoon season is usually from mid-May to November](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/maldives), so February sits outside it. Our guide to [where to honeymoon in February](/expert-advice/where-to-go-on-honeymoon-in-february) calls February "often the driest month of the year in the Maldives, with calm seas and some of the clearest water of the season."

**The honest caveat.** Most resorts are on private islands reached by seaplane or speedboat, and each guest travels on a separate fare, so check the last transfer of the day against your guests' international arrivals.

**Who it suits.** Small, private guest lists. Our [guide to Maldives resort wedding costs](/real-weddings/maldives-wedding-cost) builds its figures around 20 guests.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise a high degree of caution in Maldives due to the risk of civil unrest and the threat of terrorism.](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/maldives)"

[IMAGE: maldives]

### 2. Phuket and the Andaman coast, Thailand

**Why February works.** The Thai Meteorological Department puts [the northeast monsoon, its dry and cold season, between mid-October and mid-February, and summer from mid-February to mid-May](https://www.tmd.go.th/media/climate/climate-monthly/winter2024-2025.pdf). Our February honeymoon guide adds: "On Thailand's Andaman coast, February is usually the driest and least humid month of the year."

**The honest caveat.** Those seasons are national, not specific to Phuket, so hold a covered space anyway. For a legal marriage rather than a symbolic one, confirm the steps with the Thai authorities and the Australian embassy.

**Who it suits.** Beach weddings at a resort that can host everyone in one place.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller gives Thailand's wet season as usually May to December, and advises "[Exercise a high degree of caution in Thailand overall due to security and safety risks.](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/thailand)"

[IMAGE: andaman_coast]

### 3. Dubai, UAE

**Why February works.** Our [month-by-month Dubai guide](/destination-weddings/best-time-wedding-dubai) describes February as "among the most comfortable months, with a strong calendar of events across the city, so venues fill early."

**The honest caveat.** Our Dubai best-time guide notes that Ramadan and Eid fall in February and March in 2027, so check the exact dates for your year before you set a Gulf date.

**Who it suits.** Larger celebrations, with one hotel hosting the ceremony, reception and guests.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller advises you to "[exercise a high degree of caution due to the threat of terrorism and the unpredictable security situation in the region](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/middle-east/united-arab-emirates)," and notes that intense storms can occur between December and May and may lead to flash flooding.

### 4. Ras Al Khaimah, UAE

**Why February works.** Our [Ras Al Khaimah wedding guide](/destinations/middle-east/ras-al-khaimah) names October to April as the best time to visit, "when temperatures are pleasant for outdoor activities and ceremonies", and our February honeymoon guide says "February is one of the last mild months in the Gulf."

**The honest caveat.** The Ramadan check for Dubai applies here too. Smartraveller adds that [morning fog in winter can delay flights](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/middle-east/united-arab-emirates), so avoid tight connections.

**Who it suits.** A slower beach and desert weekend for guests who like space.

**Travel advice.** The UAE level and storm warning above apply here too.

[IMAGE: gulf_desert]

### 5. Doha, Qatar

**Why February works.** Our page on [getting married in Qatar](/get-wedded-in-qatar) names November to April as the best time, "when the weather is ideal for outdoor weddings". Smartraveller's [Qatar extreme heat warning](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/middle-east/qatar) is for June to September, when temperatures can reach over 50°C.

**The honest caveat.** The Ramadan check applies here too. Confirm your legal route with the Qatari authorities and the Australian embassy.

**Who it suits.** A polished city wedding with the reception outdoors in the evening.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller says "[Exercise a high degree of caution in Qatar due to the unpredictable security situation in the region.](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/middle-east/qatar)"

### 6. Mexico, Riviera Maya and Los Cabos

**Why February works.** Smartraveller gives [Mexico's hurricane season as June to November](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/americas/mexico), and our [Mexico wedding page](/destinations/latin-america-caribbean/mexico) calls the dry season from November to May "most popular and reliable for weather".

**The honest caveat.** Smartraveller adds that tropical storms and hurricanes can happen in other months. The dry season is the busy one, so hold room blocks early and give guests extra nights after the long trip.

**Who it suits.** Guests happy to travel far for a week by the sea.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller advises "[Exercise a high degree of caution in Mexico overall due to the threat of violent crime. Higher levels apply in some areas.](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/americas/mexico)" Check the level for your resort's state.

### 7. Queenstown, New Zealand

**Why February works.** Our February honeymoon guide says "February is one of the most settled months of Queenstown's summer, with warm, dry days and calm evenings on Lake Wakatipu." NIWA's report on [the climate and weather of Otago](https://webstatic.niwa.co.nz/library/NIWAsts67.pdf) notes that summer daily maximums can exceed 30°C, especially inland.

**The honest caveat.** Smartraveller says [weather conditions in New Zealand can change quickly](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/new-zealand), so hold an indoor space and plan shade for a warm afternoon. Confirm marriage requirements with the New Zealand registering authority.

**Who it suits.** A short trip for grandparents and young children.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller's advice is "Exercise normal safety precautions in New Zealand."

[IMAGE: queenstown_lake]

### 8. Tasmania

**Why February works.** [Bureau of Meteorology averages for Hobart](https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/averages/tables/cw_094029.shtml) show February is its driest month on average and as warm as January. For Australians it is a domestic trip with no passports needed. Confirm the legal requirements for your marriage with your celebrant.

**The honest caveat.** Those averages are for Hobart, and conditions elsewhere on the island differ. Ask your venue how it handles fire-danger restrictions on the day for candles, open flames and fireworks, and plan an evening that needs none of them.

**Who it suits.** An island wedding without leaving the country, within reach of every generation.

**Travel advice.** No overseas travel advice applies.

### 9. The Swiss Alps

**Why February works.** Our page on [marrying in Switzerland](/destinations/europe/switzerland) says winter, December to March, turns the Alps into a snowy setting "ideal for romantic elopements or glamorous ski weddings".

**The honest caveat.** Snow is never guaranteed, and mountain transfers depend on the weather, so build a buffer into arrival days. Confirm civil requirements with the Swiss civil registry office where you plan to marry.

**Who it suits.** Smaller winter weekends with guests who love the cold.

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller advises "[Exercise normal safety precautions in Switzerland](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/switzerland)" and warns that avalanches, flash floods, rock falls, mudslides and sudden weather changes occur in alpine areas, and that mountain rescues can be extremely costly.

[IMAGE: swiss_alps]

### 10. Madeira, Portugal

**Why February works.** Madeira is our pick for couples who want Europe in February without snow. The Portuguese weather service's [1991 to 2020 climate normals for Funchal](https://www.ipma.pt/bin/file.data/climate-normal/cn_91-20_FUNCHAL.pdf) give February a mean daily maximum of 19.9°C and a mean daily minimum of 13.9°C.

**The honest caveat.** The same normals count 5.5 February days of rain of 1 mm or more, for Funchal only. Choose a quinta (a historic country estate) with a covered space, tell guests to pack a layer for the evening, and confirm civil requirements with the Portuguese civil registry.

**Who it suits.** Couples who want gardens and history rather than a beach. See [weddings in Portugal](/destinations/europe/portugal).

**Travel advice.** Smartraveller advises "[Exercise normal safety precautions](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/portugal)" for Portugal, and notes that localised flooding can occur during winter, particularly in urban areas.

[IMAGE: madeira_quinta]

## Where to wait for a better month

**Bali.** We would wait, since February falls within [Smartraveller's usual wet season for Indonesia (October to April)](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia), when floods, landslides and mudslides occur regularly and heavy rains can cause significant flooding in urban areas, including in Bali. Our Bali page says the dry season, April to October, has the most reliable weather for outdoor ceremonies. To pick a date, see [Bali month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-bali).

**Fiji.** We would wait here too, since February falls within [Fiji's cyclone season (November to April)](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/fiji), though cyclones may occur at other times, and floods can cut off road access, including to the airports. [Our Fiji page](/destinations/oceania/fiji) rates February "mixed", the "wettest stretch for many areas", and points to May to October for the most settled weather.

**Greece.** We would not plan a Greek island wedding for February. Our [Greece timing guide](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece) walks through the season so you can choose a better date.

**Italy.** [Our Italy guide](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-italy) calls late May to June and September to early October the two sweet spots, and notes that many coastal and lakeside venues close from November to March.

## Planning a February date abroad

- **Valentine's week.** Our February honeymoon guide notes that "Valentine's Day brings minimum stays and higher rates to many resorts, so arriving after mid-month often brings better availability." For a wedding, treat the days around 14 February as high demand and hold your room block before save-the-dates go out.
- **Ramadan.** Ramadan and Eid follow the Islamic lunar calendar and move earlier each year, as our Dubai guide notes, so check the dates for any Gulf pick.
- **Transfers.** Seaplanes, mountain roads and winter fog at Gulf airports can all move a schedule. Price every leg and avoid tight connections.
- **A weather backup.** Ask what the covered option is, whether it is held for your date and what it costs.
- **Lead time.** Our Ras Al Khaimah guide recommends 12 to 18 months, especially for popular venues and peak season dates, and our Dubai guide says 10 to 12 months is commonly advised for peak winter dates.
- **The legal side.** Smartraveller explains that [you cannot register an overseas marriage in Australia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas), but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it happened and would have been valid under Australian law. Confirm the requirements for your passports with the destination's registering office and the Australian embassy or consulate, and check any name change with your state or territory BDM registry.
- **Insurance.** Smartraveller notes that [medical assistance is usually the highest unexpected cost](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance) for Australians overseas. For the Alps, check that your cover includes mountain rescue.

## February wedding questions answered

### Is February a good time for a wedding?

Yes, if you match the place to the hemisphere: summer in Queenstown and Tasmania, cooler months in the Gulf, winter in the Swiss Alps, and the Maldives and Thailand outside their wet seasons. Bali and Fiji are better in another month.

### What are the best places to get married in February for a beach wedding?

The Maldives and Phuket lead our list, with Ras Al Khaimah and Mexico's Riviera Maya and Los Cabos further down. For an outdoor wedding closer to home, Queenstown and Tasmania are in summer, with no long-haul flight for guests.

### Does Ramadan affect a February wedding in Dubai, Ras Al Khaimah or Doha?

It can. Our Dubai best-time guide notes that Ramadan and Eid fall in February and March in 2027. Check the exact dates for your year, and ask your venue early what changes for events during the month.

### How far ahead should we book a February wedding abroad?

We would plan from 12 to 18 months out, the range our Ras Al Khaimah guide recommends, and earlier for Valentine's week or a large room block. Our Dubai guide says 10 to 12 months is commonly advised for peak winter dates.

### Can we marry in Bali or Fiji in February?

You can, but we would wait. Our Bali page points to April to October for outdoor ceremonies, and our Fiji page points to May to October for the most settled weather. If February is fixed, look at the Maldives or Phuket instead.
