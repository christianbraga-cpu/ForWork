# Wedding Destinations in February and Our 10 Best Picks

[IMAGE: hero]

Is February a good time for a wedding abroad? It is, once you choose the right hemisphere. February is summer in Queenstown and Tasmania, part of the cooler months in the Gulf and winter in the Swiss Alps, and it sits outside the monsoon and wet seasons in the Maldives and Thailand. The same month is wet season in Bali and cyclone season in Fiji.

That is why the best wedding destinations in February are spread across four very different settings. Below are our ten picks, ranked, each with one honest caveat, followed by the places we would wait on and the planning a February date asks of you. Season wording comes from official sources or our own destination guides, linked where it appears. Sources current as at November 2026.

## How we ranked the ten

This is an editorial judgement, not a weather score. We weighed four things:

- **February conditions,** as Smartraveller, a national weather service or our own guide describes them.
- **Guest travel from Australia,** including the transfer at the far end.
- **What February gives you there** that another month would not.
- **What the calendar asks of you,** from Ramadan in the Gulf to Valentine's week.

No temperature or price appears unless an official source or our own guide publishes it. If you are coming from [our January picks](/destination-weddings/wedding-destinations-january) or still choosing a region, our [destination wedding quiz](/destination-wedding-quiz) gives you a ranked top three to start from.

## Our 10 best wedding destinations in February

### 1. The Maldives

**Why February works.** Smartraveller says [the Maldives monsoon season is usually from mid-May to November](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/maldives), so February sits outside it, and our guide to [where to honeymoon in February](/expert-advice/where-to-go-on-honeymoon-in-february) calls it often the driest month there, with calm seas. Think of a sandbank ceremony with the lagoon behind you.

**The honest caveat.** Most resorts are on private islands reached by seaplane or speedboat, and each guest travels on a separate fare, so check the last transfer of the day against your guests' international arrivals. At the time of writing, [Smartraveller's advice](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/maldives) is "Exercise a high degree of caution in Maldives due to the risk of civil unrest and the threat of terrorism."

**Who it suits.** Small, private guest lists. Our [guide to Maldives resort wedding costs](/real-weddings/maldives-wedding-cost) builds its figures around 20 guests.

[IMAGE: maldives]

### 2. Phuket and the Andaman coast, Thailand

**Why February works.** Thailand's Government Public Relations Department, citing the Department of Meteorology, puts [winter between mid-October and mid-February and summer from mid-February to mid-May](https://thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/009_142), and Smartraveller gives [Thailand's wet season as usually May to December](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/thailand). Our February honeymoon guide calls February the driest, least humid month on the Andaman coast, a natural month for a sunset ceremony facing the sea.

**The honest caveat.** Those seasons are national, not specific to Phuket, so hold a covered space anyway. Smartraveller's advice at the time of writing is "Exercise a high degree of caution in Thailand overall due to security and safety risks." For a legal marriage rather than a symbolic one, confirm the steps with the Thai authorities and the Australian embassy.

**Who it suits.** Beach weddings at a resort that can host everyone. Browse [our Asia destinations](/destinations/asia) for venues.

[IMAGE: andaman_coast]

### 3. Dubai, UAE

**Why February works.** Our [month-by-month Dubai guide](/destination-weddings/best-time-wedding-dubai) counts February among the most comfortable months, and notes that venues fill early. Think of a desert reception or a long terrace dinner under the sky.

**The honest caveat.** Check Ramadan dates for your year with official sources, as it can fall in February. Smartraveller notes that [intense storms can occur between December and May](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/middle-east/united-arab-emirates) and may lead to flash flooding, and at the time of writing it advises you to "exercise a high degree of caution due to the threat of terrorism and the unpredictable security situation in the region."

**Who it suits.** Larger celebrations, with one hotel hosting the ceremony, the reception and many of the guests. Our free [Dubai wedding magazine](/magazine/uae/get-wedded-in-dubai) is a good next read.

### 4. Ras Al Khaimah, UAE

**Why February works.** Our [Ras Al Khaimah wedding guide](/destinations/middle-east/ras-al-khaimah) names October to April as the best time to visit, "when temperatures are pleasant for outdoor activities and ceremonies", and our February honeymoon guide calls February one of the last mild months in the Gulf. Plan a beach ceremony and a desert dinner in one weekend.

**The honest caveat.** The Ramadan check and Smartraveller's storm warning and advice level for Dubai apply here too. Smartraveller adds that morning fog in winter can delay flights, so avoid tight connections. Our guide suggests planning 12 to 18 months ahead.

**Who it suits.** A slower beach and desert weekend. Search [venues across the emirate](/ae/venues-in-ras-al-khaimah) to see the range.

[IMAGE: gulf_desert]

### 5. Doha, Qatar

**Why February works.** Our page on [getting married in Qatar](/get-wedded-in-qatar) names November to April as the best time, "when the weather is ideal for outdoor weddings". Smartraveller's [Qatar extreme heat warning](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/middle-east/qatar) is for June to September, when temperatures can reach over 50°C.

**The honest caveat.** Check Ramadan dates for your year before you hold a venue. At the time of writing, Smartraveller says "Exercise a high degree of caution in Qatar due to the unpredictable security situation in the region," and flags possible airspace closures that could affect flights. Confirm your legal route with the Qatari authorities and the Australian embassy.

**Who it suits.** A polished city wedding with the reception outdoors in the evening.

### 6. Mexico, Riviera Maya and Los Cabos

**Why February works.** Smartraveller gives [Mexico's hurricane season as June to November](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/americas/mexico), and our [Mexico wedding page](/destinations/latin-america-caribbean/mexico) calls the dry season from November to May "most popular and reliable for weather".

**The honest caveat.** Smartraveller adds that tropical storms and hurricanes can happen in other months, and advises "Exercise a high degree of caution in Mexico overall due to the threat of violent crime. Higher levels apply in some areas," so check the level for your resort's state. Peak demand means room blocks go early, and your guests face a long trip, so give them extra nights.

**Who it suits.** Guests happy to travel far for a week by the sea. Our [Mexico guide by region and season](/destination-weddings/mexico-destination-wedding-guide) compares the coasts.

### 7. Queenstown, New Zealand

**Why February works.** Our February honeymoon guide calls February one of Queenstown's most settled summer months, and it is a short trans-Tasman flight. NIWA's report on [the climate and weather of Otago](https://webstatic.niwa.co.nz/library/NIWAsts67.pdf) notes that summer daily maximums can exceed 30°C, especially inland. Picture a lakeside ceremony in late light, with the mountains behind you.

**The honest caveat.** Smartraveller says [weather conditions in New Zealand can change quickly](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/new-zealand), so hold an indoor space and plan shade for a warm afternoon. Its advice is "Exercise normal safety precautions in New Zealand." Confirm marriage requirements with the New Zealand registering authority.

**Who it suits.** An easy trip for grandparents and young children. See [our Oceania destinations](/destinations/oceania).

[IMAGE: queenstown_lake]

### 8. Tasmania

**Why February works.** Our January coverage cites Discover Tasmania on the warmest months running from December to March. For Australians it is a domestic trip, with no passports or foreign marriage paperwork.

**The honest caveat.** Ask your venue how it handles fire-danger restrictions on the day for candles, open flames and fireworks, and keep a version of the evening that needs none of them.

**Who it suits.** An island wedding without leaving the country, easy for every generation. Start with our [Get Wedded in Australia magazine](/magazine/australia/get-wedded-in-australia).

### 9. The Swiss Alps

**Why February works.** Our page on [marrying in Switzerland](/destinations/europe/switzerland) describes winter, December to March, as turning the Alps into a snowy setting for chalet and ski weddings. For guests leaving an Australian summer, that contrast is the point: firelight, snow at the window and a long dinner.

**The honest caveat.** Snow is never guaranteed, and mountain transfers depend on the weather, so build a buffer into arrival days and ask early about chalet capacity. Confirm civil requirements with the Swiss civil registry office where you plan to marry.

**Who it suits.** Smaller winter weekends with guests who love the cold.

[IMAGE: swiss_chalet]

### 10. Madeira, Portugal

**Why February works.** Our list of [Europe wedding destinations](/destination-weddings/europe-wedding-destinations) cites Visit Madeira on the island's mild climate all year, which makes it our one southern European pick for February. Quintas (historic country estates) and their gardens set the scene.

**The honest caveat.** Mild is not hot. Choose a quinta with a covered space, tell guests to pack layers, and confirm civil requirements with the Portuguese civil registry.

**Who it suits.** Europe in February without the snow. See [weddings in Portugal](/destinations/europe/portugal).

## Where to wait for a better month

**Bali.** Smartraveller says [Indonesia's wet season usually runs from October to April](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia), when floods, landslides and mudslides occur regularly, and that heavy rains can cause significant flooding in urban areas, including in Bali. [Our Bali page](/destinations/asia/bali) says the dry season, April to October, has the most reliable weather for outdoor ceremonies. To pick a date, see [Bali month by month](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-bali).

**Fiji.** Smartraveller says [November to April is cyclone season](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/fiji), though cyclones may occur at other times, and that floods can cut off road access, including to the airports. [Our Fiji page](/destinations/oceania/fiji) rates February "mixed", the "wettest stretch for many areas", and points to May to October for the most settled weather.

**Greece.** We would not plan a Greek island wedding for February. Our guide to [the best months to marry in Greece](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece) walks through the season so you can choose a better date.

## Planning a February date abroad

A February wedding asks for a few things a quieter month does not.

- **Valentine's week.** Treat the days around 14 February as high demand. Ask each venue early whether minimum stays or premium rates apply, and hold your room block before save-the-dates go out.
- **Ramadan.** For the Gulf picks, check Ramadan dates for your year with official sources, as it can fall in February. An early-February date, or one after Eid, may be simpler to plan around.
- **Transfers.** Seaplanes, mountain roads and winter fog at Gulf airports can all move a schedule. Price every leg and avoid tight connections.
- **A weather backup.** No one can promise February weather anywhere on this list. Ask what the covered option is, whether it is held for your date and what it costs.
- **Lead time.** Our Ras Al Khaimah guide suggests 12 to 18 months. We would start there for any February date, and earlier for Valentine's week.
- **The legal side.** Smartraveller explains that [you cannot register an overseas marriage in Australia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas), but in most cases it is legally recognised if it was valid where it happened and would have been valid under Australian law. Confirm the requirements for your passports with the destination's registering office and the Australian embassy or consulate, and check any name change with your state or territory BDM registry.
- **Insurance.** Smartraveller notes that [medical assistance is usually the highest unexpected cost](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance) for Australians overseas.

## February wedding questions answered

### Is February a good time for a wedding?

Yes, if you match the place to the hemisphere. February sits outside the monsoon and wet seasons in the Maldives and Thailand, in the cooler months in the Gulf, in summer in Queenstown and Tasmania and in winter in the Swiss Alps. Smartraveller places it in Bali's usual wet season and Fiji's cyclone season.

### Where can we have a beach wedding in February?

The Maldives and Phuket lead our list, with Ras Al Khaimah and Mexico's Riviera Maya and Los Cabos further down. For an outdoor wedding closer to home, Queenstown and Tasmania are in summer.

### Does Ramadan affect a February wedding in Dubai, Ras Al Khaimah or Doha?

It can. Check Ramadan dates for your year with official sources, as it can fall in February, and ask your venue early what changes for events during the month.

### How far ahead should we book a February wedding abroad?

Our Ras Al Khaimah guide suggests 12 to 18 months ahead. We would use that as a starting point anywhere on this list, and go earlier for Valentine's week or a large room block.

### Can we marry in Bali or Fiji in February?

You can, but we would wait. Our Bali page points to April to October for outdoor ceremonies, and our Fiji page points to May to October for the most settled weather.
