# Couples Experiences Worth Travelling For

[IMAGE: hero]

You have a wedding coming, an anniversary to mark, or a year when you both want to go somewhere and do one thing properly. These are the couples experiences we think are worth a long-haul flight: a yacht between the Whitsunday islands, the Okavango Delta at high water, a winter week below the Matterhorn. Each sits in a destination we plan, with the months our guides or official sources name, one honest caveat, and any safety or entry rule linked to the government page that sets it. None repeats the [15 private days in our guide for couples](/travel-guides/private-tours). Sources are current as at November 2026.

## What makes a couples experience worth travelling for

A good date night works anywhere. An experience worth a flight passes three tests. Place: it could only happen there. Season: it has a window, and outside that window it changes or disappears. Privacy: you can do it as two people, not as two seats on a coach.

We chose these 20 because each sits in a destination we plan, with a page on our site to read next. Where we name a hotel, it has a profile among our venues.

## Our 20 couples experiences

Months are as our guides or the named official source word them: tendencies, not promises.

### Food and wine

**1. Harvest season on a Tuscan wine estate.** The hills roll out in vineyards and olive groves, and Chianti and the Florence countryside hold the wine estates. Our [Tuscany guide](/destinations/europe/italy/tuscany-wedding) names September as harvest season on a wine estate. **When:** late April to June, and September to early October. **Caveat:** this is wine country rather than a single sight, so it rewards several nights at one estate.

[IMAGE: tuscany-harvest]

**2. A private wine tour of Central Otago, New Zealand.** Queenstown, one of the [Oceania destinations we plan](/destinations/oceania), sits on Lake Wakatipu beneath the Southern Alps, and we pair it with private wine tours of Central Otago and lakeside dining. **When:** December to February is summer, and we suggest four to five nights. **Caveat:** New Zealand's climate agency, NIWA, notes summer highs [can exceed 30°C about inland Otago](https://webstatic.niwa.co.nz/library/NIWAsts67.pdf), so plan tastings for the morning.

**3. Food, wine and wilderness in Tasmania.** Freycinet's coast, the Bay of Fires, farm-to-table dining and the Museum of Old and New Art (MONA) give the island real range. **When:** December to March is the warmest period. **Caveat:** the range is also the catch: these are separate stops, so give it the five to seven nights we suggest. More in [our Get Wedded in Australia magazine](/magazine/australia/get-wedded-in-australia).

### Water and islands

**4. Sailing the Whitsundays and swimming over the reef, Queensland.** The Whitsundays gather 74 islands in sheltered water off Queensland. On a private charter you sail between them, swim over reef and spend nights at an island resort such as [InterContinental Hayman Island](/venue/intercontinental-hayman-great-barrier-reef-by-ihg). **When:** May to October is the driest period, and we suggest four to six nights. **Caveat:** marine stinger season runs from around November to May, so we plan reef trips for the cooler months. Commercial reef trips also carry an environmental management charge, collected by your operator: the [Reef Authority sets it](https://www.gbrmpa.gov.au/access/environmental-management-charge/what-are-charges) at AUD 8.50 per person for a full day, and AUD 9.00 from 1 April 2027.

**5. A yacht charter through Fiji's Yasawa islands.** A charter through the Yasawas visits quiet bays and villages, and the Mamanuca and Yasawa islands hold adults-only resorts and villas with their own pools. Nadi is about four hours from Sydney or Brisbane. **When:** May to October, the dry season, has the most settled weather, per [our Fiji guide](/destinations/oceania/fiji). We suggest seven to 10 nights. **Caveat:** Smartraveller notes that [November to April is cyclone season](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/fiji), that cyclones may occur at other times, and that floods can cut road access, including to the airports.

[IMAGE: fiji-yasawas]

**6. A private-island resort in the Maldives.** Most resorts here are on their own islands, reached by seaplane or speedboat, so the island is the experience. **When:** our [Maldives cost guide](/real-weddings/maldives-wedding-cost) calls May to October the low season, which can lower resort rates. **Caveat:** Smartraveller says the [monsoon season is usually from mid-May to November](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/maldives), with strong winds and rough seas, so the lower rates come with weather. Its overall advice, at the time of writing, is a high degree of caution, citing civil unrest and terrorism.

**7. An overwater villa and a motu dinner in Bora Bora.** Villas sit above a lagoon in the shadow of Mount Otemanu, and private dinners on a motu (one of the small islets around the lagoon) and boat days fill the schedule. **When:** May to October is the dry season. We suggest five to seven nights. **Caveat:** it is reached by a short flight from Tahiti and a boat transfer, so it suits a longer trip. See [the South Pacific and beyond](/destinations/oceania).

**8. Staying on after a wedding in the Seychelles.** If you [marry in the Seychelles](/destination-weddings/getting-married-in-seychelles), the days after the ceremony become the honeymoon, with no travel day in between. **When:** Smartraveller notes that [heavy rain and flooding can occur](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/seychelles), especially in the monsoon season from November to March. **Caveat:** its overall advice is normal safety precautions at the time of writing, but entry restrictions apply to travellers recently in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, because of Ebola.

### Wildlife

**9. The Okavango Delta at high water, Botswana.** Our [African honeymoon guide](/honeymoon-ideas/african-honeymoon) puts Botswana's dry season at May to October, with flood waters peaking in the Delta from June to August. **Caveat:** the rainy months bring quieter camps, but Smartraveller says the [wet season is usually from November to March](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/botswana), with heavy rain, thunderstorms and flooding on low ground around rivers.

[IMAGE: okavango-delta]

**10. Game reserves, then Cape Town, South Africa.** Our African honeymoon guide puts South African reserves at their best from May to September, and Cape Town from November to March. **Caveat:** the two windows do not overlap, so decide which matters more. Smartraveller's overall advice is to [exercise a high degree of caution](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/south-africa), noting crime and violence are serious issues, and an online traveller declaration is required to enter or leave. Marrying there too? See [marrying in South Africa](/destination-weddings/weddings-in-south-africa).

### Wellness

**11. A grand villa hotel on Lake Como.** [Villa d'Este](/venue/villa-deste) in Cernobbio splits its 151 rooms between the Cardinal's Building and the Queen's Pavilion, with a spa and the Sporting Club. Classic Rooms are from EUR 1,150 a night, according to the hotel (November 2026). **When:** its stay packages are offered from March to December. **Caveat:** this is a grand hotel, not a private villa, and January and February are out. More in [our Lake Como pages](/destinations/europe/italy/lake-como-wedding).

[IMAGE: lake-como-terrace]

**12. An overwater villa in Ras Al Khaimah.** One [Ras Al Khaimah](/destinations/middle-east/ras-al-khaimah) resort, on a peninsula ringed by mangroves and a lagoon, says its overwater villas are the emirate's first. They run from 82 to 365 square metres, with private pools, beach access and a villa host. **Caveat:** Smartraveller [raised its UAE advice](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/middle-east/united-arab-emirates) in October 2026 to exercise a high degree of caution, citing terrorism and the regional security situation, and warns temperatures can exceed 50°C from June to September.

### Culture and place

**13. Sunrise and a private desert dinner at Uluru.** Sunrise over the rock, a private dinner under the desert sky and guided walks around its base are the highlights, and visits are led with respect for the Anangu traditional owners. **When:** April to September is the cooler season. **Caveat:** two to three nights is enough, so Uluru works best inside a longer trip. More on [travelling within Australia](/magazine/australia/get-wedded-in-australia).

**14. A caldera sunset from a cliffside pool in Santorini.** Stay on the caldera side at Imerovigli, where [Rocabella Santorini](/venue/rocabella-santorini-hotel-spa) has cliffside infinity pools. **When:** May, early June or September, when the shoulder months skip the peak-summer heat. **Caveat:** the caldera sunset is famous, so expect company along the paths; a terrace of your own is what makes it private. More on [Greece](/destinations/europe/greece).

### Adventure

Smartraveller notes that [most travel insurance policies don't include adventure activities and sports](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/going-overseas-sports-and-adventure); they are usually an optional extra. Tell your insurer about each activity, and check the policy covers remote location rescues.

**15. A helicopter flight to remote peaks above Queenstown.** Helicopter flights to remote peaks give Queenstown its thrill, with lakeside dining to come back to. **When:** June to August brings snow, and December to February is summer. **Caveat:** Smartraveller notes that in New Zealand [weather conditions can change quickly](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/pacific/new-zealand), so keep a spare day in case a flight moves. More on [Oceania](/destinations/oceania).

**16. A winter week in the Swiss Alps.** Stay in Zermatt below the Matterhorn, or in St. Moritz for its lakes and skiing. For somewhere quieter, Wengen looks over the Lauterbrunnen valley. **When:** [our Switzerland guide](/destinations/europe/switzerland) puts winter at December to March, when the Alps are snowy. **Caveat:** Smartraveller warns of avalanches and sudden weather changes in alpine areas, and notes [mountain rescues can be extremely costly](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/europe/switzerland).

[IMAGE: swiss-alps-winter]

### Slow travel

**17. Terrace days on the Amalfi Coast.** On the Amalfi Coast and in Sorrento, hotels sit on terraces high above the water. The pleasure is slow: long lunches, the sea below, nowhere to be. **When:** May, June, September or early October. **Caveat:** the boat day along this coast is a separate experience, which we cover as a [private day on the water](/travel-guides/private-tours). More in [our Amalfi Coast guide](/destinations/europe/italy/amalfi-coast-wedding).

**18. Cliffs to beach in Bali, in three bases.** Base yourselves at Uluwatu for the cliffs, move to Seminyak or Canggu for the beach, and finish at a resort in Nusa Dua or Jimbaran. **When:** [our Bali guide](/destinations/asia/bali) names the dry season, April to October. **Caveat:** Smartraveller says the [wet season usually runs from October to April](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia), so April and October can go either way, and volcanic activity can disrupt flights with little notice. Its overall advice for Indonesia is a high degree of caution.

[IMAGE: bali-uluwatu]

**19. A ride on the Venice Simplon-Orient-Express.** Our guide to experiences by occasion suggests it for an anniversary: the ride itself is the point, spent in a vintage cabin. **Caveat:** the cabin is the experience, so it suits a couple who like slow days; if you want to cover ground, fly. Pair it with time in [Italy](/destinations/europe/italy).

**20. Safari, then Zanzibar.** [The same guide](/honeymoon-ideas/african-honeymoon) that covers the Delta suggests five to seven nights on safari, then three to four nights on Zanzibar. **Caveat:** Smartraveller notes [monsoons in coastal and island areas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/tanzania) of Tanzania from July to October, which overlaps the May to October safari window. It also says visitors must buy mandatory inbound travel insurance from designated national insurers, and its overall advice is a high degree of caution.

## The work behind the experience

What makes an experience work is mostly timing, and that is the part we handle.

- **Seasonal windows.** The Tuscan harvest, the Delta floods and the reef's cooler months all fix the dates, so we plan the trip around the window.
- **Fallback days.** Helicopter flights and alpine days depend on weather that can change quickly, so we keep a spare day in the plan.
- **Transfers.** Bora Bora means a flight from Tahiti and a boat; the Maldives, a seaplane or speedboat. We sequence them so you do not arrive tired.
- **Insurance and entry rules.** Smartraveller notes that for medical care overseas [you, or your insurer, must usually pay full price](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance). Check your policy covers each planned activity, and check each country's Smartraveller page, because governments set and change entry rules.
- **Who is on the ground.** Smartraveller asks you to [check operators are licensed](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/going-overseas-sports-and-adventure) and have a good reputation. Your concierge matches each experience to a local specialist from our network of 50,000+ vendors, filtered to the ones our team has actually worked with in-market.
- **How money moves.** One dedicated point of contact handles deposits, payments and currency across borders, so a Fiji charter and a Lake Como hotel sit in one plan.

## Fitting experiences into a honeymoon or wedding week

Book one or two headline experiences a week, and leave open days around them. Two full days back to back turn a holiday into a schedule, and an open day after each gives weather somewhere to go.

Keep the first day after a long-haul flight light, and the day after a wedding lighter still. Put the experience you care about most mid-trip, with a day spare to move it.

For guests, an experience works best as an optional day: listed on your wedding website, booked and paid by each guest, so nobody feels obliged. [Wonderland Studio](/wonderland-studio) is free and includes a wedding website and RSVPs for exactly this.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are good couples experiences abroad?

Ones that could only happen there, in a set season, done as two. A Yasawas yacht charter or the Okavango Delta from June to August both qualify.

### How far ahead should experiences be booked?

For the South Pacific dry season and the New Zealand summer, we suggest starting nine to 12 months ahead, because overwater villas, island resorts and boutique lodges are limited in number. Seasonal experiences, such as the Tuscan harvest in September, fix your dates first.

### Are adventure experiences covered by travel insurance?

Often not by default. Smartraveller notes that [most travel insurance policies don't include adventure activities and sports](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/going-overseas-sports-and-adventure), which are usually an optional extra, and that [medical assistance is usually the highest unexpected cost](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance) for Australians overseas. Tell your insurer about every activity before you do it.
