# Best Value Luxury Destination Weddings Ranked

[IMAGE: best-value-destination-wedding-villa-terrace-long-table-01.webp]

You have a serious budget, a guest list that is still moving, and a shortlist that runs from the Greek islands to the Indian Ocean. The question is not which place is cheapest. It is where, in our view, a well-planned wedding gives you, and the people flying in to join you, the most for your budget.

This guide to best value luxury destination weddings ranks five places our own cost guides cover, with the method on show. For each, you will see one headline figure from our guide, what that budget buys and who it suits. Every figure is linked to its guide. Sources current as at November 2026.

## How we ranked these destinations

Value, in this guide, is our editorial view of what a serious budget buys in quality, guest experience and ease at the top end. A wedding that costs less on paper but leaves your guests waiting on a hot jetty, or your planner stretched across three venues, is not good value. One that puts your money into the evening your guests remember is.

We judged each destination on three things: what our own cost guides publish, quoted as worded in the original currency with its date and guest count; what that budget buys, including what sits outside a package or quote; and season and lead time, where our pages state them. Our guides use different guest counts and currencies, so this is an editorial judgement, not a like-for-like price table. We have not converted anything to Australian dollars or used anyone else's rankings. For averages, see our overview of [what destination weddings cost in each region](/expert-advice/average-destination-wedding-cost-by-region).

## The best value luxury destination weddings ranked

[Italy](/destinations/europe/italy) holds two places on this list. [Our Italy cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-wedding-in-italy-cost) says the cost of a wedding in Italy runs from about €20,000 to over €120,000, depending on region, season and guest count, so each region earns its own place. Puglia, which our Italy guide calls the better-value region, is not ranked because we do not yet have a current cost guide with a total for it; read [why couples are choosing Puglia](/expert-advice/why-are-couples-choosing-puglia). Crete, Thailand, Mexico, France and Portugal sit outside the list for the same reason.

### 1. Santorini, Greece

**What our guide publishes.** [Our Santorini cost breakdown](/real-weddings/santorini-wedding-cost-breakdown), published in November 2026 and written for weddings in 2027, puts a standard wedding for 50 to 80 guests at €30,000 to €55,000.

**What it buys.** The guide sets out its tiers by guest count, from an elopement to a wedding for 80 or more, and treats accommodation as a separate part of the budget, so guest rooms sit on top.

**Why it leads.** In our view, Santorini leads because a serious budget there buys a clear, tiered wedding for 50 to 80 guests, with figures you can plan against before you commit.

**Who it suits.** Couples with 50 to 80 guests who want a ceremony above the sea and a clear, tiered budget to plan against. For venues and the legal side, start with [marrying in Greece](/destinations/europe/greece).

[IMAGE: santorini-wedding-ceremony-terrace-caldera-view-01.webp]

### 2. Fiji

**What our guide publishes.** [Our Fiji cost breakdown](/real-weddings/fiji-wedding-cost) was published in November 2026 and sets no single guest count. It puts a classic resort wedding at FJD 10,000 to 20,000, and luxury weddings at FJD 25,000 to 75,000 or more, in Fijian dollars.

**What it buys.** A package often covers the ceremony site, a basic reception setup and a coordinator.

**Why second.** In our view, Fiji comes second because a package already covers the basics, so a serious budget can go into styling, upgrades and the days around the wedding.

**Who it suits.** Couples who want everyone in one resort for a relaxed Pacific weekend. Start with our page on [weddings in Fiji](/destinations/oceania/fiji).

### 3. The Maldives

**What our guide publishes.** [Our Maldives cost guide](/real-weddings/maldives-wedding-cost), published in November 2026, puts a resort wedding for 20 guests at US$25,000 to US$60,000 all-in.

**What it buys.** The ceremony package, villa stays and transfers carry most of that total; most resorts sit on private islands reached by seaplane or speedboat, with each guest on a separate fare.

**Why third.** In our view, few places match the Maldives for privacy, and the cost of getting each guest there is what keeps it third.

**Who it suits.** Couples keeping the guest list to 20 or fewer who want privacy, and who may stay on for the honeymoon in the same place.

[IMAGE: maldives-overwater-deck-wedding-ceremony-sunset-01.webp]

### 4. Amalfi Coast, Italy

**What our guide publishes.** [Our Amalfi Coast cost guide](/real-weddings/amalfi-coast-wedding-cost), published in November 2026, puts a wedding for 60 to 80 guests at €60,000 to €110,000 or more.

## What makes a wedding good value at the top end

At this level, value is less about the total and more about what each part of it does for you and your guests. Four questions separate a good budget from a merely large one.

- **Exclusive use or a shared resort?** Having the venue to yourselves changes the feel of a weekend. Our Italy cost guide notes that exclusive hire of a Tuscan venue for 60 to 80 guests across three nights typically starts at around €27,000 to €30,000 in 2026.
- **How many days are your guests there?** A welcome evening and a farewell lunch turn a dinner into a weekend. Price them as their own events.
- **How well are you served?** Ask each venue and caterer how many staff will work your dinner and who leads on the night, and get the answer in writing.
- **What does the package include, and what does it leave out?** Read the exclusions first.

A package gives you a clear starting point and fewer decisions; a plan built around you takes more effort and lets you put money where it matters most. ## The hidden costs that change the ranking

The figures above can leave lines out, and these are the ones that can move a destination up or down your own list. Our guide to [the hidden costs of a destination wedding](/destination-weddings/hidden-costs-destination-wedding) covers each in depth.

- **Guest room blocks.** Ask how many rooms are held, when unbooked rooms are released and what you owe if guests book fewer. Our team handles [guest travel and room blocks](/travel), and at partner, preferred and consortia properties, Wonderland Benefits can include negotiated group rates and a property credit per booked room. Benefits vary by hotel and by program.
- **Transfers.** Seaplanes, boats and coaches between airport, hotel and venue add up across a weekend. Price every leg, including the late return after dinner.
- **A wet-weather plan.** Ask what the indoor or covered option is, whether it is held for your date and what it costs to set up. No one can promise the weather; you can agree the plan in advance.
- **Legal paperwork.** If you want your marriage legally recognised, build the paperwork into your timeline and budget, and confirm the requirements for your own passports with the destination's registering office and the Australian embassy or consulate there.
- **VAT, local taxes and catering.** Our Amalfi Coast cost guide prices catering and bar at €180 to €310 or more a head and notes that many service invoices carry 22% VAT, while our Maldives guide lists tax and service charge at 27%. Check whether each quote includes them.
- **Extra events.** Our Lake Como budget guide notes that a welcome aperitivo and a farewell lunch can add 25 to 40% on top of the wedding-day total.
- **Planner fees.** Our planner cost guide explains [how planners charge](/expert-advice/cost-of-wedding-planner): either a flat fee for a written scope or a percentage of the budget, commonly 10 to 15%, with some planners at the top of the market at 18 to 20%.
- **Exchange-rate movement.** If your contracts are in euros or US dollars and your savings are in Australian dollars, the rate can move between deposit and final payment. Figures stay in the currency each guide uses. Convert at the rate on the day you pay, and keep a separate buffer for movement.

Insurance belongs on the list too. Smartraveller says [the right travel insurance is as essential as a passport](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance), and says to always read the Product Disclosure Statement before you buy.

## Season and lead time and how they move the price

Pricing a weekday or shoulder-season date alongside a peak Saturday is a simple way to see how much timing moves a quote.

**Italy.** Our Italy cost guide notes that most Italian weddings happen between April and October, so shoulder months such as April and October give you the most leverage. At the other end, June and September dates are often gone 18 months ahead.

**The Maldives and Greece.** Our Maldives guide describes May to October as the low season, which can lower resort rates, and our guide to [when to marry in Greece](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece) walks through the months.

**Fiji.** Our guide does not set a best season, so price two or three dates side by side before you choose.
