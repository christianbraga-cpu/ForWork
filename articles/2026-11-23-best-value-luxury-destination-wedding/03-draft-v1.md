# Best Value Luxury Destination Weddings Ranked

[IMAGE: hero]

You have a serious budget, a guest list that is still moving, and a shortlist that runs from the Greek islands to the Indian Ocean. The question is not which place is cheapest. It is where a well-planned wedding gives you the most for every dollar, for you and for the people flying in to join you.

This guide to best value luxury destination weddings ranks six places our own cost guides cover, with the method on show. For each, you will see what our guide publishes, what that budget buys, when to go if we know, and who it suits. Every figure stays in the currency and wording of the guide it comes from, linked at the claim, so you can read the detail yourself. Sources current as at November 2026.

## How we ranked these destinations

Value, in this guide, means quality per dollar at the top end. It is not the lowest price. A wedding that costs less on paper but leaves your guests waiting on a hot jetty, or your planner stretched across three venues, is not good value. One that puts your money into the evening your guests remember is.

We judged each destination on three things:

- **What our own cost guides publish.** Each figure is quoted as the guide words it, in its original currency, with its date and guest count where the guide gives one.
- **What that budget buys.** Guest count, what a package or quote includes, and what sits outside it.
- **Season and lead time,** where our pages state them.

Our guides use different guest counts and different currencies, from euros for 60 to 80 guests to US dollars for 20. That makes this an editorial judgement, not a like-for-like price table. We have not converted anything to Australian dollars, worked out a cost per guest or used anyone else's rankings. For averages, see our overview of [what destination weddings cost in each region](/expert-advice/average-destination-wedding-cost-by-region).

## The best value luxury destination weddings ranked

Italy appears twice in this list, and for good reason. [Our Italy cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-wedding-in-italy-cost) shows real weddings there running from about €20,000 to over €120,000, depending on region, season and guest count, so each region earns its own place. Puglia, which that guide calls the better-value region, sits outside the list with Crete, Thailand, Mexico, France and Portugal, because we do not yet publish a total for any of them.

### 1. Santorini, Greece

**What our guide publishes.** [Our Santorini cost breakdown](/real-weddings/santorini-wedding-cost-breakdown), published in November 2026 and written for weddings in 2027, runs from €4,000 for an elopement to €55,000 or more, depending on guest count and style. Its table puts a standard wedding for 50 to 80 guests at €30,000 to €55,000, and weddings for 80 or more guests from €55,000.

**What it buys.** The guide lists a planner at €3,000 to €5,000 or more, who helps with local Greek paperwork, translators and day-of coordination. It treats accommodation as a separate part of the budget, so guest rooms sit on top.

**Why it leads.** The tiers are set out by guest count, so you can see what each step up buys before you commit. In our judgement, that clarity gives a serious budget more room to spend on the experience around the ceremony than anywhere else on this list.

**Who it suits.** Couples with 50 to 80 guests who want a ceremony above the sea and a compact, walkable weekend. For timing, read our guide to [when to marry in Greece](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece), and for venues and the legal side, start with [marrying in Greece](/destinations/europe/greece).

### 2. Fiji

**What our guide publishes.** [Our Fiji cost breakdown](/real-weddings/fiji-wedding-cost), published in November 2026, gives a typical range of FJD 10,000 to 30,000, in Fijian dollars. Within that, it puts a classic resort wedding at FJD 10,000 to 20,000, and its top tier at FJD 25,000 to 75,000 or more. The guide sets no single guest count: each guest beyond the package adds about FJD 130 to 180 or more.

**What it buys.** A package often covers the ceremony site, a basic reception setup and a coordinator. That means a top-end budget goes into upgrades, styling and the days around the wedding rather than the basics.

**Who it suits.** Couples who want everyone in one resort for a relaxed Pacific weekend, with the planning shape of a package and room to add to it. Start with our page on [weddings in Fiji](/destinations/oceania/fiji).

## What makes a wedding good value at the top end

At this level, value is less about the total and more about what each part of it does for you and your guests. Four questions separate a good budget from a merely large one.

- **Exclusive use or a shared resort?** Having the venue to yourselves changes the feel of a weekend. Our Italy cost guide notes that exclusive hire of a Tuscan venue for 60 to 80 guests across three nights typically starts at around €27,000 to €30,000 in 2026, before food, styling or guest rooms.
- **How many days are your guests there?** A welcome evening and a farewell lunch turn a dinner into a weekend. Price them as their own events.
- **How well are you served?** Ask each venue and caterer how many staff will work your dinner and who leads on the night, and get the answer in writing.
- **What does the package include, and what does it leave out?** Read the exclusions first.

A package and a plan built to order each have a place. A package gives you a clear starting point and fewer decisions; a plan built around you costs more effort to assemble and lets you put money where it matters most. [Our destination wedding packages](/destination-wedding-packages) show properties visited and vetted by the Wedded Wonderland team, and our guide to [what all-inclusive really covers](/all-inclusive-wedding-cost) helps you read any package line by line.

None of this is about spending less for its own sake. It is about making sure your money reaches the moments that count.

## The hidden costs that change the ranking

Every figure above leaves something out, and these are the lines that can move a destination up or down your own list. Our guide to [the hidden costs of a destination wedding](/destination-weddings/hidden-costs-destination-wedding) covers each in depth.

- **Guest room blocks.** Ask how many rooms are held, when unbooked rooms are released and what you owe if guests book fewer. Our team handles [guest travel and room blocks](/travel), and at partner, preferred and consortia properties, Wonderland Benefits can include negotiated group rates and a property credit per booked room. Benefits vary by hotel and by program.
- **Transfers.** Seaplanes, boats and coaches between airport, hotel and venue add up across a weekend. Price every leg, including the late return after dinner.
- **A wet-weather plan.** Ask what the indoor or covered option is, whether it is held for your date and what it costs to set up. No one can promise the weather; you can pay for the plan in advance.
- **Legal paperwork.** If you want your marriage legally recognised, build the paperwork into your timeline and budget, and confirm the requirements for your own passports with the destination's registering office and the Australian embassy or consulate there.
- **VAT and local taxes.** Our guides flag 22% VAT on many Italian service invoices, a 27% tax and service charge in the Maldives, and fees and VAT in Dubai. Check whether each quote includes them.
- **Extra events.** [Our Lake Como budget guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost) notes that a welcome aperitivo and a farewell lunch can add 25 to 40% on top of the wedding-day total.
- **Planner fees.** Our guide to [how planners charge](/expert-advice/cost-of-wedding-planner) explains that planners quote either a flat fee for a written scope or a percentage of the budget, commonly 10 to 15%, with some planners at the top of the market at 18 to 20%.
- **Exchange-rate movement.** If your contracts are in euros or US dollars and your savings are in Australian dollars, the rate can move between deposit and final payment. Record each line in its contract currency and keep a separate buffer.

Insurance belongs on the list too. Smartraveller says [the right travel insurance is as essential as a passport](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance), and suggests reading the Product Disclosure Statement before you buy.

## Season and lead time and how they move the price

Timing is one of the few levers that can lower cost without lowering quality. The details differ by destination, so use only what each guide states.

**Italy.** Our Italy cost guide notes that choosing a shoulder-season month can take up to a fifth off your venue fee. Most Italian weddings happen between April and October, so shoulder months such as April and October give you the most leverage. At the other end, June and September dates are often gone 18 months ahead. Our small wedding in Italy guide calls 12 to 18 months comfortable for a peak-season Saturday, and 6 to 9 months workable for a weekday or shoulder-season date.

**The Maldives.** Our Maldives guide describes May to October as the low season, which can lower resort rates.

**Greece.** Our guide to [when to marry in Greece](/destination-weddings/best-time-to-get-married-in-greece) walks through the months.

**Fiji and Dubai.** Our guides do not set a best season, so price two or three dates side by side before you choose. The wider rule holds everywhere: a weekday or shoulder date gives you more choice of venue, and the most popular weekends go first.

### How much should we budget for a destination wedding at this level?

Start with your guest count and destination, then read the matching guide. Our Amalfi Coast cost guide puts a wedding for 60 to 80 guests at €60,000 to €110,000 or more, and our Maldives cost guide puts a resort wedding for 20 guests at US$25,000 to US$60,000 all-in. ### Are all-inclusive packages good value at the top end?

They can be, when you know exactly what is inside. A Maldives package, for example, often covers the ceremony location, a basic floral arrangement and an officiant, while villa stays and transfers sit outside it. Read the inclusions line by line and compare them with a quote built around your own plans.

### When is the best time to book for value?

Earlier than you might think. Our Italy cost guide notes that June and September dates are often gone 18 months ahead, and that shoulder months such as April and October give you the most leverage. In the Maldives, our guide describes May to October as the low season, which can lower resort rates.

### Is a destination wedding cheaper than a wedding at home?

Not reliably. An overseas wedding is shaped differently: often fewer guests, more days, and costs a home wedding never carries, such as guest rooms and transfers.
