# Bali Wedding Packages and What They Include

[IMAGE: bali-clifftop-lawn-couple-long-table-golden-hour-01.webp]

Bali wedding packages bundle a set of ceremony and reception elements for a fixed guest number and time window, and what each one includes varies by provider, so read every quote line by line. For a wedding week with 80 to 150 guests, the package is the centre of the plan rather than the whole of it. This guide sets out what a package is built to cover, what tends to sit outside it, the two legal routes, and how to compare quotes before you sign.

Sources current as at November 2026.

## What Bali wedding packages usually cover

Check each quote against these categories, because providers fill them differently:

- Ceremony site and set-up
- Reception space and a set time window
- Food and drink, usually in tiers
- Styling and florals
- On-day coordination
- Basic audio
- Sometimes photography
- A celebrant for a symbolic ceremony
- Sometimes a room or two for the two of you

Packages come in three broad types, and the type tells you who holds the pen.

| Package type | Who builds it | What it tends to bundle | Where control sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resort or hotel package | The venue's in-house events team | Ceremony site, reception, food and drink tiers, in-house styling, rooms on the property | With the venue: its spaces, menus and supplier rules |
| Planner package | A planner who bundles suppliers | A venue booking plus styling, photography, entertainment and coordination from the planner's network | With the planner, who contracts or recommends each supplier |
| Villa package | A private villa working with a planner | Exclusive use of the villa and grounds, with the planner supplying the rest | Shared: the villa sets house rules, the planner runs the event |

[IMAGE: bali-ocean-ceremony-aisle-frangipani-01.webp]

An "all inclusive" label describes the bundle, not the wedding week. On our own packages page, guest accommodation is "Paid separately, usually at negotiated group rates", and flights are typically paid separately too.

**Our Bali packages.** Wedded Wonderland's package collection includes three Bali properties: Akohara Buahan Village Retreat, in the highlands above Ubud; Regent Bali Canggu, with oceanfront ceremony spaces and, in our page's words, "the service depth to carry a larger guest list"; and The Apurva Kempinski Bali, on the cliffs of Nusa Dua, with ballrooms for large celebrations.

Our packages page lists these as included in every package: venue and wedding date secured; a group room block held for your guests; guest travel and transfers, coordinated; an on-the-ground planner from enquiry to send-off; photography, styling and ceremony setup; and local legal and cultural requirements, handled. "Coordinated" and "handled" mean arranged, not paid for: the same page lists legal and documentation as "Paid separately, destination-dependent". We do not publish prices for these three packages, so ask for an itemised quote built on your date and guest list.

## What Bali wedding packages rarely include

This is where the real cost and work of a wedding week tend to sit. Use the list as a check: ask whether your package includes each item and, if not, who arranges it and how it is charged.

| Check whether yours includes | What to pin down |
|---|---|
| Guest accommodation and a room block | How many rooms are held, the release date, and what you owe if fewer guests book |
| Airport and venue transfers for guests | Who moves guests between villas, hotels and the venue on each day |
| Wet-season back-up space | A named covered or indoor space, and whether it costs extra to hold |
| Welcome dinner, pre-wedding events, recovery-day brunch | Whether events beyond those named cost extra |
| Extra hours beyond the time window | Overtime for every supplier, not only the venue |
| Vendor and crew meals | Whether they are charged per head |
| Service charge and government tax | Check whether quoted prices include service and tax |
| Drinks beyond the package tier | How premium wine and spirits are charged |
| Legal paperwork and certificate costs | Who prepares documents, and whose fees are passed on |
| Guest gifts, signage and stationery | Who sources, prints and places them |
| Travel and rooms for imported suppliers | A photographer or band flown in from Australia needs both |
| Local community (banjar) fees, generator hire, corkage | Our [Bali wedding budget guide](/expert-advice/bali-wedding-cost-the-complete-2026-guide) lists these among the hidden costs |

[IMAGE: bali-villa-entrance-guest-shuttle-dusk-01.webp]

For a 100-guest week, room blocks and transfers are the invisible job to start first. A room block means agreeing how many rooms to hold, the date unbooked rooms are released, and what you owe if fewer guests book than planned. Then someone has to move every guest between villas, hotels and the venue each day, with timings that match the run sheet. Wonderland Benefits, such as suite upgrades, property credit per booked room and group rates, apply at our partner and preferred properties and vary by hotel and by program. See [how our destination wedding packages are built](/destination-wedding-packages) and plan guest stays through [wedding and honeymoon travel](/travel).

Guests also cover their own entry, and a good guest pack sets it out. At the time of writing, [Smartraveller's Indonesia page](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/indonesia) says an e-Visa on Arrival (e-VOA) costs IDR 500,000, plus a small online processing fee, is valid for a 30-day stay, and can be applied for online at least 48 hours before travel. Passports need at least six months' validity from the date you plan to enter and leave Indonesia. The [Bali Provincial Government's levy page](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/faq) lists a foreign tourist levy of IDR 150,000 per person, paid once and cashless; Smartraveller adds it is separate from the e-VOA.

Smartraveller's current advice for Indonesia is "Exercise a high degree of caution", due to security risks, with higher levels in some areas. It also warns of methanol poisoning, including in Bali, which is one more reason to know where every drink at your events comes from.

## The legal side in brief

Getting married in Bali legally has its own paperwork and authorities, so check whether your package includes any legal service and who provides it. The first decision is which of two routes you are on.

| | Marry in Australia, celebrate in Bali | Legal Indonesian wedding in Bali |
|---|---|---|
| Where the legal part happens | Before an authorised celebrant in Australia | Before a minister of religion, the Civil Registry Office or the Office of Religious Affairs (KUA) |
| The Bali ceremony | Symbolic, with no legal effect | Conducted according to religion; non-Muslim marriages then registered |
| Main paperwork | A Notice of Intended Marriage (NOIM) | A CNI from the Australian side, then Indonesian notice and registration |

**Route 1: marry at home.** The [Attorney-General's Department](https://www.ag.gov.au/families-and-marriage/marriage/get-married) says you give a NOIM to an authorised celebrant no more than 18 months and at least one month before the legal ceremony, and the celebrant must be physically present with you and two witnesses over 18. An Australian celebrant cannot legally marry you in Bali, so a Bali ceremony they lead is symbolic. For other options, see [where Australians can legally marry abroad](/expert-advice/weddings-abroad-where-can-you-legally-get-married).

**Route 2: marry legally in Bali.** The [Australian Embassy in Indonesia](https://indonesia.embassy.gov.au/jakt/MarriageInd.html), citing Indonesian Law No. 1 of 1974, says "both parties must hold the same religion, if not, one party must convert to the other religion." For non-Muslim marriages, it says couples lodge a Notice of Intention to Marry with the Civil Registry Office at least 10 days before the wedding and register the marriage there afterwards for it to be legal, and that requirements vary by province. Each Australian partner needs a Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage (CNI) from the Australian office that covers Bali, which is the Australian Consulate-General in Bali. [DFAT's 2026 notarial fees](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services) start from AUD 181 for a CNI issued in Australia and from AUD 105 for an apostille; read [how to apply for a CNI](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/our-role/notarial-services/how-get-certificate-no-impediment-marriage). The Embassy notes that DFAT cannot advise on Indonesia's own requirements, and that liaising with the Civil Registry Office can be difficult without Indonesian language skills.

Questions to put to the Civil Registry Office where you will marry, or to the Indonesian Embassy or Consulate in Australia:

- Which religions are recognised for a marriage, and what proof of religion do you need?
- What are the civil registration steps in your regency, and what documents must we bring?
- How many witnesses do we need, and what age, ID or relationship rules apply?
- In what language is the certificate issued, and does it need an apostille or translation for use in Australia?

Ask the Australian Consulate-General in Bali what it charges for a CNI issued there.

Back home, a spouse born in Australia who wants a passport in a married name after marrying overseas needs a change of name certificate from their state or territory registry, according to the [Australian Passport Office](https://www.passports.gov.au/change-name-or-gender). See [what to do with a marriage certificate issued overseas](/expert-advice/overseas-marriage-certificate). Requirements change: confirm them for your own passports with the Indonesian Civil Registry Office where you marry, the Australian Consulate-General in Bali and DFAT.

## How to compare Bali wedding packages

Ask every provider for an itemised quote in one currency, with the exchange basis stated and a clear note on whether service and tax are included. If quotes arrive in different currencies, convert all of them to AUD on the same date before you compare.

Then line them up in a grid you can copy:

| Compare | Package A | Package B | Package C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inclusions, by category | | | |
| Guest number the price is built on, and the rate per extra guest | | | |
| Time window, overtime rate and music curfew | | | |
| Wet-season plan: named space, who decides, by when, at what cost | | | |
| Supplier list, and who contracts each one | | | |
| Payment schedule and currency | | | |
| Cancellation and date-change terms | | | |

**Guest numbers.** Check the base guest count behind each price. For 80 to 150 guests, the per-guest add-ons, extra rooms and larger spaces can reorder your shortlist, so compare at your real number, not the package's.

**Wet season.** Smartraveller says weather conditions can change quickly during the wet season, usually around October to April. The plan matters more than the forecast: a named covered space, an agreed time for making the call, and a price settled in advance.

**House rules.** Rules differ by venue. [Nirjhara Bali Waterfall Weddings](/venue/nirjhara-bali-waterfall-weddings), a Bali venue listed on our site, ends music at 10 PM for events that are not a full buyout, and needs hotel approval plus police and village permits for fireworks. Put each venue's rules in the grid.

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. What is excluded, in writing?
2. Are service charge and government tax in the prices quoted?
3. If it rains, which space do we move to, who decides, and by what time?
4. Who is our on-day contact, and how many of our events do they cover?
5. Which suppliers are fixed, and which can we bring in?
6. What are the overtime and noise curfew rules for each event?
7. Who handles guest rooms and transfers, and what happens to unbooked rooms?
8. What is the payment schedule, and what is the cancellation policy?
9. Is any legal ceremony service included, and who provides it?
10. Who supplies the bar, and where does the stock come from?

## When a tailored plan suits better than a package

A package suits a smaller, single-day celebration well. It starts to strain when your week has:

- More than one event across several days, such as [a welcome party](/expert-advice/do-you-need-a-wedding-welcome-party) and a recovery brunch
- A guest list well above the package's base number
- A private villa or exclusive-use estate
- Cultural or multi-faith ceremonies
- Suppliers you want to bring from Australia
- A look the in-house team does not offer

## FAQ

### What is usually included in a Bali wedding package?

The ceremony, a reception window, food and drink tiers, styling and on-day coordination, built around a set guest number. With Wedded Wonderland, the specific inclusions for each property are sent after you enquire with your date and approximate guest count, and your shortlist sets out inclusions, dates and pricing for each option.

### Are all inclusive Bali wedding packages really all inclusive?

They include the bundle they name, not the whole trip. Our own packages page lists extras such as spa, excursions or additional events as paid separately, on request. Read [what all-inclusive wedding packages really cost](/all-inclusive-wedding-cost) before you treat any label as a total.

### How much should we budget for a luxury Bali wedding?

It depends on guest numbers, the number of events, the season and the venue type, so a package price alone will not answer it. [Our Bali cost guide](/expert-advice/bali-wedding-cost-the-complete-2026-guide) sets out budgets for a 100-guest wedding, with figures in USD, so convert them to AUD on the day you compare.

### Is a Bali wedding legally recognised in Australia?

A legal Indonesian marriage generally is. [Smartraveller's advice on marrying overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) says you cannot register an overseas marriage in Australia, but it is usually recognised if valid where it happened and would be valid under Australian law, except where either person was already married, not old enough, too closely related or forced to marry.

### How far ahead should we book a Bali wedding package?

Our packages page puts the comfortable lead time for destination weddings at this level at 12 to 18 months, and says shorter timelines are workable in less seasonal regions. If you are marrying legally in Bali, plan on a CNI issued within three months of the wedding: the Australian Embassy says local Civil Registry Offices do not normally accept older ones.
