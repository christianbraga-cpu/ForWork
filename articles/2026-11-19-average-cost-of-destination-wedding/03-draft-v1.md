# Average Destination Wedding Cost by Region

[IMAGE: hero]

You have a guest list taking shape, a feeling for the kind of weekend you want, and a rough sense of what the same wedding would cost in Australia. The next question is what it costs overseas. Search for the average cost of destination wedding plans and you will find plenty of numbers, but few say what they include, which year they come from or which currency they started in.

This guide takes the other approach. It sets out what our own cost guides show, region by region, with every figure dated and in its original currency. It explains how an overseas wedding compares with one at home, the costs no average includes, and a calm way to work out your own number. Sources current as at November 2026.

## What the average cost of destination wedding budgets includes

There is no single honest average cost of destination wedding budgets, because published figures mix elopements, all-inclusive resort packages and three-day celebrations for 100 guests. The most useful benchmark we hold is for Italy: [our Italy cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-wedding-in-italy-cost) reports that foreign couples spent an average of €67,000 per wedding there in 2025, citing Florence's Centro Studi Turistici.

That figure was a rise of almost 9% on the previous year. It is also an average across every kind of wedding in Italy, from a ceremony for two to a full villa weekend, which is why it rarely matches the plan in front of you.

For a celebration of 70 to 110 guests, per-guest spend is the better guide. Our Italy guide puts a high-quality wedding at about €800 to €1,200 per guest in 2026, which it shows as around €80,000 to €120,000 for 100 guests. That sits above the €67,000 average, and it is before anyone has booked a flight.

So before you compare any figure, ask what it covers. [Our Lake Como budget guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost) is explicit: its estimates are for a three-day celebration and exclude flights, attire and guest accommodation, and it describes them as planning estimates, not quotes. Check three things in any average you read:

- **Your own travel.** Are the couple's flights and rooms inside the figure, or on top?
- **Your guests' travel.** Is any guest travel counted, or is it treated as their cost alone?
- **The planner.** Is a planner's fee included, or is it a separate line?

## How a destination wedding compares with a wedding in Australia

Is a wedding overseas cheaper than one at home? Not in a straight line. The comparison turns on five things, and each moves in its own direction.

- **Guest numbers.** This is the biggest lever, at home or abroad. Our Italy guide shows around €40,000 to €60,000 for 50 guests and €80,000 to €120,000 for 100, at the 2026 per-guest rates above. Each name you add or remove moves the total by a full per-head amount.
- **Travel.** At home, guests drive or take a domestic flight. Overseas, someone pays for long-haul flights, and you decide who.
- **Accommodation.** A wedding at home rarely needs a room block. Abroad, guest rooms become part of the plan, even when guests pay for their own.
- **Length of celebration.** A home wedding can be a single evening. Abroad, welcome drinks and a farewell lunch turn it into a weekend, and our Lake Como guide notes those two events can add 25 to 40% on top of the wedding-day total.
- **Vendors who travel.** If your photographer, celebrant or hair and makeup artist flies out with you, their flights, rooms and travel days become your cost.

[IMAGE: comparison]

The honest answer is that an overseas wedding is shaped differently rather than reliably cheaper. You may host fewer people at a higher spend per guest, with costs a home wedding never carries. We set the two side by side in [our line-by-line comparison of overseas and home budgets](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-vs-home-cost), and [our overview of what weddings cost overall](/expert-advice/how-much-does-the-average-wedding-cost) covers the wider picture.

**Who pays for what.** No rule binds you here, so decide early and say it plainly. Write down which of flights, rooms, transfers and the welcome dinner you will cover, and which your guests will book themselves, then put it on your wedding website well before invitations go out. Guests plan around clarity, and so does whoever is paying.

## Europe: Italy, Lake Como and the Greek islands

Europe is where our most detailed cost guides sit, and where the picture of long tables, old villas and evenings that run late tends to begin. The figures below come from our own cost guides, each in euros and dated.

**Italy.** Our Italy guide shows real weddings running from about €20,000 to over €120,000 in 2026 prices, depending on region, season and guest count. The range is wide because each of those three moves it, so two weddings in Italy can sit at opposite ends. For the detail by region and guest count, read [our Italy cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-wedding-in-italy-cost).

**Lake Como.** Our Lake Como budget guide gives typical totals for a three-day celebration in 2026 to 2027, before flights and guest rooms:

- **Intimate, 20 to 30 guests:** €30,000 to €65,000
- **Classic, 60 guests:** €80,000 to €150,000
- **Grand, 100 or more guests:** €150,000 to €300,000 or more

The same guide notes that Italian VAT is 22% on most services, so check whether each quote includes it. The full breakdown, line by line, is in [our Lake Como budget guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost).

[IMAGE: europe]

**The Greek islands.** We do not quote a figure for Greece in this guide. Ask the same questions you would of an Italian quote: what the venue fee covers, whether tax is included, and how guests reach the island and back. Our page on [weddings in Greece](/destinations/europe/greece) covers venues, seasons, getting there and marrying legally.

## Asia, the Pacific and further afield

Closer to home, the questions change. Instead of a villa weekend in euros, you are weighing villa buyouts, resort minimums, local levies and how many of your guests will fly.

**Bali.** Bali costs vary widely, from an intimate ceremony to a full estate event with production brought in, and any single range means little without its scope. Rather than quote one out of context, we keep the detail in [our Bali cost guide](/expert-advice/bali-wedding-cost-the-complete-2026-guide). For venues, seasons and the legal side, start with our page on [planning a wedding in Bali](/destinations/asia/bali).

**Fiji.** For an island resort, the cost questions are about what the resort includes, how guests reach the island, and who pays for that transfer. Ask for the inclusions and the per-guest minimum in writing before you compare. Our page on [weddings in Fiji](/destinations/oceania/fiji) is the place to begin.

[IMAGE: asia_pacific]

**Further afield.** We do not quote figures for [Ras Al Khaimah](/destinations/middle-east/ras-al-khaimah), [Qatar](/get-wedded-in-qatar) or [Mexico](/destinations/latin-america-caribbean/mexico) in this guide. Where a resort hosts the wedding, the same discipline applies: get the package inclusions, the minimum spend and the currency of the contract on paper, then add the costs in the next section.

## The costs no average includes

Every figure above leaves something out. This is the part of the budget that rarely appears in a headline number, and it is where an overseas wedding either holds its shape or drifts.

### Currency and deposits

If your contracts are in euros or US dollars and your savings are in Australian dollars, your budget is really two budgets. Record each line in the currency of its contract, then note the Australian dollar amount on the day each payment actually leaves your account. Agree a deposit schedule with each supplier, and keep one list of every due date in one place, so no payment arrives as a surprise.

### Mark-ups and what to get in writing

The best protection against a quote that grows is a quote that is complete. Ask each supplier to confirm in writing:

- what is included and what is extra;
- whether tax is included (Italian VAT, for example, is 22% on most services, according to our Lake Como budget guide);
- the payment schedule, the currency and who pays the bank fees;
- the cancellation and postponement terms.

Extra days belong on the same list. A welcome aperitivo and a farewell lunch can add 25 to 40% on top of the wedding-day total, so price them as their own events.

### Room blocks

Holding a block of rooms for guests protects availability, but the terms matter as much as the rate. Ask how many rooms are held, the date unbooked rooms are released, and what you owe if guests book fewer. Our team handles [guest travel and room blocks](/travel), and at partner, preferred and consortia properties, Wonderland Benefits can include negotiated group rates, a property credit per booked room and upgrades. Benefits vary by hotel and by program.

### Transfers and the wet-weather plan

Boats, coaches and cars between airport, hotels and venue add up quickly across a three-day weekend. Price every leg, including the late return after dinner. Ask each venue what its wet-weather option is, whether it is held for your date, and what it costs to set up. No one can promise the weather, but the plan can be paid for in advance.

### Legal paperwork

If you want your marriage legally recognised, build the paperwork into both your timeline and your budget, including translations and any document fees. Requirements differ by country and by passport, so confirm them for your own passports with the destination's registering office and the Australian embassy or consulate there.

## How to work out your own number

A sound estimate starts with people, not places. Work through it in this order:

1. **Fix a guest range first.** Write down a low and a high count. Every later number depends on it.
2. **Price per head at the venue, in its currency.** Ask your shortlisted venues for a per-guest figure in the currency of their contract, and multiply by both ends of your range.
3. **Add the extra days.** Price the welcome evening and farewell lunch as separate events, not as a percentage you hope will hold.
4. **Add the costs no average includes.** Rooms you are covering, transfers, the wet-weather plan, legal paperwork, any planner fee, and tax where quotes exclude it.
5. **Hold a contingency and a currency buffer.** Decide the size of each with whoever is paying, and keep them as separate lines so you can see what each one absorbs.
6. **Keep it in one place.** [Wonderland Studio](/wonderland-studio) includes a free multi-currency budget planner, alongside your guest list and timeline, with no credit card required.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a destination wedding cost?

It depends on the place, the guest count and the number of days. Our Italy cost guide reports that foreign couples spent an average of €67,000 per wedding in Italy in 2025, citing Florence's Centro Studi Turistici. Our Lake Como budget guide estimates €80,000 to €150,000 for a three-day celebration with 60 guests (2026 to 2027), before flights and guest rooms. Treat any average as a starting point, then check what it leaves out.

### What does a destination wedding cost per guest?

Per-guest thinking is the clearest way to compare. Our Italy cost guide puts a high-quality wedding at about €800 to €1,200 per guest in 2026, which it shows as around €40,000 to €60,000 for 50 guests and €80,000 to €120,000 for 100. Guest travel and rooms are on top of that, whoever pays for them.

### Is a destination wedding cheaper than a wedding in Australia?

Not reliably. An overseas wedding is shaped differently: you may host fewer people at a higher spend per guest, and add costs a wedding at home never has, such as guest rooms, transfers, extra days and vendors who travel with you. Our [line-by-line comparison of overseas and home budgets](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-vs-home-cost) sets the two side by side.

### Who pays for guests' travel at a destination wedding?

There is no fixed rule, so it is your decision. Write down which of flights, rooms, transfers and the welcome dinner you will cover, and which guests book themselves, and put it on your wedding website early. Clear terms help guests plan and help whoever is paying keep the budget steady.

### Which costs are usually left out of destination wedding averages?

Guest rooms and room block terms, transfers, a wet-weather plan, legal paperwork, planner fees, VAT and the movement of the Australian dollar against the contract currency. Our Lake Como budget guide, for example, excludes flights, attire and guest accommodation, and notes that a welcome aperitivo and farewell lunch can add 25 to 40% to the wedding-day total.
