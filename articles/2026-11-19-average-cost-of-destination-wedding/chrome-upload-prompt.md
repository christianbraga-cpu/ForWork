## Article body

[IMAGE: destination-wedding-cost-italy-villa-terrace-table-01.webp]

You have a guest list taking shape, a feeling for the kind of weekend you want, and a rough sense of what the same wedding would cost in Australia. The next question is what it costs overseas. Search for the average cost of a destination wedding and you will find plenty of numbers, but few say what they include, which year they come from or which currency they started in.

This guide takes the other approach. It sets out what our own cost guides show, region by region, with every figure dated and in its original currency. It explains how an overseas wedding compares with one at home, the costs no average includes, and a calm way to work out your own number. Sources current as at November 2026.

## What an average destination wedding cost includes

There is no single honest average for a destination wedding, because published figures mix elopements, all-inclusive resort packages and three-day celebrations for 100 guests. The most useful benchmark we hold is for Italy: [our Italy cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-wedding-in-italy-cost) reports that foreign couples spent an average of €67,000 per wedding there in 2025, citing Florence's Centro Studi Turistici.

That figure was a rise of almost 9% on the previous year. As an average, it blends very different weddings, which is why it rarely matches the plan in front of you.

For a larger celebration, per-guest spend is the better guide. Our Italy cost guide (updated November 2026) puts a high-quality wedding at about €800 to €1,200 per guest, which it shows as around €80,000 to €120,000 for 100 guests. That sits above the €67,000 average, so ask whether any figure you compare includes travel.

So before you compare any figure, ask what it covers. [Our Lake Como budget guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost) is explicit: its estimates are for a three-day celebration and exclude flights, attire and guest accommodation, and it describes them as planning estimates, not quotes. Check three things in any average you read:

- **Your own travel.** Are the couple's flights and rooms inside the figure, or on top?
- **Your guests' travel.** Is any guest travel counted, or is it treated as their cost alone?
- **The planner.** Is a planner's fee included, or is it a separate line?

## How a destination wedding compares with a wedding in Australia

Is a wedding overseas cheaper than one at home? Not in a straight line. The comparison turns on five things, and each moves in its own direction.

- **Guest numbers.** This is one of the biggest levers, at home or abroad. Our Italy guide shows around €40,000 to €60,000 for 50 guests and €80,000 to €120,000 for 100, at the per-guest rates above. Each name you add or remove moves the total by a full per-head amount.
- **Travel.** At home, guests drive or take a domestic flight. Overseas, someone pays for long-haul flights, and you decide who.
- **Accommodation.** A wedding at home rarely needs a room block. Abroad, guest rooms become part of the plan, even when guests pay for their own.
- **Length of celebration.** A home wedding can be a single evening. Abroad, welcome drinks and a farewell lunch turn it into a weekend, and our Lake Como guide notes those two events can add 25 to 40% on top of the wedding-day total.
- **Vendors who travel.** If your photographer, celebrant or hair and makeup artist flies out with you, their flights, rooms and travel days become your cost.

[IMAGE: couple-planning-wedding-budget-sydney-table-01.webp]

The honest answer is that an overseas wedding is shaped differently rather than reliably cheaper. You may host fewer people at a higher spend per guest, with costs a home wedding never carries. We set the two side by side in [our line-by-line comparison of overseas and home budgets](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-vs-home-cost), and [our overview of what weddings cost overall](/expert-advice/how-much-does-the-average-wedding-cost) covers the wider picture.

**Who pays for what.** No rule binds you here, so decide early and say it plainly. Write down which of flights, rooms, transfers and the welcome dinner you will cover, and which your guests will book themselves, then put it on your wedding website well before invitations go out. Guests plan around clarity, and so does whoever is paying.

## Europe, from Italy and Lake Como to the Greek islands

Europe is where our most detailed cost guides sit, and where the picture of long tables, old villas and evenings that run late tends to begin. The figures below come from our own cost guides, each in euros and dated.

**Italy.** Our Italy guide shows real weddings running from about €20,000 to over €120,000 in 2026 prices, depending on region, season and guest count. The range is wide because each of those three moves it, so two weddings in Italy can sit at opposite ends. For the detail by region and guest count, read [our Italy cost guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-wedding-in-italy-cost), and for venues, seasons and the legal steps, start with our page on [marrying in Italy](/destinations/europe/italy).

Figures here stay in the currency each guide uses. Convert at the rate on the day you pay, not the day you plan, and build in a buffer for the exchange rate. The section on currency and deposits below explains why that buffer matters.

**Lake Como.** Our Lake Como budget guide puts many 60-guest weddings at €80,000 to €150,000 for 2026 to 2027, before flights and guest rooms. The same guide notes that Italian VAT is 22% on most services, so check whether each quote includes it. The full breakdown, by guest count and line by line, is in [our Lake Como budget guide](/expert-advice/how-much-does-a-lake-como-wedding-cost).

**Amalfi Coast.** Our [Amalfi Coast cost guide](/real-weddings/amalfi-coast-wedding-cost) puts a wedding for 60 to 80 guests at €60,000 to €110,000 or more, with luxury multi-day celebrations at landmark villas running €150,000 to €450,000 or more. The guide was published in November 2026.

## Bali, the Pacific and further afield

Closer to home, the questions change. Instead of a villa weekend in euros, you are weighing villa buyouts, resort minimums and how many of your guests will fly.

**Bali.** Bali costs vary widely, from an intimate ceremony to a full estate event with production brought in, and any single range means little without its scope. The detail sits in [our Bali cost guide](/expert-advice/bali-wedding-cost-the-complete-2026-guide). For venues, seasons and the legal side, start with our page on [planning a wedding in Bali](/destinations/asia/bali).

**The Maldives and Fiji.** Our [Maldives cost guide](/real-weddings/maldives-wedding-cost) puts a resort wedding for 20 guests at US$25,000 to US$60,000 all-in. For [Fiji](/destinations/oceania/fiji), our [Fiji cost breakdown](/real-weddings/fiji-wedding-cost) gives a typical range of FJD 10,000 to 30,000, in Fijian dollars. **Further afield.** The same discipline applies whether you are drawn to [Ras Al Khaimah](/destinations/middle-east/ras-al-khaimah), [Qatar](/get-wedded-in-qatar) or [Mexico](/destinations/latin-america-caribbean/mexico). For destinations without a published cost guide, ask for an itemised quote. Get the inclusions, any minimum spend and the currency of the contract on paper, ask how guests reach the venue and who pays for that transfer, then add the costs in the next section.

## The costs no average includes

Every figure above leaves something out. This is the part of the budget that rarely appears in a headline number, and it is where an overseas wedding either holds its shape or drifts. Budget for travel insurance too: Smartraveller calls [the right travel insurance](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance) as essential as a passport, and suggests reading the Product Disclosure Statement before you buy. Smartraveller notes that [medical assistance is usually the highest unexpected cost for Australians overseas](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/travel-essentials/travel-insurance).

### Currency and deposits

If your contracts are in euros or US dollars and your savings are in Australian dollars, your budget is really two budgets. Record each line in the currency of its contract, then note the Australian dollar amount on the day each payment actually leaves your account. Agree a deposit schedule with each supplier, and keep one list of every due date in one place, so no payment arrives as a surprise.

### Mark-ups and what to get in writing

The best protection against a quote that grows is a quote that is complete. Ask each supplier to confirm in writing:

- what is included and what is extra;
- the tax position, and check whether each quote includes VAT;
- the payment schedule, the currency and who pays the bank fees;
- the cancellation and postponement terms;
- an English version of any contract written in another language, or time to have it translated before you sign.

Extra days belong on the same list. Our 2026 to 2027 Lake Como budget guide notes that a welcome aperitivo and a farewell lunch can add 25 to 40% on top of the wedding-day total, so price them as their own events.

### Room blocks

Holding a block of rooms for guests helps secure availability, but the terms matter as much as the rate. Ask how many rooms are held, the date unbooked rooms are released, and what you owe if guests book fewer. Our team handles [guest travel and room blocks](/travel), and at partner, preferred and consortia properties, Wonderland Benefits can include negotiated group rates, a property credit per booked room and upgrades. Benefits vary by hotel and by program.

[IMAGE: italian-lake-jetty-guest-transfer-boats-01.webp]

### Transfers and the wet-weather plan

Boats, coaches and cars between airport, hotels and venue add up quickly across a three-day weekend. Price every leg, including the late return after dinner. Ask each venue what its wet-weather option is, whether it is held for your date, and what it costs to set up. No one can promise the weather, but the plan can be paid for in advance.

### Legal paperwork

If you want your marriage legally recognised, build the paperwork into both your timeline and your budget. Requirements are set by the destination, so confirm them for your own passports with the destination's registering office and the Australian embassy or consulate there.

Smartraveller explains that [an overseas marriage is generally recognised in Australia](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/planning-trip/advice-your-planned-activities/getting-married-overseas) if it was valid where it took place and would have been valid here, and that a foreign certificate may not work as proof of a name change.

### Planners and all-inclusive packages

A planner's fee may sit inside a quote or on top of it. Our guide to [how planners charge](/expert-advice/cost-of-wedding-planner) explains that planners quote in two main ways, a flat fee for a written scope or a percentage of the budget, commonly 10 to 15%, with some luxury planners at 18 to 20%. If you are weighing a resort package, read [what all-inclusive really covers](/all-inclusive-wedding-cost) before comparing it with a villa quote.

## How to work out your own number

A sound estimate starts with people, not places. Work through it in this order:

1. **Fix a guest range first.** Write down a low and a high count. Every later number depends on it.
2. **Price per head at the venue, in its currency.** Ask your shortlisted venues for a per-guest figure in the currency of their contract, and multiply by both ends of your range.
3. **Add the extra days.** Price the welcome evening and farewell lunch as separate events, not as a percentage you hope will hold.
4. **Add the costs no average includes.** Rooms you are covering, transfers, the wet-weather plan, legal paperwork, travel insurance, any planner fee, and tax where quotes exclude it.
5. **Hold a contingency and a currency buffer.** Decide the size of each with whoever is paying, and keep them as separate lines so you can see what each one absorbs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a destination wedding cost?

It depends on the place, the guest count and the number of days. Our Italy cost guide reports that foreign couples spent an average of €67,000 per wedding in Italy in 2025, citing Florence's Centro Studi Turistici. Our Lake Como budget guide estimates €80,000 to €150,000 for a three-day celebration with 60 guests (2026 to 2027), before flights and guest rooms. Treat any average as a starting point, then check what it leaves out.

### What does a destination wedding cost per guest?

Per-guest thinking is the clearest way to compare. Our Italy cost guide (updated November 2026) puts a high-quality wedding at about €800 to €1,200 per guest, which it shows as around €40,000 to €60,000 for 50 guests and €80,000 to €120,000 for 100. Ask whether guest travel and rooms sit inside or on top of any per-guest figure, and decide who pays for them.

### Is a destination wedding cheaper than a wedding in Australia?

Not reliably. An overseas wedding is shaped differently: you may host fewer people at a higher spend per guest, and add costs a wedding at home never has, such as guest rooms, transfers, extra days and vendors who travel with you. Our [line-by-line comparison of overseas and home budgets](/destination-weddings/destination-wedding-vs-home-cost) sets the two side by side.

### Who pays for guests' travel at a destination wedding?

There is no fixed rule, so it is your decision. Write down which of flights, rooms, transfers and the welcome dinner you will cover, and which guests book themselves, and put it on your wedding website early. Clear terms help guests plan and help whoever is paying keep the budget steady.

### Which costs are usually left out of destination wedding averages?

Guest rooms and room block terms, transfers, a wet-weather plan, legal paperwork, travel insurance, planner fees, VAT and the movement of the Australian dollar against the contract currency. Our Lake Como budget guide, for example, excludes flights, attire and guest accommodation, and notes that a welcome aperitivo and farewell lunch can add 25 to 40% to the wedding-day total.
