# Brand voice

Default voice for a luxury destination wedding brand serving couples who plan from
Australia. Items marked **CONFIRM** need the brand owner's approval; until then agents
follow them as written. If the research doc or page facts include a brand voice section,
it overrides this file for that run.

## In one line

A calm, well-travelled advisor who knows the venue personally and tells you
exactly what you need to decide, in plain words.

## Principles

1. **Specific beats superlative.** "A ceremony terrace facing the old town, 2 km away"
   not "breathtaking views". Every adjective must be earned by a fact.
2. **Plain, short sentences.** Most sentences under 20 words. One idea per sentence.
3. **Answer the real question.** Costs, guest numbers, wet-weather plans, travel time,
   legal steps. Say what we know and say who confirms the rest (the couple's planner).
4. **Quiet confidence.** No exclamation marks. No urgency tricks ("only 2 dates left!").
   No discount language.
5. **Written for Australians planning from far away.** AUD, time zones, long-haul
   travel, guests flying in. Australian English spelling (organise, colour, centre).
6. **The couple and their guests are the subject.** "Your guests wake up on site"
   rather than "We offer accommodation".

## Mechanics

- **No em dashes.** Use a full stop, comma, colon or brackets instead.
- Australian English (en-AU). Dates: 7 October 2026. Numbers: 20 to 300 guests (not 20-300).
- Prices: `From AUD 38,550`.
- Headings: sentence case. H1 names the page subject (venue, destination, service or guide topic).
- "Luxury" at most once per page, and never in a heading. **CONFIRM**
- Distances in km, travel times as ranges with the source ("about 60 to 75 minutes by car").
- Venue names exactly as the venue writes them.

## Words we avoid

| Avoid | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| cheap, affordable, budget, deal, bargain, discount, save | Discount positioning | "from AUD", "what is included" |
| fairytale, dream come true, magical, once in a lifetime | Generic, every competitor says it | Describe the actual moment |
| breathtaking, stunning, world-class, unparalleled, nestled | Unearned superlatives | A specific fact |
| seamless, stress-free, hassle-free | Promise we cannot prove on the page | Say what the planner actually handles |
| book now, hurry, limited time | Pressure | "Check your date", "Start an enquiry" |

## Words we use

Investment (for total cost, **CONFIRM**), your planner, your guests, on your dates,
we confirm, set up for, from AUD, exclusive use, wet-weather plan.

## Example

> Weak: Nestled in breathtaking hills, this stunning venue makes your fairytale come true.
>
> Strong: The estate sits on a hill 2 km from the old town. Ceremonies happen on the west
> terrace at sunset. If the weather turns, the dining hall is set aside for your guests
> and keeps the same view.

## Calls to action

Primary: the action named in the page facts (default "Enquire about <page subject>").
Secondary: "See what is included", "Check your date", "Speak with a planner".
